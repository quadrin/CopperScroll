## A1c card 1: EDF revers plates resolve the "?" on segments 6–7, E1 split by the 3|4 cut, E4 on both sides of 4|5
- **Verdict:** SURVIVES. Tag kept: NEW TO THE PROJECT (readings); KNOWN BUT UNDER-DISCUSSED (scheme). **Confidence:** high for what the plates draw. **Cost:** cheap.
- **Evidence checked (300 dpi, revers foot of every plate):**
  - Pl. CXII (p. 404) seg 2: F2 … F7.
  - Pl. CXIV (406) seg 3: F8, F9, F10, a tick carrying "F 12" above "11", F12, then a partial green glyph on the right (3|4) edge. Pl. XXXII (319), the 1994 photograph, shows the same stacked 12/11 painted on the copper.
  - Pl. CXVI (408) seg 4: a broken "E" with its tick at the left edge, then E2, E3, E4.
  - Pl. CXVIII (410) seg 5: E4, E5, E6, E7, E8.
  - Pl. CXX (412) seg 6: E9 … E12, D1, D2, then a bare "D".
  - Pl. CXXII (414) seg 7: D4 … D7, then a bare "D" at the edge.
  - Pl. CXXIV (416) seg 8: D9 … D12.
  - 1988 previews: UC15246035 shows "E4" near the right end of cut 4. UC15245686 shows a lone "5", then E6, E7, E8 on cut 5. Both agree with the card.
- **Corrections and additions (none changes the verdict):**
  1. **Seg 9 (Pl. CXXVI, 418).** The plate shows a bare tick at the left edge, then C2–C6, then a bare **"C" at the 9|10 edge**. So C7 is drawn truncated, the same pattern as D3 and D8. The CSV's "C7 beyond the crop" is wrong. That makes three segments (6, 7, 9) whose last label is cut at the edge, although EDF's text puts a "?" only on segments 6 and 7.
  2. **Seg 1 (Pl. CX, 402).** It carries only the 1955 numeral "1" and no cut label. This closes the card's F1 dead end: F1 is drawn on neither segment 1 nor segment 2.
  3. **Seg 6's last "D".** It sits a few mm inside the edge, so "number cut off" is an inference from the sequence. Nothing truncated is drawn.
  4. **Seg 3 "F9".** It is drawn, and painted (photo p. 319), as an "F19"-like group. The "12" over "11" may be a 1955 correction rather than two labels on one tick. The card's wording ("one tick with two numbers") remains accurate as a description.
- **E4:** the seg 4 E4 tick sits about one mark-spacing before the seg 5 E4 tick across the cut. The two cannot be one label split by the saw. This supports the card's reading of "drawn (or painted) twice".
- **Literature check:** nothing found beyond what the card cites, and no hits in the repo.

## A1d card 1: C8 is on the loose left-edge chain, which is the 1988 CT10_FRS box
- **Verdict:** SURVIVES. Tag kept: NEW TO THE PROJECT; NEW TO THE LITERATURE (provisional, for the date bracket). **Merge note:** this card upgrades A1-3 (which R1 weakened to "link only"). **Confidence:** high (C8 on the chain); medium (chain = FRS). **Cost:** cheap.
- **Evidence:**
  - Pl. XXXIX (p. 326, 300 dpi): a two-piece loose chain lies left of the strip, and its lower piece carries a white "C8" with a tick (seen clearly).
  - EDF text p. 72 gives "repères de la découpe (C8 à C12)".
  - `private/A1/ov_CT10_FRS.png`: the box shows a long narrow cracked piece plus a broader foot piece. The outline is compatible, but at that resolution the mirror match is only medium.

  - Pl. LXXII (p. 362) is that film, with the left-edge pieces in place.
  - So segment 10's loose pieces existed as a separate lot that was brought to EDF and added by 22 Dec 1993. That fits the separately boxed 1988 FRS lot. The card did not use this.

## A1d card 2: segment 15/16 rivet-zone pieces joined in Dec 1988, separate in Feb 1994
- **Verdict:** WEAKENED. Tag kept: NEW TO THE LITERATURE (provisional), with a narrower claim. **Confidence:** medium (state change); low ("new breaks"). **Cost:** moderate.
- **Evidence:** I made my own pairs, `private/R8/c15_1988_vs_1994.png` and `c16_1988_vs_1994.png`, from UC15245152 against Pl. XLIV (p. 331) and UC15246593 against Pl. XLV (p. 332). The 1994 images show the upper rivet piece (two domes), a chip and the label-bearing lower piece as separate pieces on seg 15. Seg 16's plate is in three pieces in 1994. In 1988 each appears as one piece.
- **What fails:**
  - The 1988 previews show glossy old adhesive or backing across both zones, and a crack line already runs between the upper and lower parts on seg 15.
  - So "whole in 1988" cannot be told apart from "held by old adhesive in 1988". The card names this alternative itself, and the previews do not exclude it.
- **What holds:**
  - The pieces were joined in 1988 and separate before treatment, and EDF codes the edges "cassure récente".
- **Refinement:**
  - Annexe 4 also lists "**16 D2 (avec fragments rapportés le 22/12/93)**". So seg 16's loose pieces were a separate lot by 22 Dec 1993, which tightens the bracket to Dec 1988–Dec 1993.
  - Defensible claim: "joined (whole or glued) in Dec 1988; separate by Dec 1993 to Feb 1994".

## A1d card 3: segment 10 "huit rivets" vs 7 drawn positions
- **Verdict:** WEAKENED. Tag changed to **NEW TO THE PROJECT**. **Confidence:** high (radiograph positions); medium (8th disc = rivet). **Cost:** cheap (EDF in hand).
- **Kill test run (EDF radiographs):**
  - Annexe 4 and text p. 71 point to Pl. LXXII–LXXIV (pp. 362–364; 10 D2, 10 C, 10 G, before treatment) and Pl. CCXXX–CCXXXII (pp. 531–533; Q10 D, G, C, 1997 after treatment).
  - On **Q10C (Pl. CCXXXII)** the overlap band shows round discs and holes at height fractions **≈0.06, 0.24, 0.37, 0.48, 0.60 (hole), 0.73 (hole)**, plus a bulge at ≈0.87 on the band edge. These match all 7 EDF plate positions (0.05, 0.225, 0.335, 0.45, 0.586, 0.713, 0.872).
  - There is also a **fainter, smaller disc at ≈0.41**, between EDF positions 3 and 4. It appears on Q10C and, independently, on the 1993 film 10 C (Pl. LXXIII, ≈0.415). Fractions are measured on the film from the strip top and bottom, so a projection offset of about 0.01–0.03 applies.
- **Meaning:**
  - The verso part of the card holds: only the lower four positions are visible on the 1988 and 1994 photographs.
  - The radiographs offer a candidate eighth rivet that the plates do not draw. "Huit rivets" may therefore be right, with the plates omitting one, so the text–plate mismatch no longer shows an error in the text.
  - The 8th disc is off-axis and smaller, so it could be a corrosion nodule or adhesive spot. I would call the count "7 certain + 1 probable".
  - This also changes the spacing comparison with sheet 2/3: gaps of about 1.9 cm and 1.5 cm around the 0.41 disc.

## B2b card 2: five JSP 10 digs misfiled in WBADB; licences 733/773 crossed
- **Verdict:** SURVIVES. Tag kept: NEW TO THE LITERATURE. **Confidence:** high (a–c, the names); **high for 733/773** (was medium). **Cost:** cheap.
- **Kill test run:**
  - IMG_1146 shows the footnote marker "1" after "(איור 1)" on Hebrew p. 3, but the page has no footnote at its foot. The note is an **endnote on Hebrew p. 15** (IMG_1153; crop `private/R8/jsp10_heb_p15_note1.jpg`): "1 החפירה (**הרשאה 733**) נוהלה על ידי המחבר …".
