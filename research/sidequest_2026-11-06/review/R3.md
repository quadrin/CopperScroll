  - The sheet corners carry no printed grid values, so no marginal label contradicts the offset.
- **Literature check:**

## B3, card 3: one lead weight, two dates (CIIP 3679 year 205 vs Maresha IV "last series", year 200); conflict with CIIP 3675 (Sosibius, year 201)

- **Verdict:** WEAKENED
- **Confidence:** medium (object identity); low (that a real chronological conflict exists)
- **Cost to confirm:** needs new data (full text of Finkielsztejn, INR 17, 2022, Note C)
- **Evidence checked:**
  - CIIP p. 378: 3675 "Lead weight of agoranomos Sosibius, 112/1 BC", LΑΣ = "Year 201", SC 169.
  - CIIP pp. 379–380: 3676 commentary, "the digit E (5) was engraved larger and thicker into the mold than the Σ (200). Hence the original dating … seems to have been changed later on".
  - CIIP p. 382: 3679 SC 97, "courtyard east of Room 3", exc. reg. 97-01-101-M1.
  - Maresha IV PDF p. 259: "3. 9/98 01.101 1. M1", "a lead weight of the last series used in Maresha"; "recently redated to the year 200 SE, i.e. 113/112 BCE"; damnatio memoriae; conquest "112/111 or 111/110" (Finkielsztejn 2022:86–92 on p. 260).
- **Why weakened:**
  - (a) Maresha IV never names Agathocles. The identity rests on SC 97 + 01.101 + M1, and the text layer prints "9/98", not "97". The identity is probable, not certain.
  - (b) The "two dates" are not a conflict between two publications. CIIP (2018) itself shows the ΣΕ was recut from an original Σ (200), and Finkielsztejn's 2022 redating to year 200 follows from that. This is a dated re-reading, not a contradiction.
  - (c) The Sosibius tension depends on reading Maresha IV's "last series" literally. Maresha IV's conquest date (112/111 or 111/110) fits Sosibius' year 201 (112/1) just as well. Finkielsztejn, who authored both the 1998 and 2022 papers and the Maresha weight catalogues, will have Sosibius in view; the INR abstract (fetched) mentions only "a new reading of the date on the last series".
  - Without Note C, the residual point is a wording issue in Maresha IV.
- **Literature check:** web search "Finkielsztejn 2022 'Four Notes' Maresha weight Agathocles Sosibius date conquest Hyrcanus 112" found the INR abstract, Finkielsztejn 1998 (BAIAS 16, academia.edu 331038) and an Electrum article on Hyrcanus' conquests. None shows the tension. Repo: Agathocles appears only in the full-coverage CIIP note.
