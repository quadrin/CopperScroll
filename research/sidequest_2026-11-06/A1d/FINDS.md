FIND CARD
Title (one line): The 1955 label C8 of segment 10 is painted on the loose left-edge chain. That is the "seven fragments from lower right edge" the Zuckermans boxed separately in 1988 (CT10_FRS). So the chain belonged to segment 10 when it was cut, and was detached between 1955 and 1988. That explains why C8 is missing from the 1988 verso.
Lane: A1d
What is odd (2–4 sentences): The 1988 verso of cut 10 shows labels C9, C10, C11, C12 only. EDF's text and plate give C8–C12. EDF's Feb 1994 verso photograph (Pl. XXXIX) shows a long, cracked, two-piece chain lying loose to the left of the strip, and its lower piece carries a white "C8" with its tick. The chain's outline (long narrow strip, foot flaring to one side, transverse cracks) mirrors the 1988 CT10_FRS chain, which was photographed recto-up with its foot flaring the other way. "Lower right" on the recto is the left edge on the verso. EDF's plate draws the chain back in place at the lower left of the revers, with C8 on it, and its text records "un fragment a été replacé correctement dans la zone inférieure droite". The same check closes segments 11 and 12. The 1988 versos show B2–B6 and B7–B10. EDF's plates draw B1 and B11 at the edges that curve out of view in 1988, and B5 painted about 2 cm above its tick, as on the copper. Segments 16–23 carry no mark on their 1988 versos besides the painted number (enhanced crops of every foot and head), which agrees with EDF.

How to confirm or kill it (with data in hand, or what is missing): Overlay a mirrored CT10_FRS chain on the Pl. XXXIX chain at matched scale (crack positions should coincide). It is killed if the FRS box held a different set of pieces from the 1994 left chain (for example, if the 1994 chain also shows in a 1988 recto frame CT10_R* in place on the strip). The full-res 1988 verso TIFF would show the C8 area directly; it is not held.
Confidence: high (C8 on the 1994 chain; labels 10–12); medium (FRS chain = 1994 left chain, by shape and position)
Touches the Copper Scroll? yes (segment 10, the 9|10 cut and the C turn of the large roll)
