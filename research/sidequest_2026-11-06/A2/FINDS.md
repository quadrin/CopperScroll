FIND CARD
Title (one line): The published identification of Leo Boer's 21 March 1954 "Bedouin uncovering a vessel" as cylindrical vessel KhQ 1627 of L91 conflicts with the dated inventory sequence in de Vaux's notes.
Lane: A2
What is odd (2–4 sentences): The PEF/DANS caption (PEF P17 = DANS 12_7, dated 21-3-1954) cites H&C 1994 pp.320–321 and says the artefact is "most likely the 'cylindrical vessel' (1627) of L.91". On p.320, however, L91 is being dug down through fill on 20–25 March ("Du 20 au 23-3-1954. On descend. Du 24 au 25-3-1954. On descend toujours à travers des déblais"), and its floor is reached only on 29-3-1954. In the 1954 notes, inventory numbers track the calendar closely: no. 1500 comes from L96, first opened 23-3; nos. 1526–1601 are L89 pottery cleaned 22–25 March; no. 1624 is the L92 plate over animal bones of the 26-3 entry; L91's near-floor coins are 1579–1583. No. 1627 was therefore registered on or after about 26 March. L91's upper-fill objects, the ones lifted around 21–23 March, carry numbers 1504–1521.
Evidence (exact sources: file path / page / figure / frame / coordinates): H&C 1994 p.320, L91 notes and object list ("1627 : vase cylindrique"; "1579 et 1580 (près du sol)… 1581 et 1982 (près du sol)"), IMG_2989 (Drive `1_IbVzlNAfQ4O9rFPB1lKuBw3xUI2BF0i`). H&C 1994 p.321, L92 26-3-1954 entry and object 1624; L94 coin 1463; L95 1495/1578; L96 23-3-1954 entry and objects 1500/1502/1523 (same image). H&C 1994 p.318, L88 16–20 March with objects 1442–1499. PEF photo: `06 Imagery/PEF Leo Boer…/P17 A Bedouin man uncovering a vessel (5904070515).jpg` (Drive `1XoaZ1anH3w_0FxgGZb5slEBeFtgcl-qJ`). Anchor table: `L91_inventory_date_anchors.csv` (this folder). Private crop of the printed L91 entry: `private/A2/H94_p320_L91_entry.jpg`.
Literature check (queries run, result, tag): Web: "Wagemakers Taylor Leo Boer photographs Qumran 1954 locus 91 cylindrical vessel 1627" and "Boer Qumran photograph 1627 OR L.91 vessel Bedouin 21 March 1954". These found only the identification itself (DANS dataset easy-dataset:60496; Wagemakers ed. 2014 *Archaeology in the 'Land of Tells and Ruins'*, Taylor chapter pp.145–161, not read) and nothing questioning it. Repo `git grep` on HEAD for 1627 / "vase cylindrique" / Wagemakers: no project note. Tag: **NEW TO THE LITERATURE** (provisional: Taylor 2014 and Wagemakers & Taylor in NEA 2011 not read).
Why it might matter: The PEF caption is the only published link between a 1954 in-situ photograph and a numbered L91 object. If the link is wrong, the photo shows some other vessel, possibly another buried or set jar of 11–21 March. Candidates in the right number range include the L81 jars set "à moitié dans du cailloutis" (p.316; objects 1486–1493, with 1407 "vase cylindrique"). L91 is a cistern, so a vessel standing upright in it on 21 March would also be a stratigraphic observation that the notes do not record.
How to confirm or kill it (with data in hand, or what is missing): Kill it if the vessel was exposed on 21 March but lifted and numbered at least 5 days later. The notes show lags of that size: the L81 jars were seen 11–15 March and numbered about 1486–1493. So the card claims a tension, not an impossibility. Confirm it by comparing P17 with H&C 1994 album photos 302 and 304 (L91) and 314–319 (L81), and with the KhQ 1627 drawing or photo if one is published (Humbert–Chambon–Młynarczyk 2016 IIIA, bucket doc `Humbert-Chambon-Mynarczyk-eds-2016-…-31a119b3`, try `pdf-search "1627"`). Also check Boer's diary entry for 21 March (cited by PEF for other frames).
Confidence: low-medium
Touches the Copper Scroll? maybe: only through the project's fixed/buried-jar feature records (reading note, "Fixed jars, cupboards and concealed receptacles").

FIND CARD
Title (one line): Three misprints in the 1994 printed synthesis of de Vaux's field notes (L94 "2-4-1954", Feshkha L22 "4-4-1958", L91 coin "1982"), one of which bears on photo dating.
Lane: A2
What is odd (2–4 sentences): (a) p.321, L94: the entry opens "2-4-1954. On nomme 94 l'espace extérieur au sud du locus 92…" and is followed by entries dated 21-3-1954 and 23-3-1954. Its only coin is surface no. 1463, which falls in the 18–20 March number range. The naming date is therefore out of sequence, most likely a misprint for 20-3-1954. (b) p.361, end of Feshkha L22: "4-4-1958. Le chantier est divisé en deux loci 23 et 24." Yet the L23 entry on the same page opens "4-3-1958. Le numéro 23 est donné au bassin…", the L22 sequence runs 1-3, 2-3, 3-3, and then "4-4". This is evidently 4-3-1958. (c) p.320, L91 coins: "1579 et 1580 (près du sol)… 1581 et 1982 (près du sol)… 1583 (près du sol)". 1982 must be 1582.
Evidence (exact sources: file path / page / figure / frame / coordinates): (a, c) IMG_2989, pp.320–321 (Drive `1_IbVzlNAfQ4O9rFPB1lKuBw3xUI2BF0i`); private crops `private/A2/H94_p321_L94_entry.jpg`, `private/A2/H94_p320_L91_entry.jpg`. (b) IMG_3010, pp.360–361 (Drive `1DJkoRxmupLMC4FyVUjT-8Wa1oRNpfdH8`); private crop `private/A2/H94_p361_L22_4-4-1958.jpg`. A scan of every dd-m-yyyy date in the OCR (`Humbert_I_draft_OCR.txt`) found no other printed date outside the season windows, once the OCR's misreading of italic "1958" as "1959" is set aside (see dead ends).
Literature check (queries run, result, tag): No web or corpus hit for these corrections. The 2003 English edition (Humbert, Chambon & Pfann, *The Excavations of Khirbet Qumran and Ain Feshkha: Synthesis of Roland de Vaux's Field Notes*) is not in the corpus (only a contents-only PDF), so I could not check whether it corrected them. Repo HEAD grep for "2-4-1954" and "4-4-1958": none. Tag: **NEW TO THE PROJECT** (possibly new to the literature; not decidable without the 2003 English text).
Why it might matter: Photo-dating arguments rely on these dates. PEF P20 (Boer, 21-3-1954) is "taken in L.94", which is only possible if L94 existed by 21 March, as the corrected date makes it. A literal reading of "2-4-1954" would put the photo before the locus existed. The Feshkha "4-4-1958" would push the 1958 season about two weeks past its last other dated entries. Any project timeline built from the printed dates inherits these errors.
How to confirm or kill it (with data in hand, or what is missing): Check the same entries in the 2003 English synthesis and, ideally, the EBAF diary. For (a), check whether L94 objects other than 1463 exist in the IIIA (2016) object lists with numbers below 1500. For (b), the 1958 season end date in de Vaux, RB 66 (1959) is in folder 04 (de Vaux 1959).
Confidence: high for (b) and (c); medium for the corrected value in (a)
Touches the Copper Scroll? no

FIND CARD
Title (one line): In the 1994 repertory (Lot 2, p.370), EBAF negative numbers are not chronological, and the table contradicts the page's own statement of which numbers are glass plates.
Lane: A2
What is odd (2–4 sentences): The Lot 2 header on p.370 states that negatives 2 825–9 856 are large-format glass plates and that 11 430–15 778 are 24×36 film. Yet rows 42–70 list EBAF 9 820–9 849 as 1949, de Vaux, **24×36** (Grotte 1Q, camp des fouilleurs, cimetière tombes 1–2, "Qm., loc. 71"). Rows 26–41 list EBAF 6 995–7 953, with lower numbers, as **1953**, 6×9 or 9×12 (L38/41, the NW tower, L13, Grottes 4Qa, 4Qb, 5). So 1953 frames carry lower numbers than 1949 frames, and 1951 frames sit on both sides (6 553–6 562 Savignac; 9 853, 9 856, 11 430 onward).
Evidence (exact sources: file path / page / figure / frame / coordinates): H&C 1994 p.370 (Lot 2 table, rows 21–72), IMG_3015 (Drive `1YgEjD1tuVQsJIxzWj9-kfYNBXugJomWX`). Row alignment checked against captions: album 12/14/16 = EBAF 7 941/7 942/7 943 (rows 28–30; caption p.275 "(7 941). Façade nord de la tour nord-ouest…"). Private crops: `private/A2/H94_p370_rows21-33.jpg`, `private/A2/H94_p370_rows34-52.jpg`.
Literature check (queries run, result, tag): Web: "École biblique EBAF photothèque Qumran negative numbers chronology 1949 glass plates 24x36 Humbert Chambon répertoire" found no discussion. The 3 Nov reading note warns that EBAF catalogue-page numbers are not negative identifiers, but it does not mention this non-monotonic numbering or the header/table contradiction. Tag: **NEW TO THE PROJECT**.
Why it might matter: The reading note's next step is a frame concordance that separates "exposure date versus copy date". An EBAF number cannot be used as a date proxy, and the header's format ranges cannot be used to sort originals from copies. Caves 4Q and 5Q, found in September 1952, fit the 1953 date of the 7 9xx frames, so the 9 8xx block may be later registration of 1949 Leica film. If so, the 9 8xx block marks the start of film, not glass, in the register.
How to confirm or kill it (with data in hand, or what is missing): Read the full Lot 2 table (pp.370–394) from the page photos and test whether number order matches date order elsewhere. The EBAF photothèque register (Open Jerusalem `FR-EBAF/Ph`) is the external control. Kill it if the 1949 dates on rows 42–70 are themselves misprints, but the subjects (Cave 1Q dig, tombs 1–2) fit 1949.
Confidence: medium (the contradiction itself is high; its explanation is unknown)
Touches the Copper Scroll? no (indirectly: ASOR/EBAF frames of Cave 3Q are routed through this register)

## Dead ends

- `Humbert_I_photo_inventory.csv` is a file manifest (original filenames, Drive IDs, bytes, SHA256), not a caption table. It holds no loci, dates or orientations, so the orchestrator's within-inventory locus/date/orientation checks cannot be run on it.
- `Humbert_I_page_register.csv` maps only printed pages to images and PDF pages; it contains no captions.
- The OCR gives about 39 Feshkha dates as "1959" (pp.354–365), outside any season. The print reads 1958 (checked on IMG_3010). This is an OCR misreading of italic 8, not a source error.
- Sequential parsing of the album captions (pp.275–289) recovered only 175 of 538 caption starts, so caption numbers could not be assigned reliably. The repertory OCR (pp.369–411) is column-scrambled, so no automatic caption-to-repertory join on EBAF number was possible. Both need page-photo reading (about 15 double pages for captions).
- PEF P03 says de Vaux took an identical picture "about seven months earlier" (album 12). Album 12 = EBAF 7 941, 1953, de Vaux, so the claim is consistent. Dropped.
- The PEF captions P46, P47 and P48 cross-reference the wrong photo numbers (P46 cites "photo 46", i.e. itself; P47 cites "photos 22 and 49", and there is no 49; P48 cites "photo 48", i.e. itself). These are trivial numbering slips. Not carded.
- PEF P01 and P08 (31-12-1953; L41/38, L48/49) and P05 (L18–L37): the field-note dates put these loci in the 1953 season, so they are consistent with a December 1953 photo.
- Not reached within budget: Magen & Peleg 2018, Schulz 1960 and Masterman against the 1950s photos; the Allegro DQCAAS image content. No DQCAAS-derived product was made.
