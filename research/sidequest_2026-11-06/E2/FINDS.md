# Side-quest E2: a join nobody has made. Coin hoards (HA-ESI + project hoard catalogue) × Itiner-e Roman roads, 6 Nov 2026

**Join chosen.** Coin hoards × Itiner-e Roman roads. The key is position: HA-ESI map references converted to ITM, and catalogue lat/lon converted to ITM. Each hoard gets its distance to the nearest Itiner-e polyline. The null is the other excavations of the same corpus within 15 km (also 5 and 30 km). A second product came out of the same join: HA-ESI excavated road segments that lie far from any Itiner-e road.

**Novelty search before committing.** Searched for hoard + (itiner / "roman road" / "distance to road" / "nearest road") in main (`grep -rIil` over the clone, excluding .git/atlas-site) and in `/home/claude/swarm/branch_only` (1,876 branch-only files). The search found co-mentions only, for example the hoards_full row "Nahal Tirzah / Alexandrion road Jannaeus hoard" and a sandbox `targets.csv` text. It found no distance computation and no join. The 17 lane FINDS.md files in `out/*/` contain no hoard–road join (B4 mentions a road only for a cave height). The "Already done" list has "Roman roads" (GIS layer) and "hoards by weight / context / reverse match", but not the two together. Searched for Devora, Dabburiya, Ibthan and Kafr Kama in main and branches: they appear only in HA title and screen lists, and Devora only as a person's name in a geomorphology reference.

Files in `out/E2/`:
- `join.csv`: 136 hoards (42 HA-ESI + 94 catalogue, deduplicated by coordinate), with ITM rounded to 100 m, distance to any / Certain / Main Itiner-e road, the nearest road's name and certainty, and local percentiles against the excavation null at 5, 15 and 30 km.
- `ha_hoard_list.csv`: the 43 HA-ESI hoards I coded by hand (report id, closing date, period, firm/possible).
- `road_segments_outside_itinere.csv`: 18 HA-ESI reports of excavated road segments 2–10 km from any Itiner-e road.
- `scripts/`: the scripts (`roads.py` GPKG→ITM, `coords.py` HA map refs→ITM, `dist.py`, `an.py`, `an2.py`, `an3.py`, `near.py`).

FIND CARD
Title (one line): Coin hoards are no closer to Roman roads than the other excavations around them. Half of all IAA excavations lie within 1 km of an Itiner-e road, so any raw "hoards cluster on roads" pattern is an excavation-location effect.
Lane: E2
What is odd (2–4 sentences): Raw distances suggest that hoards hug the roads. HA-ESI hoards have a median of 0.67 km to the nearest Itiner-e road (firm hoards 0.49 km; Roman–Byzantine hoards 0.44 km), and 55% lie within 1 km. But the 4,341 other HA-ESI excavations are themselves strongly road-biased: median 0.99 km and 50% within 1 km, against 28% for 3,000 random points within 3 km of an excavation. Against the excavations within 15 km of each hoard, the mean percentile of the 115 hoards that have such a null is 0.483 (bootstrap 95% CI 0.427–0.535; Wilcoxon p = 0.56; the null expectation is 0.50). The result is the same at 5 km (0.513), at 30 km (0.486), for "Main Road" lines only (0.485), for the HA-ESI set alone (0.48), and for the 200 BCE–135 CE catalogue alone (0.49). No period subset departs from 0.5 (Roman+Late Roman 0.43, n = 11; Byzantine 0.48; Early Islamic and later 0.54).
Evidence (exact sources: file path / page / figure / frame / coordinates): Roads: `research/assets/plans/historical-landscapes/itinere_roads_gpkg.zip` → `itinere_roads.gpkg`, table `itinere_roads_simplify`, SRS 3395. 531 polylines inside lon 33.5–37.5 / lat 29–34, 8,750 km (220 Certain, 257 Conjectured, 54 Hypothetical). Hoards: (a) HA-ESI text `hadashot/derived/hadtext_pdftotext.tgz` (extracted copy `scratch/B1b/txt/hadtext`): 78 EN reports with "hoard(s)" and 88 HE reports with "מטמון", read by hand down to 43 coin hoards found in that excavation (list in `ha_hoard_list.csv`, for example HA 1219 Western Wall Plaza, 2 m from Emmaus–Jerusalem; HA 26340 Abu Ghosh; HA 26558 Nahal Shalva). (b) `research/comparanda/hoards_full/hoards.csv`, include = 1, with coordinates (129 rows, 94 distinct points). Null: map references for 4,383 HA-ESI reports, parsed with D3's `refs_raw.json`. D3's 36 known-bad references were dropped, and so were 139 reports whose EN and HE references disagree by more than 2 km. Per-hoard numbers: `join.csv` (`pct_d_all_15km` etc.).
Literature check (queries run, result, tag): Web: "coin hoards distance to Roman roads statistical analysis Judaea Palaestina hoards along roads". No regional study found. The nearest work is methodological: Viking-Age hoards against least-cost paths and reused Roman roads (PCI Archaeology rec. 348), and the Oxford CHRE / "Roman Coin Hoards across Frontiers" programme (Ashmolean), which has no road test for this region. Corpus: hoards_full README and RESULTS (context, depth and landmark only, no roads); no branch has it. Tag: **NEW TO THE LITERATURE** (no publication found that tests hoard proximity to roads in Judaea/Palaestina against a local excavation null). Wider literature not exhausted: INR/INJ hoard surveys were not searched page by page.
Why it might matter: (1) It closes a tempting inference, "hoards are road-side caches" (soldiers' pay, travellers, the scroll's "on the road" places), for this region. With the data in hand, hoards sit where excavations sit. (2) It measures a bias that affects any project test that scores HA/ESI sites by distance to Roman roads: an IAA excavation is about 1.8× as likely as a random nearby point to be within 1 km of an Itiner-e line. (3) The CI rules out a strong road preference (mean percentile below about 0.43), but not a weak one.
How to confirm or kill it (with data in hand, or what is missing): (a) Add hoards from IGCH, *Coin Hoards* I–X and Kadman 1967, which the project lists as blocked, and the ʿAtiqot hoard articles, then rerun `an3.py`. (b) Use findspot-precise coordinates in place of CHRE site-level points: 35 catalogue rows share a coordinate with another row. (c) A terrain-matched random null (Copernicus GLO-30 elevation and slope bands) was not run: no DEM was at hand locally, and the excavation null is the stricter control. (d) Kill condition: a larger, precisely located hoard set gives a local percentile CI that excludes 0.5 on the low side.
Confidence: medium (for "no detectable preference beyond excavation bias"); high (for the excavation-road bias itself)
Touches the Copper Scroll? maybe: it removes "near a Roman road" as a discriminating property of real ancient deposits in this region.

FIND CARD
Title (one line): Itiner-e has no road through the Mount Tabor corridor, but HA-ESI records two paved east–west roads there: a 7.5 m wide curbed flagstone road at Horbat Devora (8.3 km from any Itiner-e line) and an Umayyad paved road at Kafr Kama (7.5 km). 16 more excavated road segments lie 2–10 km off the network.
Lane: E2
What is odd (2–4 sentences): HA 122 report 1606 (Cinamon, "Horbat Devora") describes a paved surface 15.5 m long and **7.5 m wide in total**, with meticulously arranged curbstones and an upper level 4.7 m wide of closely fitted flagstones, running E–W north of Dabburiya. It continued in a probe 10 m east "on the known route". Its width matches imperial roads; the IAA's 2022 Acre–Sepphoris–Tiberias section is about 8 m wide (Times of Israel, 3 Dec 2022). Yet the nearest Itiner-e line is Sepphoris–Tiberias, 8.3 km away, and no Itiner-e road enters the Tabor area. HA 126 report 8521 (Kafr Kama), 6.5 km ENE on the other flank of Tabor, also exposed an E–W paved road with curbs, which the excavator dates to the Umayyad period. Itiner-e is a Roman-period dataset, so the omission matters only if the Devora road is Roman, and the report does not date it.
Evidence (exact sources: file path / page / figure / frame / coordinates): `hadtext/en/vol-122/01606_h-orbat-devora.txt` (map ref. 23576–82/73392–96; Fig. 1–2); `hadtext/en/vol-126/08521_kafr-kama.txt`. Nearest Itiner-e lines from `scripts/near.py`: Devora → fid 3232 Sepphoris–Tiberias (Certain, Roll 2009) 8,266 m; fid 3669 Ptolemais–Sepphoris 10.9 km; fid 3229 Legio–Sepphoris 11.7 km. Other gaps in `road_segments_outside_itinere.csv`: Ibthan, three reports (1794: roadbed with 1st c CE pottery; 25540: N–S "Roman road", 2nd–3rd c CE), 4.4 km from the *conjectured* Caesarea–Sebaste line (fid 3271, "TIR, Roll 1996"); Nahal Zippori 24871 ("remains of a Roman road", 4.5 km from Legio–Ptolemais); Nahal Yehudiya 26519 (Golan, walled unpaved road, 9.7 km); Nahal Kelekh East 109 (10.3 km). The excavated milestone sites agree well with Itiner-e (Rantis NW 1826: 24 m; Eshkol Reservoir 11631: 3 m; Kh. Burnat 1411: 374 m). So the gaps are not a coordinate error in the join.
Literature check (queries run, result, tag): Web: "Horbat Devora Daburiyya ancient paved road excavation Roman road Tabor"; "Roman road Mount Tabor Daburiyya Kafr Kama Legio Tiberias route milestones Roll". No publication found that identifies the Devora road or places it in the Roman network. The results were news items on the 2022 Galilee section and Tepper's paper on Legio as *caput viarum* (title only, not read). Itiner-e's own bibliography for the nearby lines is Roll 2009 / Tepper 2018. Repo and branches: "Devora", "Dabburiya", "Ibthan" and "Kafr Kama" occur only in HA title and screen lists. Tag: **NEW TO THE PROJECT** (the roads are published in HA-ESI; that they fall outside the Itiner-e network has not been stated anywhere found).
Why it might matter: Itiner-e is the project's Roman-road layer (atlas `atlas-landscape-roads.json`, the Tobler walking routes, the GIS runs). A missing Tabor corridor, or a mis-drawn conjectured Caesarea–Sebaste line, would bias any route or distance measure in Lower Galilee and the eastern Sharon. The Devora road is also a datable target. The report gives sherds only "worn" and no road date.
How to confirm or kill it (with data in hand, or what is missing): Read Tepper's Legio *caput viarum* paper and Roll's Galilee roads (not in Drive as far as checked) for a Legio–Tabor–Tiberias or Tabor road. Check TIR Iudaea-Palaestina (Drive 07) around Dabburiya / Khan et-Tujjar. Check the Mandate 1:20,000 sheet and the 1940s RAF photos for the Devora alignment. If the road is the medieval Damascus–Egypt caravan route past Khan et-Tujjar, the gap is a non-issue for a Roman dataset and the card is killed. For Ibthan: the two Ibthan road directions (E–W 1st c, N–S 2nd–3rd c) may be local village roads, not the Caesarea–Sebaste road.
Confidence: medium (that the Itiner-e network has no line within 7 km of these paved roads); low (that the Devora road is a Roman imperial road)
Touches the Copper Scroll? no (regional infrastructure; affects the project's road layer only)

## Dead ends
- Ossuary names (HA-ESI) × Ilan lexicon: only about 18 HA reports pair "ossuary" with "inscription", and only about 6 readable names come out (Alexa bar Shalom, Katana, Akyla(s), Eugenia/Atochnia of Kyros, the Jatt loculus names). That is too thin for a join, so I dropped it before opening the Ilan spreadsheet.
- Hoards × roads by period: Hellenistic hoards (n = 4) are too few to test "roads follow older routes". All period subsets are within noise of 0.5.
- Catalogue hoards by how found (excavated / looted / chance / unknown): local percentiles 0.45–0.59. I checked whether dealer hoards geocoded to towns sit artificially on roads; the evidence was not strong enough to card.
- Excavated milestones × Itiner-e: excavated milestone sites lie 3–374 m from an Itiner-e line (Tamra–Yasif 1276 at 2.3 km is a survey-area reference), so there is no gap there.
- Map-reference QA: 139 reports were dropped for EN/HE map references more than 2 km apart. They were not examined as errors (D3 and B1b/B1c cover that). HA 1377 (Givati, 264 gold solidi) dropped out this way.
- My regex for "milestone" also matched "familiar". The output was checked by hand; no effect on the results.

## Blocked inputs
- Copernicus GLO-30 DEM: not found in the local clone or the bucket listing `open-sources/` (top level). The terrain-matched null was not run; the excavation-site null was used instead.
- shapely, geopandas and GDAL are not installed. The GPKG WKB was parsed by hand (`scripts/roads.py`); point-to-segment distances were computed in numpy.

## Seed / reproducibility
- Random-point baseline: `numpy.random.default_rng(42)`, 3,000 points uniform in the bounding box of the HA sites, kept if within 3 km of an HA site. Bootstraps: seeds 0–1999.
- Run order: `roads.py` → `coords.py` (needs `/home/claude/swarm/scratch/D3/refs_raw.json` and `out/D3/ha_mapref_errors.csv`) → `dist.py` → `an.py`, `an2.py`, `an3.py` → `near.py`.
- HA scaling rule for map references: 4 digits ×100, 5 digits ×10, 6 digits ×1. OIG is converted to NIG (+50 km E, +500 km N). Ranges take the first value.
