# Side-quest B1b: Hadashot at scale, HA-ESI vols 123–129 (2011–2017). 6 Nov 2026

Worker B1b. I treated the corpus as a dataset. Input: `gs://copper-scroll-cog-1030/hadashot/derived/hadtext_pdftotext.tgz` (pdftotext of all 4,667 reports, `hadtext/{en,he}/vol-NNN/<id>_*.txt`) and `/home/claude/swarm/baseline/reports_metadata.csv`. Following the orchestrator's note, I did **not** repeat D3's map-reference audit. I used a different key that the project and D3 had not used: the **IAA permit number**, the number the IAA uses to file each excavation's archive. Each report gives it three times, which makes it testable: once in the English text, once in the Hebrew text, and once as the prefix of the figure file names (`Images//<permit>-<n>.jpg`). The figure prefix matches the Hebrew permit in 3,752 of the 4,238 reports that have figures (88.5%), and the English permit in 3,727 (87.9%). Most of the remaining mismatches are surveys, which use S-licence numbers. Data and scripts are in this folder: `ha_permit_en_he_errors.csv`, `ha_permit_citation_errors.csv`, `scripts/*.py`. The scripts read the tarball unpacked into `scratch/B1b/txt/`.

FIND CARD
Title (one line): In 7 HA-ESI reports the English text gives the wrong permit number, and in 6 of them the wrong number is another excavation's real permit. Three of the seven are in or tied to vols 123–129 (Ramla, Ibn Sina School; ʿAd Halom)
Lane: B1b
What is odd (2–4 sentences): For each report I compared the report's own permit in English against the Hebrew text and the figure-file prefix. Seven reports give an English permit that disagrees with both of the other witnesses, and those two witnesses agree with each other. In six of the seven, the wrong English number is the real permit of a different excavation at another place, reported elsewhere in HA-ESI, so the English record joins two unrelated digs. In my slice: **Ramla, Ibn Sina School** (id 2066, HA 124, Toueg) prints "Permit No. A-6171". Its Hebrew text and its 10 figure files give A-6179, and A-6171 is the permit of Ḥorbat Qishron, Golani Junction (id 2253, HA 125). **ʿAd Halom** (id 14719, HA 126, Baumgarten, a 1995 dig) prints "Permit No. 6708". Its Hebrew gives A-2385, which fits the 1995 permit series, and A-6708 is the 2013 permit of Jerusalem, ʿEmeq Lavan (id 23793, HA 127). Two more wrong English numbers point at excavations in my slice: Revadim Quarry (25629, HA 131) gives A-6808 = Modiʿin, Ḥ. Ḥadat (9556, HA 126), where HE and figures have A-6806; Ginnaton (25581, HA 131) gives A7197 = Gush Ḥalav (23808, HA 127), where HE and figures have A-7191. The other three cases are outside my slice: Ḥ. Ḥanot 25933 (EN A-8357 = Gan Ha-Darom, HE A-8537), Ḥ. Tittora 25992 (EN A-8736 = Ramla, Trumpeldor St, HE A-8710) and Batn el Yamani 350 (EN A-3545 = Kh. Dinʿila, HE A-3407). A related case of omission: Tel Shaddud (25133, HA 128). The English gives only A-6949 and Nov–Dec 2013. The Hebrew also gives A-7047 and a Feb 2014 season, and the figure files are named 7047.

A-5915, A-6708"). PDFs: bucket `hadashot/pdf/{en,he}/vol-<V>/<id>_*.pdf`. Method: `scripts/permits.py` (own permit from the first "Permit" clause), `scripts/permit_enhe.py` (EN against HE, 67 raw flags, almost all citation artefacts), `scripts/triple.py` (EN/HE/figure-prefix vote).
Literature check (queries run, result, tag): Web: "Hadashot Arkheologiyot permit number error English Hebrew discrepancy erratum" and "\"Ibn Sina\" school Ramla excavation Toueg A-6179 OR A-6171". Neither found an erratum or any discussion; results were only catalogue pages ([NLI](https://www.nli.org.il/en/journals/NNL-Journals000346867/NLI), [JSTOR](https://www.jstor.org/journal/hadaarkhexcasurv), [AWOL](https://ancientworldonline.blogspot.com/2012/12/oprn-access-journal-hadashot.html), [Levantine Ceramics, Ramla](https://www.levantineceramics.org/sites/1621-ramla)). Project: none of A-6179, A-2385, A-6808, A-7191, A-8537 or "Ibn Sina" (as a report) appears on main or in branch-only files, and D3 audited map references, not permits. The live English pages could not be re-checked (see Blocked inputs). **NEW TO THE LITERATURE.**
Why it might matter: The permit number is how the IAA files an excavation's archive and finds. An archive request built from the English page for Ramla, Ibn Sina, or for ʿAd Halom would retrieve a different excavation's file. The project keeps an archive-requests tracker with 61 requests, so this is a practical risk. The test is cheap and needs only one language plus the figure names, and it catches errors that no single-language reader sees. Together with D3's copied-paragraph and map-reference errors, it suggests that the English layer of HA-ESI 2011–2021 was produced without a cross-check against the Hebrew.
How to confirm or kill it (with data in hand, or what is missing): Open the EN and HE PDFs for ids 2066, 14719, 25629, 25581, 25933, 25992 and 350 and read the first paragraph (about 1 minute each). The figure-file prefix is a third witness independent of either text layer. A case is killed only if the IAA has a corrected English version or if the Hebrew and figures are the ones in error, which would need two independent errors agreeing. Still missing: the live-page status (blocked).
Confidence: high (2066, 14719, 25629, 25581, 25933, 25992); medium (350, whose v118 permits carry an unexplained asterisk in both languages)
Touches the Copper Scroll? no (none of these sites is in a scroll region); it does bear on any IAA archive request the project makes by permit number

FIND CARD
Title (one line): Four reports in HA 123–129 cite earlier excavations by a permit number that belongs to a dig at another place (Bet Sheʾan, Haifa, Jerusalem). Two of the cited excavations have no HA report at all
Lane: B1b
What is odd (2–4 sentences): I indexed every report's own permit (`scripts/permits.py`). Then I checked each "Permit No. A-NNNN" that a report in vols 123–129 cites for *other* work against the report that actually owns that permit (`scripts/citecheck.py`, 61 raw flags, read by hand). In four cases the owner's permit is certain, because its EN text, HE text and figure files agree, and it is at an unrelated place. So the citation is wrong in both language versions of the citing report. (1) **Yafo, Jerusalem Blvd (East)** (1652, HA 123) cites "Jerusalem Boulevard (Permit No. A-5773)". A-5773 is Bet Sheʾan, El-Muntar el-Abyad (25160). The intended permit is almost certainly A-5733, Yafo, Jerusalem Blvd (West) (2329, HA 125), a transposition. (2) **Jerusalem, Qiryat Ha-Yovel** (7484, HA 126) compares a Mamluk/Ottoman cooking pot with one "at Khirbet el-Qatt (Har Homa; Permit No. A-6652)". A-6652 is Haifa, Romema / Ḥorbat Rosh Mayim (8532, Nov 2012), and **no HA report in vols 116–137 covers an excavation at Kh. el-Qatt (Har Homa)**, so the correct permit cannot be recovered from the corpus. (3) **Bet Shemesh, Ramat Bet Shemesh C2** (24878, HA 127) reports "two buildings from the Hellenistic period and a rock-hewn pool … (Permit No. A6696, A-6704)". A-6704 is Jerusalem, Beit Ṣafafa Area A (25356, 36 figure files), and A-6696 has no report in the corpus, so these **Hellenistic buildings are mentioned once and have no traceable publication**. (4) **Jerusalem, Shuʿfat (A)** (25163, HA 129) cites "Bar-Nathan and Sklar-Parnes 2007:57; Permit Nos. A-3955, A-4120". A-4120 is Herzliyya C (981, Feb–Mar 2004). Two further conflicts are weaker: Kh. Buleiʿis (1663) cites A-5091, which is the second permit of ʿAkko, Tel ʿAkko (North) (6465); Mishkenot Shaʾananim (1881) cites A-5356, which appears in the Tel Afeq permit list (25752).
Evidence (exact sources: file path / page / figure / frame / coordinates): `ha_permit_citation_errors.csv` (6 rows with the quoted citation, the owner and the verdict). Text: tarball `hadtext/en/vol-123/01652_*.txt` and `hadtext/he/vol-123/01652_*.txt` ("שד' ירושלים (הרשאה מס' A-5773)"); owner `hadtext/en/vol-129/25160_*.txt` ("Permit No. A-5773; map ref. 246492-749/710583-645", figures `5773-*.jpg`); real Jerusalem Blvd permits in `vol-123/01714_*` (A-5565) and `vol-125/02329_*` (A-5733). `hadtext/{en,he}/vol-126/07484_*.txt` ("Khirbet el-Qatt (Har Homa; Permit No. A6652)"); owner `vol-126/08532_haifa-romema.txt` (A-6652, figures `6652-*.jpg`). `hadtext/{en,he}/vol-127/24878_*.txt`; owner `vol-129/25356_*.txt`. `hadtext/{en,he}/vol-129/25163_*.txt`; owner `vol-120/00981_*.txt`.
Literature check (queries run, result, tag): Corpus: no report title or group contains "Qatt" or "Har Homa" with a permit near A-6652. The Har Homa reports are A-4933 (866), A-4821 (1210), A-5777 (1441) and A-5333 (1857), and none of them is at Kh. el-Qatt. "A-6696" occurs only in 24878. Project: "A-5773", "A-6652" and "A-6696" are absent from main and from branch-only files, and "A-6704" appears only as Beit Ṣafafa's own record (`haesi_features/phase3_2026-10-11/part_d_online/reports.csv`). Web: the same erratum searches as card 1 found nothing. **NEW TO THE LITERATURE.**
Why it might matter: Cited permits are how a reader goes from a passing mention to the record. Here they lead to Bet Sheʾan, Haifa, Jerusalem and Herzliyya. Two of the cited excavations are found nowhere else in the corpus: an excavation at Kh. el-Qatt (Har Homa), which had a Byzantine monastery according to the Kloner survey cited in report 866, and Hellenistic buildings with a rock-hewn pool in Ramat Bet Shemesh. For these, the wrong permit is the only handle, so they are effectively unpublished finds that cannot be located.
How to confirm or kill it (with data in hand, or what is missing): Read the citing sentence in the EN and HE PDFs, then the owner report's first paragraph (2 minutes per row). The Yafo case is killed if a Yafo excavation under A-5773 exists outside HA; that is unlikely, because permit numbers are unique. The Kh. el-Qatt and Ramat Bet Shemesh cases need the IAA permit register, an archive request, which is outside the AI-only rules: record it as a need. The scan covers vols 123–129 only; `scripts/citecheck.py` runs on any volume range (B1a and B1c could run it).
Confidence: high for (1)–(3); medium for (4); low for Buleiʿis and Mishkenot Shaʾananim
Touches the Copper Scroll? maybe (the Har Homa / Kh. el-Qatt area is on the Jerusalem–Herodium side; the find is about the record, not about a scroll place)

## Dead ends
- Keyword scan of vols 123–129 for "unique / unparalleled / unexplained / enigmatic / unclear function / unknown purpose / first of its kind / no parallels" (127 files, about 170 hits): almost all are routine ("a wall whose purpose is unclear"). I read the scroll-region subset (Jerusalem, Judean Desert, Shephelah) and nothing gave a checkable anomaly.
- Naḥal David "Cave of the Pool" (05384, HA 125; niche with a man-made gravel fill, linen, Roman finds): already in the project (`registration/zissu2023_extracted.md`, `haesi_features/phase2_2026-10-10`).
- Abu Ghosh year-4 hoard (24832 "114 coins", pers. comm.): fully published (Ariel, Marco & Betzer 2018, BAIAS 36; final HA 26340). Not "never published".
- ʿAkko mass grave with 44 Syrian tetradrachms (10590, HA 126): published in INR ("A Hoard of Syrian Tetradrachms from a Mass Burial in ʿAkko-Ptolemais"; [numismatics.org](https://numismatics.org/long-table-no-115-a-hoard-of-syrian-tetradrachms-from-a-mass-burial-in-akko-ptolemais/?hc=on), [INR](https://www.lockwoodonlinejournals.com/index.php/inr/article/view/2826)).
- Christian Quarter Ashkelon tetradrachm 64/3 BCE (12641, "first of its kind … outside of Ashkelon"): not contradicted anywhere in vols 116–137. Testing it needs the numismatic literature; I did not pursue it.
- Ḥorvot Gemila (01781): a single Tyrian sheqel near Shaft 104, not a hoard and not anomalous.
- Kh. Umm el-ʿUmdan synagogue frescoes "will be published in the final excavation report" (14718): only a promise of a late final report, not an anomaly. 13 Umm el-ʿUmdan reports exist (321 … 25704).
- Near-duplicate opening text across reports with different site names (8-word shingles, all 4,667 EN reports, `scripts/dupes.py`): it finds only D3's Tiberias/Ramat Bet Shemesh paste and the Tel Poran/Kh. Khaur el-Bak pair (same permit A-8127, same project, HA 132). Everything below a Jaccard of 0.3 is same-author boilerplate. No new paste errors in vols 123–129.
- Permit collisions between different site names where both languages agree (89 shared permits, `permit_dups` output from `scripts/permits.py`): almost all are multi-season sites, survey reports citing an earlier dig, or umbrella permits of the Robbery Prevention Unit (A-6979/2013-02/-03/-04 and A-7292/2014-01/-06 are sub-permits, not errors). One genuine "same dig under another name": A-4247 is the 2004 trial dig at Ḥorevot Sokho survey sites 2 and 4 (1968, HA 124), published in 2016 as "Naḥal Timna" (25019, HA 128, map ref printed with 5 digits, 19134/62786). It is low impact.
- EN/HE excavation-date comparison (`scripts/dates.py`): no excavation is dated after its publication. EN/HE month disagreements are minor: Maʿale Shaḥarut 1750 (Jul/Jun 2009), Nein 5426 (Jul/Jun 2011), ʿAkko 5455 (Jul/Jun 2009), Ard es-Samra 1877 (May–Jun/May–Jul 2010). English omits a season in Ḥ. Ḥadat 25377 (Jul 2003) and Tel Shaddud 25133 (Feb 2014). Note that the Hebrew "מאי" also matches inside names such as מאיר and שמאי.
- Publication lag by volume (`scripts/lag.py`, excavation year from the Hebrew opening): the median is 1–4 years throughout, and the share with a lag of 10 years or more rises gradually (5 in v124 to 16–21 in v132–137). There is no sharp break.
- Same site under two names in general: the project already grouped this for its regions (`haesi_features/merged_2026-10-12/report_groups.csv`, 1,883 groups), so I did not redo it.
