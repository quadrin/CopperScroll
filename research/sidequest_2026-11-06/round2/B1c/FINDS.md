# Side-quest round 2, lane B1c: English against Hebrew, HA against ʿAtiqot (6 Nov 2026)

Round-1 B1c compared two fields of the HA reports in English and Hebrew: the excavation year and the permit number. Round 2 compares a third field in both languages, **the era (BCE/CE) of every printed date**. It also takes the EN-against-HE test and the map-reference tests into a corpus no round-1 lane audited as a whole: **the 1,420 ʿAtiqot articles** (bucket `atiqot/text/`, vols 41–121). It joins ʿAtiqot to HA for the same excavation, and it closes one lead round 1 left open.

Inputs: bucket `hadashot/derived/hadtext_pdftotext.tgz` (R2's extraction in `scratch/R2/t/hadtext/`, 4,667 EN+HE pairs); bucket `hadashot/pdf/{en,he}/` (prints of 23 Oct 2026; two slips checked on them); bucket `atiqot/text/` and `atiqot/data/articles.csv`; bucket `hadashot/derived/hadashot_features_provisional.csv` (5,709 AI-extracted rows); the repository's `research/regional/haesi_features/phase4_2026-10-11/part_e_atiqot/` and `water_systems/`. Scripts are in `scripts/`. Data: `ha_era_slips.csv`, `atiqot_mapref_conflicts.csv`, `ha_vs_atiqot_same_dig.csv`, `atiqot_nig_oig_raw_flags.csv` (the unfiltered 26 flags from `atq_pairs.py`). Every site named here is a published excavation, and every coordinate is one the publications print. Nothing is private.

## Dead ends

- EN vs HE numeric tokens (all numbers, decimals and integers of 100 or more, near-miss digit pairs): 2,933 and then 1,630 reports flagged. EN abridges and rounds, and Hebrew bidi order scrambles ranges ("518 491"), so the test cannot separate typos from translation. Dropped.
- EN vs HE find-class presence (hoard, inscription, miqveh, hiding complex, coin, ossuary, papyrus): the omissions are wording ("coin deposit", "collection of minimi", "two baths" for "two ritual baths", "refuge cave"), not missing finds. Mosaic, seal, gold, skeleton and statue keyword sets are too noisy (tesserae; names such as Goldfus). No card.
- Ruler/era test on coin dates: only one slip (26634) in 4,667 reports. Numismatic dates are clean; the slips are in pottery and period prose.
- HA × ʿAtiqot count comparison (extending round-1 card 3): the Bet Sheʾan coin purse (HA 01191) gives no count to compare; the other exact own-dig pairs (01132, 25415, 26195, 26310) are not count-bearing. Not pursued further.
- ʿAtiqot volume-wide EN-summary vs Hebrew-article reference test (`atq_enhe.py`-style): 118 flags, nearly all because most volumes mix English-language articles that have no Hebrew twin. Replaced by a per-article check of the twin conflicts.
- Petaḥ Tiqwa Mulabbis (ʿAtiqot 119) n. 13 "Permit No. A-6414/2012": a correct citation of the Giv'at Oger ostraca (HA 26161), not an error. The three Mamluk ostraca are promised for "the final report of the site"; the 2022 HA report is preliminary.
- ʿAtiqot 55 and 56 "mismatched" titles (Tell Qasile with Ḥ. Gilan coordinates; Kafr Kama glass with Rehovot coordinates): the PDFs of Hebrew articles contain the volume's shared English-summary pages, so a reference can appear in several files. Each was attributed to its own summary.
- Khirbat el-Qut (26229) Bar Kokhba hoard "E. Kogan-Zehavi, pers. comm.": not searched again (round 1 searched once).

## Reproducibility

`python3 -I scripts/era.py out.csv`, `periodera.py out.csv`, `ruler.py out.csv` read `scratch/R2/t/hadtext/` (edit `T` at the top). `join_atq.py` and `atq_pairs.py` read `scratch/B1c2/atiqot/text/` (copied from `gs://copper-scroll-cog-1030/atiqot/text/`). Hebrew needs U+200E/F, U+202A–E and the private-use range U+F000–F8FF stripped; ʿAtiqot Hebrew uses ״ for ", and its numerals often come before their label (bidi), e.g. "16693/12773 , רי"י".
