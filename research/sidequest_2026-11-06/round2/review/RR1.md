- **Confidence:** high (5 discs on seg 15; 4 full holes on seg 16 aligned with them); medium (the 2 half-holes, and so the total of 7).

- **Evidence checked:**

## A1 card 3: segment 18's five "lignes parallèles" cross a separate corner fragment, stop at the 17|18 cut, and EDF moved the fragment without saying so
- **Verdict:** WEAKENED. Tag changed to NEW TO THE PROJECT (the fit of the fragment shown by the lines). The "undisclosed move" and the "post-1955" inference fail.
- **Confidence:** medium (lines across the fragment boundary); low (the lines stop at the cut).
- **Cost:** moderate.
- **What holds:** on Pl. CLI (p. 443) the five red lines run without a break across the thick-bordered corner square and on into the main strip (`p443_win.png`).
- **What fails:**
  - **(a) "Without saying so in its text" is overstated.**
    - The seg 18 text (PDF p. 75) begins "Le segment comprend deux fragments", notes "collages non jointifs", and lists "remontage des fragments avec des bandes de papier adhésif" and "collage des fragments".
    - The reassembly of the two fragments is therefore disclosed. Only the "Modification" line is limited to the lower left.
  - **(b) The "stop at the cut" argument does not hold.**
    - EDF's seg 17 *before-treatment* plate (Pl. CXLVIII, p. 440, revers) draws a **"bande adhésive transparente"** across the top of the verso. It runs from near the left edge to within about 0.8 cm of the right (17|18) edge, at about the height of seg 18's lines.
    - After treatment (Pl. CXLIX, p. 441) the same zone carries EDF's new red "17".
    - Neither seg 17 plate has a "traces d'utilisation" legend at all.
    - So the plates' silence at the matching corner is not evidence that the lines are absent. The card's own dead end mentions this tape but does not link it to this card.
- **Kill test still open:** a photograph of seg 17's verso top-right corner (Pl. XLVI, CCV) at high dpi.

## A4 card 1: the model's elevations are Schick's 1898 feet (datum line 2,420 ft); the OS puts the church floor 2.4 m higher
- **Verdict:** SURVIVES, strengthened by the kill test.
- **Tags kept:** NEW TO THE LITERATURE for the model comparison; KNOWN BUT UNDER-DISCUSSED for the Schick and OS numbers.
- **Confidence:** high. **Cost:** cheap.
- **Text checked:** I downloaded the archive.org text and PDF myself (`palestine-exploration-quarterly_1898`).
  - p. 153: "the datum line being taken at 2,420 feet above the Mediterranean".
  - "2,444 feet" and "2,408 feet" (pp. 153–154) are verbatim.
  - p. 145: "14 feet higher than the general flooring of the church".
- **I ran kill test (1), the 1898 section plates** (PDF p. 195 = facing p. 148; p. 201 = facing p. 152), rendered at 200 dpi:
  - The scale bar gives 100 ft ≈ 276 px. Vertical scale was checked roughly as about 1:1: the Rotunda measures about 30 m floor to apex on the plate.
  - Church floor above the drawn datum line: Section I–J ≈ 145–147 px ≈ **52.5–53 ft (≈ 2,473 ft = 753.7 m)**; Section A–B ≈ 55 ft.
  - So Schick's own sections put the floor at the model's 753.5 m, within about 0.3–0.9 m, and 5–8 ft below the OS-derived 2,480 ft.
  - This is the card's first branch: the gap lies between Schick's level system and the OS, not in the modeller's placement.
- **Caveats:**
  - "Three printed levels reproduce": one of the three is the datum itself, which matches by construction. The independent matches are X (+0.53 m) and the 2,444 ft group (−0.38 m).
  - My plate readings are ±2 ft, from a halftone lithograph.

## A4 card 2: round-1 void 3 = Schick's "St Helena's Cistern, Z"; the model reproduces Schick's cistern depth order
- **Verdict:** SURVIVES for Z, which is strengthened. WEAKENED for the Y part. Tag kept: NEW TO THE PROJECT.
- **Confidence:** high (void 3 = Z); low (Y depth / order). **Cost:** cheap.
- **I ran kill test (2), the plate facing p. 152, Section M–N:**
  - The plate draws "**Z, Cistern of St Helena**" under the Coptic convent, east of the Crypt of St Helena.
  - Its floor is ≈ 26 px ≈ **9–10 ft above the datum, ≈ 2,429–2,430 ft**. The model's void 3 floor is 2,428.5 ft (740.2 m). That is a match within about 0.5 m.
- **The Y part does not reproduce:**
  - On the same section, "Y" (under "Shops") has its floor at ≈ 12 ft above the datum (≈ 2,432 ft). That is shallower than Z on the plate and about 13 ft above the model's Y pit (2,419 ft).
  - The section may miss Y's deeper east end (1889: the floor falls east), so "Y deeper than Z" and the Y depth match are not confirmed.
  - Schick's sentence "Those under the church and behind the belfry come down to … 2,444 feet" can also be read as describing Y and Z. The card does not address this reading.
- **Supporting point the card missed:** the model has a source plane `Schick1889_PEQ_LargeCistern` (node 30; world x −91…45, z −11…22.5). It runs through the card's Y pit at (−25.2, 2.2), so the pit really is the modeller's "large cistern".
