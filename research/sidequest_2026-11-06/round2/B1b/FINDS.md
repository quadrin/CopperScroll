# Side-quest B1b, round 2: Hadashot at scale (b): permits, dates, conflicting coordinates. 6 Nov 2026

Worker B1b (round 2). Inputs: bucket `hadashot/derived/hadtext_pdftotext.tgz` (all 4,667 HA-ESI reports, vols 116–137, EN and HE; unpacked in `scratch/B1b/txt/`), `baseline/reports_metadata.csv`, bucket `hadashot/pdf/{en,he}/` (the 23 Oct 2026 prints of the live pages; R2 showed they record the live state), and bucket `atiqot/text/` (1,420 ʿAtiqot articles, vols 41–121), which I joined to HA for the first time in this lane.

Round 1 used the permit number as a key between the two text layers and the figure files (B1b-1, B1b-2), and B1c used it as a clock to settle EN-vs-HE year conflicts. In round 2 I used it as a **clock against both languages at once**, and as a **join key to the cited report** (DOI-linked map labels) and to **ʿAtiqot**.

Method in one line: permits (A-NNNN) are issued in sequence. Over the 3,886 reports with a parsed A-permit and start date (3,606 of them with EN = HE on both permit and year), a report's neighbours within ±15 permit numbers normally give its start month to within 0–1 month (median absolute deviation 0). `scripts/localclock.py` compares each report's stated start month with that local median.

Data in this folder: `ha_both_language_year_errors.csv`, `ha_permit_clock_other_flags.csv`, `permit_year_bands_r2.csv`, `ha_permit_citation_errors_r2.csv`, `ha_fig1_permit_label_errors.csv`, `ha_ghost_permits.csv`. Scripts are in `scripts/`: `parse2.py` → `clock.py`, `clock2.py`, `localclock.py`, `neighbors.py`, `licyear.py`, `citeyear.py`, `citecheck_allvols.py`, `doi_permit_check.py`, `ghost_permits.py`, `atiqot_join.py`, `samepermit_xy.py`. Everything here is a published, excavated or surveyed site. No coordinates of unrecorded features appear, and `private2/B1b/` is empty.

FIND CARD
Title (one line): Nine HA reports print the wrong excavation year in both English and Hebrew. The permit clock dates each one to the month, and the stated month always matches; three have an independent third witness
Lane: B1b
Round-1 link: extends B1c-1 (EN vs HE year conflicts, where the permit clock sided with the Hebrew). This card is the class that a two-language comparison cannot see, because both languages agree.
What is odd (2–4 sentences): In these reports the EN and HE texts agree on the date, the permit is confirmed by the figure-file prefix, and yet the permit's neighbours were all issued 2–6 years away from the stated date. In every case the stated **month** equals the month of the permit neighbours, and only the **year** differs. That pattern fits a year typo in the Hebrew original that was carried into the English, not a permit issued later. Across all 69 clock outliers (≥10 months, EN = HE), 60 are offset by a whole number of years ±2 months, where about 29 would be expected by chance. Of the 15 outliers ≥20 months, 12 are whole-year offsets (about 6 expected).
- **Jerusalem, Ṣur Bahir, Deir el-ʿAmud St** (27732, HA 137, Avner, A-6916): "October 2010"; neighbours Oct 2013. **Third witness:** HA 136 report 26484 (R. Cohen) says "An excavation conducted at Khirbat Deir el-ʿAmud in 2013 uncovered a section of a white mosaic pavement … (Permit No. A-6916)" (EN and HE).
- **Khirbat el-Keik** (13681, HA 126, Kogan-Zehavi, A-6814): "June 2009"; neighbours May–Jun 2013. **Third witness:** the report itself calls the 2005–2010 seasons (A-4808, A-5141, A-5769) "the previous seasons".
- **Tiberias, Shiqmim Beach Junction** (14710, HA 126, Yannai, A-5676): "June 2006"; neighbours Jun 2009. **Third witness:** the report enlarged its area "to connect it to a previous excavation (Permit No. A5057)", and A-5057 lies in the Feb–Mar 2007 band.
- **Kh. el-Khamis** (257, HA 117, Reʾem & Kahati, A-3636*): "May 2000"; neighbours May 2002. **Third witness:** the report says the work was done "as part of the work along the separation fence between Jerusalem and Bethlehem". The barrier was approved and begun in 2002.
- **ʿElabbon** (1633, HA 123, Feig, A-5878): "February 2004"; neighbours Feb–Apr 2010 (6 years off).
- **Bareqet Pond** (25904, HA 133, Barzilai et al., A-5711): "July 2007"; neighbours Jul 2009.
- **ʿUzeir, Survey of Har Ṭurʿan** (4377, HA 125, Cinamon): "January 2010" under **License S-235/2011**, so the licence's own year contradicts the date. Neighbours: S-239/2011 Jan 2011, S-241/2011 Feb 2011.
- Medium confidence (stated date *after* the permit, which a reused permit could explain): **Gush Ḥalav** (1604, HA 122, Getzov, A-3668), "July 2007", neighbours Jun–Jul 2002; **Tirat Karmel** (975, HA 120, Segal, A-3945*), "September 2005", neighbours Jun–Sep 2003.
Evidence (exact sources: file path / page / figure / frame / coordinates): `ha_both_language_year_errors.csv` (per row: permit, figure-prefix check, stated date, number of neighbours (16–25), neighbour median, residual, third witness, file paths). Text: tarball `hadtext/{en,he}/vol-<V>/<id>_*.txt`, first paragraph. I checked the EN and HE text of all nine on the bucket PDFs `hadashot/pdf/{en,he}/vol-<V>/<id>_*.pdf` (printed 23 Oct 2026), and checked the HE page 1 of 1633 ("בחודש פברואר 2004 … A-5878") and 27732 ("בחודש אוקטובר 2010 … A-6916") by eye. Third witness for 27732: `hadtext/{en,he}/vol-136/26484_*.txt`. Neighbour lists: `python3 -I scripts/neighbors.py 6916 6814 5676 3636 5878 5711 3668 3945`. The other 61 clock flags (mostly ±12 months, often EN = HE) are in `ha_permit_clock_other_flags.csv` and are untriaged. They exclude robbery-unit blanket permits (A-6979, A-7292, A-8577), the Tel Yavne umbrella permit A-8999 and multi-season permits (Sasa A-7450, Peqiʿin).
Literature check (queries run, result, tag): Repo main and `branch_only`: no hit for A-3668, A-5878, A-6814, A-5676 or A-5711; A-6916 and A-3636 appear only as the report's own row, with no date field (`haesi_features/phase3_2026-10-11/part_d_online/reports.csv`). No swarm card has these nine; B1c's six conflicts are a disjoint set. Web: "\"Bareqet Pond\" excavation Barzilai Milevski Oron 2009" found no date statement; erratum searches as in round 1 found nothing. The old IAA permit register (`antiquities.org.il/m_digs_eng.aspx?shana=2007`, which surfaced in search) redirects to the IAA home page (see Blocked inputs). **NEW TO THE LITERATURE.**
Why it might matter: The permit clock catches errors that no bilingual check can, because the excavation date is wrong in the source text itself. The size of the shift matters: ʿElabbon by 6 years, Kh. el-Keik by 4, Shiqmim and Ṣur Bahir by 3. Anyone ordering seasons or phasing a site from HA dates (for example, Kh. el-Keik's sequence of seasons) is misled. The test is general: any IAA record that carries an A- or S- number can be dated to about ±1 month without reading it.
How to confirm or kill it (with data in hand, or what is missing): Open EN+HE page 1 of each id (1 minute each). Then read the neighbour lists. A row is killed if the IAA permit register shows the permit was issued retroactively, which is documented only for the robbery unit. The register is the missing input (blocked). For 1604 and 975, a later date is possible if an old permit was reused.
Confidence: high (27732, 13681, 14710, 257, 4377, 1633, 25904); medium (1604, 975)
Touches the Copper Scroll? no (Kh. el-Khamis and Deir el-ʿAmud are Jerusalem-area sites, but only the date of their documentation changes)

FIND CARD
Title (one line): 18 more permit-citation conflicts across HA 119–137, of which 14 can be resolved. A new DOI-label test finds 14 of 464 "[Fig. 1: A-NNNN]" map labels that do not belong to the report they label; two are numbers no report of that date could carry
Lane: B1b
Round-1 link: extends B1b-2 (cited permits that belong to unrelated digs, vols 123–129 only, matched by name tokens). This card runs it on the other 15 volumes and adds a link-based test.
What is odd (2–4 sentences): Since about HA 127, reports cite earlier HA reports by DOI, which embeds the cited report's id (`jhaesi.116.2004.<id>`), and then give the permit as a map label, "[Fig. 1: A-NNNN]". So the label can be checked mechanically against the cited report's own permit (EN, HE and figure files). 14 of 464 labels fail, all in both languages. **Five are digit slips that point at a different dig or at an impossible number:** Kefar Uriyya 26665 labels Kisilevitz 2014 (id 13705, A-6146) "A-9146", a 2022–23 number. Ḥorbat Manoaḥ 27729 labels Ofaqim (25230, A-7775) "A-9775", higher than any permit in the corpus (maximum A-9486). Bet Sheʾan Hamadya 25157 labels 13697 (A-6838) "A-6383", which is Ẓippori (SW). Ibthan 24949 labels 1865 (A-6117) "A-6177", which is ʿAkko Youth Hostel. Naḥal Shalva 26558 labels 12656 (A-4968) "A-4986", which is ʿEn Yotvata. **Three have the right label but the wrong DOI:** Iksal 26620 links Alloney Abba (837) for Iksal's A-5272 (id 728); Rahat 27697 gives DOI 26206 twice, where the second should be 26224; Jerusalem Malḥa 26679 links "Beʾeri 2015" to id 35 (HA 116, 2004). In Tiberias 25159, the report whose English opening D3-1 showed was pasted, the two labels are shifted by one entry. **Free-text citations in the volumes round 1 did not cover:** Ramla, Birkat el-Jamus 505 (HA 119) says Y. Elisha excavated a similar installation under "A-4090", which is Nein (B) (Dalali-Amos, Jan 2004). The same report's opening says it was finishing "the excavation that had begun by Y. Elisha in August 2003 (Permit No. A-3971)", and A-3971 is report 657, same site name, same map reference. Ramla Railroad 1496 (Haddad) cites "A-4762", which is Ḥorbat Bet Sheʿarim. It probably means A-4752, Haddad's own Ramla dig of March 2006 (id 963). Ramat Bet Shemesh, Ramat Avraham 27698 (HA 137) puts the 2011 Area A shaft tombs of the Kh. el-ʿAlya cemetery under "A-6345", which is Palmaḥim (North), Nov–Dec 2011, 35 km away. That 2011 excavation has no HA report of its own (the 2012–14 seasons are 24916 and 27698). In Ḥorbat Tittora 26662 the link (26659, Naḥal Qedesh, A-9020) and the permit (A-8539, Tamra) point at two different quarry reports.
Evidence (exact sources: file path / page / figure / frame / coordinates): `ha_fig1_permit_label_errors.csv` (14 rows: citing report, cited id and its own permits, label, owner of the label, verdict, HE check) and `ha_permit_citation_errors_r2.csv` (4 free-text rows). Text: `hadtext/en/vol-137/26665_*.txt` "(Kisilevitz 2014 (…2004.13705)[Fig. 1: A-9146])", with the HE identical; owner `vol-126/13705_*` (EN/HE A-6146, figures 6146-*). `vol-119/00505_*`: opening "Permit No. A-3971" and later "Permit No. A-4090"; owners `vol-119/00657_*` (A3971*) and `vol-120/00753_*` (A-4090*, figures 4090-*). `vol-137/27698_*` (HE "בשנת 2011 … A-6345"); owner `vol-124/02079_*` (figures 6345-*). Method: `scripts/doi_permit_check.py` (label form only; the "; Permit No." form after a DOI is ambiguous and was not used) and `scripts/citecheck_allvols.py` (53 raw flags read by hand: 4 kept; the rest are neighbouring place names, umbrella permits or correct).
Literature check (queries run, result, tag): Repo main and `branch_only`: none of A-9146, A-9775, A-6383 (as a Bet Sheʾan citation), A-4986 or A-4762 (as a Ramla citation) appears. B1a round 1 listed the 505/753 shared number in a raw table (`out/B1a/b1a_shared_permits.csv`) and dismissed the class as "typos". It did not identify the intended permit or the contradiction inside 505. D3 and B1c did not check citations. Web: round-1 erratum queries; nothing. **NEW TO THE LITERATURE.**
Why it might matter: In the newer volumes the Fig. 1 labels are the key that ties each dot on the location map to a record. A wrong label sends the reader, or an archive request, to another excavation. The DOI-label test is exact and cheap (464 pairs, 3% error), and it can run on every future volume. The Ramat Avraham case adds one more excavation that is effectively unpublished: a 2011 cemetery season known only from a wrong number.
How to confirm or kill it (with data in hand, or what is missing): For each row, open the citing PDF at the citation and the cited report's page 1 (2 minutes). A label row is killed if the cited report has a second permit not printed in it, which the permit register would show (blocked). For 25159, 26690, 26448, 26532 and 25147 I could not decide whether the label or the DOI is wrong. Those are marked "conflict".
Confidence: high (26665, 27729, 25157, 24949, 26558, 505, 26620, 27697); medium (1496, 27698, 26679, 26662, 25159); low (26690, 26448, 26532, 25147)
Touches the Copper Scroll? no

FIND CARD
Title (one line): HA cites 410 excavation permits that have no HA report of their own. For permits of 2005–2017 (A-4300–7999), 127 are not mentioned in ʿAtiqot either and are cited with no reference, so they are known only from a passing sentence; 43 are in Jerusalem, Bet Shemesh or Judean-hill reports
Lane: B1b
Round-1 link: extends B1b-2 (Kh. el-Qatt and A-6696 as "unpublished finds that cannot be located") from two cases to the whole corpus; new as a corpus measure.
What is odd (2–4 sentences): Only 71.6% of the permit numbers A-3200–A-8799 (4,009 of 5,600, 2000–2020) appear as the own permit of an HA report. The rate is flat at about 70–80% per 100-number block, with no sharp break; the drop after A-8800 is publication lag. Of the missing numbers, 410 are named in other HA reports as excavations with finds. For A-4300–A-7999 (at least 6 years to publish), 256 are cited, 127 have no ʿAtiqot mention and no bibliographic reference in the citing sentence, and 43 of those are cited in Jerusalem/Bet Shemesh/Judean reports. Examples where the passing sentence is the only record found:
- Ramat Bet Shemesh: "Remains of a settlement from the Persian period (A-6993)" (25907); "Two buildings from the Hellenistic period and a rock-hewn pool" (A-6696; the clock puts it in Jan 2013) (24878).
- Jerusalem, Naḥal Gillo: "structures and installations from the Hasmonean, Roman and Byzantine periods were excavated in 2013 (A-6680)" (25562).
- Har Ḥoma: "Columbaria, a ritual bath, cisterns … documented in surveys … in 2005 and 2007 (A-5125, A-5196)" (22787).
- Beit Ḥanina: Kh. Hawanit Roman road segment (A-5580) (25494).
Evidence (exact sources: file path / page / figure / frame / coordinates): `ha_ghost_permits.csv` (one row per citation: permit, citing report, the sentence, ʿAtiqot files that mention the number, and the flag `window_4300_7999_no_atiqot_no_bibref`). Literature check (queries run, result, tag): Kletter & De-Groot 2001, "Excavating to Excess?" Zilberbod; [Ynet, Feb 2014](https://www.ynetnews.com/travel/article/4489754)), and that dig has no HA report under Zilberbod 2013–14 either (a lead, not counted). Why it might matter: The project's cave, tomb, water and feature inventories are built from HA reports. About 28% of IAA excavations of 2000–2020 have no HA report, so their features reach the inventories only through passing sentences like these, or not at all. The CSV gives the owner a ready list of permit numbers to look up in the IAA archive if the AI-only rule is ever lifted.
ARGUMENT (scroll consequence, kept apart from the evidence): The project's "absence" arguments that rest on HA-derived inventories (for example, that no recorded pool, cave or installation exists in a search window) inherit this gap. For the Jerusalem hills the CSV names at least five unreported Second Temple-period or Hellenistic excavations with features of classes the scroll's entries use: a pool (A-6696), a ritual bath and columbaria (A-5125/5196), Hasmonean installations (A-6680), and a Persian settlement (A-6993). A-7148, "a rural settlement from the Second Temple period" (25385), is also unreported in HA, but its sentence carries other references, so the strict filter drops it. None is at a scroll candidate that I could see, so this weakens claims of absence by an unknown amount. It does not help or hurt any identification.
How to confirm or kill it (with data in hand, or what is missing): A row is killed if the excavation is published outside HA and ʿAtiqot (IAA Reports, Qadmoniot, JSP, theses) or in HA vol. 138 (2026, not in the corpus). That needs a bibliography search per permit; I did one for Naḥal Gillo. The 71.6% figure assumes every A-number was issued to an excavation. The permit register would test that (blocked).
Confidence: high (the counts and the passing-mention list); medium (that a given row is unpublished anywhere)

## Dead ends
- Same-permit pairs with map refs more than 1.5 km apart (`samepermit_xy.py`, 20 pairs): all known or legitimate. They are the round-1 collisions, the robbery-unit umbrella permits (A-6979, A-7292), Galili's marine survey permits (A-3817 covers ʿAtlit 2003–07 *and* Tel Ashqelon 1998–2005; A-4076), the A-8635 sub-permit (Tel ʿAgol / Bet Ha-Bad St "A-8635-01"), and multi-site projects.
- HA × ʿAtiqot year join by permit (`atiqot_join.py`, 18 disagreements): all artefacts (project start years, bibliographic years, archive notes). The ʿAtiqot permit footnotes give no new date witness for the nine reports in card 1. Only A-3945 and A-4090 occur in ʿAtiqot, in acknowledgements.
- Licence-year check (`licyear.py`, 24 flags): apart from 4377 (card 1), the robbery unit's S-1058/1070/1071/2020 licences for 2017–18 documentations are retroactive, like its A-permits. The other flags cite an earlier survey licence.
- Cited-year vs clock (`citeyear.py`): after removing URLs (HA DOIs contain "116.2004", which pollutes any year parse; cf. E1-3), only one real flag remained, the 26484 → 27732 witness used in card 1.
- Kh. el-Qatt (Har Ḥoma; round-1 B1b-2): ʿAtiqot 89 (`vol-089/iss-1/1809_*`, the monastery map) places Kh. el-Qatt among the monasteries by Har Ḥoma, and ʿAtiqot 83 (`1708_*`) cites a Georgian monastery at "Bir el-Qatt" (Corbo 1955). There is still no excavation report and no permit. English and Hebrew web searches found nothing.
- A-6696 (round-1 B1b-2): the clock dates it to Jan 2013 (A-6695 and A-6699 are Jan 2013). No Ramat Bet Shemesh report in HA or ʿAtiqot describes Hellenistic buildings with a rock-hewn pool. Ramat Bet Shemesh (C) 5438 (A-6650, Nov–Dec 2012) has only Hellenistic sherds.
- Ramat Bet Shemesh (B) 2072, whose opening says "Neighborhood C": the Hebrew says the same; it is Area B of Neighbourhood C. Not an error.
- Citecheck rejects: Ḥorbat Kosit 534 (A-3716 is the Baqa al-Gharbiya dig by the same A. ʿOuda, as the HE shows); Hulda 26448 (A-4883 is correct, id 11621); Ḥorbat Neged 25446 (A-7832 is Sederot, 300 m away).
- Volume year against online date: consistent (5 HA 121 reports are dated 2008).
- Publication coverage per 100-permit block: flat; no sharp change in time.
