# Side-quest B2: surveys against each other (6 Nov 2026)

Lane B2. Region: Old Israel Grid box E 150–200 km, N 115–160 km (Benjamin, northern Judean Desert, Jericho plain). Backbone: the WBADB site table already on `origin/sandbox/methods` (`sandbox/west_bank_inventory_2026-10-31/data/wbadb_sites.csv`, 6,950 rows, 3,599 in the box). Its `survey_ref` field names the survey and site number for each row. Joined against: ASI 1 (Kochavi 1972; Kallai, Bar-Adon, Kochavi and Gophna–Porat chapters), Finkelstein & Magen 1993 (the WBADB chapters Finkelstein, Feldstein et al., Dinur & Feig, Goldfus & Golani, Hizmi 1993a, plus the volume's English site list), ʿAtiqot 41 and the other cave surveys through `research/regional/caves_entry25/caves.csv` (492 rows in the box), and IAA ASI online records through `research/regional/asi_jerusalem/sites.csv` (1,041 rows in the box). Exploratory. No identification claim. Nothing in the repository was changed.

Data in this folder:
- `survey_join.csv`: one row for each 1967–68 site in the box (386 rows). Columns: name variants, WBADB ids, ASI code, WBADB grid, code-against-grid check, later surveys co-cited in WBADB, best later record (source, id, name, grid, distance, name similarity), number of later records within 1 km, match method (`wbadb_cocited`, `proximity+name` (≤500 m, name similarity ≥0.75), `proximity_only` (≤250 m), `none`), and status. **For rows whose status is not `rerecorded`, the grid and ASI code are blanked (`PRIVATE`).** The full file is in `private/B2/`.
- `coordinate_audit_wbadb_vs_asi_online.csv`: WBADB rows joined to IAA online records by map and site number where the two disagree by more than 250 m (20 rows; the 83/1 numbering collision is excluded).
- `benjamin1993_english_list_vs_wbadb.csv`: 95 entries of the Benjamin volume's English site list (PDF pp. 250–266) matched to WBADB by site number and ASI square.

Results for the 1967–68 sites in the box: Kallai 106 re-recorded / 6 not re-recorded / 1 possible; Bar-Adon 92 / 71 / 22; Kochavi 43 / 18 / 2; Gophna–Porat 22 / 2 / 1.

FIND CARD
Title (one line): Two WBADB grid errors (Patrich 1994a site 95, about 6.75 km; Bar-Adon 1972 site 20, about 9 km) are carried into the project's water inventory, where one cistern is counted twice
Lane: B2
What is odd (2–4 sentences): Each ASI site code (`EE-NN/xy/n`) names the 1-km square that holds the site. In WBADB, 83 of the 4,992 rows that have a code have a grid outside that square (21 of them in the box). Most are small or explained (see Dead ends). Two are real position errors. (a) Patrich 1994a (ASI Map 109/7, Deir Mar Saba) site 95, a cistern in Naḥal Qidron: WBADB WBS:4146 gives E 188140. The IAA online record (survey.iaa.org.il MapSurvey/62/site/12783) and the code 18-12/13 both put it at E ≈181389, so the WBADB point is 6,751 m too far east. (b) Bar-Adon 1972 site 20 (Kh. Umm esh-Shughut): the book prints only one coordinate, the northing. WBADB WBS:1688 filled the easting with a round 190000. The site's own ASI square code puts the easting about 9 km further east (corrected position in `private/B2/`). Effect in this project: `research/regional/water_systems/water.csv` (main) holds both `ASI-109/7/95-cistern` (35.3304 E) and `WBADB-S4146-cistern` (35.4015 E) with an empty `dup_of`. The cistern is counted twice, 6.7 km apart. The same thing happens for Patrich site 52 (Naḥal Sekhakha / Hyrcania, WBADB 594 m off), whose three WBADB rows (`WBADB-S4050-aqueduct`, `-cistern`, `-reservoir_pool`) are not linked to `ASI-109/7/52-*`. By contrast, 54 other Patrich rows and all Kloner rows are linked. Bar-Adon 20's wrong point is also in `sandbox/imagery_gaps_2026-10-27/out/site_points.json` and in the sandbox WBADB inventory.
Evidence (exact sources: file path / page / figure / frame / coordinates): ASI 1 (Kochavi 1972) p. 107, site 20 (store `01-ASI1-Kochavi-1972-indexed-81b7cd39`, PDF page 55; photo IMG_1728): only the northing is printed. ASI online `https://survey.iaa.org.il/#/MapSurvey/62/site/12783` (Map 109/7 site 95). WBADB rows WBS:4146, WBS:1688, WBS:4050. `out/B2/coordinate_audit_wbadb_vs_asi_online.csv`. Water inventory rows above (`water.csv`, `survey_rows.csv`). Scripts: `private/B2/scripts/join.py` (`code_check`) and `asio_audit.py`.
Literature check (queries run, result, tag): Web: "Greenberg Keinan West Bank archaeological database coordinate errors errata grid reference" found no errata, only the TAU project page and sourcebook (https://www.tau.ac.il/humanities/abraham/archaeological-database.html). Repo main and branch_only: "Shughut", "188140", "Patrich 1994a, Site 95" turn up only the data rows, with no note of an error. Tag: **NEW TO THE LITERATURE** (record-level errors; no published errata found).
Why it might matter: WBADB is the only public join of West Bank surveys, and the project uses it as an inventory (the sandbox fifth inventory, the water systems lane, the imagery-gap points). Site 52 lies at Hyrcania, a project place, so counts of water installations near Hyrcania are inflated by unlinked duplicates. The code-against-grid test is cheap and covers the whole database.
How to confirm or kill it (with data in hand, or what is missing): For 95: open the IAA record above. Patrich 1994 (ASI Map 109/7) is not in the store; the online record serves. For 20: check Bar-Adon's own sketch map in ASI 1, or later CORONA/HEXAGON imagery at the coded square (not done). For the water inventory: rerun `build_survey.py` with site-number links that ignore distance, and count the changes.
Confidence: high (95, 20); medium (52: 594 m could be a different datum of the record point)
Touches the Copper Scroll? maybe: inflates water-feature counts near Hyrcania (site 52) and in the Kidron (site 95) used by the project's water lanes.

FIND CARD
Title (one line): The open-air structures of Bar-Adon's 1968 survey on the Dead Sea plateau and cliff foot (sites 116–139) have had no later survey: only the 1993 cave survey went back
Lane: B2
What is odd (2–4 sentences): Of 185 Bar-Adon 1972 site rows in the box, 63 non-cave sites (round and horseshoe-shaped structures, enclosures, paths, a fortress, a robbed cemetery) have no later non-cave record within 500 m in WBADB, the IAA online records or the excavation licence list. 35 of the 63 fall in one band, Bar-Adon sites 116–139 (N 115–120 km). There the only later fieldwork in the corpus is ʿAtiqot 41 Regions XIII–XIV (Tal & Oron; Dahari). Those regions recorded caves and rock shelters, not the open-air sites between them. WBADB therefore lists these sites from a single 1968 visit, and no later survey has assessed their state. Typical later-to-1968 distances are 0.5–4.5 km (`survey_join.csv`, column `dist_m`). A second, smaller cluster lies north of the Wadi el-ʿAuja plain (sites 17–25, 31–47). There the nearest later records are scattered Zertal and Spanier rows.
Evidence (exact sources: file path / page / figure / frame / coordinates): `out/B2/survey_join.csv` (status `not_rerecorded_*`, survey Bar-Adon 1972; positions blanked). Full positions in `private/B2/survey_join_full_with_coords.csv`. Sample entries read on the page images: ASI 1 pp. 126–131 (PDF pages 65–67: sites 114–144). The cave-only scope of the later work in this band is seen in WBADB rows citing Dahari 2002 / Tal & Oron 2002 (all "Cave", "Rock shelter", "Niches").
Literature check (queries run, result, tag): Repo: no file names these Bar-Adon site numbers apart from the WBADB-derived tables. `held_asi1_deirhajleh_2026-10-30.md` lists Bar-Adon sites only by page block, and the Kochavi arrival read sites 5, 13, 61 and 98. Web: not specifically searched (time). The absence of published ASI maps for this strip is a general known fact, but no source counts what is recorded only once. Tag: **NEW TO THE PROJECT**.
Why it might matter: The brief's site-protection rule applies: these sites are known from a 1968 visit alone, in an area the 2023 looting study names. For the project, the band from Qumran to ʿEin el-Ghuweir is where Second Temple outposts and graves cluster, and the only record of the non-cave features there is a 1968 page of ASI 1.
How to confirm or kill it (with data in hand, or what is missing): Kill it if any post-1972 open-air survey of the strip (for example an unpublished IAA/SOA map, or Hirschfeld's ʿEin Gedi map extending north) records these sites. Check the IAA survey site for maps covering E 180–191 / N 115–120. With data in hand: overlay the private points on HEXAGON 1974 and the 1940s Mandate sheets to see which structures are visible, without a site visit.
Confidence: medium (the count depends on WBADB's 2007 cut-off and on the 500 m rule)
Touches the Copper Scroll? maybe: the band includes the area around ʿEin el-Ghuweir and ʿEin et-Turaba (project context), but no scroll entry is tested here.

Title (one line): Bar-Adon 1972 site 138, a 30 × 30 m "fortress" and a robbed cemetery of nine tombs about 1.2 km NNE of ʿEin el-Ghuweir, is not linked to the ʿEin el-Ghuweir excavation and has never been re-recorded
Lane: B2
What is odd (2–4 sentences): ASI 1 p. 130 site 138 (code 18-11/95/1) records near the shore: (A) a fortress built of large stones, 30 × 30 m, with a trapezoid building beside it; (B) a cemetery about 300 m away, with nine robbed tombs 1.5 m deep and Roman and Byzantine sherds. WBADB keeps these as WBS:4459 and WBS:4473 with no later reference and no link to the ʿEin el-Ghuweir excavation rows (WBE:857, licences L-10/1968, L-25/1970). Point B lies about 1.19 km NNE of WBADB's ʿEin el-Ghuweir point. The excavated ʿEin el-Ghuweir cemetery is described as about 800 m north of the settlement (Wikipedia, summarising Bar-Adon 1977). So 138B is either that cemetery as seen in the 1968 survey before its 1969 excavation (then dated Roman/Byzantine by surface sherds), or a second, robbed cemetery. Neither the 30 × 30 m structure (A) nor the robbing is mentioned in the excavation summaries found.
Evidence (exact sources: file path / page / figure / frame / coordinates): ASI 1 p. 130, site 138 (store PDF page 67, photo IMG_1716; read on the page image). WBADB WBS:4459, WBS:4473, WBE:857, WBS:4521, WBS:4546 (Bar-Adon 141b, ʿEin el-Ghuweir wall). Positions in `private/B2/B2_private_coordinates.md`.
Literature check (queries run, result, tag): Web: "Ein el-Ghuweir cemetery north of the building distance Bar-Adon 1977 graves" found https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ain_el-Ghuweir (cemetery about 800 m north; no fortress mentioned) and Orion record https://orion-bibliography.huji.ac.il/orion_editor/node/108297 (Bar-Adon's Hebrew report). Repo: the Kochavi arrival (`research/preregistration/arrivals/outcomes/kochavi-1972_2026-10-30c.json`) lists "Bar-Adon's ʿEin el-Ghuweir entry was not extracted"; no note reads site 138. Tag: **NEW TO THE PROJECT** (published in ASI 1; the link to the excavated site has not been made in any source found).
Why it might matter: ʿEin el-Ghuweir has the only Qumran-type cemetery outside Qumran on this shore. If 138B is that cemetery, the survey shows it was already robbed in 1968, which affects how its excavated sample can be read. If it is a second cemetery, it is unexcavated and unprotected. Structure A is an unstudied 30 × 30 m building near both.
How to confirm or kill it (with data in hand, or what is missing): Bar-Adon 1989, ʿAtiqot Heb. 9, ch. 5 "ʿEin el-Ghuweir" (store `Bar-Adon-1989-Excavations-in-the-Judean-Desert-Atiqot-Heb-9--31489edd`; chapter 4 starts at PDF p. 31, so ch. 5 is about PDF pp. 33–39; images only) should give the cemetery's grid or plan. If its grid is within about 100 m of 138B, the "second cemetery" reading dies. Bar-Adon 1977 (BASOR 227) fig. 1 serves the same purpose.
Confidence: low (identity of 138B open); high that the record exists and is unlinked
Touches the Copper Scroll? maybe: ʿEin el-Ghuweir is a project place; no scroll entry is argued.

## Dead ends

- Bar-Adon 56 code/grid mismatch: ASI 1 p. 113 gives a general grid (195 145) plus three sub-points, and WBADB rows are two of the sub-points. Not an error.
- Bar-Adon 120 code/grid mismatch: the book prints 18-11/08/2 at 1807 1185 (p. 127). WBADB's code 19-11/08/2 is a typo; the grid is right.
- Hirschfeld 1985 site 8 (ʿErq el-Aḥmar) code mismatch of 5.6 km: the grid matches the IAA online record; only the code is wrong.
- Kloner 2003 [83/1] rows 9–10 km from ASI online 83/1 sites 1–6: the online map uses its own numbering. Numbering collision, not an error.
- Benjamin 1993 English list (PDF pp. 250–266) against WBADB: 95 entries match. Five apparent gaps of 0.7–7 km are OCR misreading 2 as 9 (No. 83 checked on the image, p. 22*). The printed misprint No. 86 Burj Beitin (easting "14330") is already silently corrected in WBADB.
- Kallai 1972 sites inside the Benjamin 1993 area with no later record (58, 62, 106, 122, 133, 143): the Hebrew OCR suggests Kallai marks at least 106 as having no ancient remains (natural hill). Not checked on images; low value.
- Kochavi 1972 Judah sites (18) without later record (Ḥusan, Naḥalin, el-Khadr area): Ofer 1993 covers this area but WBADB carries only part of his list. A database artefact, not a survey gap.
- Name-similarity search for unmerged duplicates (`wbadb_unmerged_pairs`, 415 pairs): dominated by generic names (Aronshtam/Sion 2002 caves, Kloner sub-sites). One clean case: Kallai 129 and Kloner 2001 [102] 4 (Kh. ʿAdasa, 50 m) are two WBADB rows.
- Same name, far apart (4,580 pairs): generic names. Dropped.
- Automatic grid extraction from ASI 1: the Bar-Adon chapter (book pp. 90–151, PDF 47–77) has no text layer, and the Kallai and Gophna–Porat Hebrew OCR is too noisy. Page images were read only for the flagged entries.
- JSP 8/9/10, SBF 36, Frumkin 2015 and SWP Memoirs were not joined in this pass. They are excavation and synthesis volumes without site lists in the box beyond what WBADB and caves.csv already carry, and the budget did not allow it. ʿAtiqot 41 was used through the existing caves_entry25 crosswalk, not re-extracted.

## Seed / reproducibility

Deterministic. Scripts are in `private/B2/scripts/` (`join.py`, `asio_audit.py`, `ben93_audit.py`, `finalize.py`; copied there because the public folder takes CSV/MD only). Run `python3 -I join.py WBADB CAVES ASIONLINE OUTDIR`, with WBADB = `branch_only/sandbox__methods/sandbox/west_bank_inventory_2026-10-31/data/wbadb_sites.csv`, CAVES = `research/regional/caves_entry25/caves.csv`, ASIONLINE = `research/regional/asi_jerusalem/sites.csv`. OIG conversion: EPSG:28191 (pyproj 3.8). "Later" record = WBADB row with any reference other than the four ASI 1 chapters, any excavation-sheet row, cave rows whose lineage is not Bar-Adon and not 1972 or earlier, and all ASI online rows. Coverage proxy: 1-km cells with a later record in the 5 × 5 km neighbourhood (≥5 counts as "inside later coverage").
