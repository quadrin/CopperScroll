FIND CARD
Title (one line): The Birket al-Hamra team moved wall W114 from "Persian–Hellenistic?" (2024) to the 800 BCE Siloam Dam (2025) without saying so, and the charcoal date behind the earlier phasing is too young to be explained by old wood.
Lane: A6
What is odd (2–4 sentences): In the 2024 preliminary report, W114 is a newly found north–south pool wall. The report places it in "Stratum 4 (Persian-Hellenistic period?)", says its relation to the dam is unknown, and supports that date with Boaretto's 520–200 BCE plaster date. The report also describes the Greenhut–Mazor pool as lying "on top of" the dam and assigns it to the same phase. In the 2025 PNAS paper, the same excavators (with Boaretto) call W114 the "slanting enforcement's core" of the dam and date its straw to c. 800 BCE (RTD 12948). The PNAS text lists Boaretto's sample only as "a single 14C date of the 4th to 3rd centuries BC" for the dam, using the 1σ intervals, and neither paper mentions that W114 has moved by about 500 years. The 2020 sample (RTT 6089, charcoal, 2300±55 BP) is 322±56 radiocarbon years younger than the dam's combined straw date (2622±10 BP), a gap of 5.8σ. Old wood can only make charcoal read too old, so this sample cannot be original dam plaster with an old-wood offset. It is either later replastering or intrusive or contaminated carbon, and either way it does not date the dam.
Evidence (exact sources: file path / page / figure / frame / coordinates):
- Szanton, Vukosavović & Berko 2024, *City of David Studies of Ancient Jerusalem* 25, English section. Drive 02, file `1LzzOFexhFojSf-eypH_qso1RyLvfTKgo`.

  - Printed p. 33* (PDF p. 16): heading "Stratum 4 (Persian-Hellenistic period?)". The wall's upper thickness is about 3.2 m, with a sloping plastered west face. Note 3 says the relation to the dam is not yet clarified. The Fig. 11 caption names W114.
  - Printed p. 34* (PDF p. 17): "plastered pool exposed on top of the ancient dam wall … (520–200 BCE) … Persian-early Hellenistic (Boaretto 2020:79)".
  - Printed p. 35* (PDF p. 18): W114 and W185A described as the walls of an ancient pool.
- Regev et al. 2025, PNAS 122(35) e2510396122. Drive 04, file `12I0P3ceS0CSW0uHE7xsc6cOGNdPWLrsZ`; full text also from Europe PMC, PMC12415248.
  - Results: W001 straw and twigs (RTD 12353, 12351, 12352) and W114 straw (RTD 12948), combined to 805–795 BC (68.3%) and 809–792 BC (95.4%).
  - Introduction: "a single 14C date of the 4th to 3rd centuries BC (8)". Ref. 8 is Boaretto 2020. Ref. 7 is the 2024 report, cited as "vol. 19, pp. 24–43", but the printed English pages are 23*–42*.

Title (one line): The Cave of Salome is published as "8 km southwest of Tel Lakhish", but its own map reference puts it 8 km east-southeast.
Lane: A6
What is odd (2–4 sentences): Paran & Lifshits give map ref. 193105/605325 (Israel grid) and describe the site as "c. 1 km east of the settlement of Amaẓya and roughly 8 km southwest of Tel Lakhish". Converted (EPSG:2039 to WGS84), the reference falls at 31.5398 N, 34.9270 E. That is 7.95 km from Tel Lachish (31.5650 N, 34.8486 E) at a bearing of 111°, east-southeast, not southwest. It is also 1.6 km at 59° from Amatzia. The distance and the Amatzia relation agree with the map reference; only the compass direction from Lachish is wrong. Amatzia and the Lachish Forest both lie southeast of the tell, so the text, not the reference, is in error.
Evidence (exact sources: file path / page / figure / frame / coordinates): Paran & Lifshits 2025, 'Atiqot 117:109–133. Drive 02, file `1z6v3g8b6VxVCQsfyYiIbZqYmnR-ko-XV`, printed p. 109 (PDF p. 2), Introduction. The Fig. 1 location map (printed p. 110) does not show Lachish. Conversion with pyproj EPSG:2039; the bearing and distance were computed on the WGS84 ellipsoid. The site is a published, protected and excavated site, and its coordinates are printed in the article.
Literature check (queries run, result, tag): Web search for "Cave of Salome" / "Salome Cave" with Amatzia, Lachish, Horvat Qasra and coordinates returned press reports only (2022 excavation coverage); none gives the direction from Lachish. Repo grep for 193105, 605325 and Cave of Salome finds only catalogue and index rows. Tag: **NEW TO THE LITERATURE** (an uncorrected error in the text).
Why it might matter: It is a small error, but it is the sort that gets copied into gazetteers. Anyone placing the site from the prose rather than the grid reference would put it about 11 km from where it is, on the wrong side of the tell.
How to confirm or kill it (with data in hand, or what is missing): Plot 193105/605325 on any Israel grid base map, or on Drive 07 Palestine Open Maps or the SWP sheet, and check that it sits on the slope south of Ḥorbat Qaṣra next to Amatzia. The card is dead if the 'Atiqot online version carries an erratum.
Confidence: high

Lane: A6
What is odd (2–4 sentences): Of 32 six-figure map references in the text layer of Stern 2025, 31 are written northing/easting, against the usual IAA easting/northing order (e.g. SC 1 "611160/190767", SC 19 "611314/190317").
