FIND CARD
Title (one line): The "near Cave 1" Arad–Beersheba sherd QUM 209 (KhQ 2575), which Gunneweg & Balla use for a conclusion about the caves, came from locus 30 of the khirbeh, according to de Vaux's register and to Lemaire in the same volume.
Lane: B6
What is odd (2–4 sentences): HG2003's NAA tables list QUM 209 = KhQ 2575, "Shard, north of Cave 1". The provenance chapter uses it twice: as an outlier sherd with a carved potter's mark that matches Arad–Beersheba ware (p.392, where the number is printed "KhQ 2578"), and in the summary conclusion 1: "The shard (QUM 209) found in the vicinity of Cave 1 came from the Beer Sheba region" (p.393), which is set next to the claim that no inscribed pottery comes from caves 1, 2, 3 and 11. In the same volume, Lemaire (p.369) gives KhQ 2575 as "locus 30 au 27/03/1955, niveau inférieur", "Provenance INAA: Arad-Beershéba". De Vaux's register (HC1994 p.302) lists it under "Objets du locus 30 niveau inférieur, 1955 … 2575 : tesson marqué". The find spot "trou isolé au nord de la grotte 1Q" belongs to another object, the inscribed LYʿZRH jar KhQ 2507 (Lemaire p.364; NAA QUM 184/212, which the tables give without a locus). The "north of Cave 1" label seems to have moved from 2507 to 2575.
Evidence (exact sources: file path / page / figure / frame / coordinates): HG2003 App. I p.36 (PDF 66) row QUM 209; App. II p.41 (PDF 71) under "Caves / Cave 1": "QUM 209 Shard North of Cave 1" (checked on the page image); App. III p.45 (PDF 75); table p.390 (PDF 420) "QUM 209 KhQ 2575 Shard carved cross Near Cave 1"; text p.24 (PDF 54), p.392 (PDF 422, checked on the page image: "QUM 209 (KhQ 2578)"), p.393 (PDF 423) conclusion 1. Lemaire, HG2003 p.369 (PDF 399) entry KhQ 2575, and p.364 (PDF 394) entry KhQ 2507. HC1994 p.302, photo IMG_2980 (Drive `1UuiiSP8f5dnSeKVOoCFQfZR83T_4NC6b`); private crop `private/B6/H1994_p302_L30_objects_2575_crop.jpg`.
Literature check (queries run, result, tag): Web: `Gunneweg Balla Qumran "QUM 209" shard Cave 1 Beer Sheba`; `"KhQ 2575" OR "KhQ 2578" Qumran potter's mark Arad Beersheba neutron activation`; `review "Khirbet Qumrân et 'Aïn Feshkha II" Humbert Gunneweg 2003 neutron activation errors loci`. These found only Orion catalogue entries for the chapters and no correction. Project: "QUM 209", "KhQ 2575", "KhQ 2578" give 0 hits on main and in branch_only; `registration/qumranII2003_extracted.md` does not have it. Reviews of HG2003 (for example in DSD or RB) were not read; they are not in the store. Tag: **NEW TO THE LITERATURE** (no publication found that says it).
Why it might matter: The NAA programme is the main evidence used to argue about where the cave pottery and the site pottery were made. Here one of the few cave-area data points (and the only "imported from the Negev near Cave 1" sherd) is a sherd from the main building. With it moved, conclusion 1 loses its only positive cave example. Gunneweg's later papers on cave/settlement pottery may repeat the point (not checked).
How to confirm or kill it (with data in hand, or what is missing): Kill it if de Vaux's register card or the EBAF photo 16.910 (cited by Lemaire) shows a second sherd numbered 2575 or 2578 with a Cave 1 find spot, or if KhQ 2578 turns out to be a separate Cave 1 object (HC1994 p.302 does not list 2578; it is not in the 1955 L30 run 2520–2661). Confirm it by checking HC1994 pp.338–343 (the Cave 1Q and "nord de la grotte 1Q" entries) for 2575/2578, and the Gunneweg–Balla laboratory sample sheets (not in the corpus).
Confidence: high that the volume contradicts itself; medium-high that L30 is right (two independent witnesses, de Vaux's register and Lemaire's register-card reading, against the one NAA label).
Touches the Copper Scroll? maybe. Cave 1 vs khirbeh pottery provenance is part of the background to the cave–settlement link (3Q15 comes from Cave 3). It is not an entry-level find.

FIND CARD
Title (one line): The HG2003 NAA sample register disagrees with itself and with de Vaux's register on at least 12 samples, including a whole L61 group put in L44.
Lane: B6
What is odd (2–4 sentences): The same samples are listed three times (App. I by lab number, App. II by locus, App. III by KhQ number), and again in the inscription table on p.392. The four listings differ on object number, locus and object type, and each case can be settled against HC1994's object lists or Lemaire's catalogue in the same volume. The clearest group is HC1994 p.312, "Objets du locus 61: 1079 : plat. 1080 : plat du même type … 1110 et 1111 : assiettes … 1139 : pot type 1109". HG2003 puts KhQ 1080 (QUM 111) in **L.44** in all three appendices. App. III puts KhQ 1110 (QUM 204) in L.44 and adds a phantom row "KhQ 1110 QUM 111". KhQ 1139 (QUM 152) is a "lid" from L.58 in App. I/II but a "bowl" from L.61 in App. III. The other conflicts: QUM 200 = KhQ 192 (App. I/III, p.392, Lemaire) vs KhQ **200** (App. II); QUM 214 = KhQ 680 vs KhQ **259** (App. II); QUM 320 = KhQ 2109 (App. I, Lemaire L.110) vs KhQ **2125** in App. II, which also gives 2125 to QUM 321 in L.116; KhQ 1236 L.73 (App. III, Lemaire, HC1994) vs **L.62** (App. II); KhQ 1286 "cup" (App. I; HC1994 "gobelet") vs "**potter's wheel**" (App. II); KhQ 425 L.10A vs "**L. 108**" (App. III); QUM 262 = KhQ 16 vs KhQ **6**; KhQ 1270 listed under both L.62 and L.65 (App. II).
Evidence (exact sources: file path / page / figure / frame / coordinates): HG2003 pp.35, 36, 37 (App. I, PDF 65–67), p.39 and p.40 (App. II, PDF 69–70), p.43 (App. III, PDF 73), p.392 (PDF 422). All were checked on rendered page images, except the App. I p.38 rows (text layer only). HC1994 p.312 = IMG_2985 (Drive `1401BKvEtIwrC5Pt1jql0lK7T9mACvrbq`), read from the photo; crop `private/B6/H1994_p312_L61_objects_crop.jpg`. Lemaire entries: HG2003 pp.342, 343, 348, 353, 357, 359. HCM2016 p.178 (L61 pottery catalogue) agrees with HC1994: "1079 61.3 25/2/54 plat", "1080 61.3 25/2/54 plat", "1110 61.3 27/2/54 assiette, percée … inscription peinte". So three volumes give L61 for KhQ 1080 against HG2003's L.44. Full table with all pointers: `out/B6/naa2003_register_conflicts.csv`.
Literature check (queries run, result, tag): Web: the HG2003 review/erratum query above; `Gunneweg Balla Qumran "QUM 209"…`. No erratum or review that lists these was found. Project: "QUM 111", "KhQ 1080", "Appendix III" and "QUM 209" give no Qumran hits on main or branch_only. Tag: **NEW TO THE LITERATURE**.
Why it might matter: The locus attribution of NAA samples is the step that turns chemistry into statements about rooms (Gunneweg & Balla p.392: "pinpoint distinct rooms in the Khirbeh where scribal activities took place"). L44 and L61 are both in the south-east annexes that HCM2016 reinterprets (Chap. A, pp.157–185), so moving samples between them changes the per-locus chemical picture. Anyone reusing App. II or III as a lookup table inherits the errors.
How to confirm or kill it (with data in hand, or what is missing): Most rows are settled by page images already in hand. Still open: KhQ 1139 (lid from L.58, or "pot type 1109" from L.61?) needs the EBAF register or the object. The HC1994 readings for 680, 1236, 1270, 1286 and 425 come from draft OCR, so check them on IMG_2976/2977/2985/2986. Kill a row if HC1994 lists the object under a second locus too (searched the OCR: none for 1080, 1110, 1139).
Confidence: high
Touches the Copper Scroll? no

FIND CARD
Title (one line): HC1994 p.312 gives inventory number 1104 to two different objects, one in L60 and one in L61; HCM2016's lamp catalogue cites a wrong HC1994 page for KhQ 589.
Lane: B6
What is odd (2–4 sentences): On the same page of de Vaux's synthesised notes, "Objets du locus 60 … 1103 : support de jarre. 1104 : assiette à base annulaire" and "Objets du locus 61 … 1094 : fragment de bronze. 1104 : assiette." One running inventory number cannot be in both adjacent rooms, so one entry is a misprint (the L61 run reads 1093, 1094, 1104, 1105, 1106…). Separately, HCM2016 lamp no. 84 (KhQ 589, "Loc. 33 upper") cites "Qumran 1, 393". HC1994 p.393 is in the index pages, and the object is on p.303 ("Objets du locus 33. 589 : lampe « hérodienne »"). A scripted cross-check of 58 HCM2016 lamp entries against the HC1994 pages they cite found every other find locus consistent; for example KhQ 538 "Loc. 22, lower level" = HC1994 L36, which is "nouveau locus sous le locus 22".
Evidence (exact sources: file path / page / figure / frame / coordinates): HC1994 p.312, IMG_2985 (Drive `1401BKvEtIwrC5Pt1jql0lK7T9mACvrbq`), read from the photo (L60 list at the top of column 2; L61 list at the foot of column 2). HCM2016 p.486 (lamp catalogue no. 84) vs HC1994 p.303 (IMG_2980). Lead not verified: the HC1994 OCR also shows "536" under both L22 (p.300, "536: bol") and twice under L36 (p.304, "536: gobelet. 536: assiette plate"), and "351" under L9A and L24. These need the photos IMG_2979/IMG_2981/IMG_2976.
Literature check (queries run, result, tag): Web: `Qumran locus 60 locus 61 "1104" assiette Humbert Chambon 1994`: no hit. Project: A2 (6 Nov) found other misprints in HC1994 (L94 date, Feshkha L22, "1581 et 1982") but not these. "1104" plus Qumran gives nothing on main or branch_only. Tag: **NEW TO THE PROJECT** (small; probably never noticed in print, but too minor to claim with confidence).
Why it might matter: It is one more case of the same object number for two loci in HC1994, the base text that HG2003 and HCM2016 cite. Any locus-level count of plates/assiettes for L60/L61 (both used in HCM2016's south-east workshop discussion) is off by one.
How to confirm or kill it (with data in hand, or what is missing): Largely settled already. HCM2016's L60 pottery catalogue (p.188) has "1104 60.2/3 près du sol 24/2/54 assiette", and its L61 catalogue (p.178) has no 1104. So the L61 "1104" in HC1994 is the misprint (it may stand for another number in 1095–1103). To recover the intended number, check the EBAF register or the Donceel manuscript.
Confidence: high (that the print doubles the number and that 1104 belongs to L60); low (importance)
Touches the Copper Scroll? no
