**Question.** How many places in each fixed region could physically host each of the scroll's landform and water words? And does that terrain denominator change how an existing inventory count can be read?

## Novelty gate

- `flow accumulation`, `sky-view`, `richdem/pysheds/whitebox` and `denominator` (as a terrain count) have no hits in `main` or `sb` outside atlas bundles.
- Precedents that use a DEM:
  - sandbox runs 1 and 7 (`dem_channels.py`, `dem_build.py`: Terrarium tiles, D8 drainage area at a few named points, to find the nearest channel);
  - run 9 (Tobler walking routes);
  - run 23 (wadi form at nine points);
  - the atlas terrain layer (GLO-30 as context only).
- **What is different.** Every earlier use asked questions of the terrain at a candidate point. None counted every terrain-possible place in a region, none tested a predicate's stability or resolution, and none used the count as a denominator for an inventory or rarity figure.

### Resolution tests (F-B ii)

- **RT_cave_cliff: passed.** 359 recorded caves with precision ≤ 50 m. 64.9 % lie within 60 m of a P5x cliff cell, against an areal base rate of 11.5 %, so caves sit on this terrain 5.6 times more often than chance. The 30 m DEM resolves the *cliff host* of a cave, though not the cave itself.
- **RT_dam_channel: failed.** 32 dams. 28.1 % lie within 60 m of a P1 channel cell, against a base rate of 11.0 %. That is above chance, but below the frozen recall threshold of 0.5. By the frozen rule, P1, P2, P3, P3b and P6 are **not resolved**: their counts stand but carry no rarity reading.
  - INFERENCE, not tested: the recorded dams mostly sit on gullies smaller than 1 km², or are offset by D8 channel placement on a 30 m DSM.
- **Informative:** recorded pools in RF3 (n = 249) lie on the P4 host at 22.1 %, against a base rate of 23.1 %. That is at chance. The P4 "pool host" predicate is stable, but it does not track where pools were actually built.

### Inventory coverage

Coverage means the share of terrain units that hold any recorded point within 60 m.

- **Caves (RF2):** 58 of 1,221 cliff clusters (4.8 %) and 72 of 563 P5 faces (12.8 %) carry a recorded cave from the 635-row inventory.
- **Pools (RF3):** 74 of 7,789 P4 host clusters (0.95 %) carry a recorded pool or basin.
- **What this means.** Under the frozen §5 rule (coverage < 0.5), the inventory N values (635 caves, 1,437 water units) measure the record, not the landscape.
- **What it does not mean.** It does not show that the other faces hold caves. A cliff face is a place where a cave is possible, not a cave.

### Koḥlit (entries 11, 60): the first denominator-based rarity statement

- **Definition.** The definition used is the registered one: `research/preregistration/kohlit_rarity_2026-10-08.md` §4, with the headline from `research/rarity/kohlit/README.md`. C1 is a pool to the east, at a bearing of 45–135° and ≤ 1 km. The registered headline is **k = 0, f = 20, m = 313 of N = 333** (R1, main set, branch A, survey level).
- **C1T (terrain-possible pool to the east).** At primary values (S_pool 5°, A_pool 0.05 km², R 1 km), **310 of 333 units (93.1 %)** have a P4 host cell in their east quadrant. So do 27,163 of 28,944 lattice points (93.8 %) over all of RF4.
  - Across all 27 settings, the share runs from 51 % to 100 %. It is ≥ 90 % in 14 settings and never below 50 %.
  - Only at R = 0.5 km does the terrain narrow the set, and only partly: 51–90 %.
- **C2T_natural (cliff to the north).** 112 of 333 units (34 %), against a lattice share of 35 %. By definition, C2 also admits built cisterns, pits and shafts, so it is terrain-unconstrained.
- **Reading, under the frozen rule (≥ 90 % at primary).** The terrain does not narrow the Koḥlit set.
  - The denominator of k = 0 stays at about 333.
  - Almost every one of the 313 unknown units sits on ground where a pool to the east is physically possible, so their UNKNOWN is a gap in the records, not a terrain exclusion.
  - The settlement units are no more pool-capable than the landscape at large (93 % against 94 %).
  - **k = 0 therefore cannot be read as rarity on the ground.** It says that the record is silent, as the registered §6 reading ("rarity not measurable") already states. The terrain shows that this silence is not explained by unsuitable ground.
  - The exposed candidates (Kh. Qumran, both Kh. Marjama rows, Kh. Yanun, Yanun, ʿEin el-Ghuweir) are all C1T-possible. Tell es-Sultan is not a main-set unit.

## Outcome against the failure conditions

- **F-A.** P1, P3, P4a, P4b, P5, P5e, P6, P6m and P7 are stable in RF1 and RF2. P2 and P3b are unstable in both. P5x is unstable in RF1. So the failure condition "predicates cannot be made stable" is **not met** for most predicates.
- **F-B.** The cliff/cave host is resolved. Every channel-based predicate fails its frozen resolution test. The pool host passes F-B formally, because no test applied to it, but tracks recorded pools only at chance.
- **§4 direction rule.** The "obtained" clause is formally met: P4a, P4b, P5 and P5e are stable in RF1 and RF2, and the Koḥlit denominator is computed. The "obtained" and "partly" clauses of §4 overlap, which is a defect of the protocol, and P5's "above a channel" inherits the unresolved channel. This note therefore reports the weaker label.

## Limits

- **Modern surface.** GLO-30 is a DSM of 2011–2015. It includes buildings, roads, quarries, agricultural terraces, Jericho town, the evaporation ponds and the lowered lake. The period lake was reconstructed only as "cells below −395 m connected to the modern lake".
- **Vertical accuracy.** It is < 4 m (LE90), and < 2 m relative on slopes ≤ 20 % ([Copernicus Data Space, COP-DEM](https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/explore-data/data-collections/copernicus-contributing-missions/collections-description/COP-DEM)). P4a below 2 m is noise.
- **Host terrain, not features.** A 30 m cell cannot show a cave mouth, cistern or pool. P4 and P5 count host terrain only. Built installations (C2's cisterns, any rock-cut pool) are not constrained by terrain at this scale.
- **P5 is a composite.** The words "above a channel" come from the task brief and the על פי "above" sense, not from any one line. P7 is geographic: no line of the scroll names the Dead Sea as ים (`occurrences.json` `yam`).
- **Boundaries.** RF4 uses the geoBoundaries West Bank polygon, which is approximate. The lake-level values come from sandbox run 23's citations and were not re-checked here.
- **Exploratory exposure.** Before the freeze, the derivatives were checked at eight known points, and a dry run was made on an outside test box (see PROTOCOL §6). The aspect sign was corrected before the freeze.

## What it changes

- **For Koḥlit.** The registered k = 0 of 333 stays "not measurable" on the record. It now also has a terrain answer: about 93 % of settlement units, and of the landscape, could host a pool to the east, so terrain cannot make the combination rare, and the 313 unknowns are gaps in the record, not physical exclusions.
- **For the inventories.** For the cave and water inventories, at most 13 % of terrain-possible cliff faces and about 1 % of pool-host sites carry any record. So "1 of 635" (entry 25, B-PILLAR) and the water-lane k values are record rates, not landscape rates.
- **For future rarity counts.** Any rarity count that uses a cliff predicate can now use the RF2 denominator: P5 = 563 faces [230–983]; P5e = 349 [144–618].

**Closing line (AGENTS.md): partly.** New dataset: `predicate_counts.csv`. The cliff/cave host is a stable, resolved terrain denominator: caves fall on it 5.6 times more often than chance. The pool host is stable but tracks recorded pools no better than chance, and the channel predicates failed their resolution test. For Koḥlit, the terrain denominator shows that k = 0 of 333 cannot be read as rarity on the ground: 93 % of units could host a pool to the east.

**Second session.** A second AI-only session is justified only for one bounded fix: a finer open DEM (e.g. a 5–10 m product, if one is openly reachable) or a lower A_min recalibrated on dams as a labelled new model. Its purpose would be to test whether the channel predicates (נחל, שולי, צוק) can be resolved. Without that, the direction should stop here.
