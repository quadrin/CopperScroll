## Question and what could change

**Question.** Can a mechanical forward model choose between candidate readings of a line by rendering each one into the multi-light 1988 photographs, instead of a reader choosing by eye?

**What could change.** If the model worked, the disputed words at XII 10, IX 10, I 12, VII 11 and X 15 would get a computed ranking. That ranking would be exposed-image evidence that does not depend on a reader's knowledge of the editions.

## What was done

1. **Registration.** All 22 recto cuts that have L/C/R × top/bottom frames (cuts 2–23; cut 1 has no lit frames) were registered into multi-light stacks of 4–12 frames each (`prep.py`, `prep_registration.json`). The stacks themselves are private.
2. **Forward model** (`model.py`):
   - glyph skeletons, 17 of them from the strip 13 tracing of the scroll's own letters (column VII), with font fallbacks for the rest;
   - a U-shaped groove;
   - high-pass removal of strip curvature;
   - a Gaussian corrosion residual;
   - Lambertian shading with a fitted light vector plus a groove-darkening term per frame.
   Every candidate gets the same fitting freedom. The likelihood is a per-frame Gaussian log-likelihood, maximised over horizontal offset and a small row window.
3. **Calibration.** 25 strictly undisputed lines in columns I–X, split by a seeded draw into 12 train and 13 held-out lines. Each line gets 3–6 candidates: the ETCBC truth plus edition-confusion-weighted one-letter substitutions. Tuning on train used 8 settings; the best train MRR was 0.70 (7 of 12 first).
4. **Confirmatory run.** One held-out run, as frozen.

## Result

**New datasets.**
- [`calibration_rankings.csv`](calibration_rankings.csv): public numbers, 72 rows, one per held-out candidate, with log-likelihood, rank and margin.
- [`target_rankings.csv`](target_rankings.csv): records "NOT RUN".
- Renderings: 141 private files in `/root/novel/private_outputs/B/renderings/`.

**Held-out test: FAIL.**
- 4 of 13 held-out lines ranked their own reading first, against 2.40 expected by chance.
- Exact one-sided Poisson-binomial P(T ≥ 4) = 0.206. The frozen pass mark was T ≥ 6 (P < 0.05).
- Descriptively, the truth's mean normalised rank was 0.32 (0 = top, 0.5 = chance) and the MRR 0.53, against 0.43 by chance.
- So there is a weak tilt toward the truth, too small to pass. The train score of 7/12 shrank to 4/13 on held-out lines, as expected after selecting the best of 8 settings on 12 lines.

**Targets.** Under the frozen rule, XII 10 (R1–R7), IX 10, I 12, VII 11 and X 15 were **not scored and are not interpreted**.

## Limits and likely causes

These are INFERENCE, not tested.
- **Resolution.** Letters are about 20–30 px high in these previews, so the groove is 2–4 px wide and a one-letter substitution changes about 1 % of the band's pixels. Corrosion texture of the same scale dominates the residual.
- **Registration.** Line rows come from a nominal-position window shared by all candidates, not from authenticated neighbouring Hebrew. A wrong row makes every candidate equally wrong.
- **Templates.** One column's tracing, and font skeletons for 10 letter forms.
- **Lighting.** No calibrated light directions. The per-frame fitted light absorbs part of the signal.
- **Blocked input.** The full-resolution private WSRP TIFFs (Drive `06 Imagery/3Q15 Copper Scroll Zuckerman USC`; repo `private-media/wsrp-1988/*.enc` needs the secret `WSRP_MEDIA_KEY`) were unreachable from this container. They would give about 4× the linear resolution.
