# Lane H: a language prior for disputed 3Q15 readings (30 October 2026)

Status: confirmatory test of the method (claims C1, C2) closed. Target priors delivered. No reading is declared.
Files: [PROTOCOL_FROZEN.md](PROTOCOL_FROZEN.md), [confirmatory_result.json](confirmatory_result.json), [calibration_results.csv](calibration_results.csv), [target_priors.csv](target_priors.csv), [targets.json](targets.json), [WORKLOG.md](WORKLOG.md), code in [code/](code/).

## 1. Novelty gate

- Earlier work reads letters from images (plate checks, WSRP notes, lane B image rendering) or counts edition variants (`research/text/edition_confusions`).
- No earlier work on main or `origin/sandbox/methods` used a language model, perplexity or a reading prior. Sefaria was used only to look up passages.
- Lane H uses no image. It asks only: given the 3Q15 context, how probable is each candidate string in Hebrew of the period?
- It tests the prior first on agreed 3Q15 words with realistic rivals, and it scores word division as well as letters.
- Lane B2 (rerun session) will give image likelihoods. This lane does not combine them.

## 3. Exploratory choices (development split only)

- Normalisation: consonants only; final letters as base letters; matres lectionis kept as written; one space between words; numerals become one symbol; any break starts a new segment.
- Model: interpolated modified Kneser–Ney character n-gram, written for this lane (`code/lm.py`). One model on DSS, one on Mishnah+Tosefta, mixed 0.5/0.5 per character.
- Order 8, window 3 words each side. On the dev split a substitution task gave top-1 0.85 (DSS) and 0.89 (rabbinic) against chance 0.20. Without context it gave only 0.63–0.72.
- A division task on the dev split (true division, boundary one letter left or right, merged) gave top-1 0.82 and 0.95 against chance 0.25.
- Rival letters change one letter and keep the length. So the main calibration does not cover length or division. We therefore added the division test as claim C2 before the freeze.

One confirmatory run.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| C1 substitution, held-out | 196 | 148 (0.755) | 39.2 (0.20) | 1.4 × 10⁻⁶² | yes |
| C2 division, held-out | 76 | 61 (0.80) | 19.0 (0.25) | 7.8 × 10⁻²⁴ | yes |

- Temperature T = 1.21. Held-out negative log-likelihood: 0.688 with T, 0.697 with T = 1, 1.609 for a uniform guess. Mean probability given to the true reading: 0.63. Expected calibration error: 0.079.
- Reliability (held-out, after the run, descriptive): stated confidence 0.42 / 0.65 / 0.88 / 0.97 / 0.995 against observed accuracy 0.50 / 0.73 / 0.81 / 0.90 / 0.95. The prior is a little overconfident at the top. Read a calibrated probability above 0.95 as "about 0.9–0.95".
- Hard rivals: 110 held-out words have at least one rival that is a real word in the training text. The true word beat all such rivals in 78 of them (0.71).
- Names: the prior is weaker on proper names (top-1 0.55, mean probability of the true name 0.51).

Notes on the targets (what the prior can and cannot say):
- IX 10. The three main readings have the same six letters plus one space. So the strong preference for מן החרם is not a length effect. It reflects the phrase "from the ḥerem" in period Hebrew. Both the DSS-only and the Mishnah+Tosefta-only components agree (exploratory check after the run). The image notes of 30 Oct report an engraved final ב, not ם. The prior and the image therefore pull in opposite directions here. The prior gives no information on what is engraved.
- I 12. ניקרת is one letter longer than the others. Each extra letter costs probability, and C2 calibrates boundary shifts, not added letters. So the low value for ניקרת is partly a length cost.
- VII 11. The leader המשמרת is Muchowski's form, not the form most editions print. Both components prefer it.
- X 15 w3–5. The variant with the supplied של is longest and scores lowest, again partly a length cost.
- XII 10. These are mostly name or rare forms. The entropy is high (1.70 of 2.81 bits). The prior is weaker on names (section 5). The two components disagree: DSS-only prefers שבנה (0.69), Mishnah+Tosefta-only prefers שכינה (0.47) over שבנה (0.40). Treat XII 10 as a weak prior.

## 7. New observation

**Obtained.** A calibrated, image-free prior over the registered candidates now exists for IX 10, I 12, VII 11, X 15 and XII 10. It is shown to carry information on 196 held-out agreed words of 3Q15.

## 8. What it changes

- B2's image likelihoods can now be weighed against an independent, calibrated text prior on the same candidate lists. The combination rule must be frozen before the two are joined.
- At IX 10 the prior strongly favours the חרם readings, while the image notes favour an engraved …רב. A combined result will turn on how strong the image evidence for ב is.
- At XII 10 the prior is weak. The decision there will rest almost entirely on the image.
- No rating, ledger entry, atlas record or ACTIVE_TEST changes.

## 9. Further AI-only session

A further AI-only session is justified only to combine this prior with B2's image likelihoods under a rule frozen in advance; more language-model tuning is not justified.
