## 1. Reopening gate
Lane I closed C1, C2 and the tomb sub-test as rejected for the sources it read. It named three reopening observations: Kaftor va-Ferach, the Hütteroth–Abdulfattah defter names, and Abbot Daniel. All three are now readable. They add three new kinds of record:

  - French: the OCR long-s "f" is kept as printed. Khitrowo's notes and variants are by_editor.
  - Hütteroth text: daftar facts are by_editor false with speaker date 1596. The authors' modern identifications are by_editor true.

  - The working-directory path, generic proxy notes and the e-mail address from the host context were still visible. None of them carries test information.
  - Result: **pass**. All 28 extraction runs used the run-2 environment.
- The first launch failed: the chunk paths were relative, and all 28 instances received empty text. Those outputs were set aside unused, and the runs were repeated with absolute paths.
- Final run: 28 of 28 exited 0, with 0 malformed lines and 755 records. Input and output hashes are in `blind_extraction/extractor_inputs_sha256.txt` and `extractor_outputs_sha256.txt`.
