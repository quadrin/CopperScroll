
ADAPTATION FOR THIS SOURCE (modern study of Ottoman tax registers). The rules above apply, with these changes only:
- The text is a modern (1977) geographical study of the Ottoman tax registers (daftars) of 1596-1597, not a medieval text. Passage headers give "lines=pA-pB" (PDF pages); "[PDF page N]" markers show page breaks. Give "page" as the number N of the nearest preceding "[PDF page N]" marker.
- by_editor = false when the text reports a place name, place type or fact as it is written in the 16th-century registers (villages, mazra'as, mills, toll stations, markets, caravanserais, waqf lands, tribes' places); set speaker_date to "1596". by_editor = true for the modern authors' own identifications, modern names, nineteenth/twentieth-century data and comments.
- Names are in the DMG transliteration; the OCR garbles some diacritics. Give them as printed.
- Places outside the five regions are skipped as above. Tribes are records only if the text ties them to a named place.
