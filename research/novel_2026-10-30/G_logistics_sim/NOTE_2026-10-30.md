## Results

**1. The mass gap between readings is small.** The 45 weight sums come to about 119 t under the talent reading and 84 t under the karsh reading, at the prior-median talent of 31.5 kg. The karsh reading only rescales the 30 ככ sums (1,117.5 units). The 15 ככרין/ככר sums (2,637 talents, 56 t at 21.3 kg) are talents under both readings and dominate the mass. Paired person-night ratios (talent ÷ karsh) are 1.04–1.41 at the median.

**2. Window W1 (Nov 66 – June 68, 575 nights), crew N = 10.** Feasibility fractions F, talent vs karsh, across the 18 main cells:

| Origin | Porter | Donkey | Mule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Temple, posterior spread | 0.15 vs 0.29 | 0.86 vs 0.92 | 0.98 vs 0.99 |
| Qumran, posterior spread | 0.04 vs 0.12 | 0.79 vs 0.87 | 0.95 vs 0.98 |
| Jericho, posterior spread | 0.08 vs 0.21 | 0.83 vs 0.91 | 0.97 vs 0.99 |

The largest gap in any main cell is 0.14, below the frozen 0.25 threshold, so all 18 cells are classed *unaffected*. The verdict is unaffected and F1 fires.

**3. Crew needed for F ≥ 0.5 in W1, posterior spread.**
- Porters only: 20 people under karsh; 20–50 under talent.
- Donkeys: 10 under either reading.
- Mules: 3–10 under either reading.

**4. The all_small bracket** (every unit 1/300 talent; hypothetical, with no source) totals 0.4 t. It is feasible with 3 people everywhere.

**5. The talent reading is not physically excluded.** About 120 t over 575 nights with ≤ 10 people and pack animals is feasible in most draws. With porters alone it is not.

**6. Siege windows W2 (June 68 – April 70) and W3 (April – August 70).** These are reported, but they do not enter the verdict.
- Only the *local* (Jerusalem-only) spread is feasible, at any crew size.
- Every spread that sends entries outside Jerusalem fails the corridor rule.
- So if any deposit lies outside Jerusalem, a Temple-origin operation has to fall in W1, before Vespasian reached Jericho on 3 Desius 68 (BJ IV 8.1; citadels at Jericho and Adida, BJ IV 9.1).
- This follows from the frozen corridor rule by construction. It is not an independent finding.

**7. Sobol, setting A (talent vs karsh), Y1 = log10 person-nights.**
- ST(mode) = 0.72.
- ST(k_animals, unconstrained) = 0.14.
- ST(placement) = 0.07 and ST(origin) = 0.06.
- ST(talent_kg) = 0.035.
- ST(reading) = 0.016 ± 0.001.

ST(reading) is below 0.05 and below k_animals, so F2 fires. For crew required (Y2), the unconstrained operation length T_op dominates with ST = 0.62. In setting B, where all_small is added, the reading becomes dominant (ST 0.71). That only shows the unsourced all-small bracket is far away.

## Exploratory (after the frozen run; not part of the verdict)

`exploratory/kk_loadcount.py` and `exploratory/kk_loadcount.csv` count loads per sum, at 21.3 kg per talent and 40 kg per porter:
- Under the karsh reading, every one of the 30 ככ sums is a single porter load.
- Under the talent reading, 19 of the 30 need more than 10 porter loads; the largest needs 107.

The two readings differ in **per-entry handling** (one bag vs a caravan), not in total effort. The frozen per-entry single-trip check shows the same thing: at N = 10, Temple origin, posterior spread, 36 % of ככ entries are single-trip feasible under karsh vs 7 % under talent. Distance caps this share, because a round trip must fit in one night. Multiple trips are allowed, so this does not exclude the talent reading either.

## Limits

- The loads and speeds are broad ethnographic and ancient ranges, cited at second hand (Adams 2007 citing Cotterell & Kamminga 1990 p. 194; Łuć 2018 via imperiumromanum.pl; Bastien et al. 2016 via EurekAlert; Wikipedia "Pack animal"). Roth 1999's pack-animal chapter was not reachable in the retrieved PDF excerpt.
- The heavy talent stops at 42.6 kg. T08's 58.9 kg value (from Wikipedia) was left out by the frozen rule.
- Julian equivalents for the Josephus month dates are approximate (±1 month). W3's intramural proxy (0.35 h from the Temple) is an assumption.
- Placement scenarios come from the W2B v3 model, which is partly built from the entry order. They are scenarios, not locations.
- Ingots and vessel counts (entries 2, 7, 12, 56 and others) are left out, so the mass is a lower bound under every reading.
- Night-speed factor, animals per handler, overhead, duty and operation length have no source. Their effect is measured in `sensitivity_indices.csv`.

## Closing line (AGENTS.md)

**Not obtained.** Both datasets exist, but the frozen test shows that feasibility does not discriminate between the talent and karsh readings. The reading explains 1.6 % of the variance in effort. Transport mode and an unsourced handler ratio explain far more. Logistics therefore neither excludes nor constrains the talent reading. The only firm constraint, which follows from the frozen corridor rule by construction, is this: a regionally spread, Temple-origin concealment must predate June 68 CE. A second AI-only session is not justified. The remaining discriminator is per-entry handling against the capacity of each hiding place, and that needs measured cavity dimensions that AI alone cannot obtain.
