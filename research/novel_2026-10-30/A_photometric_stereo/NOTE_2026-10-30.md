# Direction A: photometric stereo on the WSRP 1988 multi-light previews (30 Oct 2026)

**Question.** Can relief recovered from the 1988 multi-light frames separate keyed control letters by a computed rule? If so, the rule could be applied to the target lines without a human reader.

**Conclusion that could change.** The status of I 12, IX 10, VII 11, X 15 and XII 10 as "undecidable by eye". **Result: not obtained.**

## Novelty gate

I searched `main`, `sandbox/methods` and `research/logs/` for "photometric", "normal map", "Frankot", "shape from shading", "surface normal" and "depth map".

- No relief recovery from the 1988 frames exists.
- The T10 letter-model spec (wave 1) only *proposed* RTI normals from new imaging.
- W2F used single-light copy plates.
- Method 8 aligned 1988 against 1994 images; it recovered no relief.

What is new here: PS normals and depth from frames already held, and a computed relief rule.

## What was done

**Input limit.** The private full-resolution WSRP TIFFs could not be reached:

- Drive `06 Imagery/3Q15 Copper Scroll Zuckerman USC`: the connector returns only base64 in tool output.
- `private-media/wsrp-1988/*.enc`: needs `WSRP_MEDIA_KEY`.

Everything therefore runs on the USC public previews: about 1000 px, 0.34 mm/px, roughly 25–30 px per letter height.

**1. Registration per cut and film.**

- Within one lighting group (top-lit, or bottom-lit), SIFT with a RANSAC affine fit works. The 5-fold CV median is 0.9–1.6 px, with 45–450 inliers.
- Across groups (top-lit against bottom-lit), it does not work: 3–14 inliers. The shading inverts, and some frames are rotated 180° or remounted.
- An ECC fallback (silhouette, then Laplacian magnitude of the group means) succeeded for cuts 2, 5 (colour), 6, 7 (b&w), 8 and 9 (colour).
- Results:
  - 4–6 lights registered: cuts 2, 5 (colour), 6, 7 (b&w), 8, 9 (colour).
  - 3 lights, top group only: cuts 3, 7 (colour), 10, 12, 15 (b&w), 16 and 18.
  - Fewer than 3 lights, PS not run: cuts 4, 5 (b&w), 9 (b&w), 11, 13, 14, 15 (colour), 17 and 19.
- Numbers are in `ps_runs/cutNN_{bla,col}.json`.

**2. Photometric stereo.**

- **Uncalibrated route.** I ran an SVD rank-3 factorisation, the Hayakawa constant-albedo constraint, and integrability for in-plane rotation. It did **not** recover the titled light layout: azimuth errors were 9–139°, singular values decayed slowly (σ2/σ1 ≈ 0.2), and the curl minimum for the mirror was ambiguous. It is kept as a diagnostic only.
- **Route used: semi-calibrated PS.**
  - Azimuths are fixed to the titles L/C/R × T/B.
  - One elevation is fitted per cut; the fits ranged 10–65°.
  - Frame intensities are fitted per frame.
  - Depth is integrated by Frankot–Chellappa, then high-passed.
  - Relative residual: 3–4% of the signal where 4–6 lights registered.
- Relief of the letters is visible in the normal maps of cuts 6–7 (columns II–III). Those were the only relief views inspected by eye; nothing was read from them.

**3. Locating controls.** No line-level registration of the 1988 frames existed in the repository, so I built it.

- Each cut's groove map was template-matched against letter boxes from Puech's facsimile (via the W2F transform to the copy photograph). The search covered column, rotation, scale (constrained by line spacing), aspect and tilt. Peak NCC was only 0.15–0.37.
- **Acceptance gate**, staged before its result was seen. The b&w and colour films are mapped independently and must agree within 0.5 letter height once composed through the b&w→colour SIFT registration, which has a CV of about 0.3 px.
- **Only cut 8 (column III) passed**, at 0.08 Lh.
  - Cut 18: 0.59 Lh.
  - Cut 3: 1.18 Lh.
  - Cuts 2, 6, 7, 12 and 16: 4.9–17.8 Lh.
- **Caveat found after the result.** Both films' cut-8 solutions sit at the lower edge of the aspect grid (0.30). The agreement may reflect the same degenerate horizontal squash, not a correct placement: the 7–8 control centres all fall in a 30-px-wide band.

**4. Freeze.**

- `PROTOCOL_FROZEN.md`, SHA-256 `130de878e9702fa0de41866bccceb2d29df98374c06237f3c0d4f2504a85a348`, commit `9b1860fb`, pushed before scoring.
- The rule is a priori, with no training split: the taw foot. `f_left_foot` > 0 is called ת.
- Held-out controls: the ח/ת controls on cut 8.
- Test: one-sided exact binomial, p < 0.05.
- It was fixed in advance that no target would be scored, because no target cut passed the gate.

## Result (confirmatory, run once)

- **1 of 8 correct.** Accuracy 0.125, Clopper–Pearson 95% CI 0.003–0.53, balanced accuracy 0.10, one-sided binomial p = 0.996. **Fails.**
- 6 of 8 letters had no valid window: less than 70% of the 2 Lh window lay inside the strip mask, or the foot box fell outside it. The remaining two ח/ת calls split 1–1.
- **Deviation from the frozen text, recorded.** The protocol says the held-out set is the controls inside the mask "in both films" (n = 7). The scorer included letters inside the mask in either film (n = 8, adding LC-CHH-136, which is invalid). Under the protocol's own n = 7 the result is 1 of 7, p = 0.99. Both versions fail.
- BK (ב/כ) and ה/ח: no keyed control falls on an accepted cut, so they are not testable.
- **Targets:** not located, not computed, not viewed (frozen choice).

## Limits

- **Resolution.** Preview resolution of about 27 px per letter height and 8-bit JPEG compression limit what PS can recover. A 2 Lh window on a strip about 100 px wide often leaves the mask.
- **Light directions.** These are taken from the titles, not measured. No sphere is present in the 1988 frames.
- **Weak link.** Placing a letter on a cut by facsimile template matching is the weak link. The test mostly measured that chain, not relief separability as such.
- **Exploratory files.** `cut_mapping_6*.json` are earlier exploratory mapping runs.

## Closing line

**Not obtained.** The held-out ח/ת controls were not separated (1/8, p = 0.996), and no target was scored. This changes nothing in any reading or rating. It does show that the public previews alone cannot carry a letter-level relief test: registration across lighting groups and placement of letters within a column both fail at this resolution. The remaining route is the WSRP full-resolution masters, which already exist as private TIFFs. A second AI-only session is justified only if those TIFFs (or the decrypted 1/4-scale copies) become readable in the compute environment. With them, the same code can be rerun with line placement anchored on hand-free SIFT matches at native resolution. Without them, it should not be repeated.
