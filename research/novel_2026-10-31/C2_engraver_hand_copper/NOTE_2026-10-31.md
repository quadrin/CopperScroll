## Question, and what could change

- Question: is the drawn rise in letter height at III 7 on the copper?
- If yes, the round-1 change point is a property of the engraving. It could then mark a change of hand, tool or layout at the boundary between entries 12 and 13 (III 8 is the first line of entry 13, III 8–10). If no, the change belongs to the drawing, the copy or the plate photograph, and no model of the list needs to account for it.

## Which cuts carry column III

- Puech 2006 radiograph plates: pl. CCCXXXVII ("8G 8D", PDF p. 658) and pl. CCCXXXVIII ("7G 7C", PDF p. 659), from W2F `plate_index.csv`.
- `controls_manifest.csv`, column `usc_1988_cuts_candidate`: `7;8`. Round-1 A `map_cuts.py`: cuts 7 and 8 → column III.
- The records agree with "cuts 7–8".
- A cut is a vertical strip of the column, so every line III 1–13 crosses each strip. The frames confirm this: 10–15 line bands per test frame (13 lines expected), at a pitch of 2.0–2.5 cm.

## Light codes

The six standard frames are R + {L, C, R} + {T, B}. L, C and R are three views of the curved strip: the strip is shifted and turned between them. T and B are light from the top and from the bottom. This was seen on training cut 5. It is why cross-frame photometric differences failed in exploration: frames of different views are not the same geometry.

## Why the measurement fails (exploratory evidence, training cuts 3, 5, 6)

- The engraved letters are visible by eye under bottom light (RLB, RCB). The strips, however, are crumpled and corroded, and patina blotches and holes are common.
- An automatic row profile of horizontal-stroke energy recovers the line pitch on most frames. It does not recover the letter extent reliably.
- Per-band letter height agreed across the six frames of the same cut with median Spearman ρ ≈ 0 (cut 5 −0.09, cut 6 0.00).
- Region-level contrasts varied across frames with a robust SD of 0.2–0.4. This is about the size of the effect sought.
- The strip-position split assigns bands to "before" and "after" without line numbers. Missing or spurious bands shift lines across the boundary.

## Limits

- **Scale.** The ruler was measured on every frame: 15 of 24 frames gave 153–155 px/cm at full resolution, matching the nominal 155. The others locked on a different periodic column (101–126). No decision used absolute scale.
- **Cut 7.** Its right part may hold the II/III margin (Puech names only 7G and 7C for column III). Only the inner 60 % of each strip was analysed.
- **Controls.** Cut 10 mixes column IV with the start of V. Column V has a positive drawing contrast (+12.8 %).
- **Resolution.** Half resolution was used. The failure comes from surface relief and blotches, not from pixel size. Training checks at 0.25 and 0.5 gave the same unreliability.
- **Exposure now.** Cuts 7, 8, 10 and 16 have been read and measured. They can no longer serve as reserved observations for another automatic copper test of this claim.
