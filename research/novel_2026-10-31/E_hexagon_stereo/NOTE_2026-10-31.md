## New observation
- The June 1974 pair D3C1208-400636F014 / A015 is a working stereo pair. Matching gives 2,400–10,000 tie points per window and a relief image with wadis and ridges.
- Without the panoramic camera model the heights are not recovered. The fitted height per disparity pixel ranges from 0.09 to 3.6 m against about 5.2 m expected (1.84 m pixels, base-to-height 0.35). The errors grow with slope (Qumran: 4.4 m below 10°, about 10 m above). Inside 1 km tiles the disparity explains little of the local relief (TRAIN: local NMAD 15.8 m against 31.7 m relief standard deviation).
- INFERENCE: the error is geometric (kilometre-scale, slope-dependent), not matching noise. The local affine-plus-polynomial geometry does not replace the optical-bar model over windows of 3–11 km. In QELT the cross-epipolar residual was 2.5 px against 0.3 px in QUMRAN and JERICHO_OUT.
- Coverage: F014 ends near the Kidron zone's north edge (zone at frame rows 24,536–29,104; the frame has 24,668), and cuts the south of the Qumran zone. Kidron needs F015 with A015 or A016.

## What it changes
- HEXAGON 1974 stereo without a camera model cannot supply the finer elevation model that the 30 October terrain run (direction F) named as its reopening condition. Its precision (≥ 4.4 m even on gentle slopes) is no better than GLO-30.
- Even at the target precision, the comparison with GLO-30 (30 m cells) could only find changes about 50–60 m across. Cisterns, tombs and most pools are smaller.
- GLO-30 is a 2011–2015 TanDEM-X surface model. It includes buildings, palms and orchards. Around Jericho these would appear as "1974 lower" without any ground change. Only bare-ground clusters could mean a lost depression.
- No 1978 elevation model is possible: the store holds only forward frames for 1978 (F006, F007).

## Camera model and the one dependency
- Ames Stereo Pipeline (ASP) 3.5.0 was downloaded (release asset StereoPipeline-3.5.0-2025-04-28-x86_64-Linux.tar.bz2, HTTP 200, SHA-256 202f6cf6…) and runs (`stereo --version`: 3.5.0, build 0e5ae643).
- Its KH-9 panoramic route was not run. Exact block: `cam_gen --camera-type opticalbar` needs the four frame-corner longitudes/latitudes (`--lon-lat-values`) from the USGS metadata. The store's index.json has no corner fields. EarthExplorer needs a login. My local geolocation covers only about 26 × 32 km near Jericho, not the frame corners about 100 km away.
- ASP's own guide (docs/examples/historical.rst, KH-9 section) calls the panoramic support "highly experimental" and advises not to spend much time on it.
- Reopening condition: the USGS corner coordinates for D3C1208-400636F014, F015, A015 and A016 (EarthExplorer metadata export by the owner). Then one ASP run (cam_gen opticalbar, bundle_adjust with GLO-30 control, dem2gcp) at reduced resolution would test whether the camera model removes the slope-dependent error.
