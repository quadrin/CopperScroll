## Limits

- All three readers and the coordinator are Claude models, so their errors may be correlated. Other vision models were not available in this session.
- The windows follow the protocol (§5, §6). They were resampled down to the expected target letter height (104.5 px, from the A2/B4 placement numbers on cuts 21–22) from 131–195 px, shown in luminance only, under top-centre and bottom-centre light only. A reader at native resolution, in colour, under all six lights might do better. That would be a new specification and a new round with controls never shown (§12), and it could no longer match the target series.
- The placement rests on A2's global fit. The coordinator checked each window by eye and moved 30 of them by at most 0.45 letter height.
