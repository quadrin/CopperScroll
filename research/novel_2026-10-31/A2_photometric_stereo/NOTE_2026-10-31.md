## Novelty gate

- Full-resolution USC TIFFs from the bucket (about 155 px per cm; letters about 150 px high), not the 1000-px previews of 30 October.
- Top-lit and bottom-lit frames registered without the shading sign (strip outline, then gradient magnitude).
- Letters placed by registering each 1988 strip to the photograph of the EDF copy (Puech 2006, vol. II), not by matching facsimile drawings.
- A seeded training/held-out split, rules chosen on training only, a chance baseline that allows for class imbalance.

This is a new model with a new frozen protocol. The 30 October result (1 of 8, p = 0.996) stands.

- One confirmatory run at 05:54:42 UTC; outputs in commit `0f04ef5f`. The protocol file is unchanged since the freeze.

**1. Registration across lighting groups** (`register.py`, `reg/*.json`).
- Method: strip-outline ECC (enhanced correlation coefficient) at 1/8 scale, then ECC on gradient magnitude at 1/4 and 1/2 scale. Gradient magnitude keeps its sign when the lamp moves from top to bottom.
- Check: phase correlation in 256-px patches, independent of the ECC objective.
- Bottom-centre to top-centre median residual: 1.57–2.66 px on 12 cuts (2, 3, 4, 6, 7, 8, 11, 16, 17, 18, 21, 22), 26–70 patches each.
- Failed: cut 5 (10.75 px) and cut 10 (3.63 px). Their bottom frames are not used. The odd set-ups (CT10 FRS; CT16 RET, REB, RFR) are 37–57 px off and are not used.
- Within a group, SIFT (scale-invariant feature transform) + RANSAC (random sample consensus): 71–1041 inliers, median residual 2.3–3.7 px.
- This solves round-1 failure 1 (3–14 matches across groups).

**2. Letter placement** (`place.py`, `controls.py`, `confirmatory/placements_all.csv`).
- The strip's multi-light edge image was correlated with the copy plate of its column, then ECC-refined. W2F (wave-2 plate model) locator boxes on the copy plate were then mapped into the 1988 frame.
- Gate: peak distinctiveness ≥ 1.8 and median local shift ≤ 0.15 letter height.
- Accepted on 12 cut/column pairs (distinctiveness 1.82–3.58; local median 0.013–0.095 letter height). Rejected: CT5→II, CT10→IV, CT10→V, CT11→VI, CT17→IX.
- Overlays of CT3, CT7 and CT8 were checked by eye. Line rows, the torn top edge of CT7 and the ככ of III 7 coincide.
- Coverage: 16 of 39 held-out controls and 26 of 66 training controls were placed. Most losses are on cuts 5 and 10, or the letter lies on a cut outside the delivery.
- This solves round-1 failure 3 for 12 cut/column pairs, not for all.

**3. Photometric stereo** (`ps.py`). Lambertian least squares with the titled azimuths and a fixed 30° elevation. A light is dropped where it is in shadow or saturated.
- n_y (the vertical normal component) shows the horizontal strokes cleanly.
- n_x has a step where the shadowed lamp subset changes. Side lamps leave the far half of the curved strip dark.
- Much of the letter contrast sits in albedo and occlusion, not in the normals.
- A two-light feature `tb` = (I_top − I_bottom)/(I_top + I_bottom) also works on the 2-light cuts (4, 17, 21).

**4. Training** (`train_eval.py`, `loo_train.json`). Training items were 95 training controls and W2F verified letters from columns I–X. The W2F letters exclude held-out positions and columns XI–XII.
- Leave-one-out balanced accuracy, best of 18 variants per rule:
  - ב/כ (`tb`): 0.650, n = 46.
  - ה/ח (`tb`): 0.661, n = 28.
  - ח/ת (`ny`): 0.795, n = 29.
- Every value is best-of-18, so all three are optimistic.

## Limits and deviations

- **Freeze time.** The protocol text says "about 06:10 UTC". The freeze commit is 05:54:36 UTC. The commit time is the record.
- **Training count.** Protocol step 6 says "87 items". That count predates the `tb` channel. The leave-one-out selection and the confirmatory training both used 95 items.
- **Training data.** W2F verified letters were added to the training controls, as the protocol declares. They are edition-aligned labels, not keyed controls.
- **Placement.** Placement assumes the EDF copy reproduces the original's letter positions within about 0.1 letter height (local median shift 0.013–0.095 Lh). The local refinement step never passed its threshold, so placements rest on the global affine fit.
- **Light model.** Light directions come from the frame titles. Elevation is fixed at 30°. No calibration sphere exists in the frames.
- **Exposure.** Viewed:
  - full-resolution crops of CT6, CT8 and CT3 (CT6 most);
  - overlays of CT3, CT7 and CT8;
  - the column III copy plate at half scale.

  These views include held-out letters on cuts 3, 6, 7 and 8. No held-out feature was computed before the freeze, and the rules are automatic. Round-1 held-out controls were used for training only. Not viewed: any column XII image, any target window, any sealed key. The column XII copy plate and cuts 21–22 were processed by code only.
