## Novelty gate

- C2 measured line **bands** automatically, without line numbers. Its band measure did not repeat between frames (ρ ≈ 0).
- C3 measures **each letter** at a known position. The W2F (wave-2 plate model) letter centre on the Puech 2006 copy photograph is mapped into the WSRP (West Semitic Research Project) 1988 frame with A2's text registration. Line numbers come from the edition alignment.
- Only box centres are used. The drawn box sizes never enter the copper measurement.
- Reliability is tested per letter: top-lit lights against bottom-lit lights, registered pixel to pixel by A2.

## Question, and what could change

- Round 1 found that Puech's drawing makes letters taller against line pitch from III 8 on (III 1–6 against III 8–13: 0.922 → 1.081, +17.2 %). III 8 is the first line of entry 13 (III 8–10).
- If the copper showed the same rise, the change would belong to the engraving (hand, tool, or layout). If the copper excluded it, the change would belong to the drawing or the copy.
- No identification, reading or location depends on this result. It decides only whether models of the list may treat the III 7 change as a physical feature.

## What was measured

- **Letters.** W2F verified letters placed by A2's affine fit inside A2's strip mask (margin 0.4 letter height): column III 62 (cut 7: 31, cut 8: 31), column I 96 (cuts 2, 3, 4). Copy photographs: Puech 2006, vol. II, copy A PDF p. 686 (column III) and p. 682 (column I).
- **Measure.** For each letter, gradient magnitude of every registered light (σ 2 px), averaged. Line pitch = autocorrelation of the row profile over ±2.8 letter heights. Letter extent = 20–80 % inter-quantile range of the background-subtracted row profile over ±0.4 letter height in x and ±0.5 pitch in y. h = extent / pitch, class-normalised with a table frozen from training cuts.
- **Control.** Column I: the only accepted column whose drawing contrast at the same split is below 5 % (+2.5 %). Cut 10 (C2's control) was rejected by A2.
- **Training (choices only).** Columns II, V, IX, X, XII (cuts 6, 11, 16, 17, 18, 21, 22), 288 letters.
- **Threshold.** THR = +0.038. On training, copper class heights vary with drawn class heights with slope k = 0.527, so the measure is compressed. The expected copper effect under the claim is k × 0.146 = 0.077. THR is half of that.

## Paired analysis (frozen; descriptive)

- Copper and drawing heights do not track each other letter by letter in the confirmatory columns. Class-normalised Spearman ρ: III 0.12, I 0.02. Raw: III −0.00, I 0.12. In training, the raw ρ was 0.37.
- III, copper = a + b·drawing + d·after: b = 0.42 (90 % −0.21 to 1.12), d = −0.086 (90 % −0.27 to +0.10).
- The copper explains none of the drawing's step: g₀ = 0.133, and g = 0.134 with copper as a covariate. The drawing's step "survives" the copper. With ρ near zero, this was expected and is not evidence either way.

## New observation

- The first letter-level copper heights of columns III and I: `out/letters_measured.csv` (156 letters, one row each, with top-lit and bottom-lit heights, pitch and the drawing values).
- **The measure repeats but tracks letter height only weakly.** Top-lit and bottom-lit heights agree well on column III (ρ 0.73; cut 8 0.90), but copper heights do not follow the drawn heights of the same letters (ρ ≈ 0–0.1). Class differences survive only in compressed form (tall letters 1.13×, yod 0.79×). Agreement between lights therefore measures the shared surface (patina blotches, cracks, holes), not only the letters. A light-to-light gate alone does not validate a copper letter measure.
- **Copper pitch in column III rises from III 1–6 to III 8–13** (340 → 376 px; ratio 1.10, 90 % 1.03–1.24). The drawing's own line pitch on the same letters rises by 1.15. Most of the drawn rise in h/pitch therefore comes from drawn letter heights growing more (class-normalised +30 %) than the spacing.

The crumpled strips change apparent scale along their length by up to about 25 %. So only scale-free ratios such as h/pitch are usable, and they also cancel any true change in spacing.

## What it changes

- The III 7 change remains a property of Puech's drawing only. Letter-level copper measurement at A2's positions can neither confirm nor exclude it. No model of the list should treat it as an engraving feature.
- Automatic copper measurement is now exhausted for this question on the 1988 frames. Band level failed its gate (C2). Letter level passes the reliability gate but is too weakly valid and has too few letters after III 7 (C3).
- The cuts 7–8 letter-level copper heights are now exposed. They can no longer serve as reserved observations for another automatic test of this claim.
- The lane should stop here. The decision at stake is small: round 1 already found that the drawn change does not coincide with the formula seams.

## Limits

- **Exposure.** Cuts 7 and 8 were viewed by eye by the A2, B2 and C2 workers and measured at band level by C2 before this test. The test is weaker than an unseen-image test. I viewed no copper image before the run. After the run, I viewed one 1/4-scale overlay of the RCT frames of cuts 8 and 7 to check that the mapped centres sit on text rows. They do.
- **Placement** rests on A2's global affine fit (A2's local refinement never passed). A misplaced centre measures a neighbour or a gap, which adds noise.
- **Calibration** k comes from class differences. A uniform height change may be compressed differently. Under a linear model, extent = a·height + b·pitch (the window scales with the pitch), k applies to h/pitch directly. The post-run check supports this.
- **Best-of-20 selection** of the measure on training makes the training reliability and validity optimistic.
- **Pitch fallback** (45 of 156 letters) is noisier. Own-pitch letters alone give Δ = −0.007.
- **Control** column I is one column on three cuts. Its copper contrast (−0.10) is larger than its drawing contrast (+0.03). This suggests a top/bottom effect on the strips, which the difference Δ removes only if it is common to all strips.
