## What was done

1. **Placement** (`b4lib.py`, `placements4.csv`). A2's code and outputs were reused unchanged (`a2_reused/`, SHA-256 in `sha256_inputs.txt`). Every W2F verified letter was mapped to the 1988 reference frame of A2's 12 accepted cut/column pairs (CT2, CT3, CT4 → I; CT6 → II; CT7, CT8 → III; CT11 → V; CT16 → IX; CT17, CT18 → X; CT21, CT22 → XII). Centre = box centre x and line centre y (W2F `plx`, `ply`); the box size was not used. A letter counts as placed if a square of ±0.45 letter heights (Lh) around it lies inside the strip. 436 of 1,240 letters placed; each is logged with line, word, letter index, cut, frame, box and placement confidence.
2. **Lines** (`items4.py`, `items4.json`). B2's word-level undisputed rule, restricted to columns with an accepted pair and to lines with at least 2 placed letters in undisputed words: 44 lines. Seeded split 22/22. Candidates: truth + up to 5 one-letter substitutions from the edition-confusion pairs (count_places ≥ 3, weighted by rate), only at placed letters. 73 varied positions on the held-out lines.
3. **Model** (`model4.py`). Patches of 2.0 Lh at 20 px per Lh. Channels: the six raking lights (high-passed) and two photometric ratios, top−bottom and left−right. Templates of 1.2 Lh from 256 TRAIN letters (22 letter forms). Score = channel-mean normalised cross-correlation, maximum over ±0.20 Lh × ±0.15 Lh, calibrated against TRAIN letters of other forms (z-score).
4. **Exploratory choice** (TRAIN only, leave-one-line-out; `explore/EX_V*.json`). Twelve variants. The chosen one (V12) had the best weighted pairwise accuracy on TRAIN: 0.62 (ב/כ 0.65, ה/ח 0.64). That is best-of-12 and optimistic. Top-1 accuracy over 25 forms was 0.07–0.14 for all variants (chance 0.04).
5. **Confirmatory run** (`run4.py confirm`), once: `heldout_result4.json`, `heldout_rankings4.csv`, `letter_pairs4.csv`, `target_rankings4.csv` (NOT RUN).

## New observations

1. **Correct letter positions did not rescue the template model.** With the letters placed, the held-out pairwise contests were split exactly even: the edition letter won 53 of 106 (exploratory count after the run, from `heldout_rankings4.csv`). B2's failure point (finding the letters) is removed, and the next limit appears: at the varied positions the 1988 relief does not separate the confusable letters under this model. A2 reached the same limit with relief rules (ב/כ 0.65, ה/ח 0.66 on training only).
2. **The decoy control worked as a check.** It flagged a tilt toward the substitute letter (P = 0.015). Exploratory reading of the per-pair table: on true כ the model chose ב in 7 of 8 contests at varied positions, and on true מ it chose ב in 4 of 5. The tilt toward ב appeared only on held-out letters (on TRAIN, כ beat ב in 17 of 25). With about 20 letters per pair this may be chance. Either way it is a letter-level bias, not a reading.
3. **IX 10 does not lie on an accepted cut/column pair.** Under A2's accepted CT16 → IX placement, the first two words of IX 10 (בקומעה כסף) lie on CT16. The disputed words (ETCBC מנה חרמ) map 0.15–6.4 Lh beyond CT16's left strip edge, onto CT17, whose IX placement A2 rejected. So no model that relies on A2's accepted pairs can score IX 10 until the CT17 → IX placement is solved (for example by a half-strip registration of CT17).
4. **Target coverage under letter-level placement.** Fixed before the run, from placement only: X 15 was the only target whose readings align letter by letter with W2F letter units on an accepted pair (all three differing letters place on CT18). XII 10 is a wildcard in W2F's alignment, and its readings R1–R7 differ in length. I 12's readings differ in letter count.

## What it changes

- Analysis by synthesis on the 1988 frames is now closed for both reasons: without letter positions (B2) and with them (B4). The disputed letters at XII 10, IX 10, X 15 and I 12 stay as they are. The XII 10 protocol still needs two human readers.

- A2's placement is good enough to put templates on letters (TRAIN montages show the letters centred in the window). The limit is the letter signal in these photographs, not registration.

## Limits and deviations

- **Small samples.** 256 TRAIN letters over 22 forms (4–25 per form); 157 held-out letters; about 20 held-out letters per hard pair.
- **Selection.** V12 is best of 12 variants on the same TRAIN letters. Its TRAIN figures are optimistic.
- **Positions come from the drawing.** W2F boxes are Puech's drawn letters, verified by eye on the facsimile, so the eligible positions are well-drawn letters. Only box centres were used; the local search (±0.20/±0.15 Lh) absorbs small differences of centre between letter forms.
- **Coverage.** Only columns with an accepted pair (I, II, III, V, IX, X, XII), and only the 35 % of W2F letters that place with margin. Columns IV, VI, VII, VIII and XI, cuts 1, 5, 9, 10, 12–15, 19, 20 and 23, and CT17 as column IX are not covered.
- **Light model.** No light calibration. The lights are used as separate channels, and two-light cuts (4, 17, 21) have three channels only.
- **Deviations.** None in the confirmatory run. The run took 414 s.
