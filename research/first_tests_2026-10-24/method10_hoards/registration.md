# Method 10, hoard check: registration

Written 2026-10-23, before any hoard list was compiled and before CHRE or any hoard catalogue was opened in this session.

## Scroll side (fixed)

- Sums: the 45 weight sums in `scroll_amounts.csv` (T08, SHA-256 f19960d49c4f8817a0ecf8c4f86bda6c32d6e5c76bddaf1ac5cae3d98ca73091) whose unit is ככ (30 sums), ככרין (14) or ככר (1), with status ok or uncertain. Displayed values are used. Count units (ingots, jars, vessels, staters) and doubtful sums are left out.
- Metal: as the table's object field says. Silver sums compare with a hoard's silver; gold sums with its gold; "silver and gold" and unspecified sums with its silver plus gold.

## Units (two readings, two masses each)

- Talent reading (Milik, Allegro): every ככ, ככרין and ככר sum is in talents.
- Karsh reading (Lefkovits 2000; Puech 2006): ככ sums are in karsh; ככרין and ככר sums stay in talents.
- Talent mass: light 21.3 kg (Lefkovits 2000 p. 473: 3,000 regular shekels of 7.1 g); heavy 42.6 kg (3,000 shekels of 14.2 g). One karsh = 1/300 talent: 71 g or 142 g.

## Hoard side (fixed eligibility)

- A single deposit of silver or gold (coins, ingots, bullion, scrap or vessels), published or in a database record that gives a count or a weight. Bronze-only hoards are left out. In a mixed hoard only the silver and gold count.
- Findspot inside 29.5–33.4°N, 34.2–36.0°E.
- Primary window: closing date (latest coin or dated object) from 63 BCE to 70 CE. Hoards closing 70–136 CE are reported apart, as exploratory.
- Mass: the stated total weight where given; else count × standard mass: shekel, sela or tetradrachm 14.2 g; half-shekel or didrachm 7.1 g; denarius or drachm 3.8 g; quinarius or hemidrachm 1.9 g; aureus 7.9 g. "About N" uses N; a range uses its middle, and both ends are reported.

## Rule

A sum matches a hoard when the hoard's metal mass M and the sum's mass E give E/2 ≤ M ≤ 2E.

## Predictions

- P1, talent reading: no sum matches any eligible primary-window hoard, at either talent mass. One match fails P1.
- P2, karsh reading, light karsh (71 g): at least 15 of the 30 ככ sums match at least one eligible primary-window hoard. Fewer than 15 fails P2. The heavy karsh (142 g) is reported as a second branch.
- Decision: if neither reading gives any match, the hoard route closes. A weight match is a necessary condition only. It identifies nothing; the next step would compare findspot, depth and container.
