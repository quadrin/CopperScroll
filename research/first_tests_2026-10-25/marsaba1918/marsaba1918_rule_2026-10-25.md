# Entry 35, Mar Saba 1918 aerial: rule fixed before the targets are placed on the frame

Written 25 October 2026 (about 19:30 CST) by Claude for the owner ("go for it", 25 Oct, 19:25 CST). Exploratory test; nothing here changes a rating by itself.

## Exposure before this rule
- The frame (BS Pal. 0923, Bavarian State Archive aerial, 3 January 1918, flight 304 frame 229, "west of Mar Saba", height 3,000 m, oblique) was opened once on 24 Oct only to read its legend and scale; tent rows near the centre were noted. No target was placed and no part of the frame was searched for cairns ([first reading](../../sources/readings_2026-10-24/) / project doc `gutfeld_marsaba_reading_2026-10-24.md`).
- Already known and public in the repo: Patrich 1994 (ASI Map of Deir Mar Saba) records **no cairn** between the map's western edge and Deir Mar Saba and none at a gorge mouth; three undated cairns (Sites 99, 100, 108; 3–5.4 m) lie downstream of the monastery ([extraction](../../../registration/patrich_mar_saba_map_extracted.md)).

## Targets (from findings_log F14.3; *Israel Guide* 13 pp. 194–195)
- **T1, upper canyon entrance:** printed grid 1808.1259.
- **T2, upper canyon end:** printed grid 1819.1227.
- Read as Palestine 1923 grid (EPSG:28191), 100 m digits. The CRS reading is an assumption; the result is reported for it only.
- Search radius at each target: the georeference error (below), with a minimum of 75 m. The printed grids carry ±50 m rounding.

## Data
- Frame: `07 Maps and GIS/Bavarian aerials 1918/BS-Pal-0923_1918-01-03_Mar-Saba.jpg` (10,639,017 bytes; hash recorded at intake).
- Terrain: Copernicus GLO-30 DSM tile N31 E035 (30 m), fetched from the public AWS copy.
- Reference map: Survey of Palestine 1:20,000 sheet 18-12 *Deir Mar Saba* (1940s), from the owner's Drive.

## Stage A: place the frame on the ground
1. Pick ground control points (GCPs) that are clear on both the frame and the 1940s sheet or the DSM: the monastery, wadi confluences, sharp bends, summits and saddles. Heights come from the DSM.
2. Solve one camera (position, orientation, focal length) by least squares on the GCPs (pinhole model; focal length searched).
3. Error = leave-one-out reprojection error, converted to ground metres at each target's distance.
4. **Gate A:** at least 8 GCPs and leave-one-out median ≤ 60 m on the ground near the targets. If it fails, both targets are UNTESTABLE on this frame and the test stops.

## Stage B: is each target in view?
- **Gate B:** the target projects inside the frame, at least 100 px from the edge, and the DSM line of sight from the camera to the target is clear (not in dead ground behind a ridge or under the gorge wall). A target that fails is UNTESTABLE.
- Report the ground pixel size at each target (metres per pixel along and across the line of sight).

## Stage C: can the frame show a cairn here?
- **Gate C (detection power):** the ground pixel size at the target must be ≤ 1.0 m in both directions, so that a 3 m heap spans at least 3 px. If Patrich Sites 99, 100 or 108 are in view, they are added as controls to the blind set below; if at least one of them is in view and none is marked by the scorer, the frame is judged unable to show cairns and every target becomes UNTESTABLE.

## Stage D: blind scoring
- Crops of the same ground size (about 160 × 160 m, resampled to the same pixel size) are cut around: each in-view target; each in-view Patrich cairn (control); and 10 decoy points on the same gorge and its rims, in view, chosen by a fixed random seed (2510) at least 300 m from both targets and the monastery.
- Crops are shuffled and given codes. A separate agent that is not told the hypothesis, the targets or which crops are controls scores each crop: "is there a discrete heap-like stone feature about 2–6 m across, with a shadow falling towards the north-east (sun from about 205–219°, about 24–31° high)? yes / no / unclear, and where".
- The key (code → crop) is written and hashed before scoring and opened only after the scores are back.

## Decision (per target)
- **PRESENT:** the scorer says yes with a mark inside the search radius, AND at most 3 of the 10 decoys are scored yes.
- **NONSPECIFIC:** the scorer says yes at the target but 4 or more decoys are yes (heap-like features are common on this terrain at this scale).
- **ABSENT:** Gates A–C pass and the scorer says no or unclear at the target.
- **UNTESTABLE:** any gate fails.
- Every gate value, the GCP list and residuals, the scores and the key are reported, whatever the outcome.

## What the outcome means (fixed now)
- ABSENT agrees with Patrich's survey (no cairn at the upper Kidron gorge) and adds a 1918 view, before modern changes. It does not exclude a buried or collapsed heap below about 2 m.
- PRESENT would point to a feature not recorded by the 1990s survey (lost, or missed). It would justify a field or modern-imagery check; it would not by itself identify the entry 35 cairn.
- Any change to the entry 35 rows goes to the integrator under the existing rubric.
