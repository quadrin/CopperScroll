## Tananir from Boling 1975

- Text, p. 33: the building stands "on the lower slope of Gerizim, some 300 meters from ancient Shechem". It is 18 m square. The modern name of the area is no longer Tananir; it is built up.
- Fig. 1 (location plan, 1:12,500, contours at 20 m) shows Shechem, Jacob's Well, Tell er-Ras with the spot height 831, the Tananir building (a diamond), area 8 (circular structure) and area 9 (vat).
- I put the plan on the Palestine Grid with two points that are Checked on the images: Tell Balata (176.78/180.06) and the Tell er-Ras platform (176.06/179.23).
  - The scale from these two points is 19% larger than the scale bar of the plan, so the plan is not exact.
  - With the two-point fit, the building is at **PG 176.88/179.58**. This is on the slope west of the southbound road, about 120 m south of the road fork. This agrees with the plan, where the diamond is west of that road.
  - With the scale bar and Tell Balata only, the building is at 176.90/179.66. This point is on the road, which disagrees with the plan.
- On the 1974 image (F014a) the place is built up with houses. The building cannot be seen. **Position: Probable (about 50 m).**
- The database point of E99 (176.90/179.50) is 82 m south of the two-point position.
