# Koḥlit count with measured positions, second pass: Birket Musa measured as an area. 74% of the pool is in the east sector from the database point; centre 4° inside. No number of the count is changed (14 November 2026)

## Result first

- The larger part of this work was done on 13 November (entry tests 1 and 2; the position and margin audit in the repository, pull request 127). The count program can not be run again with new points: it reads coded sheets and database rows, and the database file is not in the repository. What can be done is to compute the conditions again by hand for the units where an image gives a measured position. This note adds the one unit for which the positions became better since the audit.
- **Tulul Abu el-ʿAlaiq (S2539), condition C1, pool to the east.** The audit used the pool centre from one hand measurement (132°, "3° inside"). The pool is now measured on two images that agree to 3 to 5 m (old air photographs 9).

- The east sector of the protocol is 45° to 135°. **The centre is 4.1° inside. 74% of the area of the pool is inside the sector; the south-west quarter is outside.** The whole pool is nearer than the limit of 1,000 m.
- The database point is 32 m from the point of the south mound on the image (191373 / 139732). From the image point the centre is at 127.0°.
- **Reading:** the match of this unit on C1 holds with a measured pool. It stays a narrow match: a pool that is 26% outside the sector, with a centre 4° from the edge. The audit said "holds, 3° inside"; this note says the same with better numbers.
- **One more measured fact, not a condition of the count:** pool A(B)101 of the Pools Complex is at 333.9°, 407 m from the database point, in the north sector. Condition C2 asks for a pit (cistern) to the north, not a pool, so this changes nothing in the count. It is recorded because the two large open waters of the estate are one in the east sector and one in the north sector of the south mound.
