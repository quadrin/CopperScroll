# Kh. el-Gharur on the modern image: a stone-covered ruin of about 125 × 66 m is at my place of 1974; the camp walls are not sharp; S845 stays not decided (14 November 2026)

This note is beside the registered Koḥlit count. It does not change the count, a match, a rating or a candidate. It is not a blind test: I knew the survey plan and the two places.

## Result first

1. **A ruin is on the modern image at PG 201.430/179.838** (32.21167 N, 35.54355 E). It is a grey, stone-covered area with four sides, about **125 m east to west and 66 m north to south** (about 8 dunams), on bare ground. Light bands of earth (trenches or robbed walls) cross it and go along its north-west, south and east sides. It has small round pits.
2. **This agrees with the survey record** (Zertal 2008, site 259): a camp with sides of 116, 104, 72 and 56 m, about 9 dunams, at grid 2014/1798.
3. **The ruin is 14 m from my centre of 1974** (201.437/179.826, row B75). It is 73 m from the weak candidate of the blind second reader (201.453/179.907); that place is a dark slope north of the ruin, with no ruin on the modern image.
4. **Status: the ruin of survey site 259 is Checked by place and size** (modern image and 1 December 1974). **The form of a camp (straight walls, a trapezoid) is Probable only**: the north and south edges are nearly straight, but I cannot follow a wall round the four sides, and the trenches make the east end unclear.

6. **S845 stays "not decided; it leans to hold".** The camp question is now much better: the ruin is real and is where the count needs it. But the place of the ruin of Kh. es-Saleh is still Probable only, the margin at the database point is 3 m, and this reading of the camp is by one reader who knew the answer. I do not upgrade the unit again without a blind reading (lesson of row D16).
7. The south-east corner of the ruin, where the survey puts the sarcophagus, is at about 201.49/179.81 (B75 had 201.49/179.79).

## Limits

- One reader, not blind. The service image (Esri World Imagery) has no date on it and can be 5 to 10 m wrong in place.
- The pits can be cistern mouths or robber pits. I do not count them as cisterns.
- The light bands look recent (digging). They are not shown to be ancient walls.
