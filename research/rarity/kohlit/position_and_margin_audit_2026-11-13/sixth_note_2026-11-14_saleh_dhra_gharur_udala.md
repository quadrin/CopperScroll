## The Roman camp of Kh. el-Gharur on the 1974 image

- On tile F014b (1 December 1974) there is a rectangular area of about 105 × 70 m with a block texture, just north-east of the database point. Its size and shape agree with the survey plan. **Checked as a feature.**
- Camp centre: **PG 201.437/179.826** (37 m east and 26 m north of the database point). South-east corner, where the sarcophagus lay: about 201.49/179.79.

## The three units

**S843 Kh. edh-Dhraʿ.** The database point is the survey grid point of Bar and of Zertal (2018/1793). On the image it is on a small spur beside the road, as in the survey text. From that point, the camp is at 325.4° and 639 m, and the south-east corner is at 328° and 581 m. The point can move 135 m in any direction before C2 or C3s fails. **Holds.**

**S845 Kh. es-Saleh.**
- The two points: the database point is 2021/1791. Zertal and Bar give 2021/1790. The site was not visited.
- The written place: Glueck 1951 puts it on the east side of the road, with Wadi es-Salih below its north side and a small gully on its south side. On the image this is a spur about 250 m long, between E 202.07 and 202.30.
- The test with the measured camp:
  - C3s (the sarcophagus at the south-east corner): holds for any place on the west half of the spur (to E 202.18, at 315.5° and 977 m).
  - C2 (cisterns, taken at the camp centre): holds only up to E 202.12.
  - From the Zertal–Bar point (2021/1790), both conditions fail: the camp is at 321° and 1059 m.
- **Not decided.** A ground position is needed.

**S1032 Udala.** Both database points are Finkelstein's published points (10 m grid). From them, Tell er-Ras is at 986 m. From Kallai's point the distance is 1006 m. A tell of 2.5 ha is about 180 m across, so a single point does not settle a distance this close to the limit. **Not decided.**

## Limits

- The camp centre was read by eye on one image date (about 15 m).
- The cistern positions inside the camp are not on the plan. I used the centre for C2.
- Kallai's grid comes from a draft OCR of a Hebrew page.
