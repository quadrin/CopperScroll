# Blind second reading of 14 November: the tell and the Tell er-Ras band are confirmed; the 1943 road at the wadi and the Gharur camp are not; S845 goes back to "not decided" (14 November 2026)

This note is beside the registered Koḥlit count. It does not change the count, a match, a rating or a candidate.

## Method

- A second reader got a folder with five satellite images of 1974 (north up, map grid, no marks of mine), the scan of Glueck's fig. 130 and a sheet of four questions. The reader had no notes, no project and no web. The reader's answers are in the project, not edited: `claude/blind-second-reading-answers-of-the-second-reader-not-edited-2026-11-14.md`.
- The reader made an own fit of fig. 130 to the 1974 image: 118 patches, 5 m, scale 3.50 m for each pixel (mine: 70 blocks, 22 m, 3.47 m).

## What this does to S845 (Kh. es-Saleh)

- The two readers agree that the ruin is not out on the east part of the spur: the 1943 road is 120 to 200 m west of the spur track, and the ruin is beside that road.
- But the result now has two weak links, not one:
  1. the place of the ruin (beside a road that is somewhere between E 201.89 and 201.97);
  2. the place of the camp (my centre, or 83 m north of it).

I said before the reading that a different road place could reverse the result; the road did not reverse it, the camp weakened it.

## What I take from the reader's other remarks

- At Tell er-Ras the December shadows of a tree row fall on the band. June is the better date. A path at the foot of the platform, the edge of the planted forest and a forestry terrace are other explanations. The date and depth of the band are not known from any report (entry 57 note).
- Near Tell Abu Sidreh the reader saw a small bright oval of about 30 m at 203.052/178.053. I did not record it. Status: Seen.

## Limits

- The second reader is a model of the same kind as the first, with the same eyes. Agreement is a test of the reading, not of the ground.
- The camp question was asked without the survey plan. A reader who has the plan (a trapezoid of 116/104/72/56 m) can find more. That test is not blind.
