# Koḥlit unit S1032 Udala on the images: the tell is where the database has it; the unit stays not decided (14 November 2026)

This note is beside the registered count. It does not change the count, a match, a rating or a candidate.

## Result first

1. **No plan or measured description of the Udala features is held.** The only record is Finkelstein, Lederman and Bunimovitz 1997, pp. 699–700 (two grid points and two short texts), with Kallai 1972 for the tell. So I measured the two places on the images of 1974.
2. **Tell er-Ras (near ʿAwarta) is Checked at PG 176.74/174.04.** A round terraced summit, about 150 m across at the top ring and about 220 m with its terraces, on 1 December 1974 (F014 a) and 25 June 1974 (F012 d). The two dates agree to 35 m. The database point (176.75/174.05) is on it.
3. **A group of large trees with buildings is on the west side of the summit, at about 176.70/174.05.** This is the place that the survey gives for en-Nabi ʿUzeir ("on its west side"). Status: Possible as the shrine (no dome is read).
4. **Udala:** the houses of 1974 are in a strip of 250 m from N 173.15 to N 173.40. The database point (176.25/173.20) is in the dense south group. The survey gives 0.3 ha for the old village; which houses are the old part cannot be read.
5. **Distances from the database point of Udala:**

| To | Distance | Limit 1,000 m |
|---|---|---|
| Tell centre (measured) | 972 m at 30° | inside by 28 m |
| West side of the tell, tree group (C3s, sheikh's tomb) | 962 m at 28° | inside by about 40 m |
| Near (south-west) edge of the terraces | about 880 m | inside |
| Far (north-east) edge | about 1,090 m | outside |

6. **So the unit stays "not decided".**
   - C3s (the sheikh's tomb) holds with about 40 m to spare, if the shrine is at the tree group. My measuring error is about 35 m. This is Probable only.
   - C2 (cisterns and rock-cut caves "on the tell") cannot be decided: the 1,000 m limit goes through the tell. A little more than half of the tell is inside.
   - The unit needs C2 and C3s. Only a record of where the cisterns are on the tell can decide it.
7. **One more thing seen:** a second round hill with concentric terraces, about 230 m across, is between the two places at PG 176.60/173.58 (two dates). It is lower than the tell (540 m against 570 m). It has no name in the sources that I hold. Status: Seen. It is not Tell er-Ras by the survey grid.
