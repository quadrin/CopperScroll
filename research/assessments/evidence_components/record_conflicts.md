# Record conflicts found while rating

The raters compared each atlas entry with the repository records it rests on. These are the places where the atlas text, a table or a note disagrees with a newer or more specific record. Nothing here has been corrected yet; each item names the files to reconcile.

- **1 · Wadi Nuweiʿimeh (valley), NE of Jericho** — Atlas period 'earlier or later only' for the valley conflicts with phase5_assessments line 3 (entry 17, same valley): caves in use 1st c. BCE-70 CE (Aronshtam, Eisenberg, Sion).
- **1 · Wadi Nuweiʿimeh (valley), NE of Jericho** — Atlas evidence calls Nuweiʿimeh one valley from the NW springs to the NE lower course; F3.2 was qualified on 1 Oct 2026 (findings_log line 161).
- **2 · Wadi Nuweiʿimeh (valley), NE of Jericho** — Atlas landmark and period fields for entry 2 are empty, although research/feature_workbench/edition_predicates_2026-10-13/entries2_26 (13 Oct) records edition-specific predicates and a component contradiction at the Kidron monuments.
- **2 · Kidron valley at the Kidron monuments (Absalom, Bene Ḥezir, Zechariah)** — research/schematics/entries/entry_02.py lines 5, 77 attribute the personal-name reading only to news reports of Gibson; Milik 1960 pp. 139, 144 published it (entries2_26 README line 9 recommends the correction).
- **3 · Temple enclosure (Temple Mount)** — Leads report F12.5 proposes cistern No. 8 for entry 3 (possible/low); the atlas does not carry it.
- **4 · Tell es-Sultan (Old Jericho, Elisha's spring)** — Atlas evidence for entry 4 cites 'a mound and a nearby cemetery'; a cemetery is not among entry 4's landmarks, and the immersion installation the entry needs is not reported at the tell (phase5_assessments line 15).
- **5 · Temple enclosure (Temple Mount)** — Atlas title 'The winding stair' follows Puech; the text and translation shown follow Milik's 'foundry' (noted in the caution).
- **6 · Tyropoeon valley** — Entry 6 Tyropoeon placement has no recorded editorial source in the repo; atlas title 'The Millo cistern' follows a reading Puech 2015 and Lefkovits do not adopt here.
- **6 · Tyropoeon valley** — readings.json e6-sum: text shown sums to 41, all three editions as recorded give 42.
- **7 · Temple enclosure (Temple Mount)** — Atlas title 'House of Measures' takes המדה in the sense Puech rejects (2015 p. 40); noted in the caution.
- **9 · East Gate (Golden Gate position) and eastern wall** — Entry 9 is 'best-supported, medium' in the atlas, but feature_constraints.csv line 26 and F12.4 record that no recorded cistern meets the distance; the atlas evidence ('Partly (undated cisterns with channels)') omits this negative.
- **10 · East Gate (Golden Gate position) and eastern wall** — Atlas evidence text for entries 9 and 10 is a bare fragment ('Partly (...)'), copied from the phase-5 index column.
- **11 · Tell es-Sultan (Old Jericho, Elisha's spring)** — Atlas evidence for entry 11 gives no feature; the repo has the SWP basin (east, undated) and the relationships workbench marks the 1898-reservoir branch contradicted.
- **13 · Temple enclosure (Temple Mount)** — The Text tab form שבמלחמ ends in a מ that no edition reports (readings.json e13-millo note).
- **16 · Hyrcania (Kh. el-Mird) and its aqueducts** — Puech 2015 n. 193 rejects the Hyrcania restoration on length; the atlas caution for entry 16 mentions only Puech's pool.
- **17 · Wadi Nuweiʿimeh (valley), NE of Jericho** — The Text tab shows הכיפין 'domes(?)', which matches none of the three edition readings.
- **17 · el-Buqeiʿah plateau** — The atlas gives buqeia 'possible, medium', but the phase 5 check is low and the period fit is 'earlier only' (tables/phase5_assessments.csv line 4). The atlas period 'in use 1st c. BCE–70 CE (caves)' does not apply here.
- **17 · ʿAin Duk / ʿAin en-Nuweiʿimeh springs (Naʿaran)** — The repo records no reason specific to ʿAin Duk as an entry-17 candidate (phase3_site_index lists it only as possible).
- **18 · Wadi el-ʿAṣla / Gofet el-ʿAṣla area, NW Dead Sea** — The atlas rates entry 18 'possible, medium', but no pit is reported and the period is not stated (phase5 check_confidence low, tables/phase5_assessments.csv line 5).
