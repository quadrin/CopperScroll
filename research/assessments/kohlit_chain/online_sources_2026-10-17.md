## Opened by a collection worker, then deleted (exposed)

A photo-collection worker opened these Wikimedia Commons files while looking for photographs of Doq and the Jericho palaces. It judged that each might show the plain north of Jericho, deleted its copy, and did not describe the content. The integrator did not view them.
