# Site photographs for the atlas gallery — 17 October 2026

160 photographs of 31 atlas places: 113 modern photographs from Wikimedia Commons and 47 historical photographs from the Library of Congress G. Eric and Edith Matson collection (1898–1977; 13 air views of 1931 and 1937). Evidence note: [online imagery, 17 October](../../../sources/online_imagery_2026-10-17/README.md).

## Files

- `<id>.jpg`: the archived image, long side at most 2000 px, JPEG quality 88, EXIF removed.
  - Commons: made from Commons' standard 1920 px thumbnail (1280 or 960 px when the original is smaller). Commons refused non-standard widths.
  - Matson: made from the Library of Congress TIFF master, reduced to 2000 px and converted to RGB.
- `manifest.json`: one record per image. It holds the title, author, credit line, licence and licence URL, the verbatim rights text, the source page, the downloaded URL, the original pixel size, the source date, any camera coordinates from the source metadata, the SHA-256 of the downloaded bytes, the archive JPEG and the atlas WebP, the transformation, and the alt text, caption, relevance note and entry numbers. `lane` records which collection pass produced it.
- The atlas copies (1600 px WebP and 240 px thumbnails) are made by `tools/build_site_gallery.py`. It also mirrors this folder to `atlas/research/assets/plans/`.

## Reuse status

Each image keeps its own licence. The atlas shows the credit and licence with every photograph.
- Commons: public domain, CC0, CC BY or CC BY-SA, read from the file's own licence metadata. Files marked NC, ND, "fair use" or with no clear licence were not taken.
- Matson: each item's rights field reads "No known restrictions on publication" (checked per item on 17 October).
- No new licence is asserted.

## How the captions were made

Each image was looked at before it was kept. The caption gives the source's own identification. Where relevant it says what the photograph does not show or cannot establish. No caption claims a Copper Scroll identification, a deposit, a construction date or a measurement.

## Exclusions (frozen tests)

- No photograph of the Copper Scroll was taken or opened.
- Nothing for Tell es-Sultan or the Jericho oasis. Nothing that looks over the plain north of Jericho was kept. Files opened and deleted for that reason are listed in the [SULTAN-1 record](../../../assessments/kohlit_chain/online_sources_2026-10-17.md).
- No file naming Abu Saraj IV/17, the Cave with the Masonry Wall or Twin Cave.

## Notes on individual images

- `choziba-ihm-herodian-aqueduct-bridge`: the file is named Herodian, but its own description says the bridge belongs to an Umayyad line. The caption gives both.
- `jer-tyropoeon-excavation-1927-1928`: the Library of Congress title says Tyropoeon, but the negative sleeve says "Kedron Valley". The location is uncertain.
- `ibziq-air-view-wadi-faraa-eastern-end-1931`: regional context only. Kh. Ibziq is not in view.
- `jordan-ford-river-jordan-ford-color-slide-1950-1977`: the series heading reads "Jericho and Dead Sea area and River Jordan". The image shows only the riverbank and water.
