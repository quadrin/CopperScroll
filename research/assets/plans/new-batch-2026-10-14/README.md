# New-source native-page archive

The [member register](private_asset_register.json) identifies privately preserved full-page and full-spread derivatives by hash. Captions, scales, north arrows and credits remain with the source pages; reader manifests preserve exact provenance and rendering details. The [reading ledgers](../../../sources/new_batch_2026-10-14/README.md) define inspected scope. Archive membership alone gives no reading credit.

The original source images and extracted modern text remain private. Public records contain original analysis and retrieval locators. Two source0 inventory facsimiles have [unresolved exposure](../../../sources/new_batch_2026-10-14/visual_exposure.json); archiving does not inspect or clear them.

## Archive integrity and continuation proofs

The [integrity audit](archive_integrity_audit.json) verifies every registered member against the completed native archives1–17 by size and SHA256; each archive also passes its ZIP CRC check. Early saved snapshots of parts12/16 lacked their final members and central directories while another writer was completing those files. The [recovery utility](recover_zip_directory.py) rebuilt directories for checksum-valid complete members only. Those historical partial copies are preserved separately; use the completed originals for the full member register. No source image was visually opened during byte-level validation or repair.

The [continuation proof register](continuation_proof_register.json) locates additional private page derivatives and source metadata. It adds no reading credit beyond the linked source ledgers.
