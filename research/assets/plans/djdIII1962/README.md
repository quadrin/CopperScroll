# DJD III: plates volume (1962)

M. Baillet, J. T. Milik and R. de Vaux, with H. W. Baker, *Les ‘petites grottes’ de Qumran*, Oxford: Clarendon, 1962, **Planches**. This upload is the plates volume, not the text volume.

[Complete supplied plates volume](plates-volume.pdf) preserves cave photographs, finds and Copper Scroll photographs/drawings. PDF page 15 is Plate III: cave 8 = 3Q, the rolls in situ, and cave 19 = 2Q. [Plate III page image](plateIII-3q.png). Plate I begins at PDF page 13; Plates XLIII–LXXI document the rolls, opening apparatus, copies and column images. Images are historical published evidence; archiving supplies no new manuscript reading.
