## Observations relevant to concealment (EVIDENCE, exploratory; quotes ≤ 12 words)
- **Second Temple drain used as a refuge (IEJ 2007, pp. 166–168; Fig. 14).** The drain under the stepped street held late Second Temple pottery, several hundred coins mostly of the Jewish Revolt, and **intact cooking pots**. Street flagstones were broken "deliberately, exactly above the drain", making way into it. The authors link this to Josephus, *War* 6.370, 401–402, 429–430 (refugees in the underground passages in 70 CE). AUTHOR INTERPRETATION for the link; the deposits and the breaches are EVIDENCE.
- **Rock-cut "pool" near the spring filled with boulders and debris (IEJ 2007, pp. 153–155; Fig. 1).** The fill (about 3 m deep, about 250 m³ excluding the boulders) is a make-up under a late 8th-century BCE house floor, not a Roman-period closure. Keep it separate from the 1st-century BCE boulder blocking of the shaft above the spring in Reich & Shukron 2021 (p. 267).
- **Smoothed panel without an inscription (Siloam Tunnel paper, p. 59; Fig. 6).** At the entrance from the Round Chamber to Tunnel IV the rock bears a smoothed rectangular panel, about 38 × 48 cm, with nothing written on it.

These change no rating, test or outcome.
