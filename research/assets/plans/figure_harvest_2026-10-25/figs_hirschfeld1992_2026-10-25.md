## Most useful for which entry, and what could be measured

All links to entries are INFERENCE unless marked. Every measurement is still to be taken from the 200 dpi PNG against the printed scale bar.

**Entry 29: Castellion = Kh. el-Mird / Hyrcania (conduit and great northern reservoir candidate)**
- **Fig. 28** (p54) is the best plan of the hilltop (EVIDENCE):
  - scale bar 15 m and north arrow ✓;
  - a legend separates **Herodian** from **Byzantine** walls;
  - two small square features in the court look like cistern openings. That reading is INFERENCE; the legend does not name them.
  - What can be measured: the enclosure (the text gives about 30 × 40 m in its table, EVIDENCE); the positions of the openings; the Herodian wall lines.
  - It does **not** show the conduit or the northern reservoir: the plan covers only the summit.
- Fig. 27 (aerial photo, looking east) is useful for orientation only.
- Fig. 73 (burial cave with fresco) shows a cave with 2.5 m ceiling height and eight troughs, per the text (EVIDENCE, p143). It has no scale.

**Entry 32: Choziba / Wadi Qilt (mouth of the water outlet)**
- **Fig. 114** (p207) is the most useful (EVIDENCE):
  - a location inset with a 500 m bar;
  - a plan of the Cells of Choziba with a 15 m bar and north arrow, showing the trails, gates, the bridge and "Wadi Qilt" labelled;
  - a section of the wadi at the bridge, 15 m bar.
