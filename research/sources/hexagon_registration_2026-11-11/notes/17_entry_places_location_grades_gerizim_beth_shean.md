# Note 17: all entry places assessed again with location grades; Gerizim, Ibziq and Beth Shean on new frames; the Gerizim precinct plan

Date: 2026-11-12. Sources: `tables/phase3_places.csv` (37 places), `tables/phase5_assessments.csv` (37 assessments), the image work of 11 and 12 November (notes 15 and 16 of the repository), and the EAMENA tables. One reader, not blind.

## Result first

1. **The new work makes the place positions more certain. It does not make any entry match more certain.** I propose no change of a rating.
2. Of the 37 places, 19 now have a position that is confirmed on an image, and 7 more agree with their area. 3 cannot be tested or are not confirmed. 6 are not tested (areas inside the EAMENA table). 2 are outside the frames.
3. All confirmed positions are inside the precision that the table states. Thus no "medium" match was resting on a wrong point.
4. Of the 16 matches that the place table gives as "best supported, medium", 14 are at a place with a confirmed or agreeing position. The other 2 are entries 17 and 20 (areas).
5. The weakest match after this work is entry 30 at Tell el-Qos: the mound is not confirmed, and the feature is not in the sources.

## Three levels of a match

An entry match has three levels. The new work is about level 1 only.

| Level | Question | What the new work gives |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Place | Is the point of the table at the place? | Tested for 29 of 37 places |
| 2. Feature | Is the feature of the entry (cistern, steps, tomb, conduit) known at the place? | A small addition: EAMENA records by type. No feature is located on an image |
| 3. Reading and name | Does the entry name this place? | Nothing new from the images |

The reason for the limit: the images have a ground pixel of approximately 0.8 to 1 m and a position error of 15 to 50 m. The features of the scroll are 1 to 10 m in size. Thus the images can confirm a place. They cannot find the spot of a deposit.

## Addendum 2, 12 November 2026: fine registration at Gerizim and Ibziq

The residual of the last step is 36 m (Gerizim, 34 blocks) and 40 m (Ibziq, 56 blocks). This registration does not correct the height of a summit. Thus a control point is necessary on high ground.

**The city in the 1st century CE (Mazor and Najjar 2007, chapter 1).**

- The civic centre was first laid out in the early 1st century CE in the ʿAmal basin, south of Nahal Harod: an agora with a basilica and two temples, a theatre south of the agora, a bathhouse, and paved streets (their Plan 1.3).

**What this means for entry 58.** A monumental fountain of the 1st century is not in these two sources. A natural spring at the theatre of the early 1st century is on the plan of 1882. Thus the "mouth of the spring" has one candidate with a position inside the city of the period. This is a lead. I did not check if the spring was there in antiquity, and I propose no change of the rating.
