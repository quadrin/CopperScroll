## 1. Tell Iktanu (Prag 2024, Introduction)

What the chapter says:
- The Dead Sea Highway cuts between two foothills. The higher hill on the north side is Tell Iktanu.
- A long, lower, flat-topped gravel spur is next to it on the south side of the highway and goes to the south-west. Its local name was Katf al-Hisan.
- The north hill has a rock ridge along the top of its north and west sides.
- The south hill had a camp of road workers in 1962 and army trenches in 1967 and 1990. In 1989 the widening of the highway removed a small part of its west end.

What the image of 25 June 1974 and the elevation model show:
- North hill: closed contours, summit near −128 m; dark ridge line on its north-west side. Agrees.
- South hill: a light flat spur of approximately 600 by 150 m, top near −145 m, from south-west to north-east along the south-east side of the highway. Middle at 31.8166 N, 35.6701 E. Agrees.
- EAMENA has two records: "IKTANU" (46 m from the north hill point) and "iktanu south" (on the spur).
- The dark area east of the highway is fields. It is not part of the site.

Not done: the site plan (her Fig. 1.1) is not in this chapter. The house walls of the south hill are approximately 30 cm wide; the image cannot show them.

Figures of the chapter that help us: Fig. 1.12 (Hunting air photo 46.010 i of 1953, via APAAME), Fig. 1.13 (air view of 1966), Fig. 1.14 (map of the locality with the Palestine Grid), Fig. 1.21 (field systems of 1953 from the Hunting photographs).
