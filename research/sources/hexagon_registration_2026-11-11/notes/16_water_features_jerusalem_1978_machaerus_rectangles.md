# Water features and entries; Jerusalem on a second date; other frames east of the Jordan

Date: 2026-11-12. This note continues note 15.

## Result first

1. **Water features.** The EAMENA records agree with the Phase 5 assessments in 8 of 10 clear cases. They give no new candidate for an entry. One small lead: two cistern records in the ʿAsla area, where Phase 5 found no cistern in its sources.
2. **Jerusalem, second date.** The image of 5 May 1978 is registered independently of the image of 1974. The same ten landmarks are at their places. The entry places give the same result as in 1974. Two structures that I could not identify in 1974 are now visible: two free-standing monuments in the Kidron valley, and a sunk rectangular area at the Tombs of the Kings.
3. **Machaerus.** Two rectangles with rounded corners are on the image of 1 December 1974, west of the citadel. Status: Probable (Roman siege camps). They are new lines in the site table (50 places).
4. **Karama Ruins 15, Rings 3, Ring 4.** No result on the other frames. The image of 1978 ends before two of the three places.
5. **A date error of yesterday is corrected.** I wrote "12 Jun 1974" for the June image in the gazetteer source text. The correct date is 25 June 1974.

## 1. Entries that name a water structure or a tomb, compared with EAMENA

For each entry place inside the area of the EAMENA table, I counted the EAMENA records of the same kind within the precision radius of the place (minimum 500 m). 28 pairs of entry and feature kind are inside the area.

## 2. Jerusalem on the image of 5 May 1978 (tile F007c)

**Registration.** Window x 0, y 13500, 9000 by 10000 pixels, registered to the elevation model: 30 blocks, 7 m with a height term. The blocks are in the hills east of the city. The Dome of the Rock was 19 m from its published point; I moved the transform 19 m (one control point, as in 1974). This registration does not use the image of 1974.

**Test.** The nine other landmarks are at their places: Damascus, Herod's, Lions', Dung, Zion and Jaffa gates, the dome of al-Aqsa, the dome of the Holy Sepulchre, the Church of All Nations.

## 3. Machaerus on the image of 1 December 1974 (tile F018c)

The earlier note said that the camps were "not looked for". The citadel was already found on this tile. Control: the ring on the citadel summit is at 31.56743, 35.62365 on the image, 7 m from the summit of the elevation model.

| Feature | Position | Size | Ground |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rectangle north-west | 31.5695, 35.6194 | approximately 65 m by 30 m | Edge of a ridge above the wadi, 460 m west-north-west of the citadel |
| Rectangle south-west | 31.5650, 35.6186 | approximately 44 m by 30 m | Edge of a ridge above the wadi, 550 m west-south-west of the citadel |

- Each rectangle has a double line and rounded corners. This is the form on the APAAME photographs of the camps ("a rectangle with rounded corners", "a rectangle with rubble walls on the crest of a ridge").
- Status: **Probable**. One date. I did not assign the camp letters, because I do not have the published siege plan with coordinates.
- I looked at the ridges north, east and south of the citadel at full size. I did not see other rectangles. I did not see the siege wall as a continuous line.
- The places are south of the scroll region. No entry names them.
