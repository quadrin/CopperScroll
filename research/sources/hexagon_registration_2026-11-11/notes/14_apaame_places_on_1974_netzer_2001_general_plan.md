# Three places from APAAME photographs on the 1974 image; Netzer 2001 plans 2, 15, 23, 36 and 1

Date: 11 November 2026. Exploratory, one reader, not blind. Status words: Checked / Seen / Probable / Possible / Not confirmed.

## Result first

1. Three places that are known from APAAME air photographs, and that have no EAMENA record within 500 m, are found on the HEXAGON image of 1 December 1974: Suwwan, Karama Ruin 5 (Ain al-Heri) and el-Heri Tower. Each is Probable. The site table now has 42 places (44 with the addendum in section 1).
2. A new window of tile A017e south of Tell Iktanu is registered to the elevation model (155 blocks, 12 m).
3. The general plan of Netzer 2001 (Plan 2) and the plans 15, 23 and 36 are put on the image of 5 May 1978 with the site grid transform of note 10. No new adjustment was made.
4. Plan 1 of Netzer 2001 is a small-scale map. Its grid agrees with the Palestine grid to 80 m at two controls.
5. Nothing here bears on a scroll entry.

## 1. Places from the APAAME photographs

The photographs are those that the EAMENA database lists for the region (APAAME, Flickr, licence CC BY-NC-ND). They are not in this repository. The title of a photograph gave the name; the position in the Flickr list is probably the position of the camera.

| Place | On the image of 1 December 1974 | Position | Error | Distance from the list position |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Suwwan | Dark speckled patch, about 25 m by 30 m, on a spur top between two gullies. No straight sides | 31.8746 N, 35.6875 E | 60 m | 231 m |
| Karama Ruin 5 (Ain al-Heri) | Two joined rectangles with dark wall lines, about 22 m by 36 m and 36 m by 24 m, on a flat spur top above a gorge with vegetation | 31.7689 N, 35.6666 E | 30 m | 137 m |
| el-Heri Tower | One oval ring, about 10 m to 11 m across, on open ground | 31.7618 N, 35.6584 E | 30 m | 104 m |

- Each place agrees in form and ground with the photographs (2014 for Suwwan, 2017 for the other two).
- Each place is on one satellite image only. The CORONA film of 26 September 1967 is too light at Suwwan and does not include the other two.
- Karama Ruin 5 has the same form in 1974 and in 2017.
- Addendum (same day): two more titles are Probable and are in the site table, which now has 44 places. Karama Ruins 14: a round feature, about 11 m across, at 31.7689 N, 35.6727 E; the photographs of 2017 show a ring of stones on a ploughed plateau near a gorge edge. Karama Ruins 16: a round light feature, about 18 m across, on a flat-topped knoll above a scarp, at 31.7660 N, 35.6302 E; the photographs show a ring on such a knoll. It is in a second window (x 18400, y 2000) with its own transform (79 blocks, 10 m).
- No result for Karama Ruins 15, Karama Rings 3 and Karama Ring 4. Thin stone rings of 10 m are at the limit of the image.

Figures: `figures/suwwan_hexagon_1974-12-01_x4.jpg`, `figures/karama_ruin_5_ain_al_heri_hexagon_1974-12-01_x5.jpg`, `figures/el_heri_tower_hexagon_1974-12-01_x6.jpg`.

## 2. Window south of Tell Iktanu

- Window: tile A017e, x 14600, y 1800, 4000 by 4500 pixels, turned 180 degrees.
- Start: the Kafrein transform of tile A017e. It was 74 m too far west here. Four sun directions from 130 to 220 degrees were tried; three of them (160, 190, 220) gave the same shift.
- Result: 155 of 157 blocks, 12 m. The transform is in `transforms_corona_1967_df041d.json` under `hexagon_A017e_ain_al_heri_window`.

## 3. Netzer 2001 plans on the image of 5 May 1978

All use the site grid transform of note 10 (10 m squares; anchor column 17, row 47; grid turned 13 degrees). The overlays contain published drawings and are not in this repository.

| Plan | What agrees | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Plan 2 (general plan, with the site grid on its border) | The bends of Wadi Qelt; zone A north of the wadi; the mound and the long pool of zone B south of the wadi | Checked |
| Plan 2: long wall AH and zone F; garden front of zone B | A light line and a low rise; not clear | Probable |
| Plans 15 and 23 (Western Garden of area AB) | They cover the group of deep excavated rooms west of the two pools. The two plans come from two photographs and agree to about 1 m. Single walls are not confirmed at 1 m for each pixel | Probable |
| Plan 36 (Northern Wing of the Third Palace, detailed) | Agrees with Plan 35 to about 2 m; the shadows of the excavated walls are on the plan walls | Checked |

Open: the grid numbers are on the border between two lines. It is not decided if a number is for a line or for the centre of a square; the two options differ by 5 m.

## 4. Netzer 2001 Plan 1

Plan 1 has a 1000 m grid with no numbers. With Tell es-Sultan at about 192 / 142 of the Palestine grid, Tell es-Sultan and the palaces are each 80 m from their known positions. With this, the map gives: "The Royal Estate" (hatched area north of the palaces) centre 31.8567 N, 35.4348 E, about 660 m by 660 m; Tell es-Samarat, a strip about 370 m long, from 31.8659 N, 35.4412 E to 31.8626 N, 35.4404 E; Birket Musa centre 31.8477 N, 35.4399 E. Error about 100 m.
