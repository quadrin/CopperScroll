## Second addition, 14 November 2026: the article read on the page (the owner's copy on Drive, `1873_066.pdf`, 7 pages)

This addition replaces the limit of the first addition. The text and the plate were read directly.

- **Author.** The article is signed "C. W. W." (p. 71). The plate is signed "C. W. Wilson delt." The author is Wilson.
- **The plate** is a fold-out between pp. 66 and 67: "Ruins on the summit of Mount Gerizim", scale of English feet (bar to 200 ft), magnetic north to the left. It shows the castle with the octagonal church, the reservoir north of the castle, "Ruins of Houses" north of the reservoir, the paved terraces on the east, the "Masonry Platform, 12 Stones", the sacred rock of the Samaritans, the place where bodies were found, the place of Abraham's offering with the round steps, and "Ruins of a Town" at the south end. Excavated walls have a special sign.
- **The plate stops at the houses north of the reservoir.** The north mound is at the end of the spur, about 650 m north of the church. It is not on the plate. **There is no plan of the mound or of the ditch.**
- **The mound, p. 69 (read on the page):** at the end of the arm that runs north from the castle; "partly artificial, and isolated from the ridge by a deep ditch"; traces of steps on the four sides; a building 53 ft square with thick walls; a few Roman coins; rock cuttings for water below the mound on the north. P. 68: the spur "partly encloses the natural amphitheatre".
- **A new record to look for:** the footnote for the arm and the mound is **"Photo. 126"** of the Palestine Exploration Fund series of 1866 (Corporal Phillips). If the photograph shows the mound from the castle, it can show the ditch. The project does not hold it.
- **Edition of *The Recovery of Jerusalem*.** Wilson cites p. 465 of the London edition and says that Anderson's plan of Ebal and Gerizim was published there. The copy in the bucket is a different printing (the same passage is on printed p. 362) and has no such plate. The plan is the plate of the *Memoirs* (scan page 213).

Result: no change. The band stays **Probable** as the ditch. Not done: Wilson's plate on the image of 1974 (the church is Checked at 175.91/178.62, so this is possible; it does not bear on the ditch).
