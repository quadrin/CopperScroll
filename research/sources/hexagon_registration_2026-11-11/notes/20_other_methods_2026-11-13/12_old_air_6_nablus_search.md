# Old air photographs 6: all 160 Bavarian frames are in the session; search for Nablus frames: no air photograph, one ground photograph "Sichem" (BS Pal. 1290/a) obtained (13 November 2026)

## Result first

- All 160 full-size frames of the folder "Bavarian aerials 1918" are now in the session (2.3 GB). The work does not need the Mac for them.
- Nablus: the Bavarian collection has **no air photograph** of Nablus, Mount Gerizim, Sebastia or Samaria in the public record. Search of the record of the Bayerisches Hauptstaatsarchiv at the Deutsche Digitale Bibliothek, for "BS Pal" with each name:

| Search word | Records | What they are |
|---|---|---|
| Nablus | 5 | Mesudije railway station (line Afule–Nablus); two frames north of Jerusalem (Nablus road); el-Machruk near Jisr ed-Damiye ("Nablus-Weg"); one more Jerusalem frame. None shows Nablus |
| Sichem | 3 | BS Pal. 1290, 1290/a, 1290/b, in the group "Bodenaufnahmen" (ground photographs). Only 1290/a has an image |
| Nabulus, Garizim, Sebastie, Samaria | 0 | — |

- This agrees with the note of 10 November: Mader's directory of squadron 304 has no entry for Nablus or Mount Gerizim.
- **BS Pal. 1290/a was obtained** at the archive's full size (5,134 × 3,566 pixels), by the same method as the other frames (image server, one request at a time). The record gives the licence CC0 for this image.
- What it shows: houses of the old town with a small minaret and domes, seen from a roof or a street; behind them a bare slope with terraces and, at the upper right, a rock scarp with a small building below it. The plate has the mark "A 68". Which hill it is (Gerizim or Ebal) and which scarp is not decided. The index has a scarp, ʿIraq el-Badr (Possible, 174.93 / 180.23); this photograph can be a view of it, but the direction of the camera is not known.

## Limits

- The search reads the record titles only. A frame with Nablus in the picture and another title is not found by it.
- Other German squadrons (300 to 305) have no published list (note of 10 November).
- BS Pal. 1290 and 1290/b have no image online.
