## 2. SWP sheet XVIII at Jericho

- Image: bucket copy of the 1880 sheets, PDF page 18 (5196 × 4460 px).
- Local fit from three ties: Tell es-Sultan, Birket Musa, Kh. el-Mefjir. Scale 7.35 m for each pixel. Error about 200 m near the town (the sheet's wadi line is 200–300 m off the true bed there).
- Fourth aqueduct (to Rujm el-Mogheifir): starts beside the Eriha tower (local fit about 193.685/140.375); crosses Wadi Qelt at about 193.57/140.23 — about 650 m east of the structure.
- The sheet word "Bridge" on Wadi el-Kelt: about 192.29–192.42/140.06 — 450–600 m west of the structure.
- Both differences are more than twice the sheet error. The sheet does not draw a bridge or an aqueduct at the structure.
- Isaacs (1862 book, frontispiece "Roman Aqueduct at Jericho"; p. 83) does not give a place. AWM H02519 (c. 1917), a long low arcade with cutwaters, is the best visual match, but it has no place either.

## 3. Tell el-Qos (entry 30)

- Milik (ADAJ 4–5, 1960, and DJD III) puts Bet ha-Qos at Tell el-Qos, where Wadi Qelt leaves Jericho, on the north bank ("Tell el-Kos of the Survey").
- Points compared (PG):

| Point | Source | E/N |
|---|---|---|
| Table point (EAMENA) | site table, grade D | 194.097/140.131 |
| Old PEF georeference | earlier work | 194.172/140.279 |
| SWP "Tell el Kôs" | sheet XVIII, local fit | about 193.76/140.47 (±200 m) |
| 1941 mound symbol | sheet 19-14, registered to about 10 m | about 193.89/140.34 |

- 1941: radiating hachures, about 60–80 m across, east of the old village, with "Sch." and "Cem" to the south.
- 1945 mosaic: light open ground beside a walled school compound. No relief can be read at this scale.
- 1978: buildings and the main road.
- The 1941 mound is between the SWP point and the table point, and inside the SWP error. It is Possible as Tell el-Qos. The table point (in the 1941 "Abattoir" area near the wadi) is not changed, because the owner decides table points.
