## How the plan was put on the images

- Plate XLII is the book photograph in the bucket (`pdf/02-Humbert-I-Caves-Feshkha-coast-…/p0023.pdf`, printed p. 232). The rectified copy of step 25 (`/tmp/hc/f23v.png`) is not on the disk now. I took the plan line of the long wall from the overlay of step 36 (`feshkha_plateXLII_on_modern_2026-11-13.jpg`: one anchor at the main building, scale 3.53 plan pixels for 1 m, north at the top). I checked it against the plate photograph.
- Plan line relative to the centre of the main building (192.807/124.825): A (−50 m, +2 m), B (0, +73 m), C (+31, +106 m), D (+73, +141 m). Its bearing is about 40° (from south-west to north-east).
- On each 1974 image the line was moved by the offset of the main building in that height model (June: −62 m east; December: +33 m east, +15 m north; step 25).

## A difference that limits the result

- North of the main building, the plan draws the "piste moderne" east of the long wall. The plan's track and wall both go to the north-east, at about 35–40°.
- On the 1974 images and today the road runs due north–south here.
- South of the building the plan agrees with the road (step 25). North of point B it does not.
- So either the north part of the plan is a sketch, or the track was moved before 1974. **The plan line of the long wall north of point B can be wrong by 50 m or more.** A search along a line is then not a strong test. "Not confirmed" means "not seen", not "absent".

## Limits

- One reader, not blind. The 1974 frames are about 0.5 to 1 m for each pixel. A low stone wall of 1 m width is at the limit.
- The plan is a photograph of a book page. The step 25 rectification was not repeated.
- The modern image covers the north part with the junction, the access road and the car park.
