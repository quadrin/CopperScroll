# Search for a 1940s RAF vertical photograph of Cypros: two RAF frames of the Jericho Oasis named; the archives found; Bavarian frame 1042 can show Cypros. 14 November 2026

## Result first

- **No RAF photograph of Cypros was obtained.** The 1944–46 RAF vertical photographs of Palestine exist, but they are not open on the web.
- **Where they are:** the mapping flights of 680 Squadron RAF, 1944–46. The negatives are at the National Collection of Aerial Photography (NCAP), Edinburgh (paid search). Prints are at the Aerial Photography Archive of the Hebrew University of Jerusalem (Geography Department). That archive has an online map viewer with the photo centres, at `ccg.huji.ac.il/aerialphotos/`. I could not open it: the request needed the owner's approval and nobody answered.
- **A paper that used them is found and read:** P. Izzo, M. Fradley and A. Zerbini, "Snapshots from the past: discoveries and destruction in the Jericho Oasis", *Levant* 53.3 (2021, issued 2022), pp. 347–365, DOI 10.1080/00758914.2021.2015891. Open access on the Oxford repository (CC BY-NC-ND). The authors say that neither NCAP nor the Hebrew University could give them full coverage of the Jericho Oasis. They used **two RAF frames, mission references PS30-M 50901 and PS30-M 50911**, which cover a small part of the oasis. Their figures with RAF frames show the town (Kh. Kakun, 1944); none shows Cypros.
- **The same paper uses Bavarian frame BS Pal. 1042 for the plateau west of ʿAqabat Jabr** (Fig. 11: a looted necropolis, site 37, and a rectangular structure nearby, site 35). ʿAqabat Jabr is at the foot of Cypros. **So frame 1042 (27 March 1918), which is in the session, can show Cypros.** My notes 12 and 13 looked at 1042 only for the camp in Birket Musa (D19).
- On 1042 (an oblique view to the west into the hills; the arrow points down to the right), the road comes down from the hills in serpentines and reaches the plain beside a broad wadi bed with a tent camp of 1918. A small round knoll with a ring is at about (1495, 880) on the quarter-size frame, above the place where the road leaves the hills. **Possible as Cypros; not tested.** The frame is not registered.
