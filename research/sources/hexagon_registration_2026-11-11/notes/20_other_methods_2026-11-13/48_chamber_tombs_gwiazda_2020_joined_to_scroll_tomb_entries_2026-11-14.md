# Chamber-tomb catalogue (Gwiazda 2020) joined to the scroll's tomb entries: a join by Kloner–Zissu sector, not by position. No rating or candidate changed — 14 November 2026

## Result first

1. **The catalogue cannot place a tomb.** Its coordinates are one point for each site. 777 of the 812 "Jerusalem" tombs share one point (31.7718 N, 35.2280 E), and 61 of the 64 Jericho tombs share another (31.8611 N, 35.4618 E, in the modern town, 2.0 km from our Tell es-Sultan point). A join by distance is therefore only good at the scale of a site (kilometres).
2. **A join by Kloner–Zissu sector works for Jerusalem.** 804 of the Jerusalem rows come from Kloner and Zissu, and their names begin with the Kloner–Zissu number (sector–tomb, for example 6-2 Absalom, 6-3 Bene Ḥezir). The sector names come from the contents page of the Hebrew edition of 2003 (PDF 9, read on the page image today).
3. **Sector 6, "Kidron valley east of the Old City"** (the monuments and Silwan), is the sector of the candidate places of entries 2, 48, 51, 53, 54 and 56. It has 37 tombs in the catalogue; 31 are dated in the 1st c. BCE or 1st c. CE.
   - **Only four of the 31 have a façade: the four Kidron monuments** (6-1 Cave of Jehoshaphat, 6-2 Absalom, 6-3 Bene Ḥezir, 6-4 Zechariah).
   - **Only one has a Hebrew or Aramaic inscription: Bene Ḥezir (6-3)**, the tomb of a priestly family.
   - **No ossuary is recorded in sector 6.** All 27 other 1st-c. tombs are plain kokhim caves (6-6 to 6-32).
   - So, for the words "nefesh" (entry 2), "Yad Avshalom" (48) and "a tomb with a pillar of the exedra" (51), the catalogue gives no monumental tomb in this sector except the four that the project already uses. This agrees with the note of 12 November on the tomb of Zadok, which was made from the book.
4. **Inside the city (sector 0/31)** the catalogue has two tombs only, both at the Holy Sepulchre. No tomb is near the Temple, the south-east corner or the south wall. This agrees with Kloner and Zissu (no tombs inside the First Wall) and with the Zadok note. Entry 14 ("in the tomb that is in …", candidate Temple enclosure) has no tomb in the catalogue at its place.
5. **Entries 2 (Wadi Nuweiʿimeh) and 24 (Wadi Kuteif): no catalogue tomb within 3 km.** The nearest are the Jericho cemetery point (3.2 km from the Nuweiʿimeh point) and three Byzantine tombs at St George of Choziba (4.7 km from the Kuteif point).
6. **Entry 60 ("and graves at its mouth", Koḥlit; candidate Tell es-Sultan):** the Jericho cemetery of Hachlili and Killebrew 1999 is in the catalogue as 61 tombs of the 1st c. BCE–1st c. CE (13 with ossuaries, 2 with Hebrew or Aramaic inscriptions, none with a façade). The catalogue point is in the town, 2.0 km from the tell; the cemetery itself is on the hill slopes west of the oasis (not measured here). The catalogue does not decide where "its mouth" is.
7. **Entry 27 (candidate Tombs of the Kings):** 5-1 is in the catalogue with a façade, a Hebrew or Aramaic inscription and coffins, dated 50–130 CE.
8. **No rating, candidate or match strength changed.** The catalogue confirms what the project had from Kloner and Zissu; it adds a count and a structured table.

- Sector 6 here also has the five tombs listed under "Jerusalem, Silwan" (four of the Iron Age, one of 301–500 CE) and Qidron Cave II (401–600 CE); they are not 1st c.
- Kloner–Zissu 6-5 is not in the catalogue. The book reading of 12 October says that 6-5 is façades without preserved burial places, so it is not a chamber tomb. Zechariah (6-4) is in the catalogue, although the book says that it is a solid nefesh with no chamber found.
- The 41 Jerusalem 1st-c. tombs with a façade are listed in `jerusalem_first_century_facade_tombs.csv`. The catalogue has no field for a porch or a column. Names that refer to columns: 25-8 "Two-Columned Tomb" (Sanhedria) and 28-16 "Magharat Umm el-ʿAmed". Bene Ḥezir has a porch with two columns (known from the book, not from this catalogue). For "the pillar of the exedra" of entry 51 these are the published tombs to compare, but none is in sector 6 except Bene Ḥezir.

## Limits

- The catalogue is a compilation (Gwiazda 2020, from Kloner and Zissu 2007 and other reports). Its values were not checked against the books row by row, except for the points named above.
- The sectors are large (sector 6 runs from Gethsemane to Silwan). The join says which tombs are in the right part of the valley; it does not measure a distance from a candidate point.
- The flags (façade, inscription, ossuary) are yes/no as the catalogue codes them. "No ossuary in sector 6" means none recorded there.
- One reader; not blind.
