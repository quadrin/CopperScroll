## Result first

1. I read the 27 "none of the three agree" lines on the page images of Puech 2015 (PDF pages 36, 54, 59, 73, 79, 86, 96, 104 and 117, at 160 dpi).
2. **17 lines: SQE and Puech have the same reading.** The difference came from the PDF text layer (alternatives such as "X/Y", Puech's X(Y) correction marks, words moved by the right-to-left extract) or from my SQE line builder (alternatives written one after the other; III 2 has "מית" twice). These lines: I 12, I 13, III 2, VII 1, VIII 14, IX 1, IX 2, IX 6, X 9, X 11, X 13, X 16, XI 2, XI 9, XI 10, XI 12, XII 8.
3. **10 lines: SQE really differs from Puech** (SQE / Puech):

| Line | SQE | Puech 2015 |

| I 10 | והאצרה … מעסר | והאצר … ומעסר |
| IV 3 | בביאתך | בבואתך |

| VII 4 | restoration [ני] [הגד]ול | restoration [ני של ירחו(?)] |
| VIII 2 | אחזר | אחיה |

4. **So SQE follows Puech in 76 of the 101 lines where the three texts are not the same, not in 59.** It is close to Puech but not a copy: it differs in yod/waw (IV 3, IX 7, XI 13), in gimel–yod/resh (IX 15, X 3) and in three words of shape (IV 6, VIII 2, XI 7).
5. Two of the 10 are in the Jerusalem group of column XI: XI 7 (entry 52, the word after "under the great slab") and XI 13 (entry 55, Bethesda: בביאתך / בבואתך, "as you enter", spelling only). None is on a tomb word or on XII 10. **No rating changes.**
