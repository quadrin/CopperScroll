# Cave 3Q: the measured point from de Vaux 1953 replaces the by-eye point; the earlier views did not show 3Q. Status stays Not confirmed — 14 November 2026

## Result first

- **The point of 3Q that we used on the images was wrong by 460 m.** Steps 2 to 14 of the image linking and the aft-frame note of today used 193.10/129.50, a point taken by eye from a plan (±300 m).
- **The repository already had a measured point:** de Vaux 1953 (RB 60), pl. XX, cave 8 = 3Q, at **193.486/129.756 (Palestine grid km), ±30 m (1 sigma), ±60 m (about 95%)**. The source is `research/history/devaux1952_cave_points/` (measured on 10 October). It is also row DJD3:8 of `research/regional/caves_entry25/caves.csv`.
- **The views of the aft-frame note were about 330 m wide and centred 463 m south-west of this point. They did not show 3Q.** The text of that note about "the cliff at 3Q" is about another part of the cliff. Edit log row **D23**.
- New views at the measured point (forward and aft frames of 1 December 1974; 5 May 1978): the point is at the east edge of a level bench (about −190 to −196 m on the 30 m height model), where the slope down to the east begins. This is the top of the lower cliff band, with layered rock on the curved face directly east. **No cave mouth can be seen.** A cave of a few metres is below the resolution of these images.
- **Status of 3Q: Not confirmed (no change).** No rating or candidate changed.

- The point is at the break of slope between the bench (193.32–193.48) and the steep fall to the east. The bench is about 150 m wide. The upper cliff is to the west of it.
- A 30 m height model cannot show a small cliff of a few metres. The caves of 1952 and 1993 near 3Q are on this bench or at its edges.
- Total position error at 3Q on these images: about ±35 m (plate ±30 m; image models about 20 m on steep ground). So the cave can be on the bench edge or on the face just below it.

## Limits

- One reader; not a blind reading.
- The plate point has no local control near 3Q. The three controls of pl. XX (2–3 km south) put the plate about 30 m too far south there. If the same is true at 3Q, the point moves to about 193.49/129.79. No shift was applied.
- The view does not decide on which flank of a "small valley" the point is. The nearest clear ravine on the images is about 150 m north of the point (with caves 3 and 4 near it). The DJD III and RB texts say 3Q is on the south flank of a small valley. A small gully closer to the point is not resolved.
