# Old air photographs 1: Bavarian frame BS Pal. 1031-c (31 May 1918) put on the map grid beside HEXAGON 1978 at Tell es-Sultan. First fit to about 30 m; cut mound to the south-west is probable Tell es-Samarat (13 November 2026)

## Result first

- Frame BS Pal. 1031-c is a vertical photograph. Its title strip gives: unit 304, 31 May 1918, 7:00, "n.w. Jericho", height 4,700, focal length 50. The picture has Tell es-Sultan with the trenches of Sellin and Watzinger open, the spring and its buildings, the gardens, the road to Jericho and two mounds to the south.
- Automatic matching to the 1978 image failed (5 to 9 agreeing points). The ground changed too much: a refugee camp is north of the tell and the fields are different.
- A fit by hand with two tie points (road bend south of the tell; north tip of the tell) gives: 0.182 m for each pixel, rotation 10°. The scale agrees with the title strip: 4,700 m above the sea is about 4,950 m above Jericho, and with a 50 cm lens this is 1:9,900, or 0.18 m for each pixel.
- Check: the tell centre comes at 192.09 / 142.02. The accepted map reference is 1921.1420. The road east of the tell is 0 to 40 m off between the two images. The fit is thus good to about 30 m, not better.
- The frame covers about 191.57–193.06 east and 140.96–142.43 north.
- The mound with a deep cut, south-west of the tell, comes at 191.79 / 141.37. The archive title of the frame names Tell es-Samarat. Status: Probable Tell es-Samarat. The round mound to the south comes at 192.35 / 141.44; not identified.

## Limits

- Two tie points only. A fit of this type does not correct the tilt of the camera.
- The positions in the table are good to about 30 m. They are not for the entry-place table.
- The 1978 comparison image is a private study copy. The 1918 frame is from the Bayerisches Hauptstaatsarchiv; the conditions of use were not checked in this run.

## Not done

- A fit with six or more tie points (tell outline from the Kenyon plan of step 11, spring pool, mound tops), with tilt.
- Frames BS Pal. 1020 (north of the tell), 1037 and 1041 (Wadi Qelt mouth, Tulul Abu el-ʿAlaiq) and 0896 (Wadi Qelt channels). The Wadi Qelt frames are the ones with value for the entries: they show the channels and the pools before the modern works.
