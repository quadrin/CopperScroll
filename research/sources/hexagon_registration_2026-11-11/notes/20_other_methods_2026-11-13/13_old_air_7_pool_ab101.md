# Old air photographs 7: the sunken rectangle of frame BS Pal. 1037 is the pool A(B)101 of Netzer's Pools Complex. It was an open hollow in 1918. Birket Musa size agrees with the Survey of 1883 (13 November 2026)

## Result first

- The repository has a traced plan of the Pools Complex of Jericho (Netzer 2001, Plans 17, 20 and 22) and its registration to a modern image of 22 October 2026. I compared the sunken rectangle of 1918 with it.

| | 1918 frame (BS Pal. 1037) | Netzer's plan, pool A(B)101 |
|---|---|---|
| Form | One rectangle with a division in its west side | Two pools side by side, north and south, later joined to one pool (Herodian period) |
| Size | 20 × 35 m (from the shadow edge) | 18 × 32 m (the two pools with the wall between them) |
| Long side | North to south | North to south, 10.6° east of north |
| Centre, Palestine Grid | 191.235 / 140.125 | 191.218 / 140.134 (from the registration of 22 October) |

- The two centres are 19 m apart. The fit of the 1918 frame is good to 20 to 40 m at this place. Size, direction and place agree.
- Status: the sunken rectangle is **Checked by position and size as pool A(B)101** (earlier today: Probable the pair of excavated basins; edit log row B69). The two square basins on HEXAGON 1978 are the same pool after the excavation.
- New fact for the project: this pool was not hidden before the excavation. On 2 July 1918 it was an open hollow with steep sides and deep shadow, 20 × 35 m, on open ground about 200 m north of Wadi Qelt.
- Cross-check for Birket Musa: the Survey of Western Palestine (1883, p. 176) gives 190 × 160 yards, which is 174 × 146 m. My measure on the 1978 image is 175 × 147 m.

## Value for the entries

- Entry 29 has an assessment for the Jericho pools in the repository (`research/assessments/entry29_jericho_pools/`). A reader of the entry needs a pool that a person can find. The 1918 frame shows that the outline of A(B)101 was still visible on the surface in 1918. This is an observation about the place. It does not make the entry more or less probable, and no rating is changed.
- How deep the hollow was in 1918 is not measured. Netzer's text on the state before the excavation was not read in this run.

## Limits

- The registration of 22 October has a stated image accuracy of 5 m; the 1918 fit has three tie points, all south of the wadi.
- I took the sign of the north offset in the registration record (239.1 m) as "north of the anchor". The other sign puts the pool south of Wadi Qelt, where the 1918 frame and the 1978 image have no such feature.
- A work of 1918 at the same place and of the same size is not excluded by the image alone, but the 1978 basins at the same place make it improbable.
