# Blind second reading of the area round line I 12 (segment CT3): answers of the second reader, not edited (13 November 2026)

The reader was a separate agent with no access to this conversation, the repository, the web or any edition. It was not told the text, the line number, the entry or the candidate readings. It was given ten images (six photographs and four maps from the stack) and asked to read every line of the area letter by letter, with a confidence for each letter. The text below is its report as returned, without changes. Pixel positions are in the 1150 × 1100 crop that it was given (x from the left, y from the top).

BLIND SECOND READING. I worked only from the 10 images in /tmp/blind. I wrote zoomed crops and my own difference images to /tmp/work; these are derived from the same images and add no new source. I used no web search, no repository and no edition.

READ THIS FIRST: REGISTRATION IS NOT AS DESCRIBED
I checked registration with phase correlation against RCB, using 256-px tiles at 12 positions.
- RCT: within 3 px everywhere.
- RRB: within about 7 px in the middle and right of the strip. Left of x≈250 RRB is in shadow and cannot be used.
- RRT: within about 3 px in the centre only. It has warp "zipper" stripes at x≈340–400 (rows 0–330) and x≈820–860 (rows 380–1100). RRB has the same zipper around x≈820–860.
- RLB: not registered. It is smeared into horizontal streaks and correlates nowhere.
- RLT: not registered. The left 60% is offset. The right half is blank, apart from a pasted bright speckled rectangle (x 730–975, y 265–630) and a dark ellipse (x 800–1150, y 795–1075).
As a result, ctx_min_all and ctx_relief_left_minus_right are dominated by artefacts: the rectangle, the ellipse, and doubled strip edges. ctx_mean_all is also contaminated. ctx_relief_top_minus_bottom has bright vertical bands at x≈160 and x≈690, so it is probably also mixed with RLT/RLB.
Instead I used RCB, RCT and RRB (plus the centre of RRT), my own flat-fielded RCT−RCB difference (top minus bottom) and RRB−RCB.
How I told grooves from stains: a real horizontal groove is bright above and dark below in RCB, and the reverse in RCT. That held for every stroke I counted as a groove. The round dark pit at (560,700) looks the same in every light, so it is a pit or stain, not a stroke.

MOST USEFUL IMAGES
RCB and RCT (registered to within 3 px of each other) and RRB (within about 7 px, middle and right only) were the most useful. My own RCT−RCB difference was the best way to separate grooves from stains. RRT helped in the centre only. The left-light images and all the supplied derived images (min, mean, both reliefs) were misleading because of the misregistration and the pasted rectangle and ellipse. Strokes do line up between centre-bottom, centre-top and right-bottom light; they do not line up with either left-light image.
