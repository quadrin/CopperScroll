# Old air photographs 8: frame BS Pal. 897 (17 May 1918, south-east of Deir el-Qelt) registered automatically to HEXAGON 1978 with 148 points, 3 m rms. The Meshel–Amit channel line 18–26 on the two dates: no ancient stretch separated (13 November 2026)

## Result first

- **Method that works for the 1918 frames.** Plain feature matching fails (6 to 11 agreeing points of 40 to 60). It works in two stages: (1) a rough fit by hand from one point and the north arrow; (2) on the warped frame, a grid of 96 × 96 m windows matched against the 1978 image on gradient images (light-independent edges), search ±70 m, then a perspective fit with outlier removal, three rounds. Frame 897: **148 agreeing points, remaining error 3.1 m** (at the points; more on steep slopes between them). This is better by a factor of 3 to 10 than the hand fits of notes 1, 2, 3 and 5.
- The frame covers the south side of Wadi Qelt from about 187.6 to 188.9 east, with the deep meander of the wadi at its top. It holds points 17 to 26 of the aqueduct map of Meshel and Amit (1979), from step 22.
- **The mapped line is too coarse for this test.** The line of the map was traced by hand on a small-scale page (step 25; error about 50 m). On the 0.8 m pictures, the yellow line crosses gullies and ridges and does not follow one feature.
- **One light line along the slope** between points 25 and 26 (187.95 / 138.13 to 188.48 / 138.22, 600 m) is visible in 1918 and also in 1978. Along it the height model goes up 26 m in the first 340 m to the east and then down. An aqueduct to Jericho goes down to the east all the way. Status: **Probable path**, not a channel. The height model has 30 m cells and can be 10 m wrong on a slope, so this is not certain.
- No ancient stretch of channel is separated from modern repair by this frame. That aim of the work is not reached.

## Limits

- The perspective fit treats the ground as flat. Error on ridges and in the gorge is larger than 3 m; I did not measure it.
- The comparison image is HEXAGON 1978; the ground between 1918 and 1978 was not changed much in this area, which helps the matching.
- The 1918 frame and the 1978 image are private study copies.
