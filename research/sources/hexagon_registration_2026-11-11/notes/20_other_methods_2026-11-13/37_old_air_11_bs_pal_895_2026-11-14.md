# Old air photographs 11: frame BS Pal. 895 (13 May 1918) placed by eye south of Deir el-Qelt; the automatic fit failed; the frame does not reach Cypros. 14 November 2026

## Result first

- **Frame BS Pal. 895 is not at Cypros.** Caption on the negative: flight 304, no. 1261, 13 May 1918, "s. Der el Kelt", map square 3635, height 5200 m, lens 50 cm. It is a vertical frame with a north arrow.
- **Placed by eye** on the image of 5 May 1978: the gorge of Wadi Qelt is at the north edge of the frame (a hairpin of the gorge at about PG 189.2/139.1), the old Jericho road goes across the frame from north-east to south-west with one large loop (about PG 188.9/138.0), and badlands with fan-shaped gullies are at the south edge (about 189.1/137.4). The frame is about 1.9 km by 1.3 to 2.5 km on the ground; my two scale values (1.7 and 2.1 m for each pixel of the reduced view) do not agree. **Status: Probable place; error 200 to 300 m.**
- **The automatic fit failed.** Block matching of the gradient images was run three times from three first guesses. Each time the correlation was at the level of chance (0.15 to 0.19) and only 5 to 10 blocks of 54 to 135 agreed. A search over turn, scale and place gave a weak best value (0.25 against 0.20) at a wrong place; the side-by-side view showed that it was wrong. I do not use these fits.
- **Thus frames 894 and 895 can not test the two pits east of the Cypros summit.** My statement of the last report was wrong (edit log D18). Beit Jabr el-Fauqani (Meshel–Amit point 29, 189.34/138.94) is at or near the north-east edge of frame 895.
- Along the road in the frame there are rows of small dark dots and a thin straight dark line of about 600 m beside the loop (Probable a cable or fence line of 1918). Not identified.

## Why the automatic fit failed here (my reading)

- The frames that worked (897, 1041, 1037) were fitted at full size on ground with walls, pools and field edges. Here I used the reduced view (about 2 m for each pixel) on bare hills, where the picture is made by shadows. The sun of 13 May 1918, 1:30 p.m., and of 5 May 1978 in the morning light the slopes from different sides.
- A fit at full size on the road and the gorge rim only is the next thing to try.
