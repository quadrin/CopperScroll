# Old air photographs 3 (with addition: the row of dots is the line of the road): the plain between the mound and Birket Musa on frame BS Pal. 1041 (1918) at 0.5 m. A row of dots 370 m long goes from the mound to the east; pool centre agrees with entry test 1 (13 November 2026)

## Result first

- The frame was fitted again with five tie points (four pool corners and the mound) and a fit that allows a lean of the picture. Remaining error: 2 to 20 m. The part between the mound and the pool was then made at 0.5 m for each pixel.
- Birket Musa in 1918: corners at 191.652 / 139.520 (north-west), 191.822 / 139.515 (north-east), 191.812 / 139.355 (south-east), 191.660 / 139.365 (south-west). Centre 191.737 / 139.439. Entry test 1 used 191.74 / 139.45. The two agree to 11 m.
- New observation: a row of dark dots goes from the south foot of the mound to the east-north-east. It is 370 m long on the frame, from about 191.37 / 139.695 to 191.74 / 139.755, and almost straight. The dots are 5 to 10 m apart. It does not go to the pool: it passes 200 m north of the north wall.
- No line that goes into the pool was identified on this frame.

## Addition, same day: the row of dots is the line of the road

- The same window was made from HEXAGON 1978 and put below the 1918 picture (`bs_pal_1041_row_of_dots_1918_above_hexagon_1978_road.jpg`).
- In 1978 the road from Jerusalem to Jericho goes through this window on the same bearing as the row of dots, 15 to 20 m to the south of it. This difference is inside the error of the fit. A track is visible beside the dots on the 1918 frame.
- Status: the row of dots is Probable roadside line (trees, posts or stone heaps along the 1918 track that is the modern road). It is not a candidate for a channel.
- Earlier statement: "Seen, not decided" (channel line with plants, or fence or cable line). Reason for the change: the comparison with the 1978 road.

## Value for the entries

- On the 1918 frame the walls of Birket Musa can be measured without fields on them. The east wall is the strongest. In the Koḥlit count the pool is the "reservoir" of the Tulul Abu el-ʿAlaiq unit. The direction from the mound (191.375 / 139.75) to the pool centre is 131°; the limit of the east sector is 135°. The margin is 4°. Entry test 1 had 3° from other positions. The match stays narrow.
- The row of dots is not a channel (see the addition).

## Limits

- Positions are good to about 10 m near the pool and 20 m at the mound.
- 1918 military works (trenches, tents, tracks) are all over the plain. A straight line on this frame can be a work of 1918.
- The comparison image is a private study copy. Conditions of use of the archive frame were not checked.
