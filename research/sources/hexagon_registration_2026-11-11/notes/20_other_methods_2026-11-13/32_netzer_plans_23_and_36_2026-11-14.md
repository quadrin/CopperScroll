# Netzer 2001, Plans 23 and 36 put on the image of 1978 by the site grid. 14 November 2026

## Result first

- **Plan 36** is the "Detailed plan of the Northern Wing of Herod's Third Palace". It is the same building as Plan 35 (done on 11 November), at a larger scale. Its frame (site-grid columns 38 to 49, rows 28 to 33) encloses the excavated rooms north of Wadi Qelt on the image of 5 May 1978. **Checked**, as for Plan 35. It adds no new place.
- **Plan 23** is the "Detailed plan of the Western Garden and its surroundings in Area AB in Stage 7 and the Herodian period". Its frame (columns 12 to 16, rows 46 to 49 and part of 50) is directly west of the two pools of the Pools Complex. On the image this is the block of excavated rooms west of the pools. **Probable** (frame only; no wall tested).
- **Plan 23 has a small stepped pool** in the middle of the built part, about 3.1 × 2.5 m on the plan. By the site grid it is at about column 14.5, row 47.8, that is **PG 191.202/140.134** (about 25 m west and 8 m north of the wall between the two large pools). It is too small for the image (0.8 m for each pixel). Status: position from the plan (about 10 m).
- **The anchor of the site grid is measured again.** The wall between the two pools is at **PG 191.224/140.120** on the height model of the 1978 tile. The value that I used first today (191.218/140.134, the centre of the traced plan of 22 October) was 15 m off. The 1918 frame gave 191.221/140.116 for the same pair (old air photographs 9): 5 m.

## Method

- Rule of 11 November: wall between the two pools = column 17, row 47; one square = 10 m; grid turned 13° east of north. Columns go up to the east, rows go up to the north.
- Check of the rule before use: it puts the middle of the northern wing at 191.43/139.92; the site table of 11 November has 191.44/139.95 (36 m, inside the size of the wing).
- Plan 23: border numbers read on the photograph (rows 49, 48, 47, 46 from north to south; columns 16, 15, 14 from east to west). North on the sheet is to the side; the plan was turned for reading.

## Limits

- The photographs are oblique (one square is 177 pixels along the sheet and 143 to 167 across it). I did not correct the perspective for the detail; the pool position can be 5 to 10 m wrong.
- A border number can name a line or a square. This gives up to 5 m.
- One reader, not blind. One date.
- The stepped pool is not named: the locus number is not readable on the photograph.
