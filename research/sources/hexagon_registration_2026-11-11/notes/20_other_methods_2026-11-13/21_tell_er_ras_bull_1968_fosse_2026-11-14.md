# Tell er-Ras (Mount Gerizim): Bull 1968 against the 1974 images (14 November 2026)

## Result first

- **Bull 1968 has no plan.** Its figures are a coin, an old engraving and photographs (figs 11–17). The only plan of Tell er-Ras is Bull 1967 (AJA 71), Ill. 1. It has no scale bar. It was put on the image on 13 November (step 24 addition) with one anchor point. Status of that overlay: Probable.
- Bull 1968 gives measures in its text. I drew them on north-up orthoimages of the two dates (0.5 m per pixel), centred on the platform (PG 176.06/179.23):

| Measure in Bull 1968 | Size | On the images |
|---|---|---|
| The mound | "not more than 400 feet long and 265 feet wide" (122 × 81 m) | The bright, tree-free area is about 125 × 110 m on both dates. The size agrees to about 20–40 m; the long axis is not clear |
| The enclosure (temenos) round Buildings A and B, at mid-height of the mound | 44 m (north and south walls) × 64 m (east and west walls), oriented almost north–south | On the June 1974 image (F012d) the rubble of the 1964–1968 excavation is inside this outline, about 10 m east of its centre line. No wall line can be read |
| Building A on its platform | 74 × 45 feet (22.6 × 13.7 m); its front is 80 feet (24 m) from the north enclosure wall | Not resolved as a rectangle on either date |
| The fosse | "a wide east-west fosse" marks the south limit of the mound and cuts it off from the ridge | A dark east–west band of trees, 45–80 m south of the platform centre, on the two dates. South of it is a cleared strip about 50 m wide |

- **Status:**
  - Tell er-Ras platform: no change, **Checked** (by the 1:20,000 map, step 24).
  - Bull 1967 plan overlay: no change, **Probable**. Bull 1968 has no plan, so it cannot give an independent overlay. Its measures agree with the image only in size.
  - **Dark east–west band south of the platform: Possible → Probable the fosse of Bull 1968.** Before, I called it "possible trench of Bull". Bull 1968 says that a fosse marks the south limit of the mound. The band is in the same place on two dates. Position about PG 176.00–176.12 / 179.15–179.18. The south edge of the fosse is not fixed (the band or the strip south of it).
- Bull 1968 itself connects this fosse with the "upper fosse" of entry 57: "Whether or not this is to be identified with the 'upper fosse' on Mt. Gerizim mentioned in the Copper Scroll … will require further examination". This is a statement by the excavator, recorded here as data. No rating or candidate is changed.

## Method

- Orthoimages: height models `D74_F014a` (1 December 1974) and `J74_F012d` (25 June 1974; moved 100 m west, as in the index), 400 × 400 m at 0.5 m, north up.
- Overlay: a rectangle of 44 × 64 m (enclosure), a rectangle of 14 × 22 m (Building A, its north end 24 m south of the north enclosure wall), and an ellipse of 81 × 122 m (mound), all centred on PG 176.06/179.23.

## Limits

- One anchor point (the platform centre). Scale from the text only. North is from the grid, not from a plan.
- F012d is 100 m too far east before the correction, and the correction is good to about 30 m. The 10 m offset of the rubble is less than this.
- Trees cover the slopes, so the band may be a line of planted trees on the fosse slope, not the fosse floor.
- Bull 1968 is a JSTOR copy (from the Drive). No figure from it is used.
