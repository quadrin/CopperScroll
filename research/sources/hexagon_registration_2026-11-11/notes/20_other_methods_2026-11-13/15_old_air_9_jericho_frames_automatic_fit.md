## Limits

- The fit is to the 1978 image, which is itself on the map grid to about 14 m (height model of step 21). "3 m" is the agreement between the two images, not the error on the map.
- The perspective fit treats the ground as flat; the plain of Jericho is almost flat, so this matters little here.
