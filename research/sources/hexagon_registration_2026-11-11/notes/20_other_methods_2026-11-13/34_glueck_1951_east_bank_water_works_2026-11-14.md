# Glueck 1951, east bank: the chapter on the Plains of Moab searched for water works. One large reservoir (Tell ed-Dabakiyeh) not found on the modern image. 14 November 2026

## Result first

- Glueck 1951 (*Explorations in Eastern Palestine* IV), chapter V c, "ʿArboth Moab", printed pp. 366–404, was searched line by line for water works and for the Roman and Hellenistic periods. It was **not read in full**: I read the lines with these words and the full paragraphs of the two reservoirs.
- **One large water work is described: Tell ed-Dabakiyeh (site 217)**, about 1 km west-south-west to west of Tell er-Rameh (site 214, Livias). A long reservoir on a narrow east–west rise, about 110 m long and about 16 m wide, with a round pool of about 29 m at its east end; the west end is curved, with a wall about 1 m thick, several metres above the ground. Glueck: a canal from Wadi er-Rameh "must originally have led water" to it. Finds beside it: pieces of mosaic floor and Byzantine sherds. No Roman date is given.
- **On the modern image the reservoir is Not confirmed.** The place is under farms, plastic houses and modern ponds. A bare round mound at the east edge of the window (about 35.644 E, 31.826 N), 500 m south of the wadi, is **Possible** Tell er-Rameh. My window was centred from a remembered position of the tell, not from a source; the error can be 300 m or more.
- A second, small one: **Rujm el-ʿAzeimeh (site 224)**, a small earth reservoir (birkeh) north of Wadi el-ʿAzeimeh, with medieval Arabic sherds. Not looked for.
- Other water lines of the chapter are streams led in canals (Wadi Nimrin, Wadi el-Kefrein, Wadi er-Rameh, Wadi el-Meqtaʿa, ʿAin Sweimeh, a canal of about 3 km at Wadi el-ʿAzeimeh) and one row of shafts of a tunnel water system (site 204) north of Wadi Nimrin (fig. 110). Glueck gives no date for them.
- Periods: Tell Nimrin, Kh. el-Kefrein, Tell er-Rameh and Kh. Sweimeh have Roman and Byzantine sherds and nothing earlier; Tell el-Hammam and Tell Iktanu are earlier sites with few Roman sherds. This agrees with the site table of 11 November.

## Limits

- Word search, not a full read. A water work named with another word can be missed.
- The east bank has no registered HEXAGON window in this session; only the modern image was used. The CORONA frames of 1967 (work of 11 November) are the right images for a reservoir that farms have covered since.
- No grid position from Glueck: his places are given by distance and direction from other sites.
