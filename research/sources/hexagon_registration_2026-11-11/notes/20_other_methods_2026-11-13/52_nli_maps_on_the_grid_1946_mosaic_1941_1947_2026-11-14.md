# NLI maps put on the grid: the 1946 Jericho 1:10,000 air-photo mosaic (photographs of March 1945) registered to HEXAGON 1978 at 9 m; the 1941 1:25,000 sheet 19-14 and the 1947 1:50,000 sheet registered by their grids — 14 November 2026

## Result first

1. **The 1946 sheet is an air-photo mosaic, not a drawn map.** "Jericho, 1:10,000 Mosaic, Palestine Grid", 512 Field Survey Company, January 1946, "from air photographs dated March 1945". It covers PG E 190.655–195.450 (top) / 190.680–195.460 (bottom), N 138–143: Jericho town, Tell es-Sultan, the Hasmonean–Herodian palaces in Wadi Qelt, Birket Musa and Tell el-Qos.
   - **Registered to the HEXAGON image of 5 May 1978** by block matching: 268 blocks, **9.1 m rms** (third-order fit). The sheet says its own grid "is distorted". The printed grid lines agree with the registration within about 5–20 m at N 139–140 and about 40 m at N 141–142.
   - Checks: Birket Musa within 10–15 m of the 1978 position; the road east of Tell es-Sultan in the same place.
   - **What it shows that 1978 does not:** Tell es-Sultan in March 1945, with the trenches of the 1930s excavations on the mound and open ground north of it, before the ʿAin es-Sultan camp (1948). The two SWP aqueducts north of the tell were "Not confirmed, under the camp in 1978" (B27); this mosaic is the first image of that ground before the camp. Not yet looked at for them.
   - **Cypros is not on the mosaic.** I first read the west edge as 190.455 from the small overview; the corner labels read 190.655/190.680. The Cypros pits (190.527) are about 130 m outside. The March 1945 photographs themselves (680 Squadron, HUJI PS30 5089/5090) are still the way to see Cypros in 1945.
   - The date "March 1945" agrees with the 680 Squadron sortie of 15 March 1945 found in the Hebrew University catalogue (Probable the same photographs; not checked).
2. **1941: Survey of Palestine 1:25,000, sheet 19-14 (Jericho)**, PG 190–200 E / 140–150 N. Registered by the eleven easting and nine northing lines read from profiles over the whole map: line residuals ≤ 5 px (about 10 m; 2.1 m per pixel).
   - Checks: the Tell es-Sultan point falls on the mound hachures, Kh. el-Mafjar on its name and ruin signs, the Quruntul summit point on the small enclosure at the cliff top.
3. **1947: 1:50,000 Levant, "Jericho", provisional edition, sheet 50.H.26**, PG 174–196 E / 138–156 N (Cypros, Wadi Qelt, Jericho, Nuweiʿimeh, ʿAin Duk). Registered by its 1 km lines: rows ±1 px, columns ±6 px (about 25 m; 4.2 m per pixel).
   - Checks: Tell es-Sultan on the mound; **the Cypros summit point is within about 30 m of the spot height "15" on the hilltop**; the Quruntul enclosure; Birket Musa inside "Birkat en-Nabi…".
4. **Not registered:** Jericho 1918 (B.6, 2nd provisional edition), Jericho 1930 (a land plan, "sheet 190-140, 1a, plan I"), Central Judaea 1937, the 1938 motor map and the German Tul Kerm–Jericho 1918 (1:100,000). Their scales are small or they have no grid; they are kept as references.

## Mistake corrected before writing

- I first fitted the 1941 sheet from a frame read on the overview, and the fit locked onto the wrong lines (four check places all wrong). The profiles over the full map gave the right lines. The 1946 west edge was also misread from the overview (190.455 for 190.655). Both were found by the check places before any result was written.

## Limits

- Mosaic: a 1945 photo mosaic has relief displacement and joins between photographs; 9 m rms is the fit on blocks between E 190.75 and 195.15. Near the west and east edges the fit is less certain.
- Maps: the accuracy is that of the map drawing (1:25,000: about 10–25 m; 1:50,000: about 25–50 m), not only of the fit.
- One reader; not blind.
