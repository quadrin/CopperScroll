# Forward/aft stereo along Wadi Qelt: the surface does not resolve the channels; the aft frame of 1 December 1974 gives a second date at point 8, point 32 and Cypros — 14 November 2026

## Result first

- **The stereo surface does not separate channel cuts from paths.** Windows of 600 m at point 8, point 32 and points 36–37 (forward against aft frame of 1 December 1974, 1 m, blocks of 21 m): 35–50 % of the blocks match; the surface shows spurs and ravines (detail of about 20 m; ±5 m against the 30 m model). A channel is 0.6–1 m wide and a bench 2–3 m: too small. In the gorge the shadow of December stops the matching. Status of the method for channels: not usable. For landforms of 20 m and more it works.
- **The aft frame itself is useful.** It is sharper and better exposed than the forward frame. Ortho images at 0.5 m from the local aft model (position error 25–70 m against the forward model, larger at the west edge) beside the image of 5 May 1978:

|---|---|---|---|
| Point 8, south bank of Wadi Qelt | one clear double line along the slope, and at least two fainter parallel lines below it | the same lines | the parallel lines of B89 are on **two dates**; Probable old channel lines (no change of status) |

- The north pair of cisterns, which Meshel and Amit report as destroyed in the development works, is not seen on the 1974 frame at the place of their plan (about 100 m north of the south pair). So the frame does not show that they were open then.

## Limits

- One reader; not a blind reading.
- The local aft model is good near Cypros (fit 6.9 m) and worse at point 8 (shift of about 50–70 m); positions are taken from the forward and 1978 models, not from the aft model.
