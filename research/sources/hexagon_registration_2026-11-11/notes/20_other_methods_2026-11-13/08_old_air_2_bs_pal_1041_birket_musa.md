# Old air photographs 2: Bavarian frame BS Pal. 1041 (22 June 1918) at the mouth of Wadi Qelt, fitted on the four corners of Birket Musa. Good to 10 m at the pool, 50 to 120 m at the edge (13 November 2026)

## Result first

- Frame BS Pal. 1041 has the title strip: unit 304, 22 June 1918, "w. Jericho", height 4,500, focal length 50. It shows the exit of Wadi Qelt from the hills, a mound with excavation cuts on the south bank, and a large walled rectangle in the plain. In 1918 the plain round the pool had no fields and no houses.
- The rectangle is Birket Musa. Its four corners are sharp on the 1918 frame and on HEXAGON 1978. On the 1978 image the corners are at 175 × 147 m (inner field edge).
- Fit on the four corners (shift, turn, one scale): remaining error 6 to 9 m at the corners. Scale 0.206 m for each pixel, rotation 30°.
- Check away from the pool: the mound with cuts is 70 m off (1918 fit: 191.33 / 139.70; the same mound on the 1978 image: 191.385 / 139.745). The gorge exit, 900 m from the pool, is about 120 m off. The cause is the tilt of the camera; this fit does not correct it.
- Status: Birket Musa on the 1918 frame Checked (shape, size, place). Mound with cuts: Probable southern mound of Tulul Abu el-ʿAlaiq (Tell 1), with the cuts of the excavations before 1918.

## What the frame adds for the entries

| Feature on the 1918 frame | Value |
|---|---|
| Birket Musa without fields | The north and east walls are sharp; the south and west walls are faint but continuous. A row of small structures is inside, near the east wall; not identified (1918 camp or older). The interior has faint lines; not read. |
| Plain between the mound and the pool | Lines and shallow ditches cross it. Candidates for channels to the pool. Not traced in this run. |
| Mound with cuts, south bank | The state of the mound before the excavations of 1950–51 and 1973–87. |
| Tent groups west of the mound | 1918 military camp. Not ancient. |

Entry test 1 found that Tulul Abu el-ʿAlaiq keeps its match in the Koḥlit count by 3°, with Birket Musa as the reservoir. This frame is the best image of that reservoir before modern farming. It does not change the test.

## Limits

- The fit uses one small object (the pool). It is exact there and weak far from it.
- The four corners were picked by eye on reduced images (about 1 m for each pixel).
- The comparison image is a private study copy. Conditions of use of the archive frame were not checked.

## Not done

- A fit with tilt (eight or more tie points: pool corners, mound, wadi bends, gorge mouth).
- The lines in the plain traced and compared with the aqueduct map of Meshel and Amit and with Netzer's plans.
- Frames 1041-a, 1042, 1037 and 0896.
