# Height model 2 (with addition: what the editions and the open-questions file say): the lower Kidron gorge on the images. The gorge ends at a sharp mouth at 191.11 / 120.60; one large tree stands there in 1974 and today; no cairn identified. A third place for the words of entry 35, not decided (13 November 2026)

## Result first

- Height model 1 found the largest steep drops of the area on the lower Kidron (Wadi en-Nar), at 190.71 / 120.81 and 191.04 / 120.66. I looked at this place on HEXAGON of 1 December 1974 (tile F018 b) and on the modern image.
- The two points are inside a slot gorge about 1 km long with deep shadow. The height model has cells of 30 m, and a gorge of this width gives false steps. The points are thus **places in the gorge, not confirmed falls**. I correct the words "largest drop" of height model 1: the gorge is real, a fall of 100 m is not shown.
- The gorge ends suddenly. At about **191.11 / 120.60** the wadi comes out between two rock walls onto the open fan above the Dead Sea shore. West of this point is the gorge; east of it is open ground. This is a mouth in the plain sense of the word.
- At the mouth, on the north side of the bed, the 1974 image has one round dark object of about 20 m. The modern image has a large tree at the same place. Status: Checked as a tree (two dates). It is not a cairn.
- No cairn or stone heap is identified at the mouth on the two images. A cairn of a few metres is at the limit of the 1974 image and can be missed.
- The June 1974 tile (F015 d) does not agree with the December tile at this place; it is at its edge here. I did not use it.

## The three places for "the mouth of the ṣoq of Kidron" (entry 35)

| Place | Palestine Grid | What is there | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Upper entrance | 180.8 / 125.9 | Upper gorge entrance | entry test 4 |
| Entrance near Mar Saba | 181.9 / 122.7 | The gorge at the monastery | entry test 4 |
| Lower mouth | 191.11 / 120.60 | End of the last gorge, above the Dead Sea shore | this note |

- Which of the three the scroll means is a question of the word ṣoq and of the order of the entries. The images do not decide it. Entry 35 stays as it is.
- For the list: the lower mouth is 7.5 km south-south-west of Kh. Qumran and 4.6 km from ʿAin Feshkha.

## Addition, same day: the editions and question Q34

I read the notes on entry 35 in the repository (`data/scroll-notes.js`, entries e35-gorge and e35-yagar) and the open-questions file.

| Who | Reading | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Milik 1962 (D28, p. 268) | ṣoq = gorge, reached shortly before Mar Saba | upper stretch |
| Puech 2015 (p. 73) | gorge, "a short distance before it reaches Mar Saba on the way from Jerusalem" | upper stretch |
| Lefkovits 2000 (p. 262) | ṣoq = peak | not a gorge |
| Eshel 2002 (pp. 101–102) | yagar = the dam where the southern aqueduct of Hyrcania began | a dam in the wadi, not a cairn |
| Luria | yagar = dam | — |

- No edition puts the entry at the lower mouth. The two editions that read "gorge" put it on the upper stretch, on the way from Jerusalem.
- The lower mouth is in the project as an option of open question Q34 ("the Mar Saba stretch, or the point where the Kidron leaves the escarpment"). The update of Q34 asks to test the lower outlet as a separate place. This note gives that place its position and its image record: mouth at 191.11 / 120.60, a tree, no cairn.
- With this, the three places of the table are all examined on images, and no cairn is identified at any of them. The Hyrcania dam of Eshel's reading was not looked for on the images.

## Limits

- Position from the height model of tile F018 b (error 22 m rms).
- The distance from the mouth to the shore was not measured.
- The modern image shows a road, tanks and a settlement 300 to 600 m east and north of the mouth. Ground at the mouth can be disturbed.
