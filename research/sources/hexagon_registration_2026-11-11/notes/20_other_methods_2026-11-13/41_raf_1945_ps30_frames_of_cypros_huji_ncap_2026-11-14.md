# RAF 1945 frames of Cypros found in the Hebrew University catalogue: 14 frames of 680 Squadron, 15 March 1945; frame PS30 5089 registered to 4.9 m; the public copy is too coarse for the pits. 14 November 2026

## Result first

- **The Hebrew University aerial photograph archive (Dov Gavish archive) has 14 RAF vertical frames round Cypros**, sortie 680 PS.30, all of **15 March 1945**, in two strips: PS30 5087–5093 (north strip, along N 31.849) and PS30 5059–5065 (south strip, along N 31.828). It also has three American Colony photographs of 1934 (nos. 4312, 4315, 4316; oblique).
- The caption on each print: "680. PS.30. 15 MAR 45. F12″ // TOPO. 15000′". A 12-inch lens at 15,000 ft gives a scale of about 1:15,000; one frame covers about 3.4 km.
- **Cypros is in frames 5089 and 5090** (catalogue centres 1.04 km and 0.86 km from it; corrected from "900 m and 600 m", edit log D21). Frame 5090 is the one that Izzo, Fradley and Zerbini cite as "PS30-M 50901".
- **The online copies are small** (about 710 × 750 pixels for the frame, about 4.8 m for each pixel). The library's IIIF server gives no larger size. The catalogue names the full scans "RAF_PS30_5089_R_9110" and "RAF_PS30_5090_R_9120"; the high-resolution files must be ordered and paid for.
- **Frame 5089 is registered** to the 1978 image: search over turn and scale (best at turn 270°, 4.8 m; its centre falls 60 m from the catalogue centre), then block matching: 127 of 327 blocks agree, 4.9 m rms (one pixel of the copy). The top of the print is east.
- **At Cypros the 1945 copy shows only a lit hilltop.** The summit ruin, the lower terrace and the two pits east of the summit (about 15 m each) are below the resolution of the copy. **The pits are not tested.** The road below the hill is at the place of the 1978 road.
- Frame 5090 did not register on the copy (its coarse search failed; only 40 of 242 blocks). Not needed for the result.

## What is necessary for the pits

- The full scans of PS30 5089 and 5090 from the Hebrew University (order through geohub.huji.ac.il). At a scan of 1,200 dpi of a 9-inch negative print the ground pixel is about 0.3 m, enough for pits of 15 m.
- The American Colony photograph 4315 of 1934 is oblique, centred 250 m from Cypros: the small copy can be looked at; the original is in the Library of Congress (American Colony collection), where large scans are often open.

## Addition, 14 November 2026: do these images exist elsewhere online?

- **The RAF frames (PS30 5089, 5090 and the others): not found elsewhere online.** Two web searches found no copy on Wikimedia Commons or elsewhere. Full scans: the Hebrew University (order) or NCAP, Edinburgh, which holds the 680 Squadron negatives (paid search; Izzo et al. 2021).
- **The American Colony photograph AC 4315 is at the Library of Congress in full size.** It is Library of Congress matpc 22120, "Air views of Palestine. Air route following the old Jerusalem-Jericho Road. Jericho Road. The steep ascent from the plain. Seen across the Cherith gorge", record 2019712925, dated 1931 there (1934 in the Hebrew University catalogue). The negative number 4315 is written on the image, so the two are the same photograph. Full TIFF: 5,060 × 3,457 pixels, free (no known restrictions, Matson collection).
- The same Library of Congress series has: matpc 22121 "Gorge of the Cherith and St. George's Convent. Jericho road in foreground" (5,087 × 3,451), matpc 22118 "Mount of Temptation and gardens of Ain Duke", and views of Nebi Musa and Mar Saba (series "Air views of Palestine", 1931).
- matpc 22120 is an oblique view up the road ascent, with the Qelt gorge between the road and a foreground ridge. **Cypros is not yet identified on it;** a fit with ties is necessary.
- Files: Drive (same folder), the two full scans as JPEG; Mac, `CopperScroll/Claude outputs/LOC American Colony air views 1931/` (TIFFs and records).

- Archives Hub (Jisc) has the NCAP catalogue only to series level: "Sortie Series 680" in the ACIU collection (GB 3710 NCAP/1/1/104, 1 Sep 1944 – 31 May 1945, creator 680 Squadron RAF), the same in the MAPRW collection (NCAP/3/1/30), and "Sortie Series 680/S" (NCAP/1/1/100, 99 sorties). - Result: sortie 680 PS.30 is not found online at NCAP by any route tried. Status of "NCAP holds sortie 30": Possible (the series dates agree; not confirmed). Only a question to NCAP can confirm it.

## Addition 5, 14 November 2026: the owner's HUJI PS30 harvest

Source: Drive, `06 Imagery/Aerial imagery – gap-list harvest (2026-11-10)/HUJI PS30 – Cypros frames (2026-11-14)/` (another session of the owner): 247 catalogued PS30 frames (35.225–35.603 E, 31.780–32.011 N); 26 within 5 km of Cypros, with a contact sheet; all at the public size.

- This adds frames: this note had the two strips near Cypros only (14 frames). Status of "Cypros is covered by HUJI PS30": Checked (frame 5089 registered, B117).
- **Date:** the harvest says HUJI does not record the day. The caption printed on each frame does: "680. PS.30. 15 MAR 45". So sortie 30 is 15 March 1945; this fits the gap between NCAP sortie 25 (10 Feb 1945) and sortie 31 (17 Mar 1945).
- **Distances:** the harvest gives 5089 at 0.6 km, 5088 at 1.2 km, 5090 at 1.8 km from Cypros. From the catalogue centres in `meta.json` and the PADIS point of Cypros (31.84421 N, 35.42679 E) they are 1.04, 2.37 and 0.86 km. The harvest values are not used until their Cypros point is known. The frame fit of 5089 (centre 60 m from the catalogue centre) agrees with the catalogue values.
- **NCAP item 16:** only one item number (16) is free between sortie 25 (item 15) and sortie 31 (item 17), but five sorties (26–30) fall between them, and HUJI holds 26, 27, 29 and 30. So "item 16 = sortie 30" stays **Possible**. The harvest's statement that item 16 is "not digitised" has no source in the files.
- For the pits nothing changes: the public copies are too coarse; the full scans must be ordered.
