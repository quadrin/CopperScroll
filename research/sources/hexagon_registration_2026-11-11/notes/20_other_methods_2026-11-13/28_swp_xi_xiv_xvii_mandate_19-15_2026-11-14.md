2. **Mandate 1:20,000, sheet 19-15 "Wadi el-Mallaha" (1942), is held** with a registration of 5 November (grid in metres, 1.7 m for each pixel). I had said that no 1:20,000 sheet is held outside Nablus; that was wrong (edit log D17). Sheet 18-15 is in the same package.
3. **Kh. ed-Dash-sha is on that sheet at PG 194.97/158.68.** This is Glueck's "Khirbet ed-Dasa" (3.1 km at 104° from the square complex of Kh. Fusayil; he wrote "about 3 km. e. s. e.") and Avi-Yonah's "Kh. Dash-sha" (Roman aqueduct, 1:100,000 sheet 8, square 19/15).
   - The sheet draws a dashed four-sided outline of about 70 × 145 m where two lines marked "Ruined Aqueduct" meet, with two "Cem." signs south of it.
   - On the image of 1 December 1974 the place is on the north edge of an airstrip that follows the aqueduct line. A faint outline with straight sides, about 80 × 50 m, is 30 to 60 m north of the sheet point. **Position: Checked from the sheet. Remains on the image: Possible.** (This morning: Not confirmed; I had looked 300 m too far south-west.)
4. **Kh. el-Beiyadat on the same sheet:** a mound sign east of the road at PG 194.72/152.59. This is the round feature with a rim that I saw on the 1974 images at 194.72/152.60 (10 m). **Checked as the mound of the sheet**; it is at the place of the tower mound of the excavation (north-east part of the site). The sheet has no pool sign at my basin (194.80/152.51), 100 m to the south-east: the basin stays Possible. An outline south of the mound on the sheet (194.66/152.18) is a different thing.
