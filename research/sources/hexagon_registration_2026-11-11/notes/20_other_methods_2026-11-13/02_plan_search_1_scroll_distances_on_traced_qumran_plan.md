# Plan search 1: the scroll's horizontal measures against the traced Qumran plan, with a chance count. No entry linked (13 November 2026)

## Result first

- The scroll has eight measures that are horizontal distances and not digging depths. Two of them can be tested on a plan that has only water installations: "six cubits" from a conduit to an immersion basin (I 11–12) and "forty-one cubits" along a conduit from the entry (IV 3–4).
- The traced Qumran plan (Magen and Peleg 2018, fig. 9; 15 channel sections, 12 pools, 1 cistern) was used as a test field. It is not a claim that these entries are at Qumran.
- Six cubits from a channel: 1 of 13 water installations is in the band (L48–L49, 3.4 m from channel CH07). One more is 5 cm below the band (L71, 2.13 m). The other 11 are nearer than 1.4 m or farther than 12 m.
- Forty-one cubits along the channel from the site entry: the point is in the open north court, 10 m or more from each pool. No locus is within 3 m.
- The method works and gives a chance rate. On this plan a "pool at six cubits from a channel" occurs for 1 in 13 installations; "a pool within 2 m of a given point of the main channel" occurs for 27% of the channel length.

## Limits

- Only one site has a traced plan with typed water installations. The Jericho pools plan has one feature point. A chance rate from one site is weak.
- The plan has no gates, cairns or dovecotes. Five of the eight measures can not be tested until such features are traced.
- Channel-to-pool distance is measured edge to edge on a 1:~400 figure. The tracing error is about 0.3 m.
- "On the north" and "as you come in" are not used as conditions in this run.
