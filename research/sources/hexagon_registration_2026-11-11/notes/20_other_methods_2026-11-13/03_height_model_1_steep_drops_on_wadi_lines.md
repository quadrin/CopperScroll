# Height model 1: steep drops on wadi lines (waterfall places) for the whole study area. Wadi Qumran fall is 130 m from the aqueduct head; largest drop is on the lower Kidron. No entry linked (13 November 2026)

## Result first

- The 30 m height model was put on the Palestine Grid at 25 m (E 170–202, N 110–170) and the water lines were calculated. A "steep drop" is a place on a water line where the bed goes down 20 m or more in 100 m.
- There are 342 such places with a catchment of 0.5 km² or more. 70 have 5 km² or more. 42 have 20 km² or more.
- Check of the method: the known fall of Wadi Qumran is found at 193.04 / 127.59 (63 m in 100 m, catchment 45 km²). It is 130 m west of the dam and the aqueduct head of entry test 3 (193.16 / 127.62). The aqueduct head is thus on the east side of the fall.
- The largest drop in the area on a large wadi is on the lower Kidron (Wadi en-Nar), at 190.71 / 120.81 (103 m in 100 m, catchment 120 km²), with a second step at 191.04 / 120.66 (88 m). This is where the wadi goes over the fault cliff to the Dead Sea. Entry 35 has "the mouth of the ṣoq of Kidron". Entry test 4 looked only at the two upper gorge entrances (180.8 / 125.9 and 181.9 / 122.7). The lower fall was not examined before. This is an observation. It is not a proposal for entry 35.
- Entry 42 ("the waterfall(?) near the bend of the conduit, on the east") has no agreed place (Puech: Tekoa–Herodium sector, low). The file gives the list to search when a conduit line is available. A fall near a conduit is not rare: conduits start in gorges. Four of the eight conduit points examined have a steep drop within 250 m.

## Chance

For a point put at random in the area (1,920 km²), a steep drop is within 200 m in 2.2% of cases (all 342), 0.5% (catchment ≥ 5 km²), 0.3% (≥ 20 km²). Conduit heads are not random points, so these numbers are a lower limit only.

## Limits

- The height model has 30 m cells. A fall less than about 15 m high, or a fall in a slot gorge narrower than a cell, can be missed. A cliff can be shown as a fall when the calculated water line is wrong.
- The wadi names in the second table are from the position only.
- No conduit is available as a line with its bends, except the Wadi Qelt section of step 22 and the Qumran aqueduct of step 18 (both as images, not as coordinate lists). The "bend" part of entry 42 is not tested.
