# Cypros on the 1931 air photograph (Library of Congress matpc 22120 = American Colony 4315): view direction found, Cypros not identified — 14 November 2026

## Result first

- The full scan (5,060 × 3,457 pixels, Library of Congress, Matson collection, no known restrictions) is an **oblique view up the old Jerusalem–Jericho road ascent**. The hand caption on the negative reads "4315 ʿAkabat-Jabr …". The road climbs from the bottom left to the top; a gorge with stepped cliffs runs from the bottom middle to the upper right; a ridge fills the bottom right (with the shadow of the aircraft wing).
- I built a perspective renderer: the image of 5 May 1978 (tile M78_F007c, ortho at 8 m) draped on the 30 m height model, seen from a chosen aircraft position. The best of eight trial views is a camera **north of the gorge mouth (about PG 191.1–191.4 / 141.2–141.3, 800–900 m above sea level), looking south-west (200–210°), 24–27° down**. In that view the gorge, the road side and the hills agree in layout with the photograph. Status: **Probable** (view direction only).
- **Cypros is not identified on the photograph.** Two readings of the bottom left are possible:
  1. the dark band with trees and a small building above the road start is the bed of Wadi Qelt at its mouth; then the cliffs near the camera are the side canyon south of Cypros, and Cypros is at the bottom-left edge or outside the frame;
  2. the cliffs near the camera are the Qelt gorge; then Cypros is left of the road, above the gorge mouth, in the middle left of the frame.
- So the two pits east of the summit (edit log B103) are **not tested** on the 1931 photograph.

## Why not decided

- The 30 m height model gives stepped artefacts on the steep slopes in the render; the gorge cliffs and the road bends cannot be tied point by point.
- I did not use a placement by eye: earlier today (D18, D19) a place taken from a title or by eye was wrong.

## Not done

- A fit with tie points (road bends, gorge bends, the small building at the road start) on a finer height model; or a camera search by image correlation.
- Bavarian frame 1042 (27 March 1918), the knoll at (1495, 880) of the quarter-size frame: not tested in this step.
