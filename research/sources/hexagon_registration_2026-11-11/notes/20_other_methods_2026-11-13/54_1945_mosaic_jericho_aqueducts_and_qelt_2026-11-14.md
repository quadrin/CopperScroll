# The 1945 air-photo mosaic north of Tell es-Sultan and in Wadi Qelt: the SWP aqueduct towards Kh. el-Mefjir is the route of the 1941 "Canal" (Probable, B122); the north line is a cart track in 1941 (no change); the Qelt channels are outside or not resolved — 14 November 2026

Follows: `claude/nli-maps-on-the-grid-1946-jericho-air-photo-mosaic-…-2026-11-14.md`.

## Result first

1. **SWP "Aqueduct" from Tell es-Sultan towards Kh. el-Mefjir (the south line of sheet XVIII): Not confirmed → Probable as the route of a water channel** (edit log B122).
   - The registered 1941 1:25,000 sheet 19-14 has a **"Canal"** (dotted line) beside a double track, running north-east from ʿAin es-Sultan for more than 1 km. It is parallel to the SWP line and 50–100 m south-east of it, which is inside the 200 m error of the SWP sheet after its 410–500 m shift.
   - On the 1945 mosaic (photographs of March 1945, before the ʿAin es-Sultan camp of 1948) there are light lines along this route. At 1.7 m the canal cannot be told from the tracks, so the image adds "Seen" only.
   - The date when the channel was first built is not known: the evidence is SWP 1874, the map of 1941 and the photograph of 1945.
2. **The SWP north line (from near Tawahin es-Sukkar to the north-east): Not confirmed (no change).** The 1941 sheet has only a cart track (double dashed) along it, from Eth-Thiniya to the north-east. The 1945 mosaic shows a linear feature on that track, and it stops at N 143.0.
3. **A check of the registrations on the way:** on a 640 m window the 1945 mosaic, the 1941 map and the 1978 image put the main road north of the tell in the same place, to about 20 m. On a 1.3 km window I first read a 120 m difference between 1945 and 1978; that was a misreading of the wide view, not an error of the fit.
4. **Wadi Qelt:** the Meshel and Amit channel points 26–39 are all west of the mosaic (west edge E 190.68); point 40 is on the edge. At the Hasmonean–Herodian palaces the mosaic shows the Pools Complex as a dark rectangle at its plan position (the second date before Netzer's excavation, after the 1918 frame BS Pal. 1037). Channels along the wadi are not resolved: the printed contours cross them. No status change.
