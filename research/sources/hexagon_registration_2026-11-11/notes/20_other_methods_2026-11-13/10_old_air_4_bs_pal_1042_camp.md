# Old air photographs 4: frame BS Pal. 1042 (27 March 1918) shows a military camp in and round Birket Musa. The small objects on the June frame are camp remains, not ancient features (13 November 2026)

## Result first

- Frame BS Pal. 1042 has the title strip: unit 304, 27 March 1918, "w. Jericho", height 3,000, focal length 25. It is an oblique view, three months before frame 1041. The ground detail is coarser than on frame 1041.
- Birket Musa is in the middle of the frame. Rows of dark oblong blocks (tents or shelters) are inside the pool, along its thick wall and on the plain on all sides. Tracks go from the pool in all directions. The British army took Jericho on 21 February 1918; a German photograph of March shows the camp of that army.
- On the June frame (1041) the same places have fewer objects. The rows inside the east part of the pool, the blocks outside the east wall, the zigzag ditch and the line with short side ditches are at the places of the March camp.
- Status changes: rows of objects inside the pool, and blocks outside the east wall: from "Seen, probably 1918" to **Checked as a camp of 1918** (two dates). Zigzag ditch and comb line: stay Probable 1918 works.
- The thin rectangle of 35 × 25 m at the north-west corner of the pool is not resolved on frame 1042. It is in the camp area. Status: Seen; a camp enclosure is the first explanation.
- No ancient feature is added by this frame. Its value is negative: lines and pits near Birket Musa on 1918 frames, and soil marks of them on later images, must first be tested as works of 1918.

## Limits

- Frame 1042 was not put on the map grid. The comparison with frame 1041 is by eye, from the pool walls.
- Which army made the camp is from the date only. No unit record was read.
