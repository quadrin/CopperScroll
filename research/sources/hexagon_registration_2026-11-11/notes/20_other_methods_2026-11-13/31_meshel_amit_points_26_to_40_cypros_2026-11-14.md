# Meshel and Amit 1979, points 26 to 40 (ʿEin Qelt channels to Cypros): read, put on the grid and on the image of 1978. 14 November 2026

## Result first

- The article is: Z. Meshel and D. Amit, "The water supply of the Cypros fortress", *Qadmoniot* 12, 2–3 (46–47), 1979, pp. 67–77 (Hebrew), with a fold-out map "Aqueducts in Wadi Qelt". The bucket copy has the map in overlapping pieces (PDF pages 20 to 27) and the list of the 40 points on PDF pages 27 and 28.
- **Points 26 to 40 now have map positions** (table). The piece is PDF page 23. Its grid is the same UTM grid as page 25 (step 22). Check: the label "26" is at the step 25 position to 30 m.
- **Point 40 is a good test of the positions:** the list says "purification installations of the modern aqueduct, on the remains of an ancient pool". On the image of 5 May 1978 the point falls on a compound with dark basins. **Checked** (about 20 m).
- **The Cypros fortress is on the image of 1978 as an excavated ruin:** an oval summit with rooms at about PG 190.44/139.165 and a lower terrace with rooms at about 190.50/139.10. This is 20 m from the summit point of the third pass (190.44/139.185). **Checked.**
- **Two dark oval pits, each about 15 m long, are 85 m east of the summit** (about 190.527/139.153), between points 38 and 39 of the map. The list has at points 38 and 39 a small pool, a north pair of cisterns (1–2, "destroyed") and a south pair of pools (3–4). **Probable** as one of these pairs; which pair is not decided.
- No ancient channel is separated from the roads on the image. Points 27 to 32 fall on or beside the road that goes up to Cypros.
- **PDF page 21** is a map piece with no points on it (ground north of the wadi). PDF page 22 is the east end of the map (Jericho) with the title. PDF page 24 has the legend. Nothing more is there.
