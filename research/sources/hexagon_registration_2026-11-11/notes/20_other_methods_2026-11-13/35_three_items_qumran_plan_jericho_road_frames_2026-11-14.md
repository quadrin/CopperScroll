# Three items of the list: the Qumran measured plan found and fitted; the 1918 frames of the Jericho road listed, frame 1055 looked at; PEF photograph 126 not found. 14 November 2026

## Result first

1. **The measured survey of Kh. Qumran is found.** It is the plan of Coüasnon (architect of the excavation, survey at 1:100): Humbert, Chambon and Młynarczyk 2016, figs. 54, 55 and 56 (pp. 126–128), "Plan Période Ib / II / III, Coüasnon 1956". The book says that the general layout was surveyed with a plane table and that it "est exact et fiable" (p. 124). I did not find it before because the file name has no word for a survey.
2. **Fig. 54 fits the positions of step 21 to 2 m.** Six loci (L71, L91, L110, L77, L58, L138) were marked on the page and fitted with four values (scale, turn, two shifts). Residuals: 0.3 to 1.6 m. The plan has north at the top (turn −0.3°). One square of its grid is about 19 m by this fit, thus 20 m. **The Kh. Qumran positions of step 21 are confirmed by a second, measured plan. No position changes.** The transform is in `fit54.json`; with it each locus of the plan has a grid position.
3. **The 1918 frames of the Jericho–Jerusalem road and of Nebi Musa: about 50 frames in the two full-size folders on the Mac have the Jericho road, Wadi el-Kelt or Nebi Musa in the archive title.** I looked at one: BS Pal. 1055 (22 March 1918, flight 304, height 3000 m, lens 25 cm), "road Jericho–Jerusalem and Wadi el-Kelt at Bet Dschabr el-Fokani". It is an oblique view. Seen: a wide dark valley floor, the road with rows of dark dots beside it (Probable tents or positions of 1918), and two groups of right-angled enclosures on a rise beside a wadi fork. **The frame is not registered. Nothing is identified.**
4. **PEF photograph 126 (1866) is not found on the web** in two searches. It is in the archive of the Palestine Exploration Fund in London. Not held.
