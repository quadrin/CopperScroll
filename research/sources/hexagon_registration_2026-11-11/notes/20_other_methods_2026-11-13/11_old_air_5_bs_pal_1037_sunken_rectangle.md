# Old air photographs 5: frame BS Pal. 1037 (2 July 1918) north of Wadi Qelt. A sunken rectangle of 20 × 35 m is at the place of the two excavated basins of the palace area (13 November 2026)

## Result first

- Frame BS Pal. 1037 has the title strip: unit 304, 2 July 1918, "w. Jericho", height 4,500, focal length 50. It is a vertical photograph of the two banks of Wadi Qelt at its mouth. The south mound with its cuts and the east wall of Birket Musa are at the bottom edge.
- Fit with three tie points (south mound; north-east and south-east corners of Birket Musa): remaining error 1 to 5 m; 0.175 m for each pixel. On the north bank, 500 m from the tie points, the wadi line is 20 to 40 m off.
- On the north bank the frame shows a **sunken rectangle with dark shadow inside**, 20 m wide and 35 m long, long side north to south, with a step or division in its west side. Centre at about 191.235 / 140.125.
- On HEXAGON 1978 the same place has **two excavated square basins**, one north of the other, each about 18 to 20 m, together about 20 × 38 m. The centre of the pair is 10 to 15 m from the centre of the 1918 rectangle. This is inside the error of the fit.
- Status: the sunken rectangle of 1918 is **Probable the pair of basins** that the 1978 image shows excavated. In 1918 it was an open hollow. The name of the basins in Netzer's report was not checked in this run.
- West of it, the 1918 frame shows a low mound with pits and cuts at about 191.16 / 140.10. On the 1978 image excavated walls are at this place. Status: Seen; probable north mound of Tulul Abu el-ʿAlaiq with the cuts of the excavations before 1918.

## Value for the project

- A large pool of the palace area was open and visible in 1918, before the excavations of 1950–51 and 1973–87. A pool that is visible on the surface is a different witness from a pool that is known only from excavation: it can be a landmark for a text.
- The repository has a traced plan of the pools complex of this site (`research/models/traced_plans/jericho-pools-complex.json`). The 1918 rectangle must be compared with it and with Netzer's general plan before it gets a name.
- No entry is linked.

## Limits

- Three tie points, all in the south part of the frame. No correction for tilt.
- The size of the rectangle is from its shadow edge; the true edge can be 2 to 3 m different.
