## Kallai's entry for Tell er-Ras near ʿAwarta, and unit S1032

Read on the page: "T. er-Ras, en-Nabi ʿUzeir; 17-17/64/1; grid 1767 1741; 575 m. A tomb on the summit of a hill west of ʿAwarta. There are bell-shaped water cisterns, cup marks in the rock, cut caves and other cuttings. Beside the tomb is a ruined building, a mosque or a Samaritan prayer house. On the east slope of the hill is a rock-cut tomb with loculi. Two blocked cisterns. Pottery: Israelite II, Byzantine, Arab and Ottoman."

- The sheikh's tomb is **on the summit** (the later survey has "on its west side"). From the database point of Udala the summit is at 972 m: C3s holds by 28 m, with a measuring error of about 35 m.
- The rock-cut tomb with loculi is on the **east** slope, about 1,020 m from Udala: outside the limit.
- The bell-shaped cisterns have no place in the text.
- **S1032 stays not decided.** The entry gives the first positions, and they are on the two sides of the limit.
- Kallai lists no Hellenistic or Roman pottery at the tell. This is a point for the coders of the count, not for me.
