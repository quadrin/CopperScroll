# Old air photographs 12: the 1918 frames of the Jericho road, Cypros and Nebi Musa, frame by frame; Coüasnon's plans of Periods II and III. 14 November 2026

## Result first

- **No 1918 frame that the session holds shows the Cypros summit clearly.** Cypros (PG 190.44/139.165) is not identified on any frame. The two pits east of the summit can not be tested with these frames.
- **Frame BS Pal. 1036-f (9 May 1918) is placed: Probable,** by the camp inside Birket Musa (a square of about 140 m with rows of tents; the same camp as on 1042, edit log B68). North is to the lower left of the frame (arrow). The frame covers the plain east of the Qelt gorge mouth, about E 191.1–192.8, N 138.6–140.6, with Wadi Qelt lined with trees across its middle and large camps south of the wadi.
- **Frame 1043 (1 August 1918, overview) shows the Qelt leaving its gorge,** with trees along the wadi, the serpentines of a road coming down from the hills, and camps on the plain. North is to the right of the frame (arrow). Cypros must be on the hills south of the gorge exit in this frame; I could not find the summit ruin at the scale I looked at. **Not identified.**
- **Frame 884 (3 January 1918) shows the Nebi Musa compound,** marked on the print in German, "Kloster En Nebi Musa": a walled rectangle with an inner court and a dark structure against its east side. A second hand label, "Beduinenlager" (Bedouin camp), is at the lower right. **Checked** as the compound (label and form); the frame is oblique and not registered.
- **The automatic fit failed on every frame tried** (1036-f, 1036-c, 1043, 890; 895 in the last note). The correlation was 0.25 to 0.30 against a median of 0.19 to 0.25, and only 3 to 7 blocks agreed. I do not use these fits.
- **Coüasnon's plans of Periods II and III (figs. 55 and 56) have the same frame as fig. 54** (frame lines at the same page pixels to 1 px at 100 dots per inch). The fitted transform of fig. 54 applies to them unchanged, so the nine water-locus positions of the last note hold for the three plans where the locus is drawn. Fig. 56 (Period III) still has the channel from L136 past L110 to L71, and L91, L110, L71 and L64; it does not draw L56, L58 or L48–49 as basins.

## The square numbers on the negatives

Frames with the same square number show the same area (for example 1036-c, 1036-f, 1036-g and 1043 are all 3438). The registered frames give: 1041 = 3337 (Birket Musa), 1037 = 3338 (Pools Complex), 1031-c = 3238 (Tell es-Sultan), 897 = 3634 (east of Deir el-Qelt). The numbers are not the frame centre on a simple grid: a linear fit to these four does not hold. I use them only to group frames.

## Why the automatic fit fails on these frames (my reading)

- The frames that worked (897, 1041, 1037) were full size, with walls, pools and field edges. These are bare hills or plain with tents, where the picture is made by shadows and by camps that are not there in 1978.
- A hand fit with four or more ties on fixed points (wadi bends, the Birket Musa square, the Nebi Musa compound, road bends) is the method that can work. It needs a full-size view at each tie.
