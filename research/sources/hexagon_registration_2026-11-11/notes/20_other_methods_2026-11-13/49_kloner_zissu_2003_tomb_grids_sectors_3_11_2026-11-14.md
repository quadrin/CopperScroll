   - **Entries 2, 48 and 51 at the Kidron monuments:** the only 1st-c. tombs with a façade near the point are the four monuments themselves. Bene Ḥezir (6-3) is the only one with a Hebrew inscription. This agrees with the notes of 12 November.

## Method

1. Pages PDF 125–206 from the bucket, at 300 dpi, OCR (Hebrew and English) with word positions.
2. Lines with a grid pair were cropped; the tomb number and the grid were read again with a digit-only OCR pass.
3. I looked at the crops for all doubtful lines and for four of the 14 sheets in full (rows 0–23, 96–107, 120–143), and at all lines in other forms (four-digit grids, faulty grids).
4. Joined to Gwiazda 2020 by the number (sector–tomb). The numbers agree in name where both have a name (for example 5-5 Shimʿon ha-Ẓaddiq, 5-6 the Little Sanhedrin, 6-8 Tomb of the Virgin).

## Limits

- One reader; not blind. The headings that I did not look at on the image are OCR only (about 100 of 166 lines). The digit-only pass and the Hebrew pass agree on the grid for all of them.
- A grid gives the entrance or the complex to 10 m (or 100 m for four-digit grids). It does not give the façade direction.
- The catalogue flags (1st c., façade, inscription) are Gwiazda's, not checked against the book here.
- The candidate place points have their own precision (50–300 m; column 2 above).
