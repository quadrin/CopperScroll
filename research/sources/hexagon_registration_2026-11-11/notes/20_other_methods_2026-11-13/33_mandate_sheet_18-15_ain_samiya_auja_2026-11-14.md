# Mandate 1:20,000 sheet 18-15 (El Mughaiyir, 1942) used for ʿAin Samiya and Kh. el-ʿAuja el-Fauqa. 14 November 2026

## Result first

- The sheet is in the bucket with a registration of 5 November (world file, 1.70 m for each pixel, Palestine Grid). It covers E 179.0–191.0, N 150.0–161.2. Four of my image places are on it. **All four agree with the sheet to 25 m or less.**
- **Kh. el-Marjama: the name is now Checked.** The sheet prints "Kh. el Marjama" with a ruin outline on the spur above the spring, at my point A. Before: "Checked as a feature; name Probable".
- **Kh. Samiya: Checked by position.** The sheet has ruin signs and the name at my point C (20 m). Before: Probable.
- **New from the sheet at ʿAin Samiya:** the spring sign "ʿEin Samiya" (PG 181.56/155.26); a **"Roman Mill R."** (ruin) at PG 181.77/155.15; "Caves" in two groups east of the wadi; a "Canal" along the wadi south of the mill; "Beit el Qadi" at 181.69/154.93.
- **Kh. el-ʿAuja el-Fauqa:** ruin outline with trigonometric point 488B at PG 187.91/150.455; my point (187.91/150.48) is 25 m from it. No change (Checked).
- **The canal at ʿAuja has a name on the sheet: "Qanat Farʿun", with the words "Disused Roman Canal".** It comes from the north-west along the wadi, passes the mill and goes south off the sheet. A branch, "Aqueduct", goes south-east to El ʿAuja and "to Jericho". My mill point (188.16/150.46) is 25 m from a building sign at the place where the branch leaves the canal. Zertal and Bar 2019 call the south line "Qanat Musa" (edit log B47); the sheet supports the line as an old canal.
