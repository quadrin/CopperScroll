## Result first

- The aft frame **D3C1209-200214A018, tile e**, covers Qumran and ʿAin Feshkha. It was found by matching its overview to the forward ortho image (correlation 0.48; other tiles 0.23 or less) and fitted to the forward ortho image with a local height model: **1,394 tie points, 6.0 m rms**, area PG 189.5–196.5 / 122.5–132.0 (`D74_A018e_height_model.json`).
- Views at 0.5 m from the aft frame, beside the forward frame of the same day and a second date (5 May 1978; for ʿAin Feshkha, 25 June 1974, because the 1978 tile does not reach it):

| Item | What the aft frame shows | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **Cave 3Q** (plan point 193.10/129.50, by eye) | The cliff with dark shadow hollows and bright rock faces, the same on the three images. No single opening can be told from the shadows of the rock. A cave mouth of a few metres is at the limit of 1 m images in cliff shadow | **Not confirmed (no change)** |
| **Qumran plateau line** | The straight light line east–west from the cliff top towards Kh. Qumran is clear on all three images; its west end is at the plan point of the aqueduct (193.30/127.71) to about 10 m. A straight north–south line of about 85 m meets it at the west end; on the 1978 image more tracks join there, so it is Probable a track | **Probable (no change)**; now three images of two dates show the same line |
| **ʿAin Feshkha long wall of plate XLII** | The main building with its parallel partitions is clear east of the road. North of it the frame shows the north–south road, an east–west banked line of about 250 m, and a rounded field boundary. No line along the north-east direction of the plan | **Not confirmed (no change)**; now four images |

- The aft frame is sharper than the forward frame of the same day, as at Cypros, but it shows no new feature at these three places.
