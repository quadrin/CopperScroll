# Coüasnon's plan of Kh. Qumran (fig. 54) on the image of 1978, with positions of nine water loci; first look at the three "Wadi Kumran" frames of 1918. 14 November 2026

## Result first

- **Coüasnon's plan of Period Ib (Humbert, Chambon and Młynarczyk 2016, fig. 54) is on the image of 5 May 1978 and agrees with it.** The main building, the west block, the round cistern L110 (on the dark disc of the image), the pool L91, the long pool L71 and the south-east annexes fall on the excavated walls and hollows. Agreement by eye: 2 to 3 m, the size of 3 to 4 image pixels. **Checked.**
- The transform has four values and came from six loci of step 21 (note of this morning). The overlay is the first test of it against the image itself. No position of step 21 changes.
- **Nine water loci now have positions from the measured plan** (table). Before, only six loci had positions, and they came from fig. 8.
- **The three frames that the archive lists with "Wadi Kumran" do not show Kh. Qumran.** The captions written on the negatives are "Tabk el Kunetira" (BS Pal. 886, 3 January 1918, height 3000 m, oblique), "Wadi el Kunetra" (888, 4 June 1918, height 5000 m, lens 50 cm, vertical) and "n. Nebi Musa" (890, same flight, vertical). The archive title of 886 has "Wadi Kumran (?)" with a question mark. They show dissected hill country, a wadi and tracks. I did not register them, so I can not give their place on the grid. **Nothing is identified.**
