# Plans 11, 26 and 35 of Netzer 2001 on the image of 1978 by the site grid; Shunat Nimrin — 11 November 2026

> Correction (same day, later): one square of the site grid is 10 m, not 20 m, and the northern wing is approximately 300 m from the anchor, not 350 m. The calculation used 10 m. See note 11.

## Result first

1. **Plans 11, 26 and 35 of Netzer 2001 are now on the image of 5 May 1978.** I did not use flat scans. I used the site grid that is printed on the border of each plan.
2. **The Twin Palaces (plan 26) are Checked.** They are the excavated area south of the two pools. Before, this was Probable.
3. **The Northern Wing of Herod's Third Palace (plan 35) is Checked a second time**, with a different plan.
4. **Area AA (plan 11, Buried Palace and Fortified Palace) is Probable** on the mound west of the pools.
5. **Shunat Nimrin: no control.** We have no HEXAGON window of that place. Tell Nimrin is Possible only.
6. **Not done:** the south hill of Tell Iktanu, the frames of 1953.

## 1. Method

- Each plan has a border with numbers of the site grid. One square of the border is 20 m (two times the 10 m bar).
- For each photograph I marked the grid lines on the border. The crossing points gave a perspective correction (24 to 48 points for each plan). This removes the oblique view of the photograph.
- The site grid was put on the image with the result of plan 14: the wall between the two pools is at grid row 47, column 17, and the grid is turned 13 degrees east of north.
- Thus plans 11, 26 and 35 have no free value. Their position on the image is a prediction.

| Plan | Area | Where it falls on the image | Agreement | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 26 | AE, Twin Palaces | The excavated block south of the pools; the rows of rooms and the long north–south walls agree | 5 to 10 m | Checked |
| 35 | Northern Wing, Third Palace | The excavated rooms north of Wadi Qelt; the two courts at the west end are empty areas on the image, the rooms at the east end are dark | 5 to 10 m | Checked |
| 11 | AA, Buried Palace and Fortified Palace | The mound west of the pools, with trenches and rooms inside the frame of the plan | 10 to 20 m | Probable |

- The northern wing is 350 m from the anchor of the grid. An agreement of 5 to 10 m there shows that the turn of 13 degrees and the square of 20 m are correct.
- **Limits:** I marked the grid lines by eye on a reduced view (error approximately 3 pixels of the photograph, 1 m on the ground). The fold of the paper is not corrected. The image has 0.8 m for each pixel. I cannot test single walls.
- Plans 12, 15, 23, 25, 29 and 36 are not done.

## 2. Shunat Nimrin and Tell Nimrin

- The HEXAGON windows that we have do not include Shunat Nimrin. Thus I could not make control points there.
- With the mean of the west and east CORONA transforms, the published point of Tell Nimrin (31.9003 N, 35.6247 E) falls approximately 150 m east of the road junction, on a light area south of the main road, with faint marks of approximately 100 m.
- This is not a test. The error of the position is approximately 200 m. Status: Possible.
- To get control: cut one HEXAGON window of 1974 at Shunat Nimrin, or use a modern map.
