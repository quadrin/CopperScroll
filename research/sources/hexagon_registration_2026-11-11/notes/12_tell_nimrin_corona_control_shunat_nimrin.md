# Tell Nimrin on HEXAGON 1974; CORONA control at Shunat Nimrin — 11 November 2026

## Result first

1. **Tell Nimrin is identified on the image of 1 December 1974.** It is an oval mound of approximately 180 by 100 m at 31.9010 N, 35.6251 E. The main road cuts its north edge. Status: Checked (position and form). Checklist record sit0308.
2. **CORONA 1967 has control at Shunat Nimrin now** (43 blocks, 11 m). The mean transform that I used before was correct to approximately 50 m there.
3. **The script was correct.** The window is inside tile A017e.

## 1. Registration of the new window

- Window: `A017e_dec74_shunat_nimrin_full_x19000_y19200.jpg` (4000 by 4000 pixels, turned 180 degrees).
- Method: shading of the elevation model, block comparison. 26 blocks, 12 m (rms). Three sun directions give the same centre to 10 m.
- The first position (Kafrein transform, extended 5 km) was wrong by approximately 170 m.
- The blocks are in the hills on the east side of the window. On the flat ground at the village there are no blocks. I estimate an error of 50 m there.

## 3. 1967 against 1974 at Shunat Nimrin

- The roads, the tree avenues and the walled compound are the same.
- In 1967 the ground at the tell is light with some faint marks. The mound is not clear on CORONA.
- The rows of light lines east of the tell are not on CORONA of 1967. They are later than September 1967.
- The plantation in the oval track is later than 1967 also.
