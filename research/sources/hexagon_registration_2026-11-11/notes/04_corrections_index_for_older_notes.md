## Rules for a reader of the older notes

1. A position in a note before `hexagon-1974-automatic-registration-to-elevation-model` is not good. Use the site table.
2. A size from tile F017b or F018b is good from west to east if the note used 1 m, and good from north to south if the note used 0.88 m. For tile A017e (December, Kafrein) sizes with "about 1 m" are 7 to 16 percent too large.
3. A status word in a "Result first" list is the status at that time. The site table has the status of today.
