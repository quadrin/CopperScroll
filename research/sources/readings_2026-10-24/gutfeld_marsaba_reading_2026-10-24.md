## Bavarian aerial BS Pal. 0923, 3 January 1918 (07 Maps and GIS/Bavarian aerials 1918)
- Plate legend: "Fl. 304. 229. 3.1.18. 2¹⁵ w Mar Saba H.3000. Br. 25": flight 304, frame 229, 3 Jan 1918, 2:15 pm, west of Mar Saba, height 3,000 m. Oblique, north arrow at upper right. Scan 10,944 × 7,847 px.
- **Scale check (INFERENCE):** rows of dark dashes near the centre, 30–45 px long, are probably black goat-hair tents (about 6–10 m), which gives roughly 0.2–0.3 m per pixel in the middle distance. Film grain softens detail under about 2 m. A cairn of 2–3 m in ground-coloured stone would be about 10 px and would show only by its shadow (low winter sun at 2:15 pm helps).
- **Entry 35 not searched.** The field list's points (Israel Guide 13, grids 1808.1259 and 1819.1227) still have to be placed on this oblique frame against a terrain model. Before that, fix a feasibility rule as in Method 2: pick known features of cairn size that survive today, and stop if they cannot be seen.
