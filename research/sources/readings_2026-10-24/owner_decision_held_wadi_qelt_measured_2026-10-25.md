# Owner decision: held Wadi Qelt material may be used if it carries measurements (25 Oct 2026)

**Asked (chat, 25 Oct 2026 ~07:00 UTC):** (1) whether the held-area Pharan figures in Hirschfeld 1992 could be used; then (2) whether the same rule covers the cave sentences of Kallai sites 137, 147 and 157 (ʿEin Farʿa and Wadi er-Rawabi) that the Kochavi 1972 arrival record withholds from the rarity packets (outcomes/kochavi-1972_2026-10-19b.json, deviation D2).

**Answers (Alex Kesin):** (1) "it can be saved/used if it has measurements" (06:59 UTC); (2) "ok" (07:02 UTC).

**Effect.**
- Material from the held Wadi Qelt gorge area (HA-ESI phase 2 plan §7) may be saved and used when it carries measurements (a scale bar, dimensions or levels). Unmeasured material stays saved but unused.
- Anything so used is **exposed**: it is exploratory and cannot serve as unseen evidence for the arrival items.
- Applied: Hirschfeld 1992 Figs 2 and 57 (cleared); Figs 1 and 56 (photos, not used).
- Kochavi D2: the cave sentences of Kallai sites 137, 147 and 157 may enter the Stage 2 packets where they give measurements; those units are marked exposed. The coding still waits for full-size photos of Kallai pp. 158–193 (the Drive copies are 480 px previews).
