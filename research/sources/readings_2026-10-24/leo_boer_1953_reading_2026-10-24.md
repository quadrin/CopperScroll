Source: PEF Flickr set "Leo Boer Qumran photos (1953, with 2009 comparisons)", 49 files in the owner's Drive (06 Imagery). © Palestine Exploration Fund, all rights reserved; private research only; not committed or published. Captions are the PEF's (written by B. Wagemakers), from the repo manifest `research/sources/photo_archives/photo_manifest.csv`. The PEF "Plan of Qumran with position marks" (de Vaux's Period Ib plan with numbered arrows) gives each frame's standpoint and direction.

Atlas entries in scope: 20 Valley of Sekakah, 21 Sekakah, 22 Solomon's reservoir, 23 Solomon or Shallum's channel. Frames viewed: P01–P03, P05–P09, P17–P19, P26, P27, P33, P39–P41, P45, P46 and the plan. The others (P10–P16, P20–P25, P28–P32, P34–P38, P42–P44, P47, P48) show caves 1Q and 4Q, the "pantry" L86–89, L54–55, trenches A–B, camp scenes and people, by caption; they do not bear on the water system and were not opened.

EVIDENCE = the caption says it, or it is visible; INFERENCE = my reading.

## Findings

What is new relative to the repo (no earlier record of these frames beyond the manifest and README):
1. **The aqueduct west of the site was unexcavated at the end of 1953** (P06): a surface line of stones and a shallow trench, no exposed channel. The edged channel visitors see today (P07) was cleared later. For entries 21 and 23: any observation of the channel made from the modern surface reflects post-1953 clearing and edging, not the 1953 ground.
2. **L48/49 was already cleared to its steps by 31 December 1953**, with the damaged step in the lower flight visible and its wall rendering in better condition than in 2009 (P08 vs P09). A 1953 image of the pool before its plaster decayed.
3. **L91 was excavated in the week of 21–25 March 1954** (P17, P18, P26, P39), with spoil thrown into the ravine (P33). It was still full of earth on 21 March.
4. **The north-west pools were still buried in March 1954** (P45/P46 caption). L110, L117, L118 and L138 were therefore cleared in the later seasons; Boer shows none of them.

What changes nothing:
- Method 2: the 1978 HEXAGON frame was taken long after all eight pools were cleared, so Boer's frames do not affect that test. The 1967 CORONA frame was also after clearing.
- No frame shows the aqueduct head, the dam, the outlet south of L71, or any feature that bears on which installation the scroll names. No reading, identification, rating or outcome changes.
- Dates of construction: photographs date nothing; the earthquake reading of the L48/49 damage is the caption's (after de Vaux), not a new observation.
