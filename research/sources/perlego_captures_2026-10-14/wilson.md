# Wilson’s Arch 2025: captured-page and report reconciliation

14 October 2026. The supplied extraction report was read in full. Every supplied image was screened after its captions classified the subject as Jerusalem excavation architecture, photographs, coin catalogue or radiocarbon results. The 53 files represent 49 byte-distinct captures. Nine overview sheets were inspected; printed 155 and310 received separate full-size checks. Fine-table and plan labels outside those checks retain the report’s attribution. The original book and the earlier PLOS2020 paper were not opened in this sweep.

Source: Uziel, Solomon and Lieberman, eds., *The Excavations Beneath Wilson’s Arch*, Volume1 (2025), https://www.perlego.com/book/4913932. The report describes Chapters22 (423–443),3 (51–122), selected coin material in5 and radiocarbon results in13, with locus index123–155. Its reported reader offset is+15. It supplies no whole-book audit.

**The stone-fill comparison has a specific limit.** Captured index155 directly lists L4559 as Stratum 7, SquareA2, stone fill abutting the Western Wall, upper level719.24m; the lower-level field is blank. Its dimensions, thickness, subphase, measured pier offset and direct deposit date are absent from that row. On the same page, L4557,4558 and4561 are stone heaps assigned to Stratum 6, associated with architectural fragments or seating infrastructure. Reused older blocks do not date their deposition. The report’s theater construction account and the captured Stratum 6 plans distinguish these heaps from Early Roman surfaces. A stone-fill label does not establish the particular cairn required by entry28.

**The date table has different types of interval.** Captured Table 13.3/printed 310 directly gives feature “Most probable age” summaries of100–40 BCE for W4493,30 BCE–25 CE for the northern pier,35–70 CE for the southern pier,130–180 CE for the theater northern wall, and540–570 CE for the northern pool. These remain published Bayesian-model summaries, not measured calendar dates. The separate sample column explicitly says modeled68.2% intervals: southern samples RTD9382 give36–107 CE and RTD9004/9003 give35–103 CE. Do not assign the sample-column probability to the feature-summary column without the model definition. RTD9383’s agreement index53.3 is not a probability; the seating-fill summary’s asterisk remains unexplained in the inspected capture/report. The report attributes different older ranges to retained prose on311 and the2020 paper; neither was directly checked here. The report’s319 before66CE narrative is narrower than its quoted35–70 CE model summary, which includes66–70.

**Water and ground surfaces require separate phases.** Reported63/68/69 place northern and southern piers in successive phases and stepped plastered installations between them. Their architectural tie is extraction-reported. The northern channel’s plaster has its own model group; later southern-channel replastering240–280 CE cannot date initial construction. The northern reservoir and medieval al-Buraq pools are later installations. W4493’s possible dam function remains a hypothesis alongside fortification, consistent with the existing2019/HA-ESI record.

The report distinguishes room entrances around721.45m, tentative or assigned stone surfaces around719m, channel bottoms, and drill limits. It says the2015–2018 excavation stopped above bedrock. Captured index155 confirms L4562/4563 are exploratory drills with endpoints, not explicit bedrock surfaces. Neither a drill endpoint nor a channel bottom can supply a general ancient walking-surface datum for a cubit-depth test. The combined plans and sections retain captions, scales or elevation axes and phase legends; no geographic registration or new measurement was performed.

**Coin-locus joins cannot date every construction.** Catalogue captures194–204 contain no stratum column. The supplied report joins loci to the separately read index. It reports that Chapter 5/183 supplies no numismatic construction confirmation; the30–31 CE coin beneath the street cited inChapter22 comes from an earlier excavation, rather than a numbered pier trench in this campaign. Reported problematic loci9490,542 and34258 must remain unresolved identifiers. The captured catalogue layout was screened; the full table was not retranscribed or joined in this pass. No primary coin/context correction is made from its overview alone.

The new volume reports later results and recalibration from the same Wilson’s Arch excavation discussed in2019. It adds publication detail and analytical revision; repeated plans, model reruns and renderings add no independent excavation. The stated target is **partly obtained**: the report and captures supply dated architecture and measured levels, but no identified Early Roman cairn with the required dimensions, origin and phase-controlled relation to the arch. No identification test or outcome-ledger change follows.
