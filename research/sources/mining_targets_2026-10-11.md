## Archives: protected overlap

These archives hold material that existing protocols reserve. Read them only under those protocols:

- **IAA Mandate archive** (iaa-archives.org.il). The site files for the Qumran cliffs and marl terrace, the Jericho escarpment and the Wadi Qelt gorge overlap the arrival-register item for IAA Archive cave records. Open those files only through [ARRIVAL_PROCEDURE.md](../preregistration/arrivals/ARRIVAL_PROCEDURE.md): blind extraction first, then the integrator's comparison. Files for other sites are open for exploratory use.
- **WWI Bavarian aerials (BayHStA BS Pal.), Dalman 1925, HUJI aerial archive.** Frames that show the Tell es-Sultan north strip are opened only under the SULTAN-1 protocol. Check each frame's footprint before viewing it.
- **Images.** Online access gives no licence. Apply the asset rules in AGENTS.md (manifest, reuse status, tracing where upload is restricted).

How checked: archive.org IDs come from the archive.org search/metadata API, and I matched each to the title page in the OCR text (tome/volume, year). JSTOR journal URLs come from JSTOR's own KBART title lists. JSTOR article IDs come from a JSTOR issue contents page or Orion records. EAP volume numbers come from the title pages in the IIIF images. Nothing is guessed. "IA" = https://archive.org/details/

## Links that could not be opened or checked
- http://www.ariel.ac.il/research/rd/judea-and-samaria-research-studies: bot-protection challenge (HTTP 247, empty page). This is the official journal URL from the ISSN record.
- JSTOR article and issue pages mostly return a "Client Challenge" (JavaScript) page to scripts. Only https://www.jstor.org/stable/i27924902 could be read. The seven missing IEJ 11/1–2 stable IDs need a browser visit to the issue contents page.
- https://eap.bl.uk/project/EAP1544: AWS WAF challenge for scripts. Record pages opened via WebFetch, and the IIIF manifests open directly.
- https://www.atiqot.org.il/ opens but is no longer the IAA journal site. Use publications.iaa.org.il/atiqot.
- Niqrot Zurim: no official website found (searched English and Hebrew titles and Israel Cave Research Center).
