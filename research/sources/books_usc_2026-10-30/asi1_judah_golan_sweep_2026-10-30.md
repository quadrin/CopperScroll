## 0. Scope, coverage and held-area classification

- **Survey limits (EVIDENCE, p. 19, IMG_1774).** Team A covered about 800 km². North: the Jerusalem municipal area and the armistice line. West and south: the armistice line. East: the desert edge, "on the line between Herodium and Kh. Markaz".
  - INFERENCE: Ramat Raḥel (entry 46) lies inside the Jerusalem municipal area and is outside this survey. Herodium, ʿAin en-Naṭuf/Khareitun and Mar Saba sit on or east of the desert line. None of them has a catalogue entry: an OCR search for פרדיס / הרודיון / נטוף / חריטון found no site entry, and the photographed index pp. 86–89 lists none of them.
- **Golan limits (EVIDENCE, p. 244, IMG_1658).** The Jordan on the west, Hermon on the north, Wadi er-Ruqqad on the east and the Yarmuk on the south.
- **Held-area classification (done before reading).** Every catalogue site in pp. 19–89 lies in the Judaean hills, the Shephelah edge or the Hebron hills. All are **not held**; none is in the Wadi Qelt gorge, the Jericho plain or escarpment, Abu Saraj, the Twin Cave or IV/17. All Golan sites are **not held**. The held Bar-Adon chapter (pp. 90–150) was not opened.
- **Coverage.** I read the photographs of:
  - pp. 19, 24 and 28 (introduction and identification list);
  - pp. 36–55 in full (sheets 16-12, 17-12, 16-11, 17-11, 15-11, 15-10: the Bethlehem, Artas, Tekoa, Beit Fajjar, ʿArrub, Beth-zur and Halhul belt);
  - pp. 64–68.

  Pp. 56–63 and 69–85 (Hebron, Ziph, Carmel, Yatta and the southern hills, 20–40 km from any candidate) were triaged by OCR keyword search only: pool, aqueduct, steps, cistern, columbarium, miqveh, cairn and tomb. ## 4. Summary of proposals

- **Entry 38** (`natuf`, P): Kochavi 1972 identifies Netophah with **Kh. Bad Faluḥ (site 49, pp. 28/44)**. The site has rock-cut burial caves, a mosaic-lined pool, Hellenistic sherds and a Jannaeus coin, and lies about 3 km from the ʿAin en-Naṭuf point. Consider P 2→1, or register Badd Faluḥ as an alternative for entry 38 with F ≤ 1.
- **Entries 39/42–45** (Tekoa–Herodium): new generic rows for Tekoa (62), Beit Baṣṣa (29, rock-cut channels with Roman I) and Kh. Marine (61). No level change.
- **Entry 59**: the Golan "Bazak" of Pixner is T. el-Bazuk, a dolmen field (p. 277), with no conduit. Close that branch at F 0 if it is ever opened.
- **Entry 60**: Tell Shihab is outside the ASI 1 survey. No bearing.
- **Entry 46**: Ramat Raḥel is outside the survey (municipal boundary). No data.
- **Lanes**: add el-Ḥadab's columbarium (176) and the Qaṣra Second Temple tombs (177, plans) as out-of-sector controls if the lane boundaries admit the Hebron hills. The ʿArrub aqueduct (76) is a measured, undated water row.
