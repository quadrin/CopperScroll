  - later settlement, mainly Roman, Byzantine and Arab, that destroys or covers earlier remains;

**Later periods and features (p.155, "התקופות המאוחרות"):**
- In the Roman and especially the Byzantine period, settlement expanded and sites grew. These periods form the upper layer of the site and the most robust settlement.

- Roads whose sides are fenced (READING UNCERTAIN: "המגודרות משני צדיהן") are to be attributed to the Byzantine period. The evidence given is that disused roads of this kind are found also in areas still settled today, in fields and plots, and at abandoned ancient sites.

- Arab and Medieval periods are represented quite noticeably, though the building is poorer. Stones in second use are very common in both public and private buildings.
