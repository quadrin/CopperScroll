## 1. What entry 40 needs (from the repo)

- **Record.** `research/assessments/evidence_components/ratings.csv` row 40 (`beth_horon`) rates R1 P3 F1 D1, "possible, low". Its `decisive_next` asks for "an Upper Beth-Horon tomb field drawing with entrance bearing, channel relation and chronology". It also says HA 85 p. 31 and HA 31/32 p. 13 are not that drawing, and that "the source of those citations needs checking". `research/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md` R04 (lines 135–140) repeats this and lists HA 85 p. 31, HA 31/32 p. 13 and Jeremias 1958 p. 86 as source dependencies.
- **Readings (edition-based).** IX 7–9 has these branches: ים (Milik, Lefkovits; the plates lean this way) or דרום (Puech); החורון (Milik's print) or החורין (Puech, and Milik's own drawing); and Milik's inserted ṣade, ב<צ>ריח, against ברוח. Beth-Horon also needs Milik's rule that the article stands for "Beth". Jeremias's Horite tombs at Beit Guvrin remain an equal-rank rival (`research/sites/entry40_bethhoron_review.md`).
- **Tomb cited.** "Four undated bench tombs on the upper village's west slope". This is IAA Benjamin survey site 143, printed p. 142, at village grid 16085 14360; the atlas pin is a village anchor, not a tomb. The record also cites kokhim tombs at Kh. ez-Zeit and Peleg 2004's robbed kokhim tomb at Lower Beth-Horon, whose exterior entrance faces south.
- **What would change the rating.** For F 1→2: a specific Upper Beth-Horon tomb with a west-facing chamber or a channel. For D 1→2: Early Roman finds in or at a tomb of the upper village (frozen test U6, `registration/new_sources_round2_2026-10-17_tests.md`).
- **Where the two HA locators came from (EVIDENCE, repo).** Both first appear in the table "Additional institutional-access targets" in `research/sources/entry40_parallel_followup_2026-10-01.md` (lines 128–136). They were found by searching the JSTOR and IAA contents pages, not cited by any tomb source:
  - HA 85 (1984) p. 31 is D. Eitam, "Land of Benjamin, Survey".
  - HA 31/32 (1969) p. 13 is titled "בית חורון תחתון" (Lower Beth-Horon).

  Both were then carried forward as "tomb plan" dependencies, in FINDINGS_GUIDE F42.5, `plan/stages.json` and STAGED_OBJECTIVES R04. The repo's own HA coding already records HA31-013 as a movable stone dyeing installation from a Kallai students' survey, with no eligible feature (`research/regional/haesi_features/phase3_2026-10-11/part_a/reports.csv` line 72).

## 3. Does this supply the tomb drawing or the true source of the HA citations?

- **Tomb drawing: not supplied (EVIDENCE).** No Beth-Horon-area entry in the 1993 volume prints a tomb plan, section or entrance bearing. This covers sites 12, 22, 28, 139, 140, 142, 143, 144 and 145, and the pp. 123–125 table. ASI 1 has no Beit ʿUr entry, and its only plans are of forts on the eastern road. The site-143 bench tombs remain a group description: no individual position, dimension, bearing, channel or date. The gap stays open.
- **HA 31/32 p. 13: source found (EVIDENCE).** The 1993 survey cites it at **site 22, Lower Beth-Horon, p. 43** ("see: Hadashot Arkheologiyot 31–32, 5730, p. 13"). The cited item is the "dyeing installation", which the survey reinterprets as an Iron Age oil press. This agrees with the owner's report and with the repo's HA31-013 coding (Kallai students' survey, movable stone installation). The locator always concerned the **lower** village and an installation, never a tomb.
- **HA 85 p. 31: source context found (EVIDENCE and INFERENCE).**
  - EVIDENCE: the 1993 English foreword (p. 6*, OCR) says the Ramallah-map fieldwork "was coordinated by David Eitam". The English title page lists Eitam among the authors of the chapter containing sites 141–321.
  - INFERENCE: HA 85 p. 31, Eitam's "Land of Benjamin, Survey" (1984), is the preliminary notice of the same fieldwork. Its final publication is the 1993 Part 3, including site 143 on p. 142, so the final report supersedes it for tomb detail.
  - Limit: no citation of HA 85 was found in the 1993 OCR, but 128 body pages were omitted from that OCR as location-only.
- **Why the citations were in R04 (EVIDENCE, repo).** Both came from contents-page searches on 1 October, not from any tomb citation. They were then listed as tomb-plan dependencies by mistake. This is now resolved.
