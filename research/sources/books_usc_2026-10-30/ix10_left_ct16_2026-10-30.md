## 4. Comparison with the editions

**Sources opened:**
- `variants.csv` V0439–V0459;

**The question.** Every candidate puts a ח/ה pair, then ר, before the final letter:

| reading | who | ending | letters needed at CT16's edge |
|---|---|---|---|
| מנחה רב | Puech | …נ-ח-ה ␣ ר-ב | ה, space, ר |
| מנה חרם | Wolters, García Martínez–Tigchelaar | …נ-ה ␣ ח-ר-ם | ח, no space, ר |
| מן החרם | Allegro, Beyer, Lefkovits, Muchowski | …ן ␣ ה-ח-ר-ם | ח, no space, ר |
| Milik's hand copy | Milik | מנחהרב, letters drawn continuously | ה, ר |

The variant table marks the חרם readings as emended, per Puech: they assume ב→מ and ח→ה.

**EVIDENCE (what the images show).**
- E1. At the strip edge, IX 10 ends with a two-legged ח/ה-type letter, then a gap of ~30–45 px, then a letter with a top bar and right
  vertical whose left part runs into the edge. Seen in all four E/L lightings, with a possible echo in C and R.
- E2. The one feature that could separate ח from ה (whether the left leg meets the roof) gives different answers in T and B lightings.
- E3. The gap before the edge letter is not clearly wider than the gap to the right of the two-legged letter.
- E4. Nothing legible lies on the flange or rim to the left.
- E5. No closed final ם is visible on CT16, and none was expected: the line ends on CT17.

**INFERENCE.**
- I1. **The ר and the ח/ה order are visible only in part, and they do not decide the division.** Grade: probable.
  - The edge letter is ר-shaped as far as it survives. But it may be a ה or ח whose left leg is lost at the edge (E1).
  - If it is ר: the two-legged letter before it is ה for Puech and ח for Wolters and the others. E2 cannot settle that. E3 cannot settle where the space falls (before ר for Puech, before ח for Wolters).
  - If it is a cut ה/ח: the ר is lost at the saw cut or in CT17's pitted zone. Both letter orders then fit: Puech ח-[ה]-ר; Wolters ה-[ח]-ר.
- I2. **The slight lean to ח (E2) would favour Wolters's ח-ר over Puech's ה-ר, but only if the edge letter is ר.** Too weak to count: possible at most.
- I3. **Consistency with CT17:** consistent.
  - CT17 put IX 10's last letter, ב (probable), 1.2 cm left of CT17's cut, with only pits between.
  - CT16 shows the line's letters running to its own edge. So between CT16's edge letter and CT17's ב lie the saw kerf plus ~1.2 cm of
    pitted surface: room for about one or two letters (inference, from letter widths of ~70–80 px).
  - So the CT16 edge letter need not touch the final ב. The engraved ending …רב supported by CT17 is not contradicted.
  - Nothing here supports an engraved ם.
- I4. **Milik's copy** (מ נ [ascender] ח ה ר ב, as reported in the CT17 note): its last three letters before ב (ח ה ר) agree with E1 if the
  two-legged letter is ה and the edge letter is ר. The copy and the image agree on the shapes; neither decides the division.
- I5. **Row labels in the earlier pass** (non-blind; flagged, not resolved).
  - The follow-ups note read a "loop (ס?)" in IX 10's left half on CT16 as part of כסף.
  - The only loop-like feature in these crops is the oval blister. In the row assignment above it is in **IX 11**.
  - If that pass's loop is this feature, its כסף reading near the left zone should be re-checked. Location unknown: its crop coordinates are not tied to these.
- I6. **The earlier pass expected מנה/מנחה "at the CT16 left edge (curled flange, not examined)".**
  - At IX 10's height the flange yields nothing legible.
  - In the E/L frames, the last two letters before the edge are on flat metal: the ח/ה letter and the ר-type edge letter.

**Verdict on the brief's question:** **the image does not decide** between מנחה רב and מנה חרם / מן החרם (unresolved).
- What it adds: the line's CT16 end is "…(ח/ה) + (ר-type or cut letter) | edge".
- Every candidate predicts that sequence. The only discriminating detail, the ח/ה roof junction, is ambiguous between lightings.
