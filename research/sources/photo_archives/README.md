## What the sets show (identifications)

DQCAAS gives codes, not captions. The identifications below are inferences unless marked as evidence.

- **AF = Ain Feshkha.** The springs, shore, walls and cliff caves, with Bedouin. Allegro's Dead Sea Scrolls Fund expedition camped at Ain Feshkha and searched the cliffs behind and north of the camp (EVIDENCE: Allegro, *Search in the Desert* p. 99; Plates 9 and 12 are captioned as Ain Feshkha). INFERENCE: many AF photos are from that camp (probably winter 1962–63; the book gives no year).
- **AM = Kh. Mird (Hyrcania).** A first look showed a chapel with painted saints, a rock-cut stepped shaft, a large aqueduct wall (files `am.aq_.*`) and a hilltop ruin set (`am.n.*`). Allegro describes the same kinds of features at Mird: two rock-cut cisterns, a bridge wall carrying a plastered aqueduct, a church with mosaics, and a rock-cut tunnel of about a hundred steps on the north side (EVIDENCE: *Search in the Desert* pp. 114–120). INFERENCE: AM is Mird. This is a stronger inference than before, but no photo is captioned. Allegro's Copper Scroll Expedition worked at Mird in the first half of April 1960 and Wright published its plans and plates (EVIDENCE: Wright, *Biblica* 42, 1961); some AM photos may come from that visit (INFERENCE). The `am.n.*` hilltop set is not identified.
- **AMur = objects.** Combs, woven straw and other small finds. The code suggests Wadi Murabba'at (INFERENCE from the code only). Allegro's book never names Murabba'at, so it cannot confirm this. Christmas Cave finds (cloth, leather, matting) are another possibility that has not been checked.
- **AQ.3Q = Cave 3 (and three 1Q photos).** Cave 3 and its collapsed mouth. Allegro's book reports no visit to Cave 3, so these photos come from a visit the book does not describe (INFERENCE).
- **PEF Boer 1953.** Leo Boer's photos of the 1953 excavation at Kh. Qumran and of Cave 4, with 2009 comparison photos and a plan with position marks. The captions are the PEF's.
