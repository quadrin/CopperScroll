  - **publication listed**: 840 licences;
  - **no publication listed** (the sheet has the excavation, but "-" or "NDA" as publication): 231;
  - **not in the excavation sheet**: 105; one more (L-991/2004) is probably in the Kh. Qumran row (see below).

## Addition, later the same day: items checked

- **L-291/1982 is the Doq dig of December 1982.** Licence list: "Jebel Qurunṭul", Ehud Netzer, Hebrew University, grid 190850/142450, which is 39 m from the Doq summit point (31.8744 N, 35.4306 E = PG 190855/142411). The excavation sheet names the same licence "Tahunat el-Hawa" (Netzer). The project knows the dig: Amit 1989, p. 228 n. 5 (rooms on the north-east spur near the pools, Herodian), and Netzer 2006, p. 207 n. 25 ("The finds from the one-week dig have not yet been published"). The Doq record of 2 October (`research/measurements/cycle11/doq_records.json`) lists the permit as "unlocated"; **the licence number is now known**, so an archive request to the Staff Officer for Archaeology can name it. L-646/1994 ("Cave No. 20", Darawsheh and Solomon) is 46 m from the same point; no publication listed.
- **L-734 is published** (Broshi and Eshel 1999, caves north of Kh. Qumran). It and L-518 were missed in the first pass because they are written without a year; the four such licences are now matched (2 published, 2 not in the excavation sheet).
- **Hizmi's digs in "Cave Q-4" (L-449/1989, with Richardson) and "Cave Q-5" (L-476/1990)** are at the grid of Caves 4Q and 5Q in the marl terrace beside the ruin. The workbook lists no publication; one web search found none. Status: no publication found.
- "Cave Q-3" (Vendyl Jones, L-399/1986): no publication listed.
- "Cave of the Pillar" L-764/1997 and L-792/1998 ("Not SOA", excavator not given): no publication listed. An earlier dig in a cave of the same name (L-603/1993, Feig) is published (Sion 2002; Feig 2002); it is not known if it is the same cave.
