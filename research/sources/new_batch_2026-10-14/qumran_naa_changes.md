- **Independent laboratory work, inherited objects:** pp6–7/viewers36–37 describe150mg sampling after cleaning1.5mm,60–80mg irradiated inBudapest, short/long irradiations and gold-comparator/standard controls. Specimens originate from deVaux museum collections. Reanalysis is independent measurement, not an independent field provenance.
- **Sampling is adaptive:** p7/viewer37 describes1998 pilot sampling followed by170 samples guided by those results. p8/viewer38 says34% of important loci represented; this is locus coverage, not a probability sample of pottery, occupants or activities. Ia/Ib are deliberately pooled because specimens could not be discriminated stylistically; that analytical convention does not date construction phases.
- **Local reference is an inference with explicit conditions:** pp6–10/viewers36–40 distinguish experimental vessels made from modern puddle marl/DeadSea mud from ancient vessel manufacture. Kiln lining/stucco highCa profiles initially have no direct statistical match to pottery. Dilution correction is expressly valid only if the same clay was levigated/tempered. Their two supposed wasters fail to match sampled pottery and each other, with bad-clay/other-period explanations left open. Thus the authors' certainty of localGroupI depends on reference selection and treatment, not a matched ancient source outcrop.
