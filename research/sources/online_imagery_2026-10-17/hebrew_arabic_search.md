# Hebrew and Arabic search for features with no online images — 17 October 2026

Follows the English-language search of the same day ([sources_list.md](sources_list.md)). Exploratory. Nothing here changes an assessment.

**Method.** WebSearch in Hebrew, Arabic and English, with fetches of the most promising pages; Wikimedia Commons API (text, category and geo searches, file metadata); YouTube titles and channels from oEmbed.

**Labels.** VERIFIED: the item exists and its own caption, description or text names the feature. LEAD: the item exists, but nothing confirms that it shows the feature. The searcher did not look at image pixels; descriptions come from captions, metadata or page text. No Copper Scroll photograph was opened.

**Blocked.** pikiwiki.org.il (HTTP 403; reached only through its Commons imports); hadashot-esi.org.il (timed out); hamichlol.org.il (robots.txt); Commons (frequent HTTP 429).
