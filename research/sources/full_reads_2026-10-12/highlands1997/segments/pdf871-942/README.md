# Highlands 1997: supplied pages 871–942

All 72 assigned source leaves were consumed sequentially. The range completes the Tana/Shuweiha catalogue and bibliography, reads all of Chapter 9’s prehistoric survey, then reads the site-name index and the period index through the start of Hellenistic entries (printed 836–907). PDF 917 is a blank leaf, verified natively. Every useful figure and artifact table was checked against the scan; index rows and columns were aligned natively.

The catalogue records a stepped plastered cistern, massive walls and a cut basin at Tana et-Tahta, with much pottery collected from robber trenches (printed 845/PDF 880). Nearby burial-cave pottery also came from looter spoil (848/883). The spring channel and earlier reservoir at el-ʿEin et-Tahta are described without construction dates (849/884). These contexts cannot directly date the installations.

Chapter 9 publishes the 1967–68 survey from Prausnitz’s summary and Friedmann’s field notes, with finds studied in 1994–95 (857/892). It selected deep western wadis with perennial water; interfluves and the region east of grid 170 were not walked. Small or mixed assemblages frequently receive broad or no dates. Cupmarks and mortars at Wadi Qanah Site 2 have no dimensions or direct cut dates (858/893). Possible in-situ deposits at Iraq Fureij and Attari remain author inferences (869/904; 875/910).

Repeated names require the source coordinates. The two Ein Fawwar sites are on Wadi Qanah at G.R. 1606/1740 and 1606/1737 (862/897). Ein esh-Sheikh Sultan is near Shuqba at G.R. 1552/1531 (878/913). Neither name alone links these places to the Jericho candidates.

The bibliography and indexes repeat existing field evidence. They do not create new independent observations. Root had already read candidate context; this entire segment is exploratory. No protected manuscript image or Tell es-Sultan north-strip image was encountered. Public reproduction rights are unestablished, so the deliverables contain original notes, retrieval locators and private-render hashes.
