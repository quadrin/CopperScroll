# Highlands of Many Cultures: PDF 967–995 reading segment

All 29 supplied pages were inspected sequentially on 12 October 2026. Every period-index row was read and checked on its native page. Every map was individually reviewed, including its title, legend, symbols, place labels, coordinates and notes. Native crops resolved crowded labels on PDF 986 and 989. The image-only final map was checked in full and in four overlapping native quadrants.

The records contain 29 coverage rows, 20 original observations and 31 table/map access records. Coverage is `read` for PDF 967–994 and `read_with_source_label_gaps` for PDF 995. No other page remains unresolved in this segment.

The indexes distinguish site identifiers, names and size codes. Blank cells, unnamed entries and nonnumeric codes remain distinct. Native printed 936/PDF 971 resolves Sinjil's Mamluk size code as 5, where the extracted text reads a letter. These index codes are not sherd counts and have not been converted into physical area.

The map legends use nine circle-size ranges in hectares, with stars for questionable attribution and squares for cemeteries. The Chalcolithic map explicitly excludes two chapter 9 sites. Several maps direct readers to broader or transitional lists for possible additions. Those qualifications are preserved rather than converting every transitional entry into secure evidence for both neighbouring periods. Printed 950/PDF 985 is titled Iron Age I–II, separate from the Iron I and Iron II maps. Printed 956/PDF 991 explicitly combines Umayyad and Abbasid periods.

PDF 994 is the last numbered publication page, 959. PDF 995 has empty extracted text but contains a colour map titled *The Southern Samaria Survey Grid*. Its native footer credits Edtal N. Levy, Department of Geography, Tel-Aviv University. The leaf supplies no creation date or explicit relationship to the printed edition. It shows red site points and purple boundaries and warns that not all names are marked.

Actual white vertical and horizontal seams in the PDF 995 image interrupt portions of its title and some site names. All surviving visible content was inspected, but the missing character sequences remain unresolved. The interrupted labels have not been reconstructed from familiar names or other catalogue entries. This is the sole source-content limitation in the assigned segment.

`page_coverage.csv` records each page and its actual reading method. `observations.csv` separates source facts, author qualifications, chronological basis and limitations. `figures_access.csv` records all 12 period-index tables, the unit map, 17 period maps and the additional grid, with source/native-image hashes and provenance. `source_manifest.json` records source identity, pagination and the PDF 995 limitation.

These records are descriptive and exploratory. No blind observation, holdout, confirmatory identification or regional rarity is certified.
