## Rogem Gannim: dated contexts and transmitted observations

Greenberg and Cinamon, *'Atiqot* 66 (2011), pp. 79-106, explicitly incorporate Sion's earlier installations and retain his terrace, cave and cistern numbering; their winepress numbers differ (p. 79, supplied PDF 2). Page 86/PDF 9 names Sion's 1999 excavation of Cistern 2. These statements verify the publication dependency and feature identity. They prevent treating a second publication of that basin as an independent observation of its stairs.

The stepped phase remains a tentative Early Roman attribution. Page 86 describes broad steps, infers a bath for grape-treaders from their form and WP3's date, and retains the possibility that this basin served the earlier winepress phase as a collecting vat. Table 1 (p. 81/PDF 4) expressly qualifies the bath remodelling. Page 102/PDF 25 similarly qualifies its Early Roman hewing. Later alterations removed those steps and replastered the basin, probably for rainwater collection. Plan 1, Section 3-3 (supplied PDF 31), supplies geometry without a sealed construction context or a keyed absolute chronology.

The locus list adds a specific dating restriction (p. 103/PDF 26). L114 is a Roman soil deposit **near** Cistern 2. L118 borders Cistern 2 and has no reported content date. L101 and L104 belong to WP3; L108, L130 and L135 belong to Cave 1's cistern fill. These contexts have different relations to the stepped installation. No listed context independently dates Cistern 2's stair cutting. The appendix's regional occupation dates therefore cannot become stair-foundation dates.

**Exact claim assessed:** the accessed report establishes Early Roman construction of Cistern 2's original cut or broad stairs through a directly dated construction context. **Result: not identifiable from available evidence.** The report supports a published relative conversion sequence and a tentative Early Roman stepped interpretation. Obtaining Sion's original report would permit a separate check of its dating basis; its citation supplies no unseen or independent confirmation.
