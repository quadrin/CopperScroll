## Findings
- **Plan 1** (EVIDENCE):
  - A double-line conduit runs along the N side of the complex. It turns S at the W into the pool area, and a branch runs E past the long storerooms (A(B)272, A(B)63, A(B)233/237).
  - Shown: the twin pools with steps; the peristyle pavilion; the Buried / Fortified Palace AA1 / A(A)81 with a long wall to the S; the Twin Palaces block (AE loci); the Western Garden pools (AC loci); a further stepped pool (A(L)351) in the SE.
  - Off-sheet pointers: "AH6 AH21 AH28 →" (E), "AK7 AK18 →" (SE) and "A(M)527 … A(M)552 ↓" (S).
- **Plan 2** (EVIDENCE):
  - A peristyle court (A(G)445/452) and Area AL with pools A(L)255 (with steps) and A(L)330 (within A(L)487); A(L)324, 346, 384.
  - A double-line channel enters from the W (A(G)214 corner) and passes a small stepped installation at a bend.
  - Faint reconstructed (dashed) enclosure to the N; a fan-shaped hatched slope to the W.
- **Plan 3** (EVIDENCE):
  - Schematic: the Wadi Qelt banks; the sunken-garden façade B2/B3/B4 with a central exedra; a large rectangle (the pool) to its E; the Northern Wing (B44, "(B127)").
  - A long line (B154) runs S from the Northern Wing across the wadi to a square / round structure (the mound building, BK).
  - Main plan: the Northern Wing with its peristyle courts, the round hall and the bath suite (B-loci).
- **Plan 4** (EVIDENCE):
  - Areas FA–FE.
  - Long double-line E–W conduits pass through FD and FE (F141, F60 area).
  - Stepped plastered installations: F4 (stepped, with F1 beside it), F120 (stepped, in FC), F87 / F239 (stepped, in FE).
  - Rectangular basins F30 (with a round feature), F37, F177.
  - Square buildings F20 / F19 / F22 (FA) and F162 / F158 / F165 (FB); F128, a round installation in FB.
  - "F201 ←" points W off the sheet.
- INFERENCE: the sheets add channel layouts and pool / installation loci for the palace complex, 1.5–2.5 km SW of the tell. They are not relevant to SULTAN-1. No sheet shows RP candidates from reference_points.json.
