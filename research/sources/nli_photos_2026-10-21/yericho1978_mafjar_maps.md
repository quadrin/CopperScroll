## Kh. el-Mafjar (M. Rosen-Ayalon, "ח'רבת אל-מפג'ר", pp. 60–64)
- EVIDENCE (p. 60), dating:
  - The Umayyad period is dated "(749–661)".
  - Kh. el-Mafjar is perhaps the latest complex of the period, built under Hisham "(743–724)" or Walid II "(744–743)" (dates as printed, written backwards).
  - The site was identified in the second half of the 19th c. (n. 2: *SWP*).
  - Excavation began "רק ב-1934/5" and continued to 1948. Parts still await excavation.
  - n. 1: Hamilton, *Levant* 1 (1969) 61–72. n. 3: QDAP reports; Hamilton, *Khirbat al-Mafjar* (Oxford 1959).
- EVIDENCE (p. 61), water system:
  - Most such Umayyad buildings had "מערכות מים מסועפות ורשת תיעול וניקוז" (branched water systems and a drainage and channel network), and links to agricultural sources.
  - "אף בח'רבת אל-מפג'ר מצויה מערכת כזו", indicating large-scale water use.
  - Its sources are **[one source and its distance withheld: Mafjar water source, spring]**, plus ʿEin Duk and ʿEin Nuʿeima "כ-4 ק"מ בכיוון מערב-צפון-מערב" (c. 4 km WNW).
  - Water reached the palace by aqueduct ("אמת-מים"). Several units are marked water consumers.
- EVIDENCE (p. 61), building history:
  - The complex was built in two stages: the NW unit first, then the southern group.
  - The palace was destroyed before completion by the earthquake of "747".
  - The main entrance is in the SE, to a forecourt with arcades on three sides.
- EVIDENCE (p. 62), water features:
  - On the W side of the building is a plastered underground unit, probably a mikveh combined with a cooling hall, "שמחזור מים הבטיח בו את צינון החדר" (circulating water cooled the room).
  - The bath hall (NW): on its S side a partition forms "מעין ברכה מטויחת" (a kind of plastered pool). There are heating arrangements of Roman bath type and a latrine hall.
  - The pavilion ("ביתן") in the centre of the great court had a floor "מטויחת, בצורה בלתי חדירה למים" (waterproof plaster). "הסתעפות תעלה ממערכת הספקת המים למכלול" (a branch channel from the complex's water supply) fed it. It was probably a cooling place.
- EVIDENCE (p. 63), plan "ח'רבת אל-מפג'ר (ארמון הישאם)":
  - numbered units 1–8 (8 marked "X");
  - north arrow pointing to the plan's left;
  - scale bar 0–40 metres;
  - spot heights including "-0.47", "-0.56", "-0.39", "-0.83" (relative datum).
  - The plan shows only the palace, mosque, bath and forecourt. No aqueduct course and no surroundings are shown.
- EVIDENCE (p. 64):
  - mosaics, including the "tree of life" (lion and gazelles) in the diwan;
  - the ceramic stratigraphy is not clear;
  - after the Umayyads the site lost importance, and later partial settlement is shown only by sherds.
  - n. 4: Ettinghausen, *From Byzantium to Sasanian Iran and the Islamic World* (Brill 1972) 17–66.
  - p. 65 is blank.

### INFERENCE
- The chapter gives no channel course, levels or relation of Mafjar to the plain beyond the c. 4 km WNW source statement and the aqueduct.
- The p. 63 plan cannot serve as a registration source for the strip: it does not show the tell. It could at most give RP-MEFJAR masonry corners if registered separately.
