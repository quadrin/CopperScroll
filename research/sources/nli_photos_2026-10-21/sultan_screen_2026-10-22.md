Mask boxes are in original-image pixels (after EXIF rotation), as `[x0, y0, x1, y1]`. Every later full-resolution view of a masked photo was made with the box filled solid black first (`work_sultan/scripts/crop.py`, `masked.py`). No mask was lifted.

  - EVIDENCE: labels outside the mask are ʿEin ʿAuja, Archelais, Qanat Musa, Wadi ʿAuja, ʿEnot Dok, Naʿaran, Kh. al Mafjar, Wadi Nueima, Jordan river, ʿEin Farah, ʿEin el Fawwar, ʿEin Qelt, Wadi el Qelt, "Jericho Palace", Kh. Mugheifir.
  - EVIDENCE: the aqueducts drawn are:
    - ʿEin ʿAuja → Archelais (curving east);
    - Qanat Musa from near ʿEin ʿAuja south to ʿEnot Dok / Naʿaran, then on south past the masked area to "Jericho Palace";
    - a branch from Naʿaran east to Kh. al Mafjar;
    - the Wadi Qelt line from ʿEin Farah via ʿEin el Fawwar and ʿEin Qelt to the palace;
    - a dashed aqueduct south of Wadi el Qelt to Kh. Mugheifir.
  - The caption above the map ends a key list with "39. Southern pair of cisterns (3-4)" and "40. Purifying installations of the modern aqueduct located above remains of ancient pool."
  - INFERENCE: the Naʿaran→Mafjar branch is the only drawn link between Mafjar and the spring systems.
- Aqueducts 2002 p. 320 (Amit, Cypros):
  - EVIDENCE: Fig. 9, "Section of the run-off aqueduct (point 37)"; Fig. 10, "The third cistern out of the four belonging to the fortress".
