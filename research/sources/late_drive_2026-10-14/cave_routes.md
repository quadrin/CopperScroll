## Frumkin2015

The nine-page publisher preview contains contents, legends, acknowledgements/authors and introduction. Its contents page / viewer2 places **מכלול התאומות**, the Twin complex, at printed212; the next entry,1Q, begins216. **Obtain printed212–215, with every plan, section, caption and adjacent/folded material**. This is an exact book retrieval target, not proof of the missing northern-threshold section or a verified correspondence to the earlier Bar-Adon cave. The preview contains no body of that entry.

The introduction / viewers8–9 says it principally selects important natural caves, generally longer than about25m, with other caves retained in the cave-research database; most small caves are omitted and artificial caves are largely excluded. Therefore absence from this atlas cannot establish regional absence. The supplied legends identify floor/ceiling steps, blocked continuation and schematic walls, useful for interpreting later plans; no target geometry was inspected.

## Itah–Kam–Ben-Haim2002

The supplied [official article](https://publications.iaa.org.il/atiqot/vol41/iss2/24), DOI10.70967/2948-040X.2584, is the English text printed169–175 plus portal cover: eight viewer pages. Maps/plans/figures are referenced back to the Hebrew part1 pp.175–186, and are absent from this short English file. Full searchable text is extracted; Table1 viewer4/p.171 was visually checked.

Table1 assigns the two Jones-excavated caves to **X/48,19339/12949**, and gives X/47 at19330/12954 a different adjacent-cave description. The existing Hebrew-based cave inventory note names X/47 for Jones. Preserve this as an edition/row correspondence conflict pending native Hebrew table comparison; do not silently recode the frozen inventory.

P.169 reports61 observed cave entrances, eight more thoroughly surveyed and seven excavated, with collapsed/inaccessible portions and partial results due to time. Those are coverage limits, not a systematic negative excavation of every cave. The prior Patrich survey was unpublished and unavailable to the authors. X/35 is the balsam-juglet cave, identified as Patrich/Arubas cave13/Q3; it is distinct from Jones's northern spice cave and does not independently verify a Temple-oil designation. X/42 had a roughly2m probe to bedrock along one wall but collapse prevented continued excavation; its negative finds cannot be generalized to the full chamber.
