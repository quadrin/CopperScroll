The rest of this long report was not read end to end. A text-search absence is not a negative excavation result.

**EVIDENCE / scope:** the contents gives detailed final-report chapters for Areas A/J/F/H/D/L. The methodology table (pp. 66–67, Table 3.1) assigns the stepped Siloam pool exposure to **Area N**, and the street/drainage excavation to K2. The title's **Area L** is a refuse ravine, about 150 m north of the Gihon spring (pp. 463–464), wet-sifted without issuing locus numbers. It is not the small receiving basin's L103 channel in Greenhut–Mazor 2020. The L103 label found elsewhere in the volume cannot establish identity across excavations.

**INFERENCE / what changes:** the acquired volume narrows the publication route and prevents a false area/locus crosswalk. It does not supply the required main-side L103 junction section. Its Siloam introductory descriptions and citations are contextual, not an observed aperture/basin-floor contact. The existing main-pool result remains closed until the specified new contact observation arrives; no new rejection or confirmatory identification follows. ## Elmakyes 2024 dissertation

[Original held copy](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1HaY8C0F_PVSkb_nIlh3iIt68DfCy-RB6/view). *Water Management Strategies in the Central Mountain Region of Israel from the Iron Age to the Byzantine Period*. 233 supplied PDF pages; 16,458,285 bytes; SHA-256 `8908dc0540b2e9958db10a2e9cefd0a2c8a1ce5356e97a5a4d86d6bb8ff8cff4`.

Scope: contents and methods/site-selection introductions (viewer 5–7, 35–40, 50–55), plus complete text, captions, notes and bibliography of the embedded Deir Sam'an article (viewer 73–88; article pp. 1–15 and publisher cover). Native detailed page inspection covered viewer 76, 78–80, 83 and 86. Other articles, synthesis and ordinary dissertation leaves remain partly screened or unread; no full-dissertation reading claim.

The embedded IEJ 2023 Kh. el-Hamam article is the same publication/observation lineage as the separately held original reviewed in [water evidence](water_evidence.md). Neither the dissertation reprint nor its Hebrew expanded discussion adds independent confirmation of the reservoir geometry or dated plaster.

**EVIDENCE / observed hydraulic relations:** the Deir Sam'an article's aerial and surveyed plan/sections (article pp. 3–6, Figs. 2–3, 5–6; dissertation viewer 76–79) distinguish the connected reservoir group 2/3/4 from the independently supplied reservoirs 5/6. The authors describe sequential gravity filling and an overflow channel from reservoir 4 to the fields. Fig. 5 is labelled a reconstruction; Fig. 3 supplies surveyed levels and contacts. Aerial caption calls the circular-installation feeder T1, while the prose on p. 4 calls it T2: retain the labelling discrepancy.

**EVIDENCE / modern observation:** methods and results (article pp. 7–10, dissertation viewer 80–83) describe cleaning surviving channels and measuring water levels in 2021; the results report about 60% of maximum capacity in that year. The statement that intact ancient arrangements would reach full capacity is a reconstruction. Modern working hydraulics do not independently date ancient construction or demonstrate continuous ancient use.

**AUTHOR INTERPRETATION / function and phase:** the covered/internal reservoirs as drinking supply and external pools as productive/agricultural supply is proposed from location, roofing and outgoing channels (pp. 11–12, viewer 84–85). On p. 6, current retention challenges an earlier inference that reservoir 5 ceased use because it lacks Byzantine replastering. This is a critique of an absence-based argument, not direct proof of Byzantine operation. Note 12 (p. 13, viewer 86) makes the early quarrying/replastering model conditional on plaster attribution. Building, plaster and operating phases remain separate.

**INFERENCE / what changes:** this supplies a documented comparison for testing connectedness, gravity direction, shared phases and function without assuming that adjacent pools formed one installation. It does not establish Atara's pool/channel relation, Hyrcania's inlet or measuring origin, or a Siloam main-basin junction. No candidate score, regional rarity denominator, search tolerance or deposit coordinate is updated.
