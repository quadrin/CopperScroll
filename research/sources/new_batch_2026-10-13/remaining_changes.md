# Remaining-source change review, 14 October 2026 UTC

Exploratory change review, with source-specific coverage recorded below. The whole batch has not been read end to end. All assigned originals verify against Drive byte sizes and have SHA256 hashes. Machine-readable text is indexed; selected contexts were inspected. Large volumes and several raster/Hebrew captures remain partial. No reserved XII10 or Tell es-Sultan observation was opened.

## Findings that change the next analysis

Enot Shoim retains a late-first-century existence branch. Huqoq catalogues pottery spanning wider intervals than the revolt named in its title. Maskana demonstrates Byzantine use with two surviving construction histories. Preserve construction, use and reuse as separate claims.

Ramat Rahel adds surveyed bath levels and phase relations. Its hoard already appears in project records; the report explicitly leaves its relation to hiding modifications uncertain. Tura and Husham add fortress hydraulic controls, with ambiguous upper installations and later repairs.

Rekhesh and Umm edh-Dhiyab supply cases where stepped installations belong to an earlier Iron Age installation or a later industrial vat. The Sinnabra pipeline acquired an Umayyad date after earlier Roman attribution. Function and date require context.

Qana supplies individual courtyard opening sides and map references. Roman Roads supplies older Beth Horon references. Neither gives the dated original threshold or exact scroll measuring origin needed for identification.

The Muraq capture now has a substantive visual Hebrew review, with its missing loading panels documented. The Hippos chapter body was read in Hebrew; its military and drainage interpretations remain separate from observed features. Qana is a repeat of a source already used in recovery benchmarks.
