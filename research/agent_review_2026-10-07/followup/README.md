# Follow-up to the agent review — 7 October 2026 (afternoon, Los Angeles)

This follow-up works the Koḥlit (R07) observations listed in [GOALS.md](../GOALS.md). The project owner supplied the sources: Dorrell 1993, Warren's two books, Mazar 1995, IEJ 30 Notes and News, *Highlands of Many Cultures*, Kenyon's *Jericho* II, and three CORONA frames. Three agents read the books in parallel. The coordinator did the imagery, the Kotar check and the outreach. Each claim is labelled EVIDENCE (what the source says or shows, with page) or INFERENCE.

**Outcome: no identification, confidence grade, coordinate or deposit changes. No outcome-ledger increment is claimed.** The balance of evidence on the last entry (entry 60) moves further toward Tell es-Sultan. The pool test (entry 11) is now weaker at both candidate sites.

## What this changes

**Entry 60 ("pit/cistern north of Koḥlit, graves at its mouth")**
- **Tell es-Sultan.** EVIDENCE (Kenyon II): Roman graves of the Qumran type lie in the northern cemetery. First-century burials reuse Bronze Age shafts north of the tell. A Roman cistern lies on the north side. INFERENCE: this fits the entry reasonably well. The unsearched strip just north of the tell is the best remaining target, and the refugee camp has covered it since 1948.
- **Kh. el-Marjama.** EVIDENCE: to the north there are only Bronze Age shaft-tomb fields (Dhahr Mirzbaneh). The only Roman/ossuary tombs are at Kh. Samiya, to the S/SE (*Highlands* p. 735).
- **Kh. Yanun.** Nothing beyond the place-name.

**Entry 11 ("the pool east of Koḥlit")**
- **Jericho.** EVIDENCE: a pool or spring-house has stood at the east foot of the tell since at least late antiquity. INFERENCE: no Herodian pool is shown, so this test no longer favours Jericho strongly.
- **Kh. el-Marjama.** No pool east of the tell is documented. Kallai's pool and the CORONA dark square remain unexplained.

**Net (INFERENCE):** Koḥlit is still open. The slight archaeological lean to Tell es-Sultan from wave 2 is a little stronger, mainly because of entry 60.

## Corrections to the review

- The Kenyon copy the owner holds is **Vol. II (1965)**, not Vol. III. Puech's citation (Kenyon 1981 pp. 173–174) remains unchecked.
- *Highlands* gives Kh. Marjama's periods from Kallai's 1972 survey only. The Roman category is undivided, and the team collected no sherds there (p. 732).
- Sources disagree on where Kh. Samiya lies: Mazar puts it east of the tell (p. 87), while the *Highlands* grid reference and photo put it ~0.4 km south (p. 734).
- In GOALS item 2, the SWP's "24 × 40 ft" pool was a broken, irregular basin edged with reused blocks. It is not the 1898 reservoir that stands today (Dorrell pp. 96–97, 111).
