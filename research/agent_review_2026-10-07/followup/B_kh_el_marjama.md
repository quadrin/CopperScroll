# B — Kh. el-Marjama / ʿEin Samiya: pool, periods and tombs (GOALS R07 item 3)

Agent report, 7 October 2026, saved by the coordinator. Labels: EVIDENCE / INFERENCE.

**Sources and page mapping**
- A. Mazar, "Excavations at the Israelite Town at Khirbet Marjameh in the Hills of Ephraim", *IEJ* 45 (1995) pp. 85–117. Scanned without a text layer; OCR'd with tesseract. IEJ page = PDF page + 82.

## 1. Water installations

- EVIDENCE (Zohar 219): The spring issues "from beneath the ancient remains". The sounding was about 20 m north of it.
  - "Formerly the water was collected in a basin beneath the crypt of the Byzantine church."
  - The church was "completely destroyed" when the pumping station was enlarged.
  - No size, construction or separate date is given for the basin. It is the only reservoir in the four files.
- EVIDENCE (*Highlands* 733, citing Kallai): a Byzantine church with a mosaic floor "at the foot of the tell on its western side", renovated in the Crusader period.
- EVIDENCE (Mazar 87, 95): The spring lies in the Wadi Samiyeh ravine below the 30 m cliff on the south side.
- INFERENCE: The coordinates put the spring about 210 m SW of the tell (bearing ~230°).
- EVIDENCE (Mazar 97; Fig. 16 p. 99; Fig. 7 p. 91): the mouth of a rock-cut bell-shaped cistern (Locus 20) on the summit, south of Building 10 near the southern cliff edge.
  - It was not excavated and is undated.
  - Cup-marks and a small oil press with a drainage channel lie nearby.
- EVIDENCE (Mazar 85): a perennial spring, and a fertile valley 1.5 × 0.2 km east of the site. Irrigation by channelling the spring is Mazar's own INFERENCE ("It thus seems plausible"); he describes no ancient channel or aqueduct.
- EVIDENCE (*Highlands* 744): el-Qasr monastery, ~1.2 km SE, Byzantine. Under the building is a vaulted space (4 × 3 × 2 m). In the NW corner is a "plastered wall with two arches covered with slabs". INFERENCE: possibly a cistern.
- **None of the four files describes Kallai's rock-cut pool and aqueducts.** *Highlands* summarises Kallai 1972 pp. 172–173 only as a church, pillars, pillar bases and bossed stones (733, 735).

## 2. Periods of occupation

- EVIDENCE (*Highlands* 732): The tell has size categories only, with no sherd counts. The team collected nothing there; the list rests on Kallai 1972 p. 172.

  | Period | Category |
  |---|---|
  | EB II–III | 3 |
  | MB | 4 |
  | LB | 3 |
  | IA I | 3 |
  | IA II | 4 |
  | Hellenistic | 2 |
  | Roman | 2 |
  | Byzantine | 2 |
  | Crusader/Ayyubid | 1 |

  - Category 2 means 0.4–1 ha (p. 20).
  - "Roman" is not split into Early and Late (p. 19).
  - No Persian period is listed, and there is no "Hell-Rom" (1st-century BCE jar rims) entry (period lists pp. 905–915).
- EVIDENCE (Mazar 90): Lapp's sherds include "Roman-Byzantine". Neither Mazar's summit excavation nor Zohar's trench reports anything later than the Iron Age. Zohar found 8th-century BCE material just under 20–30 cm of topsoil (219).
- EVIDENCE (*Highlands* 734–735): Kh. Samiya, 30 dunams. Roman Q (questionable), Byzantine 4, Byzantine/Umayyad 4, Early Ottoman 1; no pottery collected. Mazar (87) says Byzantine and possibly Early Arab.
- EVIDENCE: Site 18-15/15/05 (~0.3 km E, p. 741): IB 13 (52%), MB 5 (20%), IA I 4 (16%), unidentified 3 (12%). No Roman.
- EVIDENCE: Site 18-15/24/01 (~1.3 km SE, p. 743): Byzantine 24 (75%), Byz/Umayyad 6 (18.8%), Umayyad/Abbasid 2 (6.3%).

## 3. Tombs

- EVIDENCE (*Highlands* 735, citing the Mandatory antiquities file): at Kh. Samiya, three "Roman-Byzantine burial caves, with fragments of ossuaries", cleaned in 1942.
- INFERENCE: The ossuaries suggest Early Roman Jewish burial. This is the only Second Temple–type burial evidence in the files, and it lies S/SE of the tell, not north.
- EVIDENCE: Other tomb fields:
  - **North/NNE:** Dhahr Mirzbaneh, "hundreds of shaft tombs" of the Intermediate Bronze and MB (site 18-15/15/04, ~0.5 km at 33°; p. 741; Mazar 86).
  - **South:** ʿEin Samiya cemetery, ~0.7 km S: Intermediate Bronze, reused in MB, LB and IA I (p. 731).
  - **ESE, ~1–1.2 km:** about 40 and about 60 Intermediate Bronze shaft tombs (p. 745).
- No kokhim are mentioned anywhere.

## 4. The CORONA dark square (~31.9892 N, 35.3363 E; see [D](D_corona_1967.md))

- The agent's conversion (pyproj, EPSG:28193) gives about OIG 1819.2/1551.3, roughly 420 m from the tell at 131°. The projection choice should be checked against W2-B's EPSG:28191 note.
- Nearest recorded sites:
  - Kh. Samiya's grid point, ~250 m W.
  - Shaft tombs 18-15/14/02, ~230 m S.
  - Sherd scatter 18-15/15/05, ~270 m N.
- **No source describes a pool or reservoir there.** The published aerial photos (Mazar Fig. 2 p. 87; *Highlands* Figs 8.292–293 pp. 734–735) show irrigated plots and some dark rectangles or hollows, unlabelled.
- INFERENCE: The spot is at or near the eastern edge of Byzantine Kh. Samiya.
- Conflict between sources: Mazar puts Kh. Samiyeh east of the tell (87). The *Highlands* grid reference and photo put it ~0.4 km south (bearing 167°), across the wadi (734).

## Bottom line

- **"Pool east of Koḥlit": not supported by these files.** The only documented reservoir is the destroyed basin under a Byzantine church crypt at the spring, SW of the tell. Kallai's rock-cut pool remains unlocated and undated. Kallai 1972 pp. 172–173 and Zohar's Hebrew report (HA 76 [1981] p. 19) are needed.
- **Hellenistic and Roman on the tell:** listed (category 2) from old surface reports only. There is no excavated stratum, and Early vs Late Roman is not distinguished.
- **"Pit north with tombs at its mouth": no cistern is recorded north of the tell.** To the north and NE there are only Bronze Age shaft-tomb fields. The only Roman/ossuary tombs are at Kh. Samiya, S/SE of the tell.
