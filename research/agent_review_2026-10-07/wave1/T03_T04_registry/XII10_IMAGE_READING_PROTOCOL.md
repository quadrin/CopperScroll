Sources: Milik, DJD III pp. 240, 298–299; Lefkovits 2000 pp. 425–432, 451; Wolters, *Copper Scroll Studies*, pp. 319–320; Puech 2006 pp. 206, 216 and 2015 pp. 111–113. [The earlier plate check](../../../text/plate_check.md#xii-10-entry-60-janoah-q12) is exposed exploratory evidence.

Retain these complete strings, with independent component judgments:

- R1 `שכנה`: ש–כ–נ–ה, no additional yod/waw (Puech 2006; Wolters's printed reading).
- R2 `שבנה`: ש–ב–נ–ה, no additional yod/waw (Puech 1997/2002; alternative in 2006).
- R3 `שבצח`: ש–ב–צ–ח (Milik 1962).
- R4 `שבינח`: ש–ב–י–נ–ח; allow the source's proposed י–נ ligature (Lefkovits/Beyer).
- R5 `שכינה`: ש–כ–י–נ–ה (Allegro/Lurie as reported).
- R6 `שכונה`: ש–כ–ו–נ–ה (Allegro provisional manuscript as reported by Lefkovits p. 425).
- R7 `שבצהב`: ש–ב–צ–ה–ב (Pixner, reported by Lefkovits p. 426/Puech n. 504).

Wolters permits a nun alone or one preceded by yod/waw, and treats the short vertical as potentially accidental. Puech treats the extra mark as a double burin strike. Keep these physical explanations separate from a letter count. A duplicated incised stroke does not automatically constitute an additional letter.

For the letter at the cut, retain ה, ח, ת, or unresolved: Lefkovits explicitly permits he/ḥet/taw. A secure taw can contradict the complete strings above without licensing a newly invented word. Record an unanticipated sequence verbatim and close this specification; any expanded reading set requires a separately dated exploratory revision. Wilmot's dittography/deletion proposal concerns how an engraved string is used, and remains outside this letter-identity claim.

Correction to the historical wave-1 table: Milik's preliminary second word is `שכנה`; `בחלה` is the fourth word, as W2A reports from Lefkovits pp. 425–426. The historical table is preserved and must not supply a second-word `בחלה` branch.

Where fragments divide this context, document their ordered correspondence using neighboring text and unique physical anchors. Preserve gaps; never infer an engraved join across missing copper. Record ROI coordinates in the native frame; include a whole-line/context crop and the original full frame. A new scan's higher pixel count alone does not demonstrate added stroke information. Unknown physical scale prevents metric claims but need not prevent a qualitative letter judgment when comparable local strokes resolve.

## Evidence and independent reading

Preserve source bytes and hashes. Use the native image as primary evidence; enlargements and one declared linear contrast adjustment are aids, with identical processing for each series and the unadjusted view retained. No generative filling, sharpening-derived stroke, tracing, cast repair, editorial facsimile or classifier probability supplies the decisive observation. Register images from neighbors and physical anchors, never from the hypothesized target letter.

Two independent human readers with relevant epigraphic experience receive anonymous item codes, target-line markers, neighboring lines and images of comparable undisputed local letterforms. Record those comparators' identities and selection rule; include every legible comparator in the supplied local context rather than selecting ones that favor an alternative. Withhold edition names, proposed sites, model scores and the project's prior verdicts. Record each reader's prior exposure and per-item recognition; blinding is limited when the line is recognized.

Stage 1: save a free transcription, spaces, uncountable gaps, and grades `certain / probable / possible / trace only / illegible`, with native coordinates and descriptions of grooves, cracks, light artifacts and damage. Freeze it before Stage 2. Stage 2 asks only: box-letter identity ב/כ/other; נ versus צ versus י/ו+נ (including a ligature); whether any extra mark is incised and whether it forms an independent sign; final ה/ח/ת/other; and whether additional signs or cancellation marks are visible before the next word. Each answer cites the specific native frame and feature. Keep disagreements and later revisions; reconciliation cannot overwrite the independent answers. AI inspection may be exploratory but does not substitute for this primary human rule.

An observed mark becomes a decisive letter component only when both readers grade its identity **certain**, local comparators resolve the relevant distinctions, and the feature is visible in the unadjusted original. A suspected groove/scratch/lighting ambiguity requires a separately captured original view or demonstrably informative radiograph that resolves it. Copies and rescans of one exposure add no second observation. Conflicting usable original views leave that component unresolved. No pixel-width, depth or probability threshold is asserted here; none has been calibrated for these future frames.

Absence becomes decisive only when both readers find the entire expected region intact and readable at a scale that resolves comparable small local strokes, with no shadow, glare, fold, corrosion or cut capable of hiding the required component. A mark's failure to appear in a damaged gap counts as unknown. A cut-damaged component is excluded from decisive scoring unless an authenticated earlier original image actually shows the relevant copper before that loss. Unknown before/after chronology cannot restore it.

## Deterministic outcomes

Score each complete string against **all** its components and publish every surviving alternative:

1. **Rejected:** at least one required component conflicts with a decisive observed identity or a decisive absence under the gates above. Name the contradictory component and frames. This rejects only that engraved sequence.
2. **Supported relative to the registered alternatives:** all components are decisive and the string alone survives R1–R7; no unresolved extra sign or cut-letter component remains. Shared components can be supported individually while whole-word identification remains open.
3. **Not identifiable from available evidence:** every other case, including multiple surviving strings, uncertain registration, unavailable human scoring, inadequate resolution, unresolved damage, disagreement or ambiguous extra marks. If all seven strings conflict, reject the finite set and report the observed sequence without promoting an unregistered interpretation.

These outcomes refer to letter identity. Even R4 support would require a separate linguistic argument for the place-name interpretation and a separate geographic test. Retain the existing archaeological L0–L4/MISS fields unchanged. The earlier plate verdict remains historical exploratory evidence; this freeze adds no identification, confidence or outcome-ledger increment.
