## 4. Draft letter A — University of Manchester Library (Special Collections)

Subject: Enquiry about GB 133 TPA/1/132 ("File: Dead Sea Scrolls", 1955–1966) and photographs of the 1955–56 cutting of the Copper Scroll

Dear Special Collections team,

I am writing on behalf of an independent research project on the Copper Scroll from Qumran Cave 3 (3Q15). The scroll was cut into 23 segments at the Manchester College of Science and Technology in 1955–56 by Professor H. Wright Baker. We are trying to locate any photographs, drawings or notes made during the cutting, because several disputed letters lie on or next to the saw cuts and the 1950s record shows the metal before later losses.

The Archives Hub entry for **GB 133 TPA/1/132** (UMIST, Principal's Archive, "File: Dead Sea Scrolls", 1955–1966, 1 folder) describes correspondence between John Allegro, G. Lankester Harding and Wright Baker about the arrangements for the cutting. Could you tell us:

1. whether this file contains, or refers to, any photographs, negatives, drawings, measurements or reports of the cutting (for example, records of which cut was made where, or of fragments that came away);
2. whether any other Library collection holds material from Wright Baker on this work (for example, the University of Manchester Faculty of Science Archive, GB 133 FSC, which an Archives Hub search for his name returns, or the UMIST Department of Mechanical Engineering records);
3. whether the Library holds any papers of John M. Allegro himself, or whether you can confirm that his photographic archive is held by the Manchester Museum;
4. how we could consult the file (reading-room visit, or a quotation for digital copies of selected pages), and what the terms and charges would be.

We would only want study copies at first, and we will not publish any image without the Library's written permission and the acknowledgement you specify. Please let us know the cost before carrying out any work; we have not authorised any fees.

With thanks and best wishes,
[Name], on behalf of the Copper Scroll research project (https://github.com/quadrin/CopperScroll)
[Contact details]

## 6. Why this matters for the reading questions (for the coordinator)

- Several disputed letters in `tables/plate_check.csv` lie by saw cuts (e.g. IX 7, entry 40: the direction word "stands on the far side of a saw cut", per `research_text_plate_check.md`). Pre-cut or cut-stage photographs could show strokes since lost. INFERENCE (medium): this is the only route to the metal's state before the EDF conservation of the 1990s other than the DJD III plates.
- The "Baker drawing" cited via Lefkovits for ΤΡΙ (III 7) suggests Wright Baker or his team drew letters; its custodian is unknown (see `phase4_greek_letters.csv`). Item 2 of letter A asks about it indirectly.
