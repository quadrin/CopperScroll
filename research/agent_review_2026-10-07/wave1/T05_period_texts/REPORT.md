# T05 — Period texts: attestation concordance

Agent report, 6 October 2026, saved by the coordinator. Labels: EVIDENCE / INFERENCE.

## What was done
Searched the Hebrew Bible, Targums, Mishnah, Tosefta, both Talmuds and midrash (Sefaria API, 78 Hebrew/Aramaic forms); the Septuagint and 1 Maccabees (Rahlfs); Josephus in Niese's Greek; Eusebius's Onomasticon (Wolf translation); the Madaba map labels; the Rehov inscription; the Baraita of the Boundaries; the Bordeaux Pilgrim; Ben Sira; 1QapGen; 4Q522. Result: 69 checked rows (8 are INFERENCE) in `data/attestation_concordance.csv`.

## Key findings
1. **Koḥlit: b. Qiddushin 66a, missing from the repo.** EVIDENCE, high: "King Yannai went to Koḥalit in the wilderness and conquered sixty towns there." (Coordinator check, 6 Oct 2026: Sefaria's William Davidson English reads "King Yannai, who went to the region of Koḥalit in the desert and conquered sixty cities there.") INFERENCE, low–medium: if this is the scroll's כחלת, Koḥlit is a desert district, not a Jericho tell, which weakens Tell es-Sultan. The spellings differ, so the link is unproven. Whether Copper Scroll scholarship has already made this link was not checked.
2. **1QapGen XXII 13–14 equates Shaveh with the valley of Beth-hakkerem.** EVIDENCE, read through Newman, Biblica 86 (2005) p. 221. INFERENCE, low–medium: entries 36–37 and 46 form one district south of Jerusalem, favouring Puech over Milik (repo question Q22). Josephus AJ 7.243 instead places the King's Valley 2 stadia from Jerusalem.
3. **Bet Tamar.** EVIDENCE: Eusebius nos. 268 and 698 put Beththamar near Geba; 1 Sam 14:5 calls the crag at the Michmash pass *māṣûq*. INFERENCE, low: the Michmash gorge is a new candidate for "the gorge of Beth Tamar", but it breaks Puech's sequence.
4. **Ben Sira 50:3 (Genizah MS B) uses אשיח for a Temple reservoir.** EVIDENCE, medium; the repo's lexicon has zero attestations of the word. Ezra 8:33 and 1 Macc 8:17 show the Hakkoz family handling Temple silver and gold, but no text locates their estate.
5. **Negative results.** EVIDENCE: no independent witness to Sekakah (Josh 15:61 only), ʿAṣla, a bare "ha-Melaḥ", or Kozeba in Wadi Qelt. Most rabbinic "matches" are common nouns. Correction: the Doq passage is BJ 1.56 in Niese, not 1.54.

**Moves an identification?** No. The shortlist (21, 31, 32, 49) is unaffected apart from the Doq reference.

## Blocked
DJD II (Murabbaʿat) is lending-restricted on archive.org, so Mur 45 "Meṣad Ḥasidin" is unverified. Rehov, Madaba, 1QapGen and Jerome were checked only through secondary sources.
