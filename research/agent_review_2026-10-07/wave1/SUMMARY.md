# Copper Scroll: first pass on the 19 tasks

6 October 2026. Nine parallel agents worked the ten desk tasks and drafted packets for the nine partner and field tasks. Nobody was contacted and nothing was posted. Each folder has a REPORT.md, its data files and the scripts that produced them; raw downloads were left out of this bundle (they are public sources, re-fetchable by the scripts).

**Bottom line: no hiding place or individual landmark was identified.** Several results narrow the problem, one corrects the existing project, and three separate lines of evidence point away from the current best guess for Koḥlit.

## The convergence worth following: Koḥlit is probably not Tell es-Sultan

- **Text (T05):** a baraita in b. Qiddushin 66a says King Yannai "went to the region of Koḥalit in the desert and conquered sixty cities there" (checked on Sefaria). If this is the scroll's כחלת, Koḥlit is a desert district, not old Jericho's mound. The spelling differs slightly, and whether Copper Scroll scholars have already used this passage was not checked; the CopperScroll repo does not cite it.
- **Order (T06):** a joint model of the list's order pulls the last entry (the "copy of this document" at Koḥlit) away from Tell es-Sultan (0.30 → 0.10) and north (0.07 → 0.71). The atlas rejected other Koḥlit proposals because they "break the order", but that argument only works if entries 1–19 are in geographic order, and the repo's own test says they aren't.
- **Landscape (T09):** if Koḥlit were the tell, entry 60's ground north of it has been built over by ʿEin es-Sultan camp since 1948, so 1918 aerial photos are the only surface record left.
- **Tension:** the registry agent (T03/T04), working without these results, ranked Tell es-Sultan's north cemetery as the top target for entry 60. It should be re-ranked.
