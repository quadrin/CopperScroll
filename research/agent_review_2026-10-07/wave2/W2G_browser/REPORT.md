## 1. DQCAAS Copper Scroll material
- The URL given (/photographic-collections/copperscroll/) is John Allegro's 16 mm film of H. Wright Baker's second cut, 3 Oct 1955 (first cut 1 Oct 1955), ~1.2 min, from Judith Brown (Allegro's daughter); copyright; use via DQCAAS (Prof. Joan Taylor).
- The photographs are on /allegro-manchester-copper-scroll/, catalogue "C" (numbering as in Brooke & Bond, *The Allegro Qumran Collection*, Brill 1996): 296 images — C.1A–C.23A (each of the 23 cut strips), side-by-side strip groups (e.g. C.21,23; C.21–23), C.OPEN.49 (all strips in two trays), C.UNOPENED, and C.TRANSCRIPT.1–12 (facsimile drawings, one per column, cut lines dashed). Manchester 1955–56, attributed to Allegro.
- All images ~720 × 577 px ("low resolution"); teaching/non-commercial use with acknowledgement of the Allegro estate and Manchester Museum. High resolution: collections@manchester.ac.uk; DQCAAS-digitised items via Prof. Joan Taylor. Credit: "©Allegro Estate, courtesy of DQCAAS".
- INFERENCE, high: the strip photos are too small to read letters, XII 10 included. The 12 facsimile drawings are legible:
  - XII 10–13 drawn as בשית שבצפון כחלת פתחא צפון / וקברין עליה כ שנא הכתב הזא / ופרושה ומשחותיו ופרט כ[ל] / אחד ואחד (first letter of כחלת ambiguous between כ and ב in the drawing).
  - VII 11 drawn as בדוק תחת פנת המשטח, with a clear ט, then המזרחית חפור אמות שבע.
  - X 13–17: letters before שלח ambiguous in the drawing. IX 7 legible but letter forms uncertain.
  - All seven Greek groups are drawn (I 4 ΚΕΝ, I 12 ΧΑΓ, II 2 ΗΝ, II 4 ΘΕ, II 9 ΔΙ, III 7 ΤΡ, IV 2 ΣΚ).
  - These are historical drawn readings (draughtsman not named), not photographs.

## 3. HUJI GeoHub
- A box 31.70–32.03 N, 35.29–35.50 E returned 266 aerial photos: RAF PS29 (14 Mar 1945), PS30 (15 Mar 1945), PS32 (16 Apr 1945), one PS26 — No. 680 Squadron RAF, "F12 / TOPO 15000" (12-inch lens, 1:15,000) — plus three "AC 1934–1937" frames. No WWI frames in this box.
- Online IIIF images are ~700–750 px (~4–5 m/pixel, INFERENCE). The department scanned pre-1948 frames at 600 dpi; high-resolution images are sold via the site's cart (not opened). Strips overlap ~60% (stereo pairs exist).
- Catalogue centre points are "geo-located (but not geo-coded)" and place-name subjects look auto-assigned. Example: PS29 6146 is labelled "Khirbet Qumran" but shows a meandering incised wadi. Check every frame by eye before ordering.
- Frames by target (full list in huji_geohub_aerials_jericho_qumran.csv):
  - Tell es-Sultan and the ground north: PS30 5107 (0.5–0.6 km; subjects include "Ein as-Sultan (Refugee Camp)"), PS30 5137; parallel strip PS32 5069/5070.
  - ʿEin Samiya / Kh. el-Marjama (spring at 31.9905 N, 35.3313 E per OSM): PS32 5038 "Khirbet el-Marjama", PS32 5039, PS30 6054 ("Al-Qasr / עין סמיה"), PS30 6055.
  - Qumran: PS29 6140 (Qumran probably near its lower-left edge), 6139, 6107/6108 (ʿEin Feshkha shore); 6144–6146 location doubtful.
  - Qarantal / ʿAin Duk: PS30 5108, 5109, 5136.
  - At 725 px, pits, tombs, pools, Kh. Qumran and the aqueduct cannot be resolved.

## 4. Bavarian WWI frames (BayHStA "BS Pal.")
- 1032 (22 Jun 1918, "Tell es-Sultan … und nördliche Umgebung bis Wadi en-Nueme …"), 1034, 838, 901, 980: analog only, no digital image; held in BayHStA Abt. IV Kriegsarchiv, "Bildsammlung Palästina". Reproductions must be requested from the Kriegsarchiv (fees per the GDA Gebührenverzeichnis; publication needs an "Antrag Veröffentlichungsgenehmigung"). Reproduction-order pages returned 404/403. Nothing ordered.
- BS Pal. 1031 (2 Jul 1918), north of the tell (contrast-enhanced crops): a camp of ~16 bell tents; two small walled enclosures; a walled compound ~35 × 30 m with internal cells NW of the tell (possibly the Tawahin es-Sukkar mills, INFERENCE); tracks to the Jericho–Beisan road; dotted linear features (channels with shrubs or trenches); a cut or spoil heap just N of the tell; scattered shrubs. No clear rows of pits or tomb openings.
- BS Pal. 1033 (22 Jun 1918): a polygonal walled enclosure on the Qarantal summit (INFERENCE: the late-Ottoman Orthodox enclosure over Dok/Byzantine remains); ʿAin Duk gardens with tents, a contour channel/aqueduct, a building row; a long E–W aqueduct line; circular features (threshing floors or cisterns).
- No frame names ʿEin Samiya (offline index of 2,930 records and live DDB search: 0 hits). Nearest: 568/569 "north of Kafr Malik" (5 Jun 1918, viewable) — terraced upland; spring not identifiable; worth checking against a map.
