## 2. Duplicate links (`dup_of`)

`scripts/build_survey.py` `dedup()` (about lines 277–286) links by site number. Two rules there do not hold:

- **Hirschfeld.** Any `Hirschfeld \d{4}, Site N` goes to ASI 108/2 site N. But WBADB cites two Hirschfeld surveys: Hirschfeld 1985 (= ASI Map 108/2, Herodium) and Hirschfeld 1983 (Jericho and Jordan valley monasteries, 45 WBADB rows).
  - Two rows were wrongly merged: `WBADB-S2313-aqueduct` Zawayat → `ASI-108/2/28` ʿEin es-Sakhari (37.5 km apart; −377 m against +436 m), and `WBADB-S2514-reservoir_pool` Nusib ʿUweishira → `ASI-108/2/17` Kh. Khureitun (28.9 km). Zawayat therefore drops out of the 1,437 merged units.
  - Nine more Hirschfeld 1983 rows carry a false note "same site as ASI 108/2/N (ref)". The reference link skipped the name-and-300 m test, so these rows were never compared with the right ASI record: Kypros S2589 ×3, Tell el-Qasab S2479 ×2, Andrea Church S2522, Tulul Abu el-ʿAlaiq S2539, Penthucla S2732 and S2601.
- **Kloner [101].** `Kloner \d{4}, Site [101] N` goes to ASI-online record 101/N. But ASI-online Map 101 numbers its records in its own sequence and gives the book number in brackets. Six rows are linked to records 4.1–6.8 km away; the right records lie 7–100 m away: [7] = 101/68, [8] = 101/69, [25] = 101/86, [34] = 101/95.
- **Suggested script change (not applied):** apply the Hirschfeld rule only to "Hirschfeld 1985"; for Kloner [101], match on the bracketed book number in the ASI record name, not on the record sequence. Card C3-1; review R5. Every `dup_of` link with its distance and GLO-30 heights: `research/sidequest_2026-11-06/C3/water_dup_of_link_audit.csv`.
- **Effect (INFERENCE, C3):** no clause count changes for certain. Zawayat returns as a unit, and the Nuseib pool is counted twice (WBADB E360 and HA 59-60).

## 3. Flagged, value not in hand

- **Lower Herodium.** WBADB S4287 "Lower Herodium" carries the grid of the hilltop palace-fortress (WBE:831, 172900/119300). GLO-30 puts that point at about 729 m on the upper cone; Lower Herodium lies at the foot, at about 630–650 m.
- The rows that inherit this point are `WBADB-S4287-cistern`, `WBADB-S4287-reservoir_pool`, `LIT-NET01-LH-POOL`, `LIT-NET01-LH-BATHPOOL`, and the destination point of `LIT-MAZ-HERODIUM-AQ`. So the aqueduct's recorded end sits about 80–95 m above the pool it fed.
- The correct position needs Netzer 1981 (Qedem 13) or the Netzer 2001 plans. Card C3-2; review R5.

## 4. Source note (no field affected)

- ASI Map 109/4 (Wadi Qelt) online prints "Elevation asl" without the minus sign for 18 sites below sea level (St George/Choziba, the Choziba cells, Naḥal Perat 7–9, ʿEin Qelt (1) and others).
- `water.csv` has no elevation column, so no coded value changes. Any later use of these elevations must flip the sign. Card D2-2; review R6; `research/sidequest_2026-11-06/D2/asi_elevation_vs_glo30.csv`.
