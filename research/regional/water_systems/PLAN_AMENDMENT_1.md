# Amendment 1: operational details (written before any row was coded)

Written 2026-10-11 about 04:20 UTC (10 October Los Angeles), after the plan commit `0b07fad`. **Seen since the freeze:** the eleven ASI GeoJSON files were fetched; the structure of one feature (map 108/2, site 6) was printed to learn the field names. No WBADB component text, no other ASI record and no literature page had been read. These details fill gaps in the frozen plan; they change no clause, branch, tolerance or decision rule.

1. **Plan date.** The plan's title says 10 October UTC. The commit was made 2026-10-11T04:10:20Z, which is 10 October in Los Angeles.
2. **ASI text fields.** In the English GeoJSON the fields are `name_heb` (name), `description_heb` (description), `finding_heb` (finds) and `bibliography_heb` (bibliography); despite the key names they hold the English text. There is no "additional names" field and no period list in the GeoJSON. Bibliography is not read. Records with an empty name are named "ASI map/site".
3. **Place operationalisation for the conditional columns** (names are matched in the row's own text or, for literature, in the source's words about the row; distances from `places_v2.csv` points):
   - **Q (Sekakah = Qumran / Wadi Qumran / Buqeiʿa):** MATCH if the text names Qumran, Sekakah/Secacah or the Buqeiʿa (Buqeia, Buqei'a, Bouqeia), or the point lies within 1 km of `kh_qumran` or `wadi_qumran`, or within 2.5 km (its sigma) of `buqeia`. FAIL if the point lies outside all three. UNKNOWN if unplaced.
   - **Q-Jericho (oasis):** MATCH if the text names Jericho, Tell es-Sultan, ʿAin es-Sultan, Tulul Abu el-ʿAlayiq, Naʿaran / ʿAin Duk / Nuʿeima, Doq / Dagon / Qarantal, Cypros / Kypros / Tell el-ʿAqaba or Kh. el-Mafjar, or the point lies within 2 km of `tell_es_sultan`, `jericho_palaces`, `doq` or `ain_duk`. FAIL if located beyond. UNKNOWN if unplaced.
   - **Q-Cypros:** names only (Cypros, Kypros, Tell el-ʿAqaba, Tel el Akaba); `places_v2.csv` has no Cypros point. Otherwise FAIL if located, UNKNOWN if unplaced.
   - **Q-Kidron:** MATCH if the text names Kidron, Qidron, Wadi en-Nar or Nar as the supply of the row or of its conduit. Otherwise FAIL if the source names another supply, else UNKNOWN.
   - **K-Qelt:** the names in plan §5 plus Wadi Fara/Farah, Nahal Prat/Perat, Mar Jiryis, St George; FAIL only if the source names another wadi for the row.
   - Name matching ignores case, diacritics and the ʿ/' marks.
4. **Survey "feed phrase" (entry 45, channels and wadi).** Implemented as two regular expressions in `scripts/common.py`: a feed verb within 80 characters before a channel word, or a channel word followed within 60 characters by to/into/feeding/filling/supplying and within 40 more by cistern/reservoir/pool. Wadi: a channel sentence that also has "from the wadi/nahal/stream/streambed/flood".
5. **Literature coordinates.** Under plan §6 rule (2), "the WBADB or ASI record of the same named site" is chosen by the coder by name, and the record id is given in the row's coordinate source. Under rule (3), the `places_v2.csv` points used are those listed above plus `hyrcania`, `mar_saba`, `natuf`, `ramat_rahel`, `jer_siloam`, `choziba`, `tell_el_qos`, `nuweimeh`.
6. **Survey sentence splitting.** Sentences are split at ". ", "; ", "! ", "? " and line breaks, after removing HTML tags; the abbreviations "ca.", "c.", "Kh.", "H.", "Ḥ.", "N.", "St.", "km.", "m." and "cf." do not end a sentence.
