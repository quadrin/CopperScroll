These are count-level compatibility leads. “Match” covers only the clauses scored by the stated branch. The complete machine-readable [surviving_leads.csv](surviving_leads.csv) records every surviving branch, original source pages/URLs, manually confirmed clauses, other coded clauses, aggregate status, missing fields and the next observation that could reject or discriminate it. It also retains the three dated entry-29 near misses from the earlier coding; subsequent native-source audits must be read alongside that historical overlay. The [parallel bath audit](../../../assessments/entry46_baths_2026-10-12/README.md) supplies original-page readings beyond this earlier-ledger correction.
