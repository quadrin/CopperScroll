# Amendment 1 to the caves_entry25 plan: coding calls made after the freeze

Written 11 October 2026 (UTC), after PLAN.md was frozen (commit 2ff6e5a, 04:07:54Z) and while records were being coded. PLAN.md is not edited. Every choice below was made after records were read, so each is **exploratory** (AGENTS.md, "Freeze before confirmatory evidence"). None changes the clause definitions, the aspect rule, the region, the duplicate distance or the rule sets. Where a choice touches a row the README names, the row is listed.

## Coding of words and drawings

1. **Position words read as facings.** A record that puts an opening "in the eastern wall" or a window "to the north" is coded as that facing word (IV/1 northern opening E; VI/45 E and W; 1952 site 33 E and N). A position "in the eastern corner" is not (ASI 109/4 site 104). INFERENCE.
2. **What is not a pillar.** A stone partition (VI/53), a drawn but unnamed free-standing rock mass (X/51, Plan 2), a rock column on the ledge outside a cave (lower Wadi Makkuk Cave 1, ESI 7-8 p. 93), pillars that carry a wall in front of a cave (Mar Saba Complex 34) and modern roof posts (Choziba Cell 10) are coded unknown, not match. PLAN 4.3 reads "standing in the cave or between its openings". These are the lane's near misses for B-PILLAR.
3. **What is a terrace.** Natural ledges are not (PLAN 4.3). Coded match: courtyard, forecourt, porch ("porche", 1952 site 27), platform ("plate-forme", 1952 site 37), a built open surface or a retaining wall forming a level surface in front of the mouth (Choziba Cell 4; 'Ein Gedi site 180 cave 2), a terrace or vegetable-garden terrace built in front (Choziba Cells 11, 16), the nickname 'Terrace Cave' alone (II/61, weak). A terrace built inside a cave (ASI 147 site 412) is not coded.
4. **Openings in built closing walls.** Doors and windows in a wall built across a cave front are not counted as the cave's openings; n_openings is left unknown (Choziba Cell 1; ASI 109/4 site 31; ASI 147 site 415 cave 2; Laura of Jeremias Cell 10 cave; Mar Saba Complex 34).
5. **XII/53.** "The opening ... was divided in two by a pillar (W2); its southern part was blocked by a large boulder" is coded as two openings (PLAN 4.3: a blocked opening counts) with a northern and a southern member. The hall's opening "faces east" is read as a cave-level word. INFERENCE for the count.
6. **N_min results.** PLAN 4.2 defines N_min but not how a merged group is scored. A group counts once; its result is the best of its members (all-match, then unknown, then fail), and per clause the best member value.
7. **Clusters.** A statement that covers every member of a counted group is applied to the cluster row (WBADB Surveyed 4225, "three caves, fronted by terraces").
8. **Plan use.** Plans were opened for counts and for the PLAN 4.3 pillar fail rule only where the result could change a row that already had two openings or a pillar/terrace: II/3, IV/1 and VII/3 (no pillar drawn: C2p fail), VI/46 (one mouth: C3 fail), X/51 (see 2). Other published plans were not opened (a coverage limit, listed in the README).
9. **Plan measurement constants.** Arrow endpoint e = 2 px on the raster, as in the repository's IV/17 measurement (rules.json allows 0.5 mm at print or 2 px). The XII/53 and IV/17 interior points used to orient the chord normals are inferred (coded/plan_measurements.json).

## Sources and merging

10. **Atiqot 41 Region X.** On p. 176 the per-row notes and sizes are offset by one row from the numbers; the grid column is used, and the note 'two caves excavated by Vendyl Jones' is assigned to X/47 (WBADB S3703 'Two Caves X/47').
11. **Misprinted grids.** II/19, XI/19, XII/52, XII/53 and XIII/14 keep their printed grids but are placed in the region by description and are left out of the 50 m test.
12. **Index.** The Index's permit numbers replace text-layer note assignments where they differ (XI/13 L-642, XI/18 L-605, XII/53 L-608, XIII/8 L-661). The Index's hand fixes are listed in scripts/intake/a41_index_3_clean.py.
13. **ASI admission.** Records that name caves only in passing are excluded. Caves used as reservoirs, churches or cells are admitted. For the Mar Saba Great Laura, the cave rows are the complexes the source types as caves (types 1a, 1c, 1d, 2a) plus complexes whose description names a cave.
14. **Patrich survey.** The 30 numbered caves of the Na'aran-Jericho cliff (ESI 7-8 p. 93) are added though they almost certainly repeat Atiqot 41 Regions IV-VIII; no crosswalk exists, so they stay in N_rows and N_min.
15. **WBADB.** Rows citing an inventoried source by author-year (the Atiqot 41 region authors, Patrich 1994a for the Mar Saba map, Bar-Adon 1989) are crosswalks only; the citation patterns are in scripts/intake/wbadb_extract.py. Rows in both workbook sheets are one row. Precision is half the rounding unit of X/Y.
16. **Same-label flags.** 'Q3' is used by ESI 6 for the Copper Scroll cave (1952 no. 8) and by Atiqot 41 p. 180 for Patrich and Arubas Cave 13 (X/35); WBADB has a 'Cave Q-3' (Jones 1978). The three are flagged as possible duplicates under the proper-name rule; the conflict is reported, not resolved.
17. **Exposure after the audit.** XII/49, XII/52 and XII/53 are reclassified 'exposed-repo' because tables/phase5_reports.csv holds their report titles and one-line period summaries; their openings, pillar and facing were first read here. X/47 is 'exposed-repo' through a WBADB quotation in the Koḥlit Stage 2 packet E754.
18. **s_pillar_between.** The position of each coded pillar was classified by hand (scripts/score.py, PILLAR_BETWEEN).
