Read because the frozen source L5 (Netzer 2001, *Palaces*) points to the Jericho towers, and a keyword search of the file had already shown those lines. ## B. Coding rules clarified while coding

1. **Mixed date labels.** A columbarium dated by a label wholly inside the window is D, even when another label for it overlaps or lies outside (WBADB E785 "Hel and Rom1 columbarium cave"). Kh. Kakul's columbarium is D because the excavation record lists it under its "Late Hellenistic – Early Roman" phase.
2. **Rows.** A record that names several columbaria without telling them apart gives one row with `n_columbaria` null. Columbaria the source describes separately get separate rows. A second source for the same columbarium is kept as a row with `dup_of` and is not counted.
3. **Setting "isolated".** A columbarium on a slope, cliff or valley with no built site in its record, or in a burial-cave or quarry context, is "isolated". This is what PLAN §2 C44-F means by "an isolated cave, rock face, quarry or cemetery away from any built site".
4. **Fort clause.** At survey level a record that names a fort, fortress, fortification or stronghold at the same site is a MATCH even when the columbarium lies outside it (co-occurrence, as frozen). At text level it is a FAIL when the source places the columbarium outside the fort.
5. **Access.** "Steps lead (down) to the floor" of a pit, cistern or cave, a shaft, and an opening in the ceiling count as "way down from above". One access opening "in the center (the middle of the cross)" of a cruciform cave (109/368) is read as a ceiling opening: INFERENCE, flagged `check_flag = yes`. A ceiling opening that the source does not call the entrance, where the source names a side entrance as the original one (109/326a), is UNKNOWN.
6. **Reconstructions and assumptions.** A published reconstruction drawing (Netzer's Fig. 100 for the Masada towers) and an excavator's stated assumption ("We assume that in all these cases", *Palaces* p. 14: a dovecote on the ground floor, a watchtower above) are coded as the source's description for storeys and access, with the basis written in `lev_basis` / `access_basis`. The access code for the Masada towers is this worker's reading of the drawing (no ground-floor door; an outside ladder to an upper door; openings between levels): INFERENCE, flagged for checking.
7. **Text-only mentions** (Josephus, *Jewish War* 5.181, "cots for tame pigeons" in Herod's Jerusalem palace) are not rows: no remains are recorded.
8. **Parts of a site.** For S-C, "southeast" and "southwest" are UNKNOWN; "north", "east", "west" are FAIL; "south" is MATCH.
9. **Uncertain identifications** ("columbarium?", "possibly a columbarium", "columbarium-like") are rows with `uncertain_identification = yes`. "Niches resembling those of columbaria" (104/49) is a comparison and is excluded (`data/excluded.csv`).
