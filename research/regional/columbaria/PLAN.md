# Columbaria (dovecotes) in Judea, scored against entries 38 and 44: frozen plan

Written 11 October 2026 UTC (10 October Los Angeles) by the columbaria lane worker, before any feature record was screened, read or coded. Exploratory regional inventory and count. It makes no identification claim and changes no registered outcome. Rules: `AGENTS.md`, the shared lane rules of 10 October, and the Koḥlit rarity precedent (`research/preregistration/kohlit_rarity_2026-10-08.md`).

Labels: EVIDENCE (the source says it), INFERENCE (our reasoning), CLAIM (an author's assertion without a field record), BK (background knowledge, not from a source read here).

## 1. Question

Within a fixed region, how many recorded columbaria (dovecotes) satisfy each clause of entry 38 (IX 1–3) and entry 44 (IX 17–X 2), how many fail, and how many are unknown? The answer is "k match, f fail, m unknown, of N" per entry, reading branch and level. It measures how common each combination is in the record. It cannot identify a site (§9).

Clauses of 44-D:
- **C44-F (in the fortress).** Survey level: the same source record names, at the same site, both the columbarium and a fort, fortress, citadel, stronghold, castle or fortification (Hebrew מצד, מצודה, מבצר, ביצור, or the source's own English). Text level: the source places the columbarium inside the fort (within its walls, in one of its towers, or in a rock-cut space under it). FAIL at both levels: the source places the columbarium outside any fortification (explicitly outside the fort, or in an isolated cave, rock face, quarry or cemetery away from any built site). A village or farm with no fort mentioned is UNKNOWN. Sensitivity F+: a tower (a free-standing columbarium tower, or a tower or "migdal" at the site) also counts, because Puech (n. 316) allows a tower-shaped dovecote.
- **C44-S (in the south).** S-A, S-B1, S-B2 from the row's point; S-C from the source's words or plan (MATCH: the columbarium lies in the southern part of the fort or site; FAIL: in another named part; else UNKNOWN).
- **C44-L (storeys).** L2 for V-P, L3 for V-T. MATCH: the source describes the columbarium, or the building or rock-cut complex that holds it, with at least two (L2) or three (L3) superposed storeys, floors or levels joined by stairs, ladders or shafts. FAIL: the source describes it as a single chamber or a single-storey structure, or gives a section with one level. UNKNOWN otherwise. Rows of niches are not levels. Recorded at text level only: which level the niches occupy (V-T wants an upper storey; V-P the second level from the top).
- **C44-A (its way down from above).** MATCH: the source describes entry to the columbarium, or to its level, by descending from above (stairs down from the surface or roof, a shaft, a hatch, or an opening in the ceiling). FAIL: the source states that the only entrance is at floor level from the side. UNKNOWN otherwise.
- **C44-P (period).** §6.

## 3. Region

**Judea**: the Judean hills from Jerusalem to Hebron and south to the Yattir–Eshtemoa line, the Shephelah, the Judean Desert and its Dead Sea fringe, and the Herodian fortresses on the west side of the Jordan and Dead Sea (Herodium, Hyrcania, Masada, Cypros; the Jericho palaces sit on the edge). Machaerus and Alexandrium lie outside.

Boundary: the polygon with vertices, in WGS 84 (EPSG:4326), longitude/latitude: (34.85, 31.90), (35.55, 31.90), (35.45, 31.30), (34.75, 31.30). A row is in the region if its point lies inside (ray-casting test on decimal degrees). A row with no point but a named site is placed from a stated gazetteer (Pleiades, Wikidata, or the source's grid reference) with its precision recorded. A row that cannot be placed within 2 km is listed but not counted ("unplaced").

INFERENCE: the polygon follows the brief and the natural edges (Jordan and Dead Sea in the east; the line of Gezer, Tel Zafit and the western Shephelah in the west; south of Masada and Eshtemoa in the south; north of Jerusalem, Jericho and Cypros in the north). Its western edge cuts the Shephelah–coastal plain transition. It was drawn before any feature was screened.

## 5. Eligibility and units

- **Columbarium word.** English: columbarium, columbaria, columbarium cave, dovecot(e), dove-cot(e), pigeon-house, pigeon loft, pigeonhole(s) used of a dovecote. Hebrew whole words with prefixes ב ה ו כ ל מ ש allowed: קולומבריום, קולומבריה, קולומבריא, קולומבריונים, קולומבריומים, שובך, שובכים, שובכי, and יונים only beside שובך or as "מגדל יונים" or "מערת יונים". The ASI and WBADB screens are case-insensitive regex matches; the exact patterns are frozen in Appendix A. `scripts/terms.json` copies them, and a test checks that the copy equals the appendix.
- A record is excluded if its columbarium word refers to a funerary urn-niche tomb, or if the word is part of a place name only with no feature (both noted, not deleted).
- Rows of small niches described without a columbarium word are not columbaria here. Their count is not attempted.
- **Unit.** One row per columbarium that the source distinguishes. A record that mentions several columbaria without telling them apart gives one row with `n_columbaria` = the number given, or null if only a plural is given. The same columbarium in several sources gets one `dedup_group` (same site name, or points within 300 m and the same site); headline counts are by dedup group, with row counts beside them.
- Any period is eligible. Dating is a clause, not an entry condition.

## 6. Periods

Window: Hasmonean to 135 CE (167 BCE – 135 CE). Date codes, from the source's words about the columbarium itself (the same sentence or an explicit statement):
- **D** (in window): Hasmonean, Herodian, Early Roman, first century BCE or CE, late Second Temple, Bar Kokhba, or a stated range inside the window.
- **L** (outside): every label lies wholly outside the window (e.g., Iron Age, Persian, Byzantine, Early Islamic, Crusader, Ottoman; Hellenistic only if stated as before 167 BCE).
- **P** (partly): a label that overlaps the window but is not inside it (Hellenistic, Roman, Second Temple, Hellenistic–Roman, Late Roman spanning 135 CE).
- **U** (undated): no date for the columbarium itself.
A site's period list never dates the columbarium; it is recorded as `site_periods`. Clause code: D = MATCH, L = FAIL, P or U = UNKNOWN. Sensitivity "period relaxed": D, P and U pass; L fails.

## 7. Tolerances and levels

- C38-N survey level, primary R = 1.0 km from N1 or N2; sensitivity R = 2.0 km. MATCH if d ≤ R; FAIL if d > R + p, where p is the row's point precision (ASI and WBADB: 100 m unless the source states otherwise); UNKNOWN between, or if p > 2 km.
- Latitude lines (S-A, S-B1, S-B2): MATCH if the point is south of the line by more than p; FAIL if north by more than p; UNKNOWN otherwise.
- **Levels of the count.** *Survey level* uses points, co-occurrence in a site record and the source's words. *Text level* needs the source's own words or plan for every clause.
- **Combined verdict** for a branch: FAIL if any clause fails; MATCH if every clause matches; UNKNOWN otherwise. Silence is never a FAIL.

Registered combinations (survey level and text level each; primary period rule and "period relaxed" each):
- 38-D-N1: C38-N (N1) ∧ C38-P.
- 38-D-N2: C38-N (N2) ∧ C38-P.
- 38-D-N0: C38-P only (denominator).
- 44-D-P (Puech): C44-F ∧ C44-S (S-A) ∧ C44-L (L2) ∧ C44-A ∧ C44-P.
- 44-D-T (text shown): C44-F ∧ C44-S (S-A) ∧ C44-L (L3) ∧ C44-A ∧ C44-P.
- Sensitivities for 44: S-B1, S-B2 or S-C in place of S-A; F+ in place of C44-F.

## 8. Coding

- A script screens S1 and S2 and writes the candidate rows with matched terms and points. It also flags clause keywords (Appendix A): fort words; tower words; storey and level words; access words; part-of-site words; Naṭof place words.
- One coder (this worker) reads the full description of every candidate row and codes the clause fields. Codes other than UNKNOWN need a source statement, quoted in 12 words or fewer in `quote` (only for evidence-bearing codes) with its page, figure or record id. No second coder is available in this lane: the README says so, and any MATCH on a distinctive clause (fort text level, L2, L3, access, S-C, N text level) is listed by row for the integrator to check.
- Literature rows (S3) are coded by hand into `data/literature_rows.csv` with printed pages and figures.
- `scripts/score.py` applies §5–§7 to `columbaria.csv` and writes `scores.csv` and `summary.json`. It is deterministic; the tests check that it reproduces the committed outputs.
- Each row records whether its site was exposed before this freeze (§10).

## 9. What results would mean (decision wording)

- Report, per branch and level: "k match, f fail, m unknown, of N", by dedup group and by row, with the coverage gaps (sheets not online; West Bank areas without full survey; literature not read).
- **Small k, small m**: the combination is uncommon in the recorded inventory of the region. **k ≥ about 5**: a fit to one site carries little weight. **k = 0**: no recorded columbarium fits; any current or future proposal rests on evidence outside this inventory; this is not a rejection. **m ≫ k**: rarity is not measurable from the available evidence.
- **What does not follow**: an identification (AGENTS.md needs discrimination plus an unseen prediction; the main candidates are exposed); anything about a deposit or the treasure; anything for branches 38-O, 38-T, 38-G, 38-Dr, 38-L, 44-T, 44-M, 44-G, or places outside the region; rarity on the ground rather than in the record.
- No registered outcome, ledger count or closed test changes.

## Appendix A. Frozen patterns (Python `re`, IGNORECASE; Hebrew letters are the range U+05D0–U+05EA)

Hebrew prefix rule: a Hebrew pattern may be preceded by up to two of ב ה ו כ ל מ ש, and must not be preceded or followed by another Hebrew letter except where the pattern itself allows letters.

Columbarium (eligibility):
- `columbar\w*`
- `\bdove[\s\-]?cot(?:e|es|s)?\b`
- `\bpigeon[\s\-]?(?:house|houses|loft|lofts|cote|cotes|holes?)\b`
- Hebrew: `קולומב[א-ת]*`; `שובך`; `שובכ(?:ים|י|ות)?`; `(?:מגדל|מערת|שובך|שובכי)\s+ה?יונים`

Clause flags (they only mark rows for reading; they never set a code):
- fort: `\b(?:forts?|fortress(?:es)?|fortified|fortifications?|citadels?|strongholds?|castles?|castellum|burgus|qasr|kasr)\b`; Hebrew `מצד`, `מצודה`, `מצודת`, `מבצר`, `ביצור(?:ים)?`, `מבוצר(?:ת)?`
- tower: `\b(?:towers?|migdal)\b`; Hebrew `מגדל(?:ים)?`
- levels: `\b(?:storeys?|story|stories|floors?|levels?|upper|lower|two[\s\-]stor(?:e)?y|tiers?)\b`; Hebrew `קומ(?:ה|ות|תי|תיים)`, `מפלס(?:ים)?`, `עלי(?:י)?ה`
- access: `\b(?:stairs?|staircases?|steps|shafts?|descen\w*|hatch\w*|ceiling)\b`; Hebrew `מדרג(?:ה|ות)`, `פיר`, `ירידה`, `יורד(?:ים|ת)?`, `תקרה`
- south: `\b(?:south\w*)\b`; Hebrew `דרומ(?:י|ית)?`, `דרום`
- Naṭof place: `na[tṭ]uf|na[tṭ]of|kh[ao]?reit[uo]n|chariton|bad+[\s\-]*fal[uū][hḥ]|netoph\w*`; Hebrew `נטוף`, `נטופה`, `חריטון`, `בד פלוח`
