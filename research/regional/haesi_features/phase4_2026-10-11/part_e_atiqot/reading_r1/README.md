# Phase 4, part E, reader r1: ʿAtiqot PDFs read and coded

**What it is.** Reader r1's reading of 89 ʿAtiqot articles from the part-E list (tiers T1, U and T3 in full, T2 positions 1–60), coded under the frozen phase-2 plan. Exploratory; no identification claim.
**Main result.** 85 articles read and coded, 4 dropped as outside the region; 144 feature rows (43 dated in the window). Most rows are in the Jerusalem hills (Z4); the Jordan Valley rows come from the ʿAujah aqueduct study.
**What stays unknown.** T2 positions 61–208 belong to other readers. Many rows stay `unknown` for date, and several finds reports code a context that the site report (read elsewhere) also codes.

## Sources and access

- PDFs: the owner-approved downloads in `~/Downloads/IAA portal volumes/phase4/` on the owner's Mac, one file per unit (`<unit_id>.pdf`). All 89 matched the SHA-256 in `../../downloads_2026-10-11.csv`. Nothing else in `~/Downloads` was listed or opened.
- Text: `pdftotext -layout`, `-raw` and, for Hebrew, default order, into `$HOME/p4text/r1/` on the Mac. All files had a text layer; no OCR was needed. No PDF or extracted text is in the repository.
- Units read and coded: 85.
- Dropped as outside the region: 4.
- Not read (time limit): 0.
- Not read for any other reason: none. The two flagged laboratory studies were read to the end of their introduction only (NOTES, C6).

## Method

- Order: tier, then `order_position_in_tier` (T1, U, T3, T2 1–60).
- Each unit: map reference converted with the phase-1 `geo.py` and zoned with the phase-2 `common.zone_of`; held checks at the first line; then features coded with the phase-2 classes, in-window rule, labels and columns (`scripts/build_r1.py`).
- Finds and specialist reports: coded only for the eligible find contexts they record (NOTES, C5); otherwise they are report rows with no feature rows.
- Quotes are 12 words or fewer. The span read and its SHA-256 (whitespace collapsed) are in `reports.csv`; `scripts/verify_spans.py` found every quote in its span.
- Choices not settled by the plan are listed in `NOTES.md` (C1–C13).
