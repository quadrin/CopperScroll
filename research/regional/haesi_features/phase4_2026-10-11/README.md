# Regional inventory phase 4: ʿAtiqot, IAA Reports, QDAP, nineteenth-century surveys, ADAJ

**What it is.** Phase 4 read five more source groups on 11 October 2026 under the phase-2 frozen rules, unchanged. The scope was frozen in [PLAN.md](PLAN.md) (commit b002e03), and five workers ran one part each.
**Main result.** 592 units in the region were read and coded, giving 718 feature rows with one zone: 152 dated by the source to the window, 306 dated outside it, 260 undated. A further 94 SWP rows sit in squares split between two zones. With the owner's approval, the 249 ʿAtiqot and IAA Reports PDFs were downloaded to his Mac on 11–12 October ([manifest](downloads_2026-10-11.csv)) and read by four readers.
**What stays unknown.** Rows are not yet deduplicated (finds reports repeat their sites' features) against phases 1–3 or across sources: the same tomb or hoard can appear in QDAP, ADAJ and an IAA report. Most survey rows are undated. Placement is by name for every QDAP row and for most survey rows.

All of this is exploratory. It makes no identification claim, and no registered result changed.

**What the in-window rows are:**

- **QDAP, all in Jerusalem and its hills:** a Tyropoeon drain, kokhim tombs, the ʿAin Karim stepped chamber, a quarry, the Citadel's Tenth Legion drain and the Silwan shekel hoard (findspot from a dealer).
- **ADAJ:** Kh. Qumran and ʿAin Feshkha installations (built sites, read normally), Murabbaʿat, Tulul Abu el-ʿAlayiq and a Herodian cave at Siyar el-Ghanam.
- **Nineteenth-century surveys:** none. The authors rarely date the features themselves.
- **ʿAtiqot and IAA Reports (130 rows):**
  - Jerusalem: tombs, miqvaʾot, cisterns, channels and quarries, including the Western Wall Plaza and Givʿati features, the Siloam and Tyropoeon dams, and the Horbat Mazruq cisterns and hiding complex.
  - Desert and valley: ʿEn Gedi irrigation works, and the ʿAujah aqueduct system with its pools and dam.
  - Choices that move these rows:
    - IAA Reports earth-dug pits and silos were coded as rock-cut installations (exploratory choice C in the reading NOTES);
    - two Cardo works dated before 130 CE count under the plan's "up to 135 CE".

## Not read

- ʿAtiqot: nothing unread. Long reports were read at every passage naming an eligible feature, plus the dating and description passages. Three long Hebrew reports were coded from their English summaries.
- IAA Reports: two chapters are missing from the portal scans. Manaḥat Ch. 14 (the miqveh) and Efrata App. 1 (the Byzantine winepresses) are separate chapter PDFs on the portal and were not downloaded.
- QDAP vol. 6 nos. 3–4 (archive.org returned HTTP 500 for the OCR text) and vol. 10 nos. 2–3 (not on archive.org). The British Library EAP1544 page images (records -63 and -69) could fill both.
- Guérin *Judée* II–III and *Samarie* I (time limit); 443 SWP and Guérin entries that could not be located; SWP II pp. 401–408 (missing from the scan).
- ADAJ 6/7 p. 116 (blank in the scan) and ADAJ 11 "Notes & News" (missing). The Arabic parts were read from page images, because the Arabic OCR model was blocked by the proxy.
