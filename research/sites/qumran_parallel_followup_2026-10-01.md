### Original evidence and interpretation

**De Vaux 1953:** Fig. 1 depicts the main building's phases I/II with north arrow and 0–20 m scale; no datum or upper gorge. On p. 312, damage crosses two interior pools north–south, with eastern subsidence; the external pool is also considered affected. Filled damaged works, a replacement southern cistern and altered channels are described. Damage and masonry are observations; earthquake attribution and phase explanation are interpretations. Page 317 explicitly leaves the earthquake date uncertain. The report's provisional phase labels must not automatically be equated with later Ia/Ib/II terminology.

**De Vaux 1955:** p. 379 gives the later argument for a 31 BCE earthquake and subsequent Archelaus-period return; p. 380 describes a later channel after 68 CE. These are successive chronological interpretations of settlement evidence, not dates excavated at the upper boulder or wall. Reading the earlier and later reports together prevents treating an evolving interpretation as an independent observation.

**Reeder 2011:** p. 178 discusses total-station mapping and a cemetery spreadsheet/CAVEPLOT/Illustrator workflow; p. 190 gives a general aqueduct account citing Schulz and de Vaux. The checked captions concern site/cemetery/caves and an ERT section. No diagnostic upper-feature connection was recovered from this text. The chapter belongs to the already known survey programme, not an independently corroborating survey. Because the figures were not inspected, their coordinate content remains unverified.

**De Vaux 1952:** p. 173 reports an aqueduct traceable to wadi basins. That general continuity supplies neither the modern numbered-feature crosswalk nor a boulder/pothole sequence.
