These are the authors' documented collection and laboratory procedures. Their construction-age interpretation assumes short-lived straw was gathered close to building and stored briefly. The appendix supplies no independent test of storage duration and no per-sample basket/locus/coat table. Published cleaning controls do not establish perfect contamination removal by themselves.
