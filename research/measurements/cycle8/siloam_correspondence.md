# Siloam: installation correspondence and phase check

Research date: 2 October 2026, America/Los_Angeles. Cycle 8 archaeology thread. Entry 49's textual alternatives were checked independently. Its instructions supply no compass bearing or cubit offset for this assessment. No candidate coordinates, confidence changes or question closures result.

## Correspondence result

The 2022 excavation report explicitly associates **W50 with stepped pool L124 and Guthe plate III**. Its Fig. 2 positions this installation southeast of Birket al-Hamra; Fig. 3 plots W50 and channel L408 separately. L408 has no dating finds, and its municipal-drain identification remains probable. These observations establish a published southern-pool correspondence. They supply no GHJK or grooved-tank identification.

The 2014 report places L124 north of gateway L127, after pavement L115, in phases 3d–3c. Fig. 3 distinguishes this stepped pool from blocked channel L133 and tower reservoir L119. Fig. 4 depicts other architectural/destruction sections. The reservoir's insertion into the existing tower is tentative. None of these phases can date Bliss's small tank. **The 2020 Greenhut–Mazor pool has not been independently equated with L124/W50 in this check.**

Earlier project reading of Guthe pp. 65–68 and plate II records GHJK, approximately 9 x 2.70–3.20 m, and an adjoining approximately 1 m channel at the tunnel-mouth pool. Later masonry in its older western wall provides relative sequence, with no calendar date. This thread did not re-inspect those original pages.

Earlier direct reading of Bliss–Dickie pp. 134–135, Plate XI and General Plan II records a separate small grooved tank at the southern drain exit near gate d2, southwest of the Old Pool. Its 3 ft 4 in square, 25 in height and floor 5 ft above the drain bottom convert to 1.016 m, 0.635 m and 1.524 m. The 1898 interpretation places its construction after drain disuse. Its modern locus and calendar date remain unestablished.

## Date association requiring inspection

Greenhut and Mazor's 2020 publisher abstract dates their own dam-side pool to the later first century CE from foundation pottery/coins and proposes Great Revolt construction. Full plans, contexts and finds tables remain unread. That abstract does not establish its identity with the installations above.

The 2024 Birket al-Hamra conference report, printed p. 34* / PDF 17, tentatively places the Greenhut–Mazor pool in an earlier phase and cites plaster radiocarbon results 520–200 BCE. Boaretto's original 2020 publisher abstract locates samples on the outer dam face and reports a discrepancy between Persian/Hellenistic results and archaeological seventh-century–586 BCE dating. Establish sampled feature, material and plaster coat before treating these accounts as dates for one installation. No date transfers to GHJK or the grooved tank.

**Next discriminating check:** compare the 2020 excavation plan/locus register and foundation coin contexts against Boaretto's sample table and plaster-layer description. Verify whether the pool matches L124/W50 and whether dated material belongs to pool construction, earlier dam fabric or repairs. A separately published modern-locus correspondence still remains necessary for GHJK or Bliss's tank.
