# Doq: the December 1982 soundings and their publication trail

Reviewed 2 October 2026. Entry 31 still requires an individually identified guard/drying room, its eastern corner, an ancient surface and an installation phase. This pass traced the missing excavation record through the original supplied pages and public publication catalogues.

## Original pages checked

David Amit, “מפעל המים של מבצר דוק (דגון)” [The Water System of the Fortress of Doq (Dagon)], in *אמות המים הקדומות בארץ־ישראל: קובץ מחקרים* (1989), p. 228 note 5, confirms excavations during Hanukkah in December 1982 under Ehud Netzer. Rachel Bar-Nathan, Rivka Birger, Amit and a Kfar Etzion fieldschool youth work group participated. Amit reports a room complex down the northeastern spur near the small pools and attributes the remains to the Herodian period. The archived chapter supplies neither a locus plan nor a permit identifier.

**Attribution correction:** the note names ז׳ משל, Zeev Meshel, as the proposer of the pools’ possible use as miqvaot. Note 3 names him fully. The earlier cycle 8 note’s erroneous expansion to “Dan Meshulam” is corrected in both mirrors this cycle. The archaeological interpretation remains a proposal.

Ehud Netzer, *The Palaces of the Hasmoneans and Herod the Great* (2001), pp. 70 and 72, reports limited soundings at Qarantal. Original p. 140, chapter 9 note 9 (PDF 144), dates his work to 1982 and records Herodian remains including an Ionic capital. That note explicitly cites **Ehud Netzer and Rivka Birger, “A Byzantine Monastery at Nuseib ‘Uweishira, West of Jericho,” pp. 191–200**. I read the supplied original page images; OCR only located the pages. This author’s account supplies a precise publication lead. It supplies no room function, corner or surface measurement.

## Negative coverage

The public [Hadashot Arkheologiyot Hebrew series index](https://publications.iaa.org.il/ha_hebrew_series/), including the exposed contents for issues 83–86, and its [older index page](https://publications.iaa.org.il/ha_hebrew_series/index.2.html), including issue 82, supplied no separate Doq/Dagon heading. The [English-series index](https://publications.iaa.org.il/esi_english_series/) and [Favissa archival-report list](https://publications.iaa.org.il/favissa/) likewise supplied no target record. These are catalogue-scope negative results; they establish neither destruction of field files nor complete absence of a publication. Jericho winter-palace and Herodium reports concern other sites.

[Nira Naveh’s Netzer bibliography](https://herodium.org/wp-content/uploads/2014/nezer/Bibliography.pdf) lists the 1990 Netzer–Birger chapter but yielded no separately titled Doq report. Searches of Greenberg and Keinan’s [West Bank excavation sourcebook](https://www.emekshaveh.org/he/wp-content/uploads/2013/09/WBADB_sourcebook.pdf) under Doq, Dok, Duk, Dagon and Qarantal yielded no 1982 record or permit. Its Quruntul entries concern later cave excavations. Search engines returned many unrelated hits under the short name “Dok”; no reliable permit identifier emerged.
