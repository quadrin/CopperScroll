## Result

**Coin 20 belongs to pool foundation L105, basket 1092, and dates to year four of the revolt, 69/70 CE.** The catalogue on p. 90* and Hebrew discussion on p. 91* establish this association. Fig. 1 on p. 89* illustrates coin 20. The English summary calls fill coin 19 the latest; it omits equally late foundation coin 20. The dates agree, while the abbreviated summary lacks the foundation detail required for this test.

Accepting the published foundation context, coin 20 gives a **terminus post quem after its minting in 69/70 CE** for installation of the excavated pool floor/foundation. Ariel supports the excavators’ inference that installation finished in 69 or 70. The coin supplies the lower date bound; an exact completion deadline requires the archaeological upper bound. Greenhut–Mazor’s alternative explanations for two post-Revolt jar rims still leave sealing chronology unresolved.

The younger L105 foundation assemblage contains Agrippa I and revolt years two, three and four. Its latest issue establishes the relevant terminus. The three older L105A coins belong to a deeper context and cannot make the later L105 assemblage earlier.

## Context and precision limits

L105A comprises the upper part of original L107, assigned a separate locus during finds processing because it resembled L105. Its three coins were recorded at 632.20 m, above the Iron Age pottery layer recorded at 630.84–631.50 m (p. 89* and note 2). Ariel offers older source soil or an unrecognized separate phase as explanations for this older group. Neither explanation is demonstrated. Neither changes the published L105 association of coin 20.

Coin 17’s year-three date concerns its deposition in L103. The coin is not reported as a sealed conduit-construction foundation find, so its date cannot independently date construction of the channel. Greenhut–Mazor’s observed cut through pool plaster provides the relative-phase evidence; the channel’s inferred post-pool installation remains a separate argument.

The catalogue dates coin 3 to 5/6 CE, while the narrative uses a broader Augustus interval. Use the item row for its issue date. The printed catalogue repeats inventory number 138406 in separate grouped rows; no silent correction or inferred inventory-number mapping has been made. Catalogue number plus locus/basket identifies the extracted records above.

## Consequence for entry 49

The published coin contexts support the late construction chronology of the L102/L104 pool linked to L124/W50/Guthe plate III. If L103 postdates that installation, its conduit-as-trough comparison also requires a deposition model late enough to accommodate construction after the year-four coin. A hypothesis requiring this particular floor/channel before that issue conflicts with the documented phase, unless a distinct earlier feature phase is established. No date for the whole scroll is adopted here.

This narrows the eligible phase; it does not identify L103 as the terminal trough/gutter. Its form, ancient underside and textual identity remain unverified. The lower dam plaster L108 and its charcoal date stay separate from the pool foundation. Guthe’s tunnel-mouth GHJK and Bliss’s grooved tank remain unjoined to a dated modern locus. No coordinate, site confidence grade or confirmed deposit changes.
