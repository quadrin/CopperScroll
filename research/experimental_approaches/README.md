# Copper Scroll research methods and results

Reviewed 30 October 2026. Identify and locate real places described in the Copper Scroll by connecting textual readings, ancient geography and archaeological remains, comparing alternatives and deriving checkable predictions.

Choose a question about a place, a set of candidates or the scroll’s geographic interpretation before choosing a technique. Evidence synthesis and exploratory hypotheses are valid research. These method records describe what has already been tried and the limits of conditional techniques.

[Current research direction](https://github.com/quadrin/CopperScroll/blob/main/research/ACTIVE_TEST.md) | [Drive source index](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1A08WOW_9PIkryXauw6WS1mSgT11NREpK/view)

The results below include desk analyses and proposals requiring new photographs, samples, equipment or institutional access. A conditional field technique is relevant only when a particular site question needs an observation it could supply. Method numbers retain the original proposal IDs and give no priority.

**1. AI extraction from excavation reports — Partial test; accuracy unmeasured.** Mechanical checks passed on 50 reports. Two-model agreement is not an accuracy estimate; the hand-coded answer key was dropped on 24 October. Provisional extraction can help locate reports; it does not validate candidate dates or feature identities. Remaining condition: Use existing rows provisionally. A bounded independently checked sample would be needed for an accuracy claim; do not resume hand-coding as an inherited task.

Evidence: [First method tests, 23–24 October: extraction, imagery, registration and hoard results](https://github.com/quadrin/CopperScroll/blob/main/research/first_tests_2026-10-24/README.md); [AI extraction from excavation reports sources](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1LEjarurJlVDWgpIqj40yPBOQh27enY5p).

**2. AI on pre-development imagery — Resolution gate passed on one frame.** HEXAGON 1978 resolves large open Qumran pools; CORONA 1967 did not resolve the eight tested pools. The blind marking rule also marks ordinary rooms. Supports inspection of open features around four metres and larger on the tested film; it establishes no buried-feature detection or new site. Remaining condition: A detector on unseen ground and pre-development RAF imagery have not been validated by that test. Original RAF scans remain a dependency.

Evidence: [First method tests, 23–24 October: extraction, imagery, registration and hoard results](https://github.com/quadrin/CopperScroll/blob/main/research/first_tests_2026-10-24/README.md); [Royal Air Force historical-imagery access and inspection record](https://github.com/quadrin/CopperScroll/blob/main/research/regional/raf_1940s/README.md); [AI on pre-development imagery sources 1](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1Mjm7PQC-YtSvhVH_fj59_b3QtE5yij3s); [AI on pre-development imagery sources 2](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1RIMetQQjp4CiBdohnczpxEU74jRDaRJp).

**3. Thermal and photogrammetric drone survey — Proposal; feasibility data awaited.** The 23 October Experimental Approaches dossier proposes comparing ground-discovered openings with ordinary imagery. No completed test of the additional detection value of thermal imaging was found in the reviewed results. Assess added detection value before treating a new survey as useful. Remaining condition: Israel Antiquities Authority survey records must first show a consequential detection gap; a partner-led flight depends on that result and appropriate access.

Proposal source: [Copper Scroll Experimental Approaches, 23 October 2026, method 3](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1Xysgs_RWljnWYL6qyPvZvSOOIIPvSQYW/view).

**4. A search-theory map — Groundwork exists; predictive test unrun.** The project already has coverage and search-effectiveness analyses. The proposed pre-2017 prediction test against later cave finds has no recorded result in the reviewed material. A map is worthwhile only if it changes a real choice and survives a comparison with a simpler baseline. Remaining condition: Requires dated search footprints, survey outcomes and defensible detection rates. Existing speculative weights cannot supply a calibrated map.

Evidence: [Search-effectiveness model: assumptions and limits](https://github.com/quadrin/CopperScroll/blob/main/research/models/search_effectiveness/README.md); [Excavation and survey coverage: recorded gaps](https://github.com/quadrin/CopperScroll/blob/main/research/feature_workbench/coverage/README.md); [A search-theory map sources](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1g6vOBv2sb7MA6aB3cgUNVDv_HKQHejjz).

**5. Muon imaging — Partner-dependent proposal.** No project-specific void-detectability simulation or field result is recorded in the reviewed material. Keep as a conditional technique; no detector campaign follows from the dossier's precedent alone. Remaining condition: A named target with detector access below it, plus a partner simulation of signal and run time.

Proposal source: [Copper Scroll Experimental Approaches, 23 October 2026, method 5](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1Xysgs_RWljnWYL6qyPvZvSOOIIPvSQYW/view).

**6. Dating the installations — Source work done; sampling pilot unrun.** The project has evaluated construction phases and plaster-dating literature. The proposed five-fragment screening pilot has no recorded result. Dating a repair or unrelated plaster cannot settle the original installation's age. Remaining condition: A date-bearing construction contact that would decide a consequential candidate claim, suitable material and a licensed sampling partner.

Evidence: [Research question register: dating hypotheses and existing source work](https://github.com/quadrin/CopperScroll/blob/main/research/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md); [Dating the installations sources](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1AAmpkatwJN1m7lgghqDuOGUrYtlYG9vO).

**7. Imaging both faces of the strips — New photographs received; new imaging unrun.** The University of Southern California supplied 93 colour photographs of the front (recto). Public previews of front and back (verso) photographs also exist; back photography is therefore not an untested idea. Image arrival and a new imaging campaign are different achievements; study access does not grant publication permission. Remaining condition: New 3D or reflectance imaging of the original backs remains a separate proposal. Determine whether existing photographs leave a specific consequential observation unavailable.

Evidence: [University of Southern California photograph receipt and access record, 29 October](https://github.com/quadrin/CopperScroll/blob/main/research/sources/books_usc_2026-10-30/wsrp_usc_plan_2026-10-29.md); [Column XII, line 10: photograph release and reader exposure, 30 October](https://github.com/quadrin/CopperScroll/blob/main/research/sources/books_usc_2026-10-30/xii_images_released_2026-10-30.md); [Imaging both faces of the strips sources 1](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/171e-Pa9GiWMUuYbSGYkLTWuGmSCxbACA); [Imaging both faces of the strips sources 2](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1hspYwJm5ns-O9zHIXg0MNz1gbmHrb_0b).

**8. Computational re-reading — Partial results; early-photo leg stopped.** Registration of 1988 and 1994 photographs reached median errors of 0.12–0.19 mm on tested segments. Matching to the early plates in Discoveries in the Judaean Desert, volume III, failed the acceptance test and stopped. Recent colour-photo readings remain exploratory; several words are lost or unreadable. Do not count registration accuracy, a tentative letter lean or an exposed reading as an independently confirmed location. Remaining condition: A distinct better observation or calibrated fresh-reader test must justify reopening a specific reading. The column XII, line 10 (column 12, line 10) images are exposed to earlier atlas users.

Evidence: [First method tests, 23–24 October: extraction, imagery, registration and hoard results](https://github.com/quadrin/CopperScroll/blob/main/research/first_tests_2026-10-24/README.md); [Colour-photograph reading observations and limits, 30 October](https://github.com/quadrin/CopperScroll/blob/main/research/sources/books_usc_2026-10-30/wsrp_followups_2026-10-30.md); [Computational re-reading sources](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/15QeOGkcgC_BUTgRjiRyDJmwA71dPkylE).

**9. Unidentified fragments and the explanatory copy — Proposal; retrieval benchmark unrun.** No completed known-join recovery test or identification of an explanatory-copy fragment is recorded in the reviewed material. This remains a long-shot hypothesis. Common-word matches cannot establish that a second copy existed. Remaining condition: A fixed distinctive-text query and a test on known fragment joins at realistic fragment sizes, with a chance baseline and suitable corpus access.

Evidence: [Unidentified fragments and the explanatory copy sources](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1Ks9yYiA69U54p6o1Rf2Ll2ESu-geon7_).

**10. The hoard check — Weight test complete; identification open.** Under the registered default talent reading, 0/45 sums matched; under the 71 g karsh reading, 27/30 matched. Milik's alternative numeral at VIII 9 leaves a talent exception. Thirteen hoards supply the karsh matches. The smaller unit is compatible with known hoard sizes. Weight alone identifies no scroll deposit. Remaining condition: Precise findspot, depth and container records would be needed for a discriminating comparison; the weight screen should not be rerun without a correction.

Evidence: [First method tests, 23–24 October: extraction, imagery, registration and hoard results](https://github.com/quadrin/CopperScroll/blob/main/research/first_tests_2026-10-24/README.md); [The hoard check sources](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/17Eox5OeGt0LLCmmYCRm3ryH9G6KgthH_).

**11. Forecasting and blind panels — Proposed; expert-panel pilot unrun.** A reading protocol and letter controls exist. No completed human-epigrapher panel or forecasting tournament is recorded in the reviewed results. A panel can clarify disagreement; a tournament needs enough resolvable questions to justify its overhead. Remaining condition: Choose a question whose answer can resolve, arrange qualified independent readers and appropriate image rights, and record prior exposure.

Evidence: [Letter-reading control protocol: design rather than a completed expert panel](https://github.com/quadrin/CopperScroll/blob/main/research/text/letter_controls/PROTOCOL.md); [Forecasting and blind panels sources](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/171e-Pa9GiWMUuYbSGYkLTWuGmSCxbACA).

HEXAGON and CORONA are historical US satellite-imagery programs. RAF means Royal Air Force. Recto means the front of a manuscript surface; verso means the back. Column XII, line 10 means column 12, line 10. A talent and a karsh are alternative ancient weight units tested against hoard sizes; VIII 9 means column 8, line 9.
