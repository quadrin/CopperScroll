## Terms used in the results

HEXAGON and CORONA are historical US satellite-imagery programs. Royal Air Force aerial photographs are a separate source. Recto means the front of a manuscript surface; verso means the back. Column XII, line 10 means column 12, line 10 of the scroll. A talent and a karsh are alternative ancient weight units tested against hoard sizes; VIII 9 means column 8, line 9.
