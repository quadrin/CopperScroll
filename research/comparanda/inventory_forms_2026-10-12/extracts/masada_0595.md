**V, electronic-edition scope only.** Exact edition metadata date: “Most likely 66-73 CE” (spacing normalized here).

Short verbatim commentary excerpt: “the numeral 23 is repeated twice, first in Greek characters (ΚΓ) and then through Phoenician symbols” (spacing normalized here).

The current XML expressly encodes `<num xml:lang="grc" value="23">ΚΓ</num>`, and subsequently `לג <num value="23">` with a Phoenician 20 sign and three strokes. Its transcription preserves lost and illegible text and `ע<unclear>וזר</unclear>ל`. The fruit interpretation remains tentative.

The explicit unit-number relation supports a contents-measure reading in this edition. It does not identify stored contents, authenticate actual jar volume, map a numbered place, or identify Copper Scroll units.

The archival XML groups Greek rho and ΚΓ in an original-text span, whereas the current XML separately marks ΚΓ as a numeral. Both are versions of this edition-derived record; they supply no additional field observation. The original field campaign is Yadin 1963–1965. Catalogue no. 595 is a modern locator.
