# Entries 20–23: what the column V plates can constrain

30 September 2026. This is a text-first check for the Sekakah cluster. It uses the earlier **blind** reader records in [`registration/plate_check/`](../../registration/plate_check/) and a subsequent, non-blind inspection of Puech's published column V plates. It is **not** a fresh independent manuscript transcription. The two readers saw coded crops before edition or place names; their full-line transcriptions were weak, so only their targeted observations below carry weight. No image or crop is committed.

## Assessment and next image request

These plates preserve a useful *constraint hierarchy*: the conduit head, Sekakah, the north wording, a feature east of a named reservoir, and a 60-cubit relation survive in edited form; the west link and entry 21 stone/depth do not survive as readable grooves. Entry 23's Solomon/Shallum division remains open. The existing blind packet supports those limits, rather than a new place identification.

For a genuinely new blind pass, obtain a usable copy of the original DJD III **pl. LVII** photograph and **pl. LVI** hand copy, or full-resolution USC InscriptiFact photographs **AWS 21/23** of cuts 10–11. See the [image-access audit](column_v_image_access_2026-09-30.md) for exact catalog IDs and the limits of the public previews. Give a specialist **V 1–3, V 5–9 and their radiographs** without edition names, proposed locations or completed words. Ask separately for (1) every preserved stroke at V 1's break, (2) attachment and punctuation of `מן הצפון`, (3) the noun after “great,” (4) the V 5 east-ending and V 7 group/entry boundary, and (5) spacing around the initial waw of V 9 and the final letter of `הרגב`. Save the raw answers before revealing the candidate restorations.
