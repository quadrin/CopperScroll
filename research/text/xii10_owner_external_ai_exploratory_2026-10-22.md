## Scoring (fixed now)
- Reference: `research/agent_review_2026-10-07/wave1/T10_outreach/data/inventory_letters.csv` (ETCBC letters, per-letter flags).
- The scorer aligns the external AI's image lines to scroll lines by their content. Control letters are those in columns XI and XII, **excluding lines XII 8–12**, where `candidate_undisputed` is true.
- Reported: the number of control letters the model attempted, how many it read exactly, how many it gave as an alternative set that includes the reference letter, and how many it read as another letter. A control read with high confidence as the wrong letter is listed.
- The model's transcription of XII 8–12 is reported verbatim, with no score and no outcome id.
- If the model says it recognises the document, or its control accuracy is at the level of recall, the result is labelled "possibly from memory". It then says nothing about legibility.
- The owner's own reading is reported as given, with no score.
