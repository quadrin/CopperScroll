**Other Wolters 1996 readings on these pages that bear on open checks (edition readings only; they settle no plate question):**
- **VII 14–15** (plate check: Koziba or Buz): Wolters prints הכוז at the end of VII 14 and בא at the start of VII 15, translated "the Koziba". This agrees with the round-4 locator's line split ([plate_check.md](../plate_check.md)).
- **VIII 9 and VIII 13** (the plate-check sums 4/7 and 66/67): Wolters prints ככ 4 and ככ 66.
- **X 11** (the number after the vessel word): Wolters prints וכלין כופרין עסרין, "twenty expiatory vessels".
- **X 13:** חפו[ו]ר גמות שתין עסרה, "dig twelve notches".

**Effect.** 39 of the 41 secondhand rows are confirmed firsthand for Wolters 1996. Two rows conflict on ה versus ח, in opposite directions: V0315 (VII 14, ḥ secondhand, ה printed) and V0341 (VIII 2, h secondhand, ח printed). Both are flagged. The pattern suggests that underdots in the secondhand transliterations are not reliable, so other ḥ/h rows from the same route deserve a check. `variants.csv` is not edited; the flag is recorded here. No rating changes.

**Not photographed:** cols I–IV, VI and XII, and the "Overview" chapters. Wolters's text of XII is an edition transcription, not an image, so it falls outside the XII 8–12 image reservation; it is still not needed for any open test.
