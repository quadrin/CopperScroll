**Other Wolters 1996 readings on these pages that bear on open checks (edition readings only; they settle no plate question):**
- **VII 14–15** (plate check: Koziba or Buz): Wolters prints הכוז at the end of VII 14 and בא at the start of VII 15, translated "the Koziba". This agrees with the round-4 locator's line split ([plate_check.md](../plate_check.md)).
- **VIII 9 and VIII 13** (the plate-check sums 4/7 and 66/67): Wolters prints ככ 4 and ככ 66.
- **X 11** (the number after the vessel word): Wolters prints וכלין כופרין עסרין, "twenty expiatory vessels".
- **X 13:** חפו[ו]ר גמות שתין עסרה, "dig twelve notches".

**Effect.** 57 of the 62 secondhand rows agree with Wolters 1996. Five are flagged: V0315 (VII 14, ḥ secondhand, ה printed) and V0341 (VIII 2, h secondhand, ח printed), which conflict on ה/ח in opposite directions; V0018 (I 3), V0109 (II 5) and V0116 (II 6), where the row cites Wolters 1990 or 1992 and the 1996 text differs (אריח; העצין; no בו). The last three may be real changes between his publications. The pattern suggests that underdots in the secondhand transliterations are not reliable, so other ḥ/h rows from the same route deserve a check. `variants.csv` is not edited; the flag is recorded here. No rating changes.
