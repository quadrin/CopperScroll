## Reader's notes (INFERENCE, kept separate)

1. **Reference frame of the site 65 pool (INFERENCE).** The text places the pool relative to the church ("צמוד לכנסיה, ממזרח"), and the church relative to the tell ("לרגלי התל, בצדו המערבי"). Read literally, the pool is at the western foot of the tell, on the church's east side, so it lies between the church and the tell. It is not "east of the tell".
2. **"שרידי קמרון הקירוי" (INFERENCE, ambiguous).** The definite article and the word order (pool + remains of *the* roofing vault) most naturally make this the pool's own roof, so the pool would be a covered pool. Grammatically, though, the phrase could refer to a vault of the church. I flag this as ambiguous.
3. **Dates do not attach to the features (INFERENCE).** At sites 65 and 66, no period is attached to the pool, aqueduct, mills or spring. The sherd lists are site-wide. Any date for a water feature would have to come from somewhere other than these two pages.
4. **The site 65 aqueduct's reference "road" (INFERENCE).** The road is undefined in the entry. It could be a road named in the Introduction or on the survey map. On these pages alone, the aqueduct's position cannot be fixed relative to the tell.
5. **The site 66 mill's reference point (INFERENCE).** No reference is named, so the mill is 300 m north-east of the ruin by default. The relation of this mill to the "many water mills along the stream" of site 65 is not stated.
6. **Map reference order (INFERENCE).** The printed pairs (e.g. 1682 1525 for Kh. Bir Zeit) read as east then north on the Palestine grid, in units of 100 m. The source gives no legend on these pages.
7. **Distance between sites 65 and 66 (INFERENCE).** The printed references are 1816 1554 and 1817 1550. They differ by about 400 m north–south, which is consistent with the stated "400 m south".
