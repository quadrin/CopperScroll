## English facts (p. 187, col. 1)

- Number 152. Survey no. 17–13/66/1. Hebrew ח' עַלְמִית; Arabic خِرْبَة عَلْمِيت; transliteration Kh. ʿAlmīt.
- Map ref. as printed: 1760 1369 (176000/136900). Elevation: 638 m.
- EVIDENCE: a ruin on the summit of a hill, 2 km north-east of ʿAnātā ("2 ק"מ צפונית־מזרחית לעֲנָתָא"). The reference point is the village of ʿAnātā.
- EVIDENCE: building remains, cultivation terraces, natural caves ("מערות טבעיות"), rock-cut water cisterns ("בורות מים חצובים") and remains of rock-cuttings. No number, size or position within the site is given for any of them.
- EVIDENCE, sherds: Israelite I, Israelite II, Roman, Byzantine, Arab, Medieval and Ottoman.
- Cross-reference: "לעניין הזיהוי ראה מבוא" (for the identification see the Introduction).
