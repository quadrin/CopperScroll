### Site 28: Tell er-Rās; en-Nabī ʿUzeir

- **Number:** 28. **Survey no.:** 17–17/64/1
- **Hebrew:** תֵל אֶ־רָס; אֶ־נַבִּי עֻזֵיר. **Arabic:** تَلّ الرَّأْس ; النَّبِي عُزَيْر. **Transliteration:** T. er-Rās, en-Nabī ʿUzeir
- **Map ref. / elevation as printed:** "1767 1741   575 מ'." (176700/174100; 575 m)
- **Location in the book:** begins at the foot of p. 167, col. 2 (heading and first line), and continues at the top of p. 168, col. 1.

Facts:
- EVIDENCE: a tomb on the summit of a hill west of ʿAwarta ("קבר בפסגת גבעה שממערב לעַוֶרְתָא"). The reference point is the village of ʿAwarta; the tomb is on the hilltop. No distance is given.
- CLAIM/TRADITION: the site's second name, en-Nabī ʿUzeir, is a traditional name for the tomb. The caption of the first photo calls it the tomb of "Elazar ben Aharon" (see captions).
- EVIDENCE: water cisterns of the bell type ("בורות מים מטיפוס פעמון"). No number, size or position is given, so they are on the site generally.
- EVIDENCE: hollows in the rock ("שקערוריות בסלע"), rock-cut caves ("מערות חצובות") and various other rock-cuttings. No position or size is given.
- EVIDENCE: next to the tomb, a ruined building ("ליד הקבר מבנה חרב"). CLAIM: Kallai calls it "a mosque or a Samaritan prayer house" ("שהוא מסגד או בית תפילה שומרוני"). No dimensions are given.
- EVIDENCE: on the eastern slope of the hill, a rock-cut tomb with loculi ("במורד המזרחי של הגבעה קבר חצוב ובו כוכים"). Its position is the eastern slope of the same hill. No dimensions are given.
- EVIDENCE: "2 בורות סתומים", two blocked cisterns. No position is given. The sentence follows the eastern-slope tomb, but it does not say they are on that slope.
- EVIDENCE, sherds: Israelite II, Byzantine, Arab and Ottoman.
- No date is tied to any one feature. There is no absence statement and no cross-reference to another site number. There is no "see the Introduction" line, and site 28 is not in the p. 158 identification list.
- **Photo captions.** Two photos follow the entry in p. 168, col. 1, with nothing between them and the entry:
  - (1) 'קבר "אלעזר בן אהרון".' ("The tomb of 'Elazar ben Aharon'.") The photo shows a large dressed-stone tomb monument with a sign.
  - (2) "מערת קברים במורד המזרחי." ("Burial cave on the eastern slope.") The photo shows a rock-cut doorway with a scale rod.
  - Neither caption prints a site number.
- **Table check (pp. 189–190):** 28 is in the Iron II, Byzantine, Arab and Ottoman rows, which agrees with the sherd line.

### Site 38: Kh. Sāra

- **Number:** 38. **Survey no.:** 17–16/92/1
- **Hebrew:** ח' סָרָה. **Arabic:** خِرْبَة سَارَة. **Transliteration:** Kh. Sāra
- **Map ref. as printed:** "1792 1626" (179200/162600). No elevation is printed.
- **Location in the book:** p. 169, col. 1.

Facts:
- EVIDENCE: a ruin on the northern slope of a hill, east of Kh. Seilūn ("ממזרח לח' סֵילוּן"). The reference point is Kh. Seilūn. No distance is given.
- CLAIM: Kh. Seilūn is equated with Shiloh, in parentheses: "(שילה)".
- EVIDENCE: remains of a wall in the ruin ("שרידי קיר"). No dimensions are given.
- EVIDENCE: a rock-cut cistern ("בור חצוב בסלע"), in the ruin. No size or more exact position is given.
- EVIDENCE: about 200 m north-west of the ruin, the crushing stone of an olive press ("כ־200 מ' צפונית־מערבית לה ממל של בית בד"). The reference point is the ruin.
- EVIDENCE, sherds: Byzantine and Arab. Coins: Byzantine (4th century CE). The coins are tied to the site as a whole, not to one feature.
- There are no caves, tombs, pools or shafts, no absence statement and no cross-reference. There are no photos or plan.
- **Table check:** 38 is in the Byzantine and Arab rows.

### Site 100: Kh. Kafr Nāte

- **Number:** 100. **Survey no.:** 17–14/74/1
- **Hebrew:** ח' כַּפְר נָתֶה. **Arabic:** خِرْبَة كَفْر نَاتَة. **Transliteration:** Kh. Kafr Nāte
- **Map ref. as printed:** "1771 1449" (177100/144900). No elevation is printed.
- **Location in the book:** p. 179, col. 2.

Facts:
- EVIDENCE: a ruin on the eastern slope of a flat spur, east of the road from Deir Dibwān to Mukhmās ("ממזרח לדרך מדיר־דִבְוָן למִחְ'מַס"). The reference point is the road. No distance is given.
- EVIDENCE: in the ruin, remains of buildings, fences or field walls ("גדרות"), cisterns ("בורות") and caves ("מערות"). No number, size, type or position is given for the cisterns and caves beyond "in the ruin".
- EVIDENCE: ancient building stones, some of them reused ("חלק מהן בשימוש משני"), and rock-cuttings ("חציבות").
- EVIDENCE: 300 m south-west of the ruin, the tomb of Sheikh ʿAmar ("300 מ' דרומית־מערבית לחורבה קבר שֵיח' עַמַר"). The reference point is the ruin. CLAIM/TRADITION: "Sheikh ʿAmar" is the tomb's traditional attribution.
- EVIDENCE, sherds: Medieval and Ottoman only.
- No date is tied to any one feature. There is no absence statement, no cross-reference and no "see the Introduction" line. Site 100 is not in the p. 158 list.
- **Photos:** none of this site. The photo directly above the entry in col. 2 has the caption "שבר ארכיטרב מעוטר בשריגי גפן ובאשכולות ענבים." ("Fragment of an architrave decorated with vine tendrils and grape clusters.") It follows the end of site 99, has no site number and is not attributed to site 100. The photo in col. 1, captioned "המגדל העגול." ("The round tower."), belongs beside site 97.
- **Table check:** 100 is in the Medieval and Ottoman rows.

### Site 115: Tell Maryam

- **Number:** 115. **Survey no.:** 17–14/51/1
- **Hebrew:** תֵל מַרְיַם. **Arabic:** تَلّ مَرْيَم. **Transliteration:** T. Maryam
- **Map ref. as printed:** "1755 1418" (175500/141800). No elevation is printed.
- **Location in the book:** p. 182, col. 1.

Facts:
- EVIDENCE: a fortress on the summit of a hill, 1 km west of Mukhmās ("ק"מ אחד מערבה למִחְ'מַס"). The reference point is the village of Mukhmās.
- EVIDENCE: the fortress plan is square, 26 × 26 m ("תכנית המצודה ריבועית ומידותיה 26 × 26 מ'"). Only the north wall is clearly visible; on the other sides only stretches survive.
- EVIDENCE: in the centre, a rock-cut cistern of elliptical shape, 10 × 6 m ("במרכז בור חצוב בצורה אליפטית, מידותיו 10 × 6 מ'"). The reference point is the fortress or summit ("במרכז"). Rock-cut troughs lie beside it ("לידו שקתות חצובות").
- EVIDENCE: at the foot of the hill, on the northern slope, a pool of 15 × 7 m ("לרגלי הגבעה, במורד הצפוני, בריכה שמידותיה 15 × 7 מ'"). The reference point is the hill. The text does not say whether the pool is rock-cut, built or plastered.
- EVIDENCE, sherds: Israelite I, Israelite II, Roman ("עיקר הממצא", the bulk of the finds) and Byzantine. No period is tied to the fortress, cistern or pool.
- No caves, tombs or shafts are mentioned, and there is no absence statement.
- Cross-reference: "לעניין הזיהוי ראה מבוא" (for the identification see the Introduction). See C.1 below for the p. 158 text.
- **Photos:** none of this site. The photo higher in col. 1, captioned "פתח למערת קברים." ("Entrance to a burial cave."), stands above the heading of site 114 and is not attributed to site 115.
- **Table check:** 115 is in the Iron I, Iron II, Roman and Byzantine rows.

### Site 119: Kh. ed-Duweir

- **Number:** 119. **Survey no.:** 17–14/71/1
- **Hebrew:** ח' אֶ־דְוֵיר. **Arabic:** خِرْبَة الدُّوَيْر. **Transliteration:** Kh. ed-Duweir
- **Map ref. as printed:** "1771 1412" (177100/141200). No elevation is printed for the site; the text names hill trig point 618.
- **Location in the book:** p. 182, col. 2.
- **Note:** the English index on p. 192 has two entries named "Duweir, Kh. ed-": numbers 119 and 120. Only 119 is extracted here.

Facts:
- EVIDENCE: a ruin on the southern slope of the hill with trig point 618, 400 m south of Kh. el-Qubbe ("400 מ' מדרום לח' אֶל־קֻבֶּה (אתר 118)"). The reference points are trig point 618 and site 118.
- EVIDENCE: building remains, decorated building stones, a stone with a cross ornament ("אבן עם עיטור צלב") and mosaic stones (tesserae, "אבני פסיפס"). No positions are given beyond "in the ruin".
- EVIDENCE: a well mouth and troughs ("פי באר ושקתות"), in the ruin. No size or more exact position is given.
- EVIDENCE: a natural cave that was used for storing water ("מערה טבעית ששימשה לאגירת מים"). No position or size is given.
- EVIDENCE: channels and rock-cuttings ("תעלות וחציבות"). No position is given.
- EVIDENCE: in the north of the ruin, the tomb of Sheikh Dweir, built of stones taken from the ruin ("בצפון החורבה קבר שֵיח' דְוֵיר הבנוי אבנים שנלקחו מהחורבה"). The reference point is the ruin. CLAIM/TRADITION: "Sheikh Dweir" is the tomb's traditional attribution.
- EVIDENCE, sherds: Byzantine, Medieval and Ottoman. No period is tied to any one feature.
- There is no absence statement and no "see the Introduction" line. Cross-reference: site 118. Site 119 is not in the p. 158 list.
- **Photo caption:** one photo in col. 2, directly below the entry, captioned "פי באר ושקתות מאבן." ("Well mouth and troughs of stone."). It prints no site number. It is placed immediately after site 119 (the last entry in that column), and its wording matches the entry's "פי באר ושקתות".
- **Table check:** 119 is in the Byzantine, Medieval and Ottoman rows.

### C.3 Gophna & Porat chapter: title page and pp. 196–197

- **Title page (efe312fb):** "הסקר בארץ אפרים ומנשה" (The survey in the land of Ephraim and Manasseh). Authors: **רם גופנא; יוסף פורת** (Ram Gophna, Yosef Porath). The facing page is blank. The printed page number shows only as show-through and cannot be read.
- **Heading on p. 196:** "מבוא" (Introduction). p. 197 continues with period sections: "התקופה הכנענית התיכונה I" and "התקופה הכנענית התיכונה II". The p. 196 sections after the opening paragraphs are "התקופה הכנענית הקדומה".
- **Survey area:** Team (חוליה) D surveyed the northern part of the Samaria hills block, north of the Fāriʿa and Shechem wadis. South of that, it surveyed a narrow strip on the western slopes of the Samaria hills, from Naḥal Shechem in the north to the Aijalon valley in the south.
- **Method:** the team examined the sites marked on the maps (tells, ruins and existing Arab villages). It recorded topographic setting and surface sherds, period by period. The text says the typical hill site is the khirbeh, usually on knoll tops or spur ends and built in terraces. Most khirbehs have been damaged by stone clearance and cultivation, a process that peaked in the last generation. A few sites have the shape of an artificial tell, generally near inner valleys or the large valleys around the Samaria hills.
- **Coverage and completeness (p. 196, col. 1, para. 2):**
  > "הסקר הארכיאולוגי עסק בבדיקת האתרים המסומנים במפות: תלים, חורבות ויישובים ערביים קיימים. מתוך כ־450 אתרים, המצויים בשטח הנסקר, לפי המסומן במפות, נבדקו ע"י החוליה כ־250, שהם 55 אחוז. אתרים בודדים נוספים, שלא נודעו עד כה, נתגלו ע"י החוליה במהלך הסקר."
  - Translation: "The archaeological survey dealt with examining the sites marked on the maps: tells, ruins and existing Arab villages. Of c. 450 sites in the surveyed area, according to what is marked on the maps, c. 250 were examined by the team, which is 55 per cent. A few additional sites, not known until now, were discovered by the team during the survey."
- Also on p. 196, verbatim: "במקומות בודדים ונידחים בלבד ניתן עדיין לראות חורבות שלא נפגעו." ("Only in a few remote places can undamaged ruins still be seen.")
