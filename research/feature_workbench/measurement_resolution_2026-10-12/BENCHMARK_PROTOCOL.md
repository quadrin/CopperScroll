# Finite geometry benchmark preparation contract

Status: prepared, not run and not empirically frozen. A repository commit of this contract records the proposed procedure; it does not reserve truth, assign reviewers or supply an eligible case. Existing PR31/32 source families remain exposed. This extends the existing [reserved recovery procedure](../../shared_tools/recovery_benchmark/RESERVED_BATCH.md), without replacing its audit gates or opening an incoming record.

## One claim and finite alternatives

Claim: a scale-bar plan measurement can distinguish explicitly defined physical reference spans at the accuracy required for a specified entry branch. Documentary prose agreement, independent survey accuracy and source-feature recovery receive separate scores.

For the first intake, the curator selects exactly one plan/field-record family with two named endpoints, coordinate/vertical datum, unit and phase metadata, a readable unaltered scale and an independently validated second measurement record. The curator must establish that the reference reflects a distinct measured observation rather than a redrawing or repeated description. If no such family exists, intake ends ineligible; no substitute or manufactured truth follows. Named possible dependencies are IV/17's L-656 threshold/section record and a separately measured survey; archive existence, independent lineage and usability remain unverified. A received file goes through the existing arrival protocol before screening.

The curator fixes one scalar span and one bearing/component relation only if their physical endpoints and north convention are observed; otherwise the missing relation stays unscorable. For scalar geometry compare the legacy fixed unit choices **0.445, 0.500 and 0.525 m** as exploratory scenarios only. For a three-cubit threshold claim their exact predictions are **1.335, 1.500 and 1.575 m**. The broader **0.40–0.60** continuum remains a separate unresolved-unit branch and cannot be replaced by a convenient winning unit. A field reference that matches one target receives no Copper Scroll identification score.

## What must be fixed before reviewer access

The coordinator supplies finite lists of all allowed endpoint definitions, face/surface datums, phase assignments, axes and unit options from the source and textual alternatives. Every unobserved definition remains null. Fix the plan raster hash, scale/bar picks procedure, coordinate convention and independent reference provenance. The reviewer must receive only the permitted model/plan packet, without the answer key or access to the independent reference.

The primary measurement is the span between the fixed named endpoints, not a longest floor chord substituted after disagreement. Annotate each endpoint once and record every predeclared ambiguous edge variant. Pick scale-bar outer ticks without calibrating to the target answer. Freeze pixels, variants, predictions, declared errors and packet hashes in an actual commit before truth release. Never move endpoints, fit a correction to the answer, drop a failed case or reinterpret the reference after grading.

The reviewer predicts a finite interval for the independently defined physical span before release. The error model must identify endpoint, scale, datum, survey, drafting/distortion and transform-differential components, with evidence for their bounds or covariances. A missing component makes independent accuracy unscorable. The interval must have enough resolution for the precise comparison: non-overlapping equal-window requirements follow `E<G/2`; for the closest 0.500/0.525 three-cubit pair that requires **E<0.0375 m**. This is a mathematical resolution requirement, not a presumed survey tolerance. Independently reported reference uncertainty must also enter the comparison under the specified model.

Fit and withheld registration controls retain the applicable existing protocol's point-count, hull and truth-access rules. Never use the test target as a control. Report fit residuals and held-out residuals separately; a good fit cannot supply an independent pair-local accuracy estimate. Scalar-length tests can discard common rigid translation/rotation but must retain scale/differential error; cardinal relations also retain angular uncertainty.

## Scoring and one-pass stop

The coordinator validates reference provenance and then runs the existing reserved-batch audit. Missing roles, exposure history, verified reference, actual pre-reveal commit or a case remain hard blockers. A hash alone proves neither separation nor independent observation lineage. Reviewers who have seen a reference or a derivative report cannot score that case as unused.

Reveal truth once after the recorded freeze. Report every predeclared branch, including false precise claims, discrepancies, correct abstentions and unmeasurable targets. Compare the reviewer interval with the independently measured reference interval. Entirely disjoint intervals contradict that span prediction under the fixed assumptions; overlap supplies compatibility only. Ability to distinguish the competing intervals is a separate result, and an unresolved reference cannot become a pass.

Close the finite benchmark with one of the registered terminal outcomes. A failed prediction retains the pixels and complete result. Any revised endpoint, datum or model produces a new exploratory procedure; the same revealed truth cannot serve as its independent confirmation. Broader calibration needs later independent families, with its selection and sample-size rule fixed before they are sampled.
