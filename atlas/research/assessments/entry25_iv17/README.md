## Requirements and observations

**Two openings and pillar:** the description explicitly reports two openings in the eastern cliff, northern and southern spaces, and a rock-cut dividing pillar. The northern opening is reported as 0.8 m wide; it leads to a 2.2 × 4.5 m space. The southern space is approximately 2.15 × 4.20 m; masonry partly blocks its mouth. These support the pillar branch. They do not resolve whether the ancient landmark term meant a pillar or terrace.

**Internal connection:** Plan 5 depicts a continuous interior route around the inner end of the pillar; no drawn dividing wall closes that route. This is a two-dimensional interpretation of surveyed geometry. The plan supplies no passage clearance, level changes or proof of an ancient traversable connection. Entry 25 does not explicitly require passage between its mouths, so connectivity adds no independent identification criterion.

**Northern versus southern:** after orienting the plan by its north arrow, the right opening is the northern one; the left, partly blocked opening is southern. Page-left/page-right are not compass labels. The repeat’s early frozen-key reversal remains audited and unchanged. Northern-minus-southern midpoint offsets are about 2.63 m north and 3.30 m east, with midpoint separation about 4.22 m. Thus the plan distinguishes a northern member even though the pair is not on a north–south line.

**Facing:** the independent present-aperture chord normals are approximately 123° northern and 119° southern, clockwise from the drawn north arrow. These point southeast at their nominal values. Northern endpoint sensitivity is approximately 99.5–140.8°; southern is 104.0–132.4°. Preserve the pilot’s separate broader bounds as well. These are chord-normal proxies, not surveyed passage bearings; north convention, scan distortion and pre-blocking geometry remain unknown.

Cave-level eastern-cliff aspect has published support. A requirement of exactly 90° is in tension with the nominal proxies, but the edition supplies no such precision and systematic errors remain unquantified. No exact-east rejection follows. If future work uses an angular sector, it must declare that sector as a project assumption before testing. The southern current gap cannot substitute for the full earlier mouth.

**Construction phase:** the inspected p. 63 interprets use from the first half of the second century BCE to the first half of the first century BCE. It assigns blocking a concealment role during conflict involving Hasmoneans. This is an excavator’s reconstruction. Survey coins and excavation arrowhead basket numbers have no published sealed wall/threshold contact in the inspected chain. The entrance, pillar and wall dates therefore remain unresolved. An earlier archive README’s “third century” summary is corrected to “second century” from the page image and prior phase audit.

**Northern digging datum:** no ancient threshold elevation, threshold-specific section or dated floor contact is supplied. Three cubits becomes 1.20–1.80 m only under the project’s exploratory 0.40–0.60 m unit range. Under a vertical model, the target relation is z = z_threshold − depth; z_threshold is unknown. Under a horizontal model, direction and origin within the mouth remain unknown. The cave’s reported 1.4–1.7 m height supplies neither missing datum and does not limit excavation below its floor.
