# Eshel/Greenhut 1993: missing plate recovery

14 October 2026 UTC. Target: the images cited as Pl. Aa and Pl. Ab on printed p.254 in Hanan Eshel and Zvi Greenhut, “Ḥiam el-Sagha, a Cemetery of the Qumran Type, Judaean Desert,” *Revue Biblique* 100/2 (April 1993), pp.252–259. This was a physical-copy repair, not a new interpretation of the cemetery.

**Not obtained.** A bounded check found the issue's separately archived Back Matter but did not recover its pages or captions. The supplied article remains complete for its available text and Figs.1–3; the cited plate images remain uninspected. Neither the Back Matter title nor its position in the issue establishes that it contains the missing plate.
