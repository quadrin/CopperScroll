The [registration directory](../../registration/) contains the calculations, source points, object joins and feature records. Source PDFs and protected book scans remain outside Git. The diagnostic figure uses original project-created points.

**Assessment:** Hyrcania remains possible, low; Christmas and Salvadora remain regional research leads without an assigned entry. These results improve source and feature specificity without establishing a Copper Scroll deposit.

The most useful LLM workflow now is to search for missing **relationships** in records: an accession attached to a bag, a bag to a trench, a trench to a plan, or a surviving structure to a surveyed control point. Preserve every source and reject joins that rely only on a shared prefix, nearby number or familiar landmark word.
