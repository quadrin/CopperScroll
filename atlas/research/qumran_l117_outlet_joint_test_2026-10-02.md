# L117 outlet, raised sides and steps: illustration test

2 October 2026 (UTC). R01 / historical Q38, Q41–Q42. Original project-authored assessment.

## Result

**Inconclusive:** the available plan and pool section establish a measurable architectural profile, but the inspected coverage does not establish that L117's outlet, raised sides and added steps were made in one alteration. This leaves the proposed chronological linkage unproved. It does not demonstrate separate dates or exclude a common alteration.

The specific citation also needs qualification: Stacey's [2009 overflow discussion](https://bibleinterp.arizona.edu/articles/stacey), paragraph beginning with the channel's relation to the pool's late stage, invokes de Vaux 1973 p. 9 for raising L117 together with L110. That page explicitly describes heightening the **round cistern L110**, the higher supply channel, basin 119 bis and redistribution to the three cisterns. It does not explicitly describe heightening L117 or adding its steps in that paragraph. Other records could supply that relationship; this citation alone does not.

## Hypothesis and retained reading conditions

Hypothesis: a documented construction contact connects L117's outlet to the raised sides and added steps, allowing those components to share an alteration date. Required evidence includes an identifiable masonry/plaster joint, levels, component order and a context tied to that particular alteration.

This is a construction-phase subtest within the existing entries 20–23 model. The edition alternatives, proposed Qumran/Sekakah association, intake versus subsection-head alternatives and conditional fissure geometry remain as recorded in the [originals review](https://github.com/quadrin/CopperScroll/blob/main/research/sources/qumran_supplied_originals_review_2026-10-01.md). No reading alternative is eliminated by a pool section.
