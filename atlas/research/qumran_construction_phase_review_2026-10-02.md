2 October 2026 (UTC). R01 / historical Q38, Q41–Q42.

The supplied report documents a local sequence around cistern L110: an earlier channel beneath room floors, then a higher central channel, a raised cistern and replacement settling pool. It does not provide a surviving continuous junction that dates each upper-gorge feature through that sequence. Retain relative order separately from absolute dates and from the proposed functions of the waterworks.

## Competing chronologies and their dependencies

Taylor and Gibson 2020, printed pp. 215–216, note 58 (supplied offprint PDF pp. 51–52), explicitly leave the original aqueduct date uncertain. They report use in Period II and cite de Vaux 1973 p. 8 for Period Ib, Humbert et al. 2016 pp. 425–432 for construction after approximately 31 BCE, and Magen–Peleg's Hasmonean architectural comparison. These are attributed positions in an inspected published discussion. The original de Vaux and Humbert pages were not newly inspected in this pass.

[Published offprint](https://kclpure.kcl.ac.uk/portal/files/139004734/Iron_Age_Qumran_Taylor.pdf).

David Stacey's [June 2017 authored response](https://bibleinterp.arizona.edu/articles/2017/06/sta418020) was read directly, scoped to dam/channel dating and floors (paragraphs beginning with the dam discussion through the northern-dump argument). He proposes a dam around 20–15 BCE, connects internal redistribution to it, reconstructs higher floors lost through erosion, and uses the L117 overflow above the northern dump as a chronological argument. These are his interpretations of earlier excavations and comparisons, not new excavated observations. A date for a later overflow would not alone date initial aqueduct construction. Verify the contact, deposited material and construction linkage in the original records.

The contradiction to preserve is the construction date of particular components, not simply which period label an author uses. A later distribution-channel alteration can coexist with an earlier supply route. The currently inspected evidence does not identify all those components as one construction event.
