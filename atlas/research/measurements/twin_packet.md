## Parallel measures

### TC-01 — Opening identity and dimensions

Status: descriptive pair ready; metric measurement awaiting source.

Record the northern and southern exterior openings as separate features. For each, digitize both jambs, mouth centre, sill and roof boundary from a scaled survey; report clear width and height in metres at a specified section plane. Identify any walling, erosion or collapse that changes the boundary. Preserve surveyed modern and reconstructed ancient outlines separately. Record original photograph/plan labels before linking Cave A/B to the cardinal units.

Dependency: cave-specific scaled entrance elevation or surveyed 3D dataset with feature labels, scale/calibration and construction-phase attribution. An unlabelled photo supplies neither identity certainty nor metric dimensions. Entry 25 specifies two openings; it supplies no required entrance width or height. These dimensions document the arrangement and anchor subsequent work.

### TC-03 — Cave aspect and opening bearings

Status: published eastward aspect ready; numeric bearings awaiting source.

Retain two textual models: cave-level eastward aspect, supported by Milik 1960 p. 140 item 27; and the stricter project requirement that each mouth face east. Define an outward horizontal normal to each surveyed mouth plane. Record azimuth clockwise from true north, the survey's original north convention and any grid/magnetic correction. If irregular mouths produce unstable normals, report the angle range from reasonable boundary fits and document the fitting method. Record the overall cliff/cave aspect independently.

Dependency: scaled oriented entrance survey and an explicit north convention. Bar-Adon's east-facing description supports the qualitative comparison under both models. It supplies no azimuth precision. Do not choose a numerical definition of “east” merely to obtain a pass. Report the bearings first and retain any declared directional tolerance as a sensitivity assumption. Original entry-specific Puech/Lefkovits apparatus verification remains pending at the links in the direction review.

### TC-06 — Northern threshold datum and depth envelope

Status: awaiting source; unit sensitivity calculation ready as a conditional model.

Identify every surviving/reconstructed northern sill or entrance-floor phase. Record datum elevation, surface extent, formation/use date and relation to excavation trenches. Once a dated datum is defensible, model three cubits using 0.40–0.60 m/cubit: 1.20–1.80 m below that datum under the retained vertical-depth interpretation. Store the hypothetical depth interval relative to the datum, not as a located deposit. If the datum elevation is represented by an interval, propagate that interval into the target elevation interval.

Dependency: threshold-specific stratigraphic section, benchmark and recorded trench/locus depths connected to a dated surface. The report's several-metre deposit cannot provide the missing datum. The wording does not by itself fix the precise horizontal digging point within the opening; preserve that uncertainty separately from the depth range. Do not attach this model to the modern path, present dung surface or site-centre marker.
