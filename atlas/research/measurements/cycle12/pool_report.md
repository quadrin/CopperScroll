## Phase/contact result

**Observed in figures:** Figs. 9–10 show the larger northern stair complex over an ashlar course. Fig. 11 shows W114's sloping plaster face. Fig. 12 shows plastered phase 3b beneath ashlar phase 3a after removal; these are northern stair/seating phases. Figure 5 supplies an excavation plan, with a tiny north marker and scale, rather than a vertical receiving-pool section.

**Authors' interpretation:** W114 and W185A belong to an earlier large pool. Its relationship to the dam remains expressly unresolved in p. 33*, note 3, owing to depth and incoming water. Page 34* tentatively includes the small dam-top pool using the cited radiocarbon summary.

**Assessment:** No figure establishes an older L105 floor, revises its foundation coin association, or joins W114 to the small receiving-pool floor. Whole-article text contains no L105/L105A/L103/L108 labels. Keep cycle10's conditional post-minting 69–70 CE floor terminus. Older dam, larger pool and northern stair/seating phases remain separate chronological observations.
