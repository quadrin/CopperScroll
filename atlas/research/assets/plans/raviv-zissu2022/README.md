# Raviv and Zissu 2022: access and inspection

[Authenticated Drive article](https://drive.google.com/file/d/16W1oIoz9ttdOz26TTFKFAXoPA9WC3w9F/view), *Opera Ipogea* 2 (2022), pp. 39–54, DOI 10.57588/SSIOI22022/39-54. The [manifest](manifest.json) records the file hash, inspection scope, figure credits and restricted reproduction status.

Pages 40–43 distinguish earlier underground facilities, their later hiding adaptations and purpose-hewn hiding complexes. Figures 6A–6B show interruption of an earlier quarry’s entrance and wall at H. Qasra; Figure 9 describes an originally purpose-hewn complex at H. Naqiq. These are regional comparisons. They do not supply IV/17’s missing wall/threshold contacts or construction phase. Modern photographs remain linked at the authenticated source; no open licence is inferred.
