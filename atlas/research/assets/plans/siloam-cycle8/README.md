# Siloam: original plans and site photographs

Archived 2 October 2026. Thirty-five original Israel Antiquities Authority JPEGs, unchanged: 2013 Figs. 1–13, 2014 Figs. 1–15 and 2022 Figs. 1–7. Total: 3,080,809 bytes. Credit: **Israel Antiquities Authority**, with individual publication metadata in the [manifest](figure_manifest.json). Original report captions remain available at each report link; labels below are project-authored inventory descriptions.

The manifest records exact original URLs, SHA-256, dimensions, access date, inspection flags, credits and reconstruction status. An archived image does not imply its archaeology or measurements have been assessed. Figure numbering belongs to its own report. No geographic registration or newly computed distance is attached to these files.

## Pending originals

Twelve figure groups in Szanton, Vukosavović and Berko, *New archaeological discoveries in the excavations of Birket al-Hamra in Jerusalem* (2024), plus Guthe's original plates and Bliss–Dickie's original plans, remain pending. The manifest gives exact page/figure locators, source URLs and observed access limits. Selective text access to the 2024 PDF is not an original-image archive. Isolated artifact illustrations in 2014 Figs. 16–18 are excluded from the geography/ruins scope; in situ masonry Fig. 14 is included.
