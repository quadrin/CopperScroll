- [Hamam el-Malih plans and context](hamam-el-malih-plans-context.pdf): part 4 PDF pages 12–16, printed pp. 299–303. [Figure 227 page](fig227-hamam-malih.png), printed p. 301, shows the aqueduct/bath complex.
- [Desert fringes, unit 21](desert-fringes-unit21-context.pdf): part 6 PDF pages 66–68, printed pp. 553–555.

These are selected context pages, not an inventory of every figure in the book. Eastern Manasseh’s Buqei‘ah must not be equated with the Judaean Buqei‘a near Qumran on the name alone.
