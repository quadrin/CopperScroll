# Entry 46 bath source figures

Original figure/map/plan/photo/table pages from Billig 2018, Greenberg and Cinamon 2011, and Reʾem and de Vincenz 2006. Source publication locators, captions, hashes, attribution and noncommercial reuse conditions are in manifest.json. The current ʿAtiqot journal policy licenses article content under CC BY-NC 4.0, with separately owned third-party content excluded: https://publications.iaa.org.il/atiqot/policies.html.

Pages are uncropped, unannotated raster format conversions preserving original figure captions, scales and north arrows. Text-only pages and Armon Fig. 1 credited to Amit 2010 are not included. Relative asset_path values in the assessment’s manifest resolve from that evidence folder; research_archive_target and atlas_archive_target resolve from the repository root.
