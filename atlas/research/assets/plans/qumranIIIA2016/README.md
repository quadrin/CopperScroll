# Qumran III A (2016): attachment identity

The reattached 536-page PDF is Jean-Baptiste Humbert and Alain Chambon, Khirbet Qumrân et Aïn Feshkha: Fouilles du P. Roland de Vaux, III A: L’archéologie de Qumrân — Reconsidération de l’interprétation; Les installations périphériques de Khirbet Qumrân (Vandenhoeck & Ruprecht, 2016). [Title and copyright pages](title-copyright.pdf), source PDF pages 3–4, verify this identity. The book includes a notice by Hervé Monchot and Qumran Terracotta Oil Lamps by Jolanta Młynarczyk. Copyright page gives electronic ISBN 978-3-666-54054-7.

This is distinct from Humbert–Gunneweg volume II (2003), which contains Katharina Galor’s “Plastered Pools, A New Perspective” and L138 Fig. 3 p. 294. The volume II image-recovery gap remains open.
