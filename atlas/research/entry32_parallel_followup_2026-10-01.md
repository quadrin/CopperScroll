## Textual conditions retained

The project's edition records in `text/readings.json` preserve the alternatives that affect this test: Milik 1962, D20 p. 265, and Puech 2006 p. 192 / 2015 p. 67 associate the place name with Koziba; Lefkovits 2000 p. 236 reads Buz. Eshel 2002, CSS pp. 105–106, interprets the water landmark as the aqueduct head. The second landmark differs between Milik's retaining wall, Puech 2015 p. 68's parapet/mountain, and Eshel's rock, CSS p. 105. These are inherited edition-based readings, not a new manuscript transcription.

The wall test therefore remains conditional on both the outlet interpretation and a built second landmark. Under the existing outlet-plus-stone-row model, three cubits correspond illustratively to 1.35–1.65 m using 0.45–0.55 m per cubit. Neither conversion establishes ancient ground level; the entry supplies no bearing. A springhead or rock/mountain reading requires a different spatial test.

## Porath: route control rather than a wall date

Yosef Porath, [*טיח באמות מים כאינדיקטור כרונולוגי*](https://benyehuda.org/read/71196), electronic edition of the 1989 chapter. Bibliographic chapter range: pp. 69–76; **individual printed pages have not been verified**. Cite the HTML subsections **אמת פרת צפון** and **אמת פרת דרום**, and notes 31–34. Text is publicly accessible with publisher permission; the linked Fig. 1 image was not recovered/inspected.

Prat North carries **Ein Qelt** water. Porath reports B's foundation inside A's former channel: observed relative superposition. Later route C crosses north to south on an arched bridge about **500 m west of the monastery**, then follows the southern slope eastward. Prat South instead draws from **Fara, Fajira and Fawwar**, passing Cypros farther east. Note 34 relates a late open-channel segment near Beit Jabr Fauqani to Meshel–Amit **segment 28**, not outlet Point 25. Destination history contributes to the proposed dates; older plaster recipes can persist later.

**Project inference:** a south-bank channel need not belong to the Cypros supply. No checked passage expressly correlates C's wall with SWP's opposite-monastery wall or provides a plaster sample/section from its lower culvert. Bank position and regional plaster chronology therefore cannot select its phase.
