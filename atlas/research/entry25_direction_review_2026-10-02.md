**Published translation:** Milik describes a cave having two entrances and facing east (p. 140). His p. 147 commentary discusses two east-facing caves corresponding to VI 2 and VI 8/9; these are successive entries, not the two mouths of entry 25. He proposes a conspicuous double cave between W. Jaufat Zabin and Cave XI. This geographical proposal is his interpretation; these pages supply no measured entrance survey or dated threshold. Do not treat his location proposal and its repetition as independent corroboration of Twin Cave.

**Project inference:** the unvocalized הצופא after plural פתחין invites an attachment check. Its apparent singular form is insufficient by itself to settle the original grammar or exclude an interpretation involving both mouths. Milik's translation supplies an explicit cave-level reading. “Northern opening” identifies the northern member of a pair; it does not require that mouth to face north.
