# Yadin on Bethso and the Shaveh (entries 36–37)

**Labels:** **Evidence** = Yadin states it. **Inference** = drawn here. **BK** = none used.

- **Evidence (p. 302).** Context: Josephus' First Wall (*War* 5.145) runs from Hippicus past "Bethso" to the Gate of the Essenes. Yadin places both Bethso and the gate in the western section of the city, Bethso near Hippicus. He follows Robinson, Schwarz and Dalman in reading Bethso as *bêt ṣôʾâ*, a latrine (nn. 26–29).
- **Evidence (p. 302, n. 30).** He reports Milik's proposal (*DJD* III p. 274, and p. 241) that Bethso is *bêt ha-Šawe*, the השוא of the Copper Scroll, identified with the graveyard south-west of Jerusalem. He finds it hard to accept, because the Copper Scroll names השוא, not בית שוא.
- **Evidence (p. 303).** "It is almost certain that השוא is to be identified with עמק השוא, the vale of the king in the *Beth ha-Kerem* plain according to the Genesis Apocryphon", quoting 1QapGen xxii 13–14 (בעמק שוא והוא עמק מלכא בקעת בית כרמא). He says this makes the identification with Bethso, in the north-western part of the city, impossible.
- **Evidence (pp. 302–303).** Yadin gives no location for the Beth ha-Kerem plain, no map reference and no modern valley name.

## Effect (applied under test U1)

- **Inference.** Yadin and Puech both take the Shaveh to be the Genesis Apocryphon's "valley of Beth ha-Kerem". That is one shared identification, against Milik's Bethso chain. But neither Yadin nor the repo's Puech record puts the Beth ha-Kerem plain in a named, located valley, so the identification fixes a name, not a district. U1 requires a located valley. **No P level changes** for 36 or 37 (jer_shaveh, jer_baqa, jer_kidron_mon).
- **Inference (jer_baqa).** Yadin explicitly rejects Milik's Shaveh = Bethso step, which is the basis of jer_baqa's P. Under U1 this does not lower P, because Beth ha-Kerem could itself be in the Baqʿa / Rephaim plain; it changes the basis from "Milik's chain" to "Milik's chain, rejected by Yadin; the Beth ha-Kerem identification is unlocated".
- **Next record (Q22).** A source that locates the Genesis Apocryphon's Beth ha-Kerem plain (for example, the Ramat Raḥel identification of Beth ha-Kerem and the plain between it and the city), argued independently of the Copper Scroll.
