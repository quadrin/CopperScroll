# Plate check, round 4: the four lines round 1 could not locate (frozen 17 October 2026, before any crop was made)

**Purpose.** In round 1, four lines were not resolved because the crops did not show the target securely ([results](../../../research/text/plate_check.md), "Next" item 1). This round re-crops them. Each line is fixed first on Puech's facsimile, not by height, and then two fresh readers repeat the blind questions. Questions: [QUESTIONS.md](QUESTIONS.md).

**Images.**

  - image A: the copy photograph (pl. CCCLXXI for column VII, CCCLXXIII for VIII, CCCLXXVII for X);
  - images B and C: the column's two radiograph plates (CCCXLV–CCCXLVI, CCCXLVII–CCCXLVIII, CCCLI–CCCLII).

**Locating (non-blind).** The locator finds each line on Puech's facsimile plate for the column. Then they mark it on each photograph and radiograph by counting lines and matching word shapes, not by height alone. Each crop shows the target line with one line above and below, and the locator records an arrow position for the target row. The locator records a confidence. A crop the locator cannot place with at least "probable" confidence is not shown, and that image counts as missing.

**Readers.** Two fresh subagent sessions (R7, R8), each with only the crops, the [reader instructions](../INSTRUCTIONS.md) and, after Stage 1 is saved, the questions. They are not told the column, entry, editions, earlier rounds or this file.

**Decision rule (fixed now; the same as round 2).**
- **Supports X:** both readers report the discriminating letter, sign or count as X, graded at least "probable" on some image in Stage 1 or Stage 2, and neither grades the alternative "probable" or "certain".
- **Leans to X:** one reader reports X at "probable" or better, and the other reports X at "possible" or cannot tell; no one grades the alternative "probable" or better.
- **Cannot decide:** anything else, including split readers.
- An answer about the wrong line is not counted; if that leaves one reader, the best verdict is "leans".
- After the blind pass, a non-blind check against the same crops may downgrade a verdict, never upgrade it.
- For counts (P56, P63), a reader's count supports X only if it equals X and the reader does not report damage that could hide strokes. A count reported as "at least n" is compatible with every X ≥ n and decides nothing alone.

**Round 1 is set aside.** Round 1's answers on these lines came from crops that did not securely show the line, so they are not combined with this round. This round's verdict stands alone.

**Effect on ratings (fixed now; the same as round 2).** A "supports" verdict that agrees with two or more editions against one can make R 2, under the rubric's definition. "Leans" against an anchor reading makes it "a reading the plate check leans against" (R 1). "Cannot decide" changes nothing. No P, F or D level moves on a plate result alone.
