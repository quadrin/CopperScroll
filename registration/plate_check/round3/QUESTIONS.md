# Stage 2 questions, round 3 (open only after writing your Stage 1 file)

Answer only for your assigned items. Positions count words or letters from the right unless stated. "Cannot tell" is a valid answer; say what would be needed. Grade every letter you give: certain, probable, possible, illegible.

P38. The word after a word read as בית (or similar): give each of its four letters. (a) First letter: ה (left leg free of the roof), ח (left leg joined to the roof) or ת (left leg with a foot to the left)? (b) Second letter: מ or ב? (c) Third letter: ד (square corner, roof projecting to the right) or ר (rounded corner)? (d) Fourth letter: ה, ח or ת? Say which stroke decides each answer.
P68. The second word (after a word beginning ב-ש or similar): give each letter. (a) Is the letter after ל an ה (left leg free) or a ח (left leg joined to the roof)? (b) Is there a further letter after it, such as a closed box (ם), before the next word? (c) Are any letters marked as cancelled?
P71. (a) The first word: give its letters. Is its second letter ר (rounded corner) or ד (square corner)? Is its third letter ו, י or ר? Is its last letter י, ו, ר, ן or other? (b) Further along, a word beginning ב-צ: give each letter. Is its third letter ו, י or ר? Is anything written above the line near it?
P79. Find the word that precedes a word beginning ה-ט (or similar). Give each of its letters. (a) Before the ק, is there one letter (a closed box with a flat base, מ) or two letters (a short נ followed by a small י)? (b) What follows the ק? (c) How does the word end?
P80. Find a word beginning ה followed by ס (or a similar letter). Give each letter. (a) Is the letter after ס a ב (base stroke projecting beyond the right vertical) or a כ (rounded, no projecting base)? (b) Is the last letter a long final stroke (ן) or other?
P82. Find the word that follows a word beginning ב-נ (or similar). Give each of its letters. Is its last letter a closed box (ם/מ), ב, ט, or other? Then find a later word of four or five letters containing ח or ה: give each letter. Is its first letter י, ט or ה? Is its second letter ה or ר (or other)? Is there a ו in it, and where?
P85. The second word (after a word beginning ב-ק): give each letter. (a) Is the letter after ל an ה (left leg free) or a ח (left leg joined to the roof)? (b) Is there a further letter after it, such as a closed box (ם), before the next word? (c) Are any letters marked as cancelled?
P96. After the first word (which may begin ב-ח), describe every letter or trace up to the next clearly legible word. How many letter-spaces are there? List each recognisable letter with its position and grade. Are traces of ש, ל, ד, י, א, ט, נ or ב visible? What is the last letter before the next word?
P99. The first word: give each letter. (a) Is the second letter ק? (b) Is there a ו after the ק? (c) Which letters follow, in order: מ, ע, ב, ה or others? (d) What is the last letter?
