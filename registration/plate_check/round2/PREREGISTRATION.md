**Readers.** Two fresh subagent sessions (R3, R4), each with only the crops, the instructions and, after Stage 1 is saved, the questions. They are not told the column, entry, editions or this file.

**Decision rule (fixed now).**

- **Supports X:** both readers report the discriminating letter or sign as X, graded at least "probable" in Stage 1 or Stage 2, and neither grades the alternative "probable" or "certain".
- **Leans to X:** one reader reports X at "probable" or better, and the other reports X at "possible" or cannot tell; no one grades the alternative "probable" or better.
- **Cannot decide:** anything else, including split readers.
- An answer about the wrong line (crop offset) is not counted; if that leaves one reader, the best verdict is "leans".
- After the blind pass, a non-blind check against the same crops and Puech's commentary may downgrade a verdict (to "leans" or "cannot decide") but never upgrade it.

**Effect on ratings (fixed now).** Under the rubric's R definitions: a "supports" verdict against an edition's letters counts as a plate-check finding against that reading; "leans" against an anchor reading makes it "a reading the plate check leans against" (R 1). A "supports" verdict that agrees with two editions against a third can make R 2 ("one edition dissents on the letters while the others and any plate check agree"). "Cannot decide" changes nothing. No P, F or D level moves on a plate result alone.
