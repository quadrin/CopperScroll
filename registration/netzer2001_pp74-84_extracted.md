# Netzer 2001 pp. 74–84: the two early pools A(B)101 and their supply (entry 29)

**Labels:** **Evidence** = Netzer states it. **Inference** = drawn here. **BK** = none used. Quotes are from OCR text unless marked as checked on the page image.

## The pools (pp. 74–76)

- **Evidence.** The visible combined pool is about 32 × 18 m; all sidewalls except the eastern survive almost to full height; the central podium was "created when the two original pools were combined into one" (p. 74).
- **Evidence.** The western walls of the two pools are aligned; the eastern walls are at a slight angle to each other (p. 74). All sidewalls were free-standing, with foundation trenches (Sounding A(B)23, west of the northern pool) (p. 74).
- **Evidence.** Walls of fieldstones and cobbles in lime mortar; bottoms and lower walls, to about 1 m below the top, coated with hydraulic ash-lime plaster; upper walls, benches and tops with lime plaster (p. 75).
- **Evidence.** Both pools were entered by flights of steps 2.3 m wide, from the south-west corner along the west wall; the upper part of the northern flight "was dismantled when the two pools were joined together" (pp. 75–76).

## The Plaza (pp. 76–77)

- **Evidence.** The Plaza around the pools was about 45 m (E–W) by 40 m. The pool tops were meant to be level with the Plaza floor, so the pools and benches lay below it (p. 76). In Sounding A(B)23, next to the northern pool, the fill under the Plaza floor was 1.70 m deep (p. 77).

## The Na'aran Conduit through the complex (pp. 78–79)

- **Evidence.** "Both the Na'aran Conduit and the Pools Complex were planned and executed at the same time" (p. 78).
- **Evidence.** The conduit came from the west, turned north 7.4 m west of the southern pool, ran 6.8 m, turned east again, and ran "close to the southern wall of the northern pool", attached to it "to prevent subsidence"; it then ran about 13 m east to the Plaza's edge and turned south (p. 78).
- **Evidence.** That whole section "has been missing ever since the time when the pools were combined in the days of Herod"; it "can be reconstructed with precision on the basis of the surviving sections on either side" (p. 78).
- **Evidence — levels (p. 79).** Conduit bottom about +100.10 at the Plaza's west edge and about +99.50 near the pools (about 3%). Top of the pools about +100.40; the intended maximum water level +100.10–100.20. So the conduit was about 60 cm too low to fill the pools directly. Conduit about 40–50 cm high, plus 20–25 cm of slabs and floor.

## Filling the pools (pp. 80–84)

- **Evidence.** The planners, Netzer says, did not foresee that the pools could not be filled from the conduit between them (p. 80).
- **Evidence — first solution (pp. 80–82).** A minor channel left the conduit's sidewall 7.4 m west of the southern pool (conduit bottom +100.08), through a "platform" (130–140 cm wide), then through wall W239 at +100.40, south 150 cm, and east at +100.23 toward the southern pool. "We assume that at this initial stage the northern pool received its water from the southern one via a small channel laid above the Na'aran Conduit" (p. 82).
- **Evidence — second solution (p. 82).** A new minor channel, built into the conduit's northern sidewall from further west, followed it and "only in its last 4.5 m did it diverge ... up to the northern swimming pool (see Ill. 112)". Stage 4, "theoretically even during Stage 3". "Only some parts of the channel are visible, as later another narrow channel, issuing from Settling Tank A(B)99, was built right on top of it."
- **Evidence — last Hasmonean system (pp. 83–84).** In Stage 6 Settling Tank A(B)99 (90 × 90 × 90 cm) fed two channels: the southern into the southern pool, the northern "built on top of and into (practically replacing) the earlier channel ... supplying water to the northern swimming pool"; "these two channels are preserved along almost their entire lengths" (p. 84). "Unfortunately, we do not know exactly how the combined pool [A(B)101] was supplied with water in the Herodian period" (p. 84).
- **Image (Ill. 112, p. 80; viewed in the Drive copy).** A short section of the covered conduit with its slabs in place, seen from the south-west, and a plastered channel running alongside it. The caption: "Note the channel emerging northward in order to feed the northern swimming pool." The point where the channel meets the pool wall is not in the frame.

## Inference for entry 29

- The northern early pool has a recorded supply: a channel of Stage 4 (or 3), replaced in Stage 6 by a channel from A(B)99, preserved "along almost their entire lengths". The text gives no inlet, aperture or level at the pool wall itself, and no plan of that point in the pages read; Plan 14, the pool plan, is a pocket sheet not in the scan.
- The levels (pool top +100.40, water +100.10–100.20, conduit bottom +99.50 by the pools) are new for the repo.
- The main conduit was attached to the southern wall of the northern pool. That relation is part of the reconstructed (missing) section, not an observed contact.
- The supply dates are Hasmonean stages (before Herod's joining of the pools), so they do not date a northern-pool inlet in the window. The ratings (R 1, P 3, F 2, D 2) do not change. The decisive record is now Plan 14 and any section at the northern channel's end.
