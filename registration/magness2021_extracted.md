## 8. Consequences for the project identifications

| Status | Project target | Result of this cross-check |

| Inference | **T20 — Feshkha/Ghuweir, entries 23, 25, 26** | The two spring settlements must remain separate candidates with different evidence. Both complexes are Herodian in Magness’s reassessment; Feshkha’s destruction plausibly coincides with Qumran in 68 CE, while Ghuweir’s end remains uncertain. Her account does not identify either with these Copper Scroll entries or establish a cave/heap/trench configuration at Feshkha. |

| Inference | **R01 — Qumran waterworks, entries 20–23** | Numerous named pools/channels support an extensive hydraulic setting, including pre-31 BCE use. | Inference | **ha-Melaḥ — entries 6, 13, 14** | An eastern cemetery and stepped water installations exist, but they do not establish the project’s named locality or the linked cistern/pit/tomb relationships. ## What the book does NOT say in the sections checked

- **[Evidence — absence within scope; (a)]** No exact foundation year for Qumran; no demonstrated site-wide abandonment after 31 BCE or around 9/8 BCE in Magness’s latest chronology; no evidence requiring earthquake and fire to coincide. Her 1995 gap belongs to an earlier argument. Precise causes/dates of every localized alteration are not supplied.
- **[Evidence — absence within scope; (b)]** No absolute date for every channel, pool widening, blocked entrance, partition or floor. No complete synchronism of locus floors with de Vaux’s phases. No feature is explicitly identified as ha-Melaḥ’s cistern “under the steps” or pit “on its north side”; no equation of R01/entries 20–23 with particular loci is given.
- **[Evidence — absence within scope; (c)]** No exact L120 burial year in her current model; no proof it was buried in 68 CE; no container typology more specific than three pots; no complete reliable reconstruction of each pot’s coin contents after mixing; no full publication of the hoard or Seyrig’s notes; no definitive temple-tax identification.
- **[Evidence — absence within scope; (d)]** No proof Feshkha or Ghuweir was sectarian or belonged to Qumran’s community; no secure date for Ghuweir’s earlier fire or final abandonment; no identified industry at Feshkha; no particular nearby Feshkha cave, stone heap or ancient trench corresponding to the project’s entries. No ancient cemetery at Feshkha comparable with Qumran’s is described; the specific graves reported there are Bedouin.
- **[Evidence — absence within scope; (e)]** No reliable dates for all individual Qumran burials; no complete excavated burial count or secure total population; no complete metric extent of the cemetery; no named built chamber tomb or hard-rock-cut burial cave identified with entry 14. A cemetery east of Qumran is not an identification of that entry’s tomb.
- **[Evidence — scope limit, (a)–(e)]** No explicit Copper Scroll concordance for T20, R01, entries 6/13/14/20–26, or the ha-Melaḥ = Kh. Qumran identification occurs in these passages. These absence statements apply to the requested sections, not to an unperformed search of the entire book.
