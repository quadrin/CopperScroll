# Run 12 rule: entry 22's "fissure" is the 31 BCE earthquake crack at Qumran

Fixed 27 Oct 2026 before any new evidence was gathered for this run. Exploratory. Prior exposure: run 1 found a crack east of L71 (Magen & Peleg 2018 pp. 51, 67, Fig. 71) and noted de Vaux's crack through L48/49 and L50 (de Vaux 1973 p. 20).

## Hypothesis
Entry 22: "In the fissure (בסדק) that is in Secacah, east of the Reservoir of Solomon: vessels of offering, and their record beside them." A fissure is not a normal hiding place. At Kh. Qumran a long earthquake crack, which de Vaux dated to 31 BCE, runs through the site. If Sekakah is Qumran, the "fissure" may be that crack, and the "Reservoir of Solomon" a reservoir lying just west of it.

## Predictions (each can fail)
- **E1:** a published plan shows a reservoir or large plastered pool lying **west of** the earthquake crack line, with the crack within 15 m of its east side.
- **E2:** the crack was open (not filled and built over) at some time in 31 BCE–68 CE. Evidence: de Vaux's and Magen & Peleg's statements on repairs, fills and the crack's later history.
- **E3:** the reservoir in E1 is distinctive enough to carry a name: among the 3 largest at the site by capacity (Galor's list, Magen & Peleg's dimensions).
- **E4 (control):** apply E1–E3 to Hyrcania and Kh. es-Samra, the places that tied with Qumran in runs 1 and 7, using any recorded crack, fault or fissure.

## Records
De Vaux 1973 (Schweich) pp. 20–24 and plans; de Vaux's RB preliminary reports 1954–56 (plans with the crack); Humbert & Chambon 1994 plans; Magen & Peleg 2018 Figs. 9, 71–75; Galor's reservoir list; the repo's registration of Magen & Peleg Fig. 9 (research/assets/plans/imagery-registration-2026-10-22/). Report each crack segment's mapped course.

## Decision
- **Supported** (exploratory): E1–E3 pass at Qumran, and E1 fails or is unknown at both controls.
- **Rejected:** the crack passes no reservoir within 15 m on its west side (E1 fail), or the crack was sealed by 31 BCE-period rebuilding throughout (E2 fail).
- Otherwise **not identifiable**. Report the crack's full course, every reservoir within 30 m of it, and which side each lies on.
