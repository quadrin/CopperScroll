# Run 12 result: entry 22's "fissure" as the earthquake crack at Qumran

Integrated 27 Oct 2026. Rule: [RULE.md](RULE.md), commit a76791d, SHA-256 `adc5d0b9…f712`. Exploratory.

## Verdict
**Supported (exploratory) on the literal branch, but fragile.** It is the first sandbox run to pass, and the pass rests on a low-resolution copy of one plate, a 5 m³ margin, and a dating dispute.

**Branch A (literal: de Vaux's plotted fault, 1973 Pl. XXXIX).** The fault runs N–S through L39/L51, L50, L48/49, L66, L73, L72, then bends SW past L76.
- **E1 pass:** the crack lies **3.3–3.6 m east of the east end of L56/58** (the "long cistern"). Smaller pools also lie west of it within 15 m (L57 ~5 m, L67 ~7 m, L55 and L34 ~12–14 m).
- **E2 pass, contested:** de Vaux 1973 pp. 20–21, 27: L49 and L50 were split by the crack, not repaired, abandoned in Period II. Magen & Peleg 2018 pp. 41–42: L48–49 sank and went out of use by the end of phase B (before 68 CE). Against: Humbert 2016 pp. 25, 119, "le séisme n'a pas affecté Qumrân avant son abandon".
- **E3 pass only if L56/58 is one installation:** Galor 2006 Table 1 p. 259: L71 300 m³, L91 250, **L56/58 115**, L110 110 (3rd by 5 m³; North and Laperrousaz rank it 2nd or 3rd). L58 alone (85 m³) is 4th, so E3 would fail. Treating it as one is defensible: de Vaux p. 27 (divided only in Period II); Magen & Peleg p. 44 ("an integral part of pool L56").
- **E4 controls: unknown at both.** Hyrcania: no crack recorded at the double pool (Patrich 1989 pp. 254–255, Figs. 22–23); Mar Saba Site 70 mentions unlocated cistern-wall fissures. Kh. es-Samra: no fissure recorded (Cross & Milik p. 11; Mar Saba Site 91).

**Other branches (deviations):** B, Magness's SW–NE crack in the western building: E1 fails. C, Magen & Peleg's eastern-dump cracks: E1 passes for L71 (run 1), but E2 is unknown — Magen & Peleg p. 67: "no evidence that the cracks resulted from an earthquake, or that they came into existence in 31 BCE". D, paved-square cracks south of L77: unknown.

## What changes
On this reading the "Reservoir of Solomon" is **L56/58, not L71**: L71 lies east of the crack. Runs 1 and 7 used L71 for J5 and J6; they have not been re-scored. Corroboration: Magen & Peleg p. 43 report that de Vaux attributed the sinking of the east end of W602 to the crack, and Fig. 100 shows W602 along the L54–L57 cells.

## Caveats
1. The fault line was traced from a 573×699 px web copy of Pl. XXXIX (deadseaquake.info), reading the dash-dot symbol as the fault on J. Williams' note there; not checked against the original legend. Tracing error ~±1 m.
2. E3's margin is 5 m³.
3. E2 depends on de Vaux and Magness against Humbert.

## Decisive next records
