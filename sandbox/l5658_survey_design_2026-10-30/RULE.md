# Run 24 rule: could a non-invasive survey test the L56/58 lead?

Fixed 30 Oct 2026, before any evidence is gathered. Owner request, 30 Oct 2026 10:50 America/Los_Angeles: "#6, 7, 8 seem interesting". Exploratory. Sandbox rules in [../README.md](../README.md) apply. Nothing here proposes excavation, and nothing is sent to anyone.

## Starting point
Run 12 (with addendum A) puts entry 22's "fissure that is in Secacah, east of the Reservoir of Solomon" at the 31 BCE crack in de Vaux 1973 Pl. XXXIX, 2.3–5 m east of L56/58. Run 17 links entries 21 and 23 weakly. Entry 22 gives no depth and no weight: "vessels of offering, and their record beside them".

## Question
Is there a survey that, if the deposit were there, would very probably detect it, so that a null result would count against the lead? If no such survey exists, the lead cannot be tested without digging, and the record should say so.

## Gate G0 (applied first)
From the excavation records (de Vaux 1973 and the 1996/2003 field notes; Humbert & Chambon; Magen & Peleg 2018), find what was dug at and east of L56/58, and to what depth: the cistern interior, its floor, the strip between L58's east face and the crack, and the crack's fill. Record each as **excavated below floor**, **excavated to floor**, **not excavated** or **unknown**, with page and figure.
- If the whole target zone (below) was excavated to below the depth a deposit could lie, the verdict is **already tested by excavation**, and the run reports what was found there. The survey part is then reported only for completeness.

## Target zone (fixed)
- **Z1 (entry 22):** the crack line for 15 m along its length opposite L56/58, from the surface to 3 m deep, 2 m either side of the line.
- **Z2 (entry 21, secondary):** the conduit head named by run 17, 3 cubits below the surface.
- **Z3 (entry 23, secondary):** the strip 60 cubits from the channel toward the nearest heap considered in run 17, 3 cubits deep.

## Target branches (fixed)
- **T-a:** a group of metal vessels, total silver or bronze mass 5 kg (small) or 50 kg (large).
- **T-b:** one 1st-century storage jar or chest of metal vessels (a void or a contrast body about 0.6 × 0.6 × 0.8 m).
- **T-c:** a scroll-sized object alone ("their record"). Expected undetectable; included so the plan says so.
Talents are not used for entry 22, because it gives none. For Z2 and Z3, the entry's talents use the project's value 21.3 kg per talent and a low value of 10 kg, as two branches.

## Methods considered (all, equally)
GPR (several frequencies); electrical resistivity tomography (ERT); electromagnetic induction (EMI, conductivity and in-phase); metal detection (pulse induction, large loop); magnetometry; muon imaging (method 5); microgravity. Each method gets the same assessment.

## Inputs to source
Soil: marl, salinity, moisture, plaster and stone fill at Qumran (wave 1 T09 notes GPR performs poorly in saline marl; check the source behind it). Published geophysics at Qumran (Reeder & Jol 2002/2006; any later survey). Published detection depths for each method against targets of these sizes in comparable soil. Clutter: walls, plaster, modern conservation, metal from earlier digs, tourist walkways.

## Output per method and zone
Expected detection probability P(detect | target present) for each target branch, as a range with its basis (published case, physics estimate, or INFERENCE); main reason it fails; false-positive sources; whether a null is informative.

## Criteria (fixed)
- **Testable:** at least one method (or a pair) gives P(detect | T-a large or T-b) ≥ 0.8 in Z1, on a published or physics basis, with its false positives manageable by a second method.
- **Partly testable:** P between 0.5 and 0.8, or ≥ 0.8 only for T-a large.
- **Not testable non-invasively:** below 0.5 for T-a large and T-b.
- **Already tested:** gate G0, above.

## Controls (same assessment)
The same plan is assessed at L71 (the other cistern of runs 1 and 12, east of the crack) and at Hyrcania's double pool (run 1 control), so that the method choice is not tuned to the favourite. Their soils and clutter are sourced the same way.

## Also reported
Who grants permits for survey at the site (name the authority from a current source; no assumptions), and an order-of-magnitude cost and duration for the best plan, marked as estimate.
