# Run 9 scoring conventions (written before the score table)

Written after step 1 was hashed and after reading Netzer 2001 pp. 22–76 (pdf 26–80) and Netzer 1981 (text search only), before any pair was scored.

- **Unit.** One building (or building complex) per row, as the inventory source defines it. Every entry is scored against every building in the inventory (a building without the loanword feature fails the loanword relation).
- **Region.** 31.40–32.55 N, 35.00–35.60 E. Buildings outside it (Masada, Machaerus, Caesarea, Kh. el-Muraq if west of 35.00) are listed and scored but marked `out`, and left out of the decision counts. A sensitivity line gives the counts with them.
- **Date.** The feature must be dated by its source to 100 BCE–135 CE. A building with no window date is listed with `date=unknown` and kept in the count as unknown (missing data is unknown).
- **pass**: a source reports the feature in the relation the entry states.
- **fail**: (a) the source states that the feature is absent there, or (b) the place was reported cleared or excavated and the source's own inventory of that feature at the building puts every example elsewhere, or (c) the only example the source reports contradicts the relation (e.g. it calls the cistern small where the entry says great, or it lies on the wrong side).
- **unknown**: anything else, including features a source does not mention.
- **Textual-only features** (known from Josephus or the New Testament, not from excavation or survey) are scored `pass(text)`. They count as pass in the main count. A sensitivity count treats them as unknown.
- **Relation-level evidence follows the best candidate feature** at the building: if any recorded feature could satisfy the relation, the relation is scored on it.
- **Pair result**: `lead` if every relation passes; `no-fail` if none fails; `fail` if any fails.
- **Names** (Koḥlit, Zadok, Mattiyah) are unknown unless a source ties the name to that building. Unclear-sense relations (3.R4 חליא) are unknown everywhere.
- **Depth relations** (12.R3 nine cubits) are unknown everywhere unless a source gives a deposit or bedrock depth at that corner.
