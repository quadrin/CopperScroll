## Exploratory follow-up (INFERENCE; ADAJ gives no denominations)
Under any denomination mix of about 1.05–3.2 kg, some ʿEin Feshkha pair passes all four conditions: all coins at 3.8 g (denarii/drachms) → entry 26 at karsh light (p = 0.56); Roman tetradrachms + Nabataean drachms (3,195 g) → entry 25 (light and heavy) and entry 26 (heavy) (p = 0.32–0.55). Not significant, and the hoard closes under Hadrian (≥117 CE); ADAJ reads it as loot from "a hold-up". The way forward is the coins themselves (disposition "Department of Antiquities, Jordan?") or Callegher's Qumran/Feshkha coin volumes.
