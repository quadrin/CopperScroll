## Identity of places
A Bar-Adon site is the place named above if its printed grid (old Palestine grid) is within 300 m of the position the earlier run fixed, after conversion. Names alone do not decide it. If two sites qualify, both are reported. If none does, the place is "not in Bar-Adon" for that unit.

## What can change a verdict
- **Pass:** Bar-Adon describes the feature the criterion needs, at the position or relation the criterion needs (for example a cistern and a crack east of it), with a period that meets J7 on the lenient reading.
- **Fail:** Bar-Adon gives a plan or a description that positions the relevant features and shows the relation is impossible (for example the only reservoir has nothing on its east side because the site ends there). A fail needs a measured plan or explicit positions.
- **Silence stays unknown.** A site description that does not mention a fissure, cairn or pool does not make it absent.
- Every unit is reported, including those that stay unknown.
