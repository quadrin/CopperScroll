# Run 5 rule: if the pit is in Janoaḥ, Koḥlit is a mound just south of Yanun

Fixed 27 Oct 2026 before any analysis for this run. Exploratory. Conditional on the Janoaḥ reading of XII 10 (Lefkovits, Beyer), which the text evidence leans against (claude/janoah_independent_test_plan_2026-10-26.md).

## Hypothesis
XII 10 puts the pit "in Janoaḥ, north of Koḥlit". With Janoaḥ at Kh. Yanun (Israel Ref. 18425/17385) or Yanun village (18370/17245), Koḥlit lies south of Yanun. One place must then fit all Koḥlit entries (Linear B):
- **K1** (entry 4): a mound (tell or khirbe on raised ground) with occupation of the Hellenistic or Roman period.
- **K2** (entry 11): a pool or reservoir on its east side (bearing 45°–135°, within 500 m).
- **K3** (entries 19, 60): two or more pits, shafts or cisterns north of it (bearing 315°–45°, within 1 km), one of them "eastern" relative to another.
- **K4** (entry 60): Yanun lies within 15 km and within 45° of due north of it (so "north of Koḥlit" holds for a pit at Yanun).

## Candidates and controls
- **Candidates:** every site 2–15 km from either Yanun point in the sector 135°–225°, with Hellenistic or Roman material, from the West Bank Archaeological Database (WBADB, as used in research/rarity/kohlit), Finkelstein et al., *Highlands of Many Cultures* (Drive), and the SWP Memoirs. Aqraba (Akrabeta) is expected among them; it is not given special status.
- **Controls:** the same criteria applied to sites in the mirror sector 315°–45° (north of Yanun), 2–15 km. The text predicts no fit there; if control sites fit K1–K3 as often as southern ones, the cluster does not discriminate.
- List both sets, with coordinates and sources, before scoring any of K2–K3.

## Scoring
pass / unknown / fail for K1–K3 per site, as in run 1 (pass needs a published survey or excavation record; silence is unknown). **Joint fit:** K1–K4 all pass. Report every site.
- **Supported relative to controls** (exploratory): at least one southern joint fit, and the share of southern joint fits more than twice the northern share.
- Cross-check: the workbench's contradicted pairing (Yanun with Kh. el-Marjama, pool west of the tell) must come out failing K2 here too; if not, the rule is mis-applied.
