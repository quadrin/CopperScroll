## Verdict
**The model survives, but is not preferred over the control.** Neither new check fails; the Qumran control does as well or better.

(J1–J6, J8, J9; J7 folded in.) Alternative scorings (control J1 and J9 as in run 1; queried graves not counted) leave the model at best tied.

- **J8 (features within 6 km): pass.** Dam 44 to the Samra reservoir is 4.96 km along the valley (4.48 km smoothed); largest pair 5.19 km.
- **J9 (entry 24's tomb on a Jericho–Samra route): weak pass.** Mar Saba Site 19: eight stone-capped mounds recorded as "graves?", in a tributary of Wadi Khashna on the Hyrcania Valley road, ~200 m S of Cross & Milik's north entrance to the Buqeiʿa (BC3). Undated.
- **J5 still unknown** at both: Samra's 18×22 m cistern has no recorded fissure and no date.

## Structural finding
**Naḥal Sekhakha is a tributary of Wadi Qumran** on the DEM. It joins ~3.4 km below the model's downstream point and makes up 19.33 of the control's 46.76 km² catchment. So the "valley of Secacah" relation cannot separate Samra from Qumran: Qumran's whole drainage contains Samra's valley.

## Correction to run 1 (sandbox; no main record affected)
Run 1 scored Qumran J1 (Jericho route) unknown. Cross & Milik 1956 p. 7 (and n. 4, citing de Vaux 1953) record "the road west from Qumran and ʿEn Feshkha" meeting the Jericho–Bethlehem track at Kh. Abu Ṭabaq. On that source Qumran J1 passes. Run 1's RESULT is left as written; this note supersedes its J1 cell for Qumran.

## Exploratory follow-ups
- A reservoir with a crevice to its east exists in the model valley, but is Byzantine and outside S: Mar Saba Site 63 (EN pp. 78–79), 5×8 m reservoir at 18418/12577, a structure "near crevice in S face of gorge" ~20 m east; 0.6 km below dam 44, ~3.1 km from Samra.
- Wright 1961 pp. 19–20 calls the Wadi Kuteif staircase (Milik's entry 24 tomb, D12) identical to the stepped tunnels in Naḥal Sekhakha (Mar Saba Site 66).
- Records that would change scores: a dated record of dam 44 or the northern aqueduct (Haber et al. 2024; Mount Hyrcania Project report, both on Drive, not opened); Stager 1976's plan of the Samra cistern; a dated plan of the Site 19 mounds.
