## Exploratory notes
- Coming from Jericho through the Buqeiʿa, the Hyrcania tunnels lie *past* Hyrcania. They fit "on the way from Jericho to Secacah" only via the plateau route or if Secacah lies at or west of Hyrcania — which run 7's Kh. es-Samra model does not give.
- Records that would move strict T3–T5: Gutfeld 2006 (*BAR* 32.5) full text and any final report; a dated description of the Wadi Abu Shaʿle path; Peleg 2009 on the Wadi Ekteif tombs; a period for Mugharet Ekteif.
