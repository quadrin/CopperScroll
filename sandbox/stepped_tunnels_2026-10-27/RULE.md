# Run 16 rule: entry 24's "tomb in the Wadi of the Vault" is a rock-cut stepped tunnel

Fixed 27 Oct 2026 before any new evidence was gathered for this run. Exploratory. Prior exposure: Milik (D12) took the Wadi Kuteif staircase as "probably a tomb"; Wright 1961 pp. 19–20 called it identical to the stepped tunnels in Naḥal Sekhakha (run 7).

## Hypothesis
Entry 24 (V 12–14): "In the tomb (קבר) that is in the Wadi of the Vault (נחל הכפא), on the way from Jericho to Secacah, dig seven cubits: 32 talents." "Kippa" can mean a vault or arch. The Judean Desert has a small class of deep rock-cut **stepped tunnels** in wadi sides (Wadi Kuteif; Naḥal Sekhakha below Hyrcania; others). If the class was understood as tombs (or contained burials), one member on a Jericho–Sekakah route in a vaulted or arched setting is the best fit for entry 24.

## Steps
1. **Inventory (fixed by sources, not by fit):** every recorded rock-cut stepped tunnel or stepped descending shaft in wadi sides between Jericho and Wadi en-Nar/Qumran (Patrich 1994; Cross & Milik 1956; Wright 1961; Bar-Adon; ASI 109/4, 109/5, 109/7 online records; Hyrcania reports incl. Morgan 2002, Haber & Gutfeld 2024). Write the list to a file with its SHA-256 before step 2.
2. **Score each tunnel** pass / unknown / fail on: T1 in a wadi (נחל); T2 vaulted or arched element at the entrance or inside (built or cut); T3 burial evidence (bones, ossuaries, loculi) or a source calling it a tomb; T4 on a documented Jericho–Qumran or Jericho–Buqeiʿa route (as run 7 J9); T5 period evidence for 100 BCE–135 CE (cutting, use or finds); T6 depth: a digging depth of 7 cubits (~3.1–3.7 m) is physically possible in its floor fill (fill depth recorded ≥3 m, or unknown).
3. **Control class:** ordinary rock-cut tombs (loculus tombs, shaft tombs) in the same wadis, scored on the same T1–T6.

## Decision
- **Lead:** a tunnel passing T1, T3 and T4 with no fail.
- **Class supported** (exploratory): stepped tunnels give a lead and score better on T1–T6 than the ordinary-tomb controls (more passes, no more fails, per member on average).
- Report every member of both classes.
