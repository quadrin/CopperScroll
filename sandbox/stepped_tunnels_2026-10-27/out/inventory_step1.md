# Run 16 (stepped_tunnels_2026-10-27), step 1 inventory and scoring protocol

Written before the step-2 scoring table was filled. Membership is fixed by what the sources record, not by fit.

## Inclusion rule (literal reading of RULE.md step 1)
A member is a rock-cut tunnel or shaft that descends by cut steps, cut into a wadi side or cliff, recorded in a source, between Jericho (N) and Wadi en-Nar/Kidron and Qumran (S/E), inside the area of ASI maps 109/4, 109/5, 109/7 and the Qumran–Buqeia surveys.
- Strict class A: features recorded as a tunnel, staircase or gallery (not as a cistern).
- Broad add-on B (sensitivity only): tunnel cisterns or reservoirs that sources call a tunnel and record with descending steps.
- Excluded: natural cave passages without steps (Abeles 2002, Baruch 2002, Dahari 2002 rows in WBADB); open reservoirs with stairs (Bir Khashna, Patrich Site 9; Bir el-Malqi, ASI 109/4/91); summit cisterns with stairs (Hyrcania cistern P, Patrich 1989 p. 257); Mar Saba Complex 35 corridor (built steps, level corridor; Patrich 1994 Site 98); the barrel-vaulted aqueduct tunnel at Hyrcania (point 49; no steps); paths with steps.

## Scoring protocol (fixed here)
- T1 wadi: pass if a source places it in a wadi/valley side or bed; fail if a source places it on a hilltop, summit terrace or hillocks away from a wadi; unknown otherwise.
- T2 vault/arch: pass if a source records an arched or vaulted element at the entrance or inside; fail if a source describes the closure/roof and it is flat or a slab; unknown otherwise.
- T3 burial: pass if human bones, ossuaries or loculi are recorded, or a source calls it a tomb (strict: identifies it as one; lenient: also proposes tomb as its function); fail if a source identifies a non-funerary function and no human remains are recorded; unknown otherwise.
- T4 route: strict pass if a documented and described Jericho-Qumran or Jericho-Buqeia route (Cross & Milik 1956 pp. 7, 9 n. 9; Patrich 1994 EN pp. 5-6 road D and routes C1-C3; Dorsey 1991) crosses the feature's ravine within 500 m; lenient also counts mapped but undated paths whose labelled destination is Jericho or the Buqeia (e.g. Wright 1961 Fig. 1 "approach road from Jericho"; Patrich 1989 Fig. 1); fail if the position is known and no route of either kind comes within 1 km; unknown otherwise.
- T5 period: pass if cutting, use or finds are dated within 100 BCE-135 CE; fail if the only dating evidence is outside it; unknown otherwise.
- T6 depth: pass if fill is recorded to 3 m or more (sources' "depth" of fill or clearance); fail if the recorded fill or pit depth is under 3 m; unknown if not recorded.
- Decision as RULE.md. Per-member averages of passes and fails are compared for the class test.
