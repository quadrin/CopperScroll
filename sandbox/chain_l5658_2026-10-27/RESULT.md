# Run 17 result: following the chain from L56/58

Integrated 27 Oct 2026. Rule: [RULE.md](RULE.md), commit ebabf7e. Conditional on run 12's fragile lead. Exploratory.

## Verdict
**The chain holds under the rule's wording, but only weakly.** No link fails: C1 is unknown, C2 and C3 pass. C1's actual prediction — a *great* heap of stones or boulder 24–35 m from the channel — is not met. The chain survives only because ordinary heaps lie within 50 m, so the fail clause does not trigger. Under a stricter reading ("no great heap or boulder"), **C1 fails and the chain is broken**.

- **C1 (entry 23, Trench of Solomon → great heap, 60 cubits): unknown.** No great heap or boulder is recorded within 60 m of L56/58 or within 50 m of any channel on Magen & Peleg Fig. 9. Ordinary heaps lie close by: grave heaps 22.7 m+ from the channel (Reeder's 2002 map, Freund 2012 Fig. 3.1); "piles of masonry" at the sluice L137 (de Vaux 1973 p. 9); Period Ib debris heaps 0–13 m (p. 25). The tower's stone glacis is 21.2 m from L56/58, still short of 24–35 m. Counting graves as "the heap" (rogem as burial heap) gives a pass, but one that cannot discriminate: a field of ~1,100 graves gives a heap at every distance beyond ~23 m.
- **C2 (the crack is inside Secacah): pass, and discriminating.** The fault 3.3–3.6 m east of L58 runs inside the main building's eastern wing, between L58 (with the channel along its east wall W607) and room L74, kiln L66 and L43 (de Vaux p. 20; Magen & Peleg pp. 41–44, Figs. 46–47). At L71, the reservoir runs 1 and 7 used, the crack lies in the eastern refuse dump outside the walls.
- **C3 (entry 21, one water system): pass, but weak.** The aqueduct feeds the sluice and settling basin (L132/137), the central channel, settling pool L83, then L56, with L58 filling over the partition wall W609 (de Vaux pp. 8–10; Galor 2006 pp. 262–263; Magen & Peleg pp. 43–44, 90, 94). Almost every Qumran pool would pass this.
- **C4:** not re-scored (no independent new source).

## Exploratory notes
- On Fig. 100 the channel wraps the north and east sides of L56/58, and the Pl. XXXIX fault runs ~2 m east of the channel leg along W607. "The Trench of Solomon" and the fissure would then run side by side. New observation, not tested.
