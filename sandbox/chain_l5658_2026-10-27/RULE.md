# Run 17 rule: follow the chain from L56/58 (Ephesus method)

Fixed 27 Oct 2026 before any new evidence was gathered for this run. Exploratory, and conditional on run 12's fragile lead (entry 22's Reservoir of Solomon = Qumran L56/58, beside de Vaux's earthquake fault).

## Idea
Wood found the Artemision by following one fixed point to the next. If L56/58 is "the Reservoir of Solomon" (entry 22), the neighbouring entries make predictions about what lies near it. Each link below uses a relation **not** used to choose L56/58, so each can fail.

## Links
- **C1 (entry 23, Solomon branch):** "From the Trench of Solomo[n] as far as the great heap of stones (הרגם; Puech הרגב, boulder), sixty cubits." If the reservoir is Solomon's, "the Trench of Solomon" is a channel belonging to the same installation: the channel that feeds or leaves L56/58 (Milik 1960: the site's main distribution channel). **Prediction:** a recorded great stone heap or boulder lies 24–35 m (60 cubits at 0.444–0.525 m, ±10%) from that channel, measured along the channel's course or from its end. **Fail:** the channel's whole course is on a published plan and no heap or boulder is recorded within 0–50 m of it.
- **C2 (entry 22 itself):** the "fissure" must be **in Secacah** and hold "vessels of offering and their record beside them". **Prediction:** the crack segment east of L56/58 lies inside the settlement, not in a dump or outside the walls. **Fail:** that segment lies outside the main building.
- **C3 (entry 21):** "At the head of the water conduit of the valley of Secacah, on the north side" — the aqueduct's head should be the head of the system that ends in L56/58. **Prediction:** the main aqueduct's course reaches L56/58 directly or through one distribution channel (no separate source). **Fail:** L56/58 is fed only by a different conduit.
- **C4 (entry 20):** a cairn or dam in Wadi Qumran (run 1 already: cemetery heaps at the lenient width; dam unknown). Re-score only with any new source.

## Records
Magen & Peleg 2018 Figs. 9, 82, 100 and the channel descriptions (pp. 76–90); de Vaux 1973 pp. 8–11 and Pls.; Ilan & Amit 2002 (aqueduct and distribution); Galor 2006; the repo's traced plan (research/models/traced_plans/qumran-settlement.json) and registration (research/assets/plans/imagery-registration-2026-10-22/qumran.json).

## Decision
- **Chain holds** (exploratory) if C1–C3 pass, or pass/unknown with no fail.
- **Chain broken** if any of C1–C3 fails. A broken chain does not reject run 12, but it removes the support that neighbouring entries would add.
- Report every channel, heap and boulder within 60 m of L56/58 with measured distances.
