## Conventions used

- `feature_id`: `qum3a:E<n>` for ch. 11 elements (`E270A`, `E270B`, `E951a`, `E951b` as printed), `qum3a:L<n>` for loci (`L48-49`, `L119bis`). `site_name` = "Khirbet Qumrân" on every row.
- `grid_printed` = the "Coord." cell as printed (including "(D4" with no closing bracket at E258, "(E/5)" at E326, "G/H/I 4-5" at E951a); `grid_system = unknown`; `position_basis = plan`; `notes` start with "IIIA plate grid". `figure` = the element's "Planche" (e.g. `pl. II`). Loci have no plate column, so `figure` is blank.
- Class translation: mur, cloison, cloisonnement, partition, murette, clôture, contrefort, renforcement, soutènement, blocage, bord/bordure (edge walls of pools, cisterns and channels), glacis, reprise/restauration of a wall → `wall`; porte, passage, entrée, seuil, ouverture → `doorway`; escalier, emmarchement, degré(s) → `steps`; canal, aqueduc, drain, évacuation, dérivation, alimentation, raccordement, collecteur, buse → `conduit`; trop-plein, déversoir, bouche → `outlet`; puisard → `pit`; pilier, pile, socle → `pillar`; bassin/cuve/bac inside a room, and "petit bassin" loci without steps → `basin`; "bassin à degrés", "bassin profond", other bassin loci → `pool`; réservoir → `reservoir`; citerne (L. 110) → `cistern`; "bain rituel" (L. 138) → `ritual_bath`; chambre, couloir, réduit, atelier, compartiment, appentis, passage loci → `room`; "espace à ciel ouvert"/cour → `court`; niche, placard, banquette, four, vanne, margelle, silo, caisson, sols and pavements, saillant, lines of stones, moulin, portique, fenêtre → `other`. "Tranchée antique = faille 3 séisme" → `fissure` (construction `natural`).
- Loci are classed from their own ch. 12 entry. Exceptions: L. 161–164 are keyed `other` because ch. 11 (E514–516, E525) calls them silos; L. 8 "Cage d'escalier" is keyed `steps` (head noun), L. 17 "Couloir à degrés" and L. 85 "Passage à degrés" are keyed `room` with `steps_where = inside`.
- `construction = built` on every ch. 11 row (the list is of "éléments construits"); blank on loci.
- Relations: for elements, the loci in the entry are `next_to` for walls and doorways and `inside` for installations. A single locus also becomes `parent_id`. A niche or recess "dans le mur N" has `parent_id = E<N>`. Repairs, restorations and narrowings ("reprise/restauration/rétrécissement de N", "vestige du mur N") have `parent_id = E<N>`. "sous" → `below`, "au-dessus"/"sur" → `above`, "vers"/"au bassin" → `leads_to`, "de"/"depuis"/"aval de"/"dérivé de" → `fed_by`. For loci, every element number in the entry (walls between asterisks, doors, drains, stairs) is keyed `next_to:qum3a:E<n>` (per QUMRAN_NOTES), except where the entry says a channel feeds it (L. 50 ← 337, L. 67 ← 346, L. 136 ← 650: `fed_by`). "traversé par le drain/aqueduc N" is keyed `next_to` and noted, because the schema has no "crossed by" relation.
- `n_openings` on loci = the number of doors/passages the entry lists (as printed, including mis-numbered ones, which are noted). On elements: the kilns E317/E318 ("gueule au nord/sud": 1, N/S), the tower entrance E385 ("par le sud": 1, S), the exterior door E399 ("dans le front nord": 1, N) and E510 (three "regards verticaux": 3).
- Plaster: `yes` only where the text says "enduit(e)(s)" (E417, E756). Mortar floors, basins and vats ("fond/sol/bassin/cuve de mortier": 13 rows, including L. 173) are keyed `unknown`, with "mortier" in `notes`. Stone-and-mortar construction is noted only.
- Steps: n_steps only where a count is printed: E214, E229, E326 (3 each); E308 "degré" (singular) is keyed as 1 (medium). `steps_where` on stepped pools = `into_feature`; on steps rows it gives the stair's place relative to its locus (`inside`, `into_feature`, `unknown` when it lies between two loci).
- Sizes: only E951a (segments a 49 m, b 13 m, c 8 m, keyed as sub-rows E951a.a–c) and E951b (22 m). No other dimension is printed in these lists.
- Pillars: E789 portico, pillars a, b, c (3); E908 wall of four pillars A–D (4); L. 35 "pilier 302" (1); L. 77 "quatre socles" (4) and L. 86 "deux socles" (2), with the bases keyed as pillars and noted.
- Period: copied only where printed (Age du Fer, post 68, tardif/tardive, niveau 4). `in_window = no` only for the Iron Age walls E396–398, and `unknown` for the other printed periods.

## Source inconsistencies (keyed as printed, noted on the rows)

- The locus entries cite element numbers that are not in ch. 11: 337, 348, 386, 587, 821, 847, 951 (ch. 11 has 951a/b), 956, and "270" (ch. 11 has 270A/B). Some cross-references conflict: L. 8 gives the stair pier as 386 (ch. 11: E248); L. 25 lists "passages 322" (E322 is a reinforcement); L. 74 lists "porte 324" (E324 is a mortar basin); L. 77 lists "porte 854" (E854 is steps); L. 17 lists "passage 232" (a wall); L. 12/13 list "415" as a door (E415 is a floor in the doorway); L. 115/116 give "four 536/535" (pisé silos in ch. 11); L. 148 and E592 give "glacis 252" (E252 is a pisé box); L. 51 lists "porte 337" while L. 50 calls 337 a drain; L. 134 lists "porte 685" (a line of stones); E406 cites "L. 48" (the locus is L. 48-49); L. 153 is a surface level in ch. 12 but a half of pool L. 58 in E374.
- E511 "partie ouest du L. en L. 123 et 167": the locus number is missing. L. 1 "partagé en L. 2/189" (191 would be the late partition). E380 and E381 have identical entries, and so do E219/E220.
- Relation targets that have no row: L10A, L9A, L48, E270, E337, E348, E386, E587, E821, E847, E951, E956.
- 22 rows have no relation or parent (tower walls E242–245, E253–255, residence walls E269, E315, E347, site walls E785, E786, E900, E951a/b, Iron Age walls E396–398, E277, E673, E710, L. 45). They are kept for class, construction, position and, for some, period or length.
