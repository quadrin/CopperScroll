## Problems and unreadable items

- **2551:1 and 2551:2 (VII/1, VII/3): scale bars labelled 20 m.** At that scale the caves come out as about 89 × 52 m and about 133 × 62 m, which is implausible against the loci and features drawn. The sizes are entered from the printed bar, as instructed, with confidence low and a note that a 2 m bar would divide them by 10. VII/3's caption says its north is estimated.
- **2552:3 (VIII/10):** The opening count and directions are left blank, because several free ends in the outline could lead outside and the plan alone does not say which. Sizes are left blank because the only bar sits under the section, whose lengths do not match the plan's.
- **2552:4 and 2555:2 (VIII/9):** A complex of about 115 m whose east side is open along a ledge. Discrete openings cannot be counted, and there is no single main space. Openings, directions and sizes are left blank.
- **2549:1 (V/49):** There are two openings, each closed by a built wall (W1, W2). Their widths are not entered because the extent of each gap is unclear (a dashed continuation, and a dash-dot line). Confidence low.
- **2547:2 (V/50):** The directions and widths of the two small openings B and C come from small, irregular gaps (low confidence within the row).

## Conventions used

- Pillar, step and niche counts are left blank when the plan draws none. They are not entered as 0, because a plan that omits a feature does not state that there is none.
- Directions are the normal to the line joining the two rock edges of the gap. A value within 10° of a sector boundary is given as `X/Y`, with the sector containing the bearing first.
- The main space is the largest chamber. What was measured is stated in `notes`.
