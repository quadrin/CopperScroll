## Relations for one valley V
- **A1:** V has a ruin of window-period occupation (100 BCE–135 CE) with steps going down or in toward the east (entry 1).
- **A2:** V has two similar paired features (domes, mounds, tumuli or cairns, per the variants) close together, with ground between them (entry 17). Paired = within 100 m of each other.
- **A3:** the ruin (A1) and the pair (A2) both lie within V's bounds.
- **A7:** each feature used existed in 100 BCE–135 CE (as J7 in run 1; strict and lenient).

## Bounded valley
For each candidate, the valley as the candidate's proponents define it, reduced to a channel network: features within 200 m (strict) or 500 m (lenient) of a channel draining ≥0.25 km².

## Candidates and controls
- **Candidates:** Wadi Nuweiʿimeh, NE of Jericho (Milik, Puech; Eusebius "north of Jericho"); el-Buqeiʿa (Noth, Allegro, Eshel).
- **Controls:** Wadi Qelt between Jericho and the Herodian palaces; Wadi el-ʿAujah (both valleys with ruins and window-period remains that no edition proposes for Achor).

## Decision
As run 1: joint fit = A1–A3 pass with A7; discrimination only if a candidate fits jointly and no control does. Report every place, both readings, every feature considered.
