# Run 11 result: Linear B joint fit for the Valley of Achor (entries 1 and 17)

Integrated 27 Oct 2026. Rule: [RULE.md](RULE.md), commit c420100, SHA-256 `05c016db…d18c`. Exploratory.

## Verdict
**Not discriminating.** No place fits jointly under the edition reading עכור ("Valley of Achor"), and none is excluded.
- **עכור (A1 required):** A1 — a window-period ruin with steps going down or in **toward the east** — is unknown at all four places. No published record of any of the valleys gives such a stair. Best counts (A1/A2/A3/A7 pass/unknown/fail): Wadi Nuweiʿimeh 3/1/0; Wadi Qelt (control) 3/1/0; el-Buqeiʿa 2/2/0 strict, 3/1/0 lenient; Wadi el-ʿAujah (control) 0/4/0.
- **עבור "go past" (A1 dropped):** Nuweiʿimeh fits jointly (cave IV/1 and rock shelter IV/2, ~10 m apart with a path between; Sion 2002, ʿAtiqot 41 [Heb.] pp. 46–48; Jannaeus coins, late Hellenistic–Early Roman sherds; 144 m from the wadi). But the control **Wadi Qelt also fits** (the Hasmonean Twin Palaces, mirror-image mansions with a 4.5 m strip between, built 76–67 BCE; Netzer 2001 *Palaces* pp. 31–32), so the result does not discriminate.

## Method
Entry 17's paired-feature word scored under four readings: the rule's list (domes/mounds/tumuli/cairns), Puech's cavities, Milik's tamarisks, Lefkovits's buildings. Strict/lenient width (200/500 m) and dating as run 1. "Steps toward the east" read literally as a 45°–135° bearing (any eastward component as a labelled deviation). Valleys from a filled Terrarium DEM (D8, ≥0.25 km²); reach end points for the controls chosen by the agent (the rule set none). - **A1 is the bottleneck.** Decisive records: plans with stair orientation — Netzer *Final Reports* I Plans 24 and 26 (mikvaot), Zertal & Bar 2019 MHCS 5 Site 139 (Villa ʿAujah), a plan of the Jisr Abu Ghabush stepped cistern (Taha in Sparks et al. 2020 p. 277).

- **Correction candidate for the main ratings:** the Region IV cave pairs at ʿAin Duk (IV/1–IV/2, IV/5–8; Jannaeus coins, 144 m from the wadi) fit entry 17 under Puech's reading better than the V/49–V/48 pair now in ratings.csv, which does not list them.
