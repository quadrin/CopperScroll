# Evidence: candidate 2, ʿEin Feshkha

Gathered 26–27 Oct 2026 by one evidence agent (rule 2db8e8b). Condensed by the integrator; verdicts, features, distances and pages are as reported.

- **S:** centre of de Vaux's main building (OSM way 1204731303), 31.71574 N 35.45085 E, ±50 m (Hirschfeld 2004 map ref. 1929/1249, p. 37).
- **Valley:** the Nahal Peshkha / Tanur fan channel, ~110–190 m NNE of S on Survey of Palestine 1:20,000 sheet 19-12 (1940s); OSM נחל פ'שחה at 369 m N. INFERENCE: the 1:20,000 legend has no wadi symbol, so reading the thin fan lines as drainage is an inference (they start at "Waterfall" points and match OSM). De Vaux 1959 p. 233 speaks of "le Wady le plus proche".
- **Alternative valley, "lower Wadi Qumran fan" (Milik 1962):** the nearest Wadi Qumran channel is 2.57 km away, so J2 **fails** under that reading.
