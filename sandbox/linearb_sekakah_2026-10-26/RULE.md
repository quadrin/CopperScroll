# Run 1 rule: Linear B joint fit for Sekakah (entries 20–24)

Fixed 26 October 2026, before any evidence was gathered for this run. Exploratory (see the sandbox README).

## Idea

Linear B was solved because one sign value had to work in every word that used it. Here, one place must work in every entry that names it. A candidate for Sekakah is scored on all of its entries together, not entry by entry. Controls get the same search.

## Bounded areas

- **S:** within 500 m of the candidate's site point.
- **Valley:** the wadi channel nearest to S, from 3 km upstream to 3 km downstream of its closest point to S.
- **Route (J1):** any documented ancient road or track between Jericho (Tell es-Sultan or Tulul Abu el-ʿAlayiq) and S.

## Candidates

From the editions and the research files:
1. **Kh. Qumran**, with Wadi Qumran (Puech; Milik 1960).
2. **ʿEin Feshkha**, with the lower Wadi Qumran fan (Milik 1962 puts the valley by the cultivated edge of ʿEin Feshkha).
3. **Kh. es-Samra**, el-Buqeiʿa (Milik 1962: the biblical Secacah).
4. **Kh. Abu Ṭabaq**, el-Buqeiʿa (another Iron Age Buqeiʿa fort in the Josh. 15:61–62 town list discussion).
5. **Kh. el-Maqari**, el-Buqeiʿa (the third Buqeiʿa fort in the same discussion).

## Controls

Sites with water systems in the same district that no edition proposes for Sekakah:
1. **Hyrcania** (Kh. el-Mird).
2. **ʿEin el-Ghuweir.**

## Scoring

For every candidate and control, each relation is scored:
- **pass:** a published primary record (excavation, survey or measured plan) shows the feature in the bounded area. Give the source, page or figure, and the distances used.
- **unknown:** no record shows it, or the record is not at the needed scale. Silence is not absence.
- **fail:** a published record of the bounded area that would have shown the feature, and does not; or one that contradicts the geometry (for example, the reservoir has no ground to its east inside S).
- J7 is applied to each feature that passes. A feature with no evidence of existence in 100 BCE–135 CE becomes **unknown**.

## Decision

- For each place, report the best branch combination (J3-cairn or J3-dam; J4-flow or J4-side; J6 in or out) and its counts of pass, unknown and fail.
- **Joint fit:** a place fits jointly in a branch combination if every relation in it passes.
- **Discrimination:** the cluster result favours a place only if it fits jointly and no control fits jointly in any combination. If a control also fits jointly, the cluster is **not discriminating**.
- A place with any **fail** in every combination is **excluded for this cluster**. This excludes only the Sekakah assignment, not the site.
- Report all places and all combinations, including misses.

## Limits

- Most evidence on Kh. Qumran has been read before and was used to choose Qumran. This run is exploratory.
- J1 and J2 are expected to pass for every place. They are kept to show that they do not discriminate.
